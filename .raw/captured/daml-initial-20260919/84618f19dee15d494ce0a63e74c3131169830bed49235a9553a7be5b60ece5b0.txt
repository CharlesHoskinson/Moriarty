parseNumeric

Attempt to parse a
Numeric
value from a given
Text
.
To get
Some
value, the text must follow the regex
(-|\+)?[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?
In particular, the shorthands
".12"
and
"12."
do not work,
but the value can be prefixed with
+
.
Leading and trailing zeros are fine, however spaces are not.
Examples:

parseNumeric "+12.0" == Some 12
