data Formula t
A
Formula t
is a formula in propositional calculus with
propositions of type t.
Constructors:
Proposition t
Proposition p
is the formula p
Negation (Formula t)
For a formula f,
Negation f
is ¬f
Conjunction [Formula t]
For formulas f1, …, fn,
Conjunction [f1, ..., fn]
is f1 ∧ … ∧ fn
Disjunction [Formula t]
For formulas f1, …, fn,
Disjunction [f1, ..., fn]
is f1 ∨ … ∨ fn
