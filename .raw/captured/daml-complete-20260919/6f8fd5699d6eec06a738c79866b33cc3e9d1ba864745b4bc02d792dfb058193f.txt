Allocation requests can only be received and actioned from the Splice Portfolio, they cannot be initiated or created.
​
Allocate Assets to an Allocation Request
There are two ways to allocate assets to an allocation request.
From the Dashboard, select the allocation request.
Or navigate to
Offers > Allocations
and select the allocation request.
Click
Allocate
for each trader leg where you are the sender.
If your primary party does not have enough assets available for allocation, the
Allocate
button is disabled and a message indicates that you have insufficient funds to complete the allocation.
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
Review the allocated request in
Offers > Allocations
.
The allocated request remains in the Incoming tab because it is still pending and cannot be executed until the
Allocate Before
date. Until that date, you can withdraw your allocation if needed.
​
Withdraw Allocations
Allocated offers can be withdrawn until the
Allocate Before
date.
Navigate to
Offers > Allocations
and select the allocated allocation request.
Open the allocation request and click
Withdraw
for one or more transfer legs.
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
You can re-allocate or withdraw one or more legs as many times as needed until the
Allocate Before
date.
