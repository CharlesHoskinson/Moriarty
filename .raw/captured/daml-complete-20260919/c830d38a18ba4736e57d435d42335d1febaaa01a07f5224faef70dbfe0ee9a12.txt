Enabling pre-approvals for an asset pre-authorizes incoming transfer offers to your primary party. Once enabled, any incoming transfer of a pre-approved asset is automatically accepted into your primary party, with no signing required.
​
Enable a Pre-Approval
Go to the
Settings
page and navigate to the
Pre-Approvals
section.
Toggle on pre-approval for the asset of your choosing.
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
If the asset you want to enable pre-approval for is not listed in the Pre-Approvals table, you first need to
add the registry
associated with that asset. Once the registry is added, the asset appears in the table.
Pre-approval contracts only apply to the primary party. See
Switch Parties
to change which party is primary.
