How Super Validator nodes earn and distribute Canton Coin rewards
Copy page
Copy page
Each Super Validator, in each round, creates an SvRewardCoupon.
These SV minting rights are granted by the tokenomics committee in exchange for operating an SV node or contributing key infrastructure or activity to the network. The tokenomics committee uses the CIP process to record the SV rights. The current list of SV rights is thus available from the
CIP repo
