traffic_status
MemberTrafficStatus
required
The current traffic state for the member on the synchronizer under
actual

target

actual
limit as purchases take time to be incorporated into the limit.

ActualMemberTrafficState

​
total_limit
integer (int64)
required
Current extra traffic limit set for the member on the given synchronizer. An extra traffic top-up is complete once total_limit matches total_purchased.
​
target
TargetMemberTrafficState
required
Total purchased traffic; may exceed limit in
actual
