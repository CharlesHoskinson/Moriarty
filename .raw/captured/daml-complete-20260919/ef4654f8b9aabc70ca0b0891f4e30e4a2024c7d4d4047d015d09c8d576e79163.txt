Send, withdraw, accept, and reject transfer offers in Splice Portfolio.
Copy page
Copy page
All transfers are sent and received through your primary party. To act from a different party, first
switch your primary party
.
​
Send a Transfer Offer
From the Dashboard or your primary party view, click
Transfer
in the top right corner of the screen.
Complete the transfer form and select
Make Transfer
.
The
Description
field is where any memo tag or ID received from an exchange should be pasted.
If the recipient party has pre-approved the asset being transferred, an offer expiration date is not required.
Review the transfer details on the confirmation screen.
To complete the transfer, go to your CIP-103 compliant wallet to complete signing.
Verify the transfer in
Offers > Transfers
.
If the recipient party has pre-approved the asset, the completed transfer appears in the transaction history as soon as it completes on-chain.
​
Withdraw a Transfer Offer
You can withdraw a transfer offer you have sent only if the recipient has not yet accepted it and has not set up pre-approval for the asset being transferred.
Go to
Offers > Transfers
.
Select the offer you want to withdraw.
Click
Withdraw
.
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
​
Accept or Reject a Transfer Offer
There are two ways to respond to a transfer offer.
From the Dashboard, select the transfer offer.
Or navigate to
Offers > Transfers
and select the transfer offer.
Click
Accept
or
Reject
.
To complete this action, go to your CIP-103 wallet to complete signing.
If accepted, review the completed transaction in your primary party’s transaction history.
