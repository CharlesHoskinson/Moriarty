Switching parties means changing the party used to conduct transactions.
These steps apply only if you are using the
Wallet Gateway
as your signing provider. Other CIP-103 wallets manage the primary party in their own interface.
​
Switch Your Primary Party
Navigate to the bottom of the side navigation and click the
Wallet Gateway
button.
A pop-up window appears where you can review existing parties in the
Parties
tab. The selected party has a
Primary
tag to indicate which party you are currently operating from.
Click
Set as primary
on another party to change it.
You can also create a new party using the
+ New
button at the top. Check
Set as the primary party
if you want to use it right after creating it.
Go back to Splice Portfolio to continue conducting transactions with the new party.
