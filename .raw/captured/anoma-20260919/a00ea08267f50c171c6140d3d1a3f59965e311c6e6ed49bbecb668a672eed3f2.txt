Anoma Explorer
You can use the
Anoma Explorer
to view all Anoma protocol activity.
The explorer displays all indexed transactions, tags, resource logics, and external calls across existing Protocol Adapters, shows deployed Protocol Adapter contracts, and enables configuration of blockchain networks and RPC endpoints for supported blockchains. It also includes a GraphQL Playground for executing GraphQL queries against the Envio HyperIndexer.
