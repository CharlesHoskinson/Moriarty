Description
Towers of Hanoi is a puzzle with three rods and n disks of decreasing size. The disks are stacked on top of each other through the first rod. The aim of the game is to move the stack of disks to another rod while following these rules: 1. Only one disk can be moved at a time 2. You may only move a disk from the top of one of the stacks to the top of another stack 3. No disk may be moved on top of a smaller disk The function
hanoi
computes the sequence of moves to solve puzzle.
