We differentiate between two kinds of proofs depending on how closely they interact with sensitive data:
Raw RM proofs
- the proofs produced directly by RM for each transaction. Include compliance proofs, logic proofs, and delta proofs.
Aggregated proof
- combines the raw RM proofs into a fixed number of proofs per transaction. Currently, aggregation results in two proofs per transaction: aggregated SNARK proofs and a delta proof (untouched).
