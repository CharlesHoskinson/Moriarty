# Permission reference: literature, terms and proposed requirements

Research snapshot: 2026-09-19. This is descriptive reference material, not a deployed Moriarty specification. `sources.json` contains capture hashes and freshness metadata; `claims.json` separates source-supported observations from design inferences. PDF coverage is in `visual-coverage.json`. The companion [explanation](explanation.md), [how-to](how-to.md) and [tutorial](tutorial.md) serve different reading needs.

P02–P06 share an Ethereum standards ecosystem and some authors; P07/P08 share OAuth authors and assumptions. They are distinct specifications, not five or two independent audits of one mechanism. CAKE's references to NEAR and AA do not independently validate their implementation. P09, P10 and P12 provide different mechanisms and cannot be combined to claim an implemented composite system.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning in the proposed Moriarty discussion | Not implied |
|---|---|---|
| Permissionless deployment | A developer may deploy supported programs without maintainer/provider/reviewer approval, subject to native network validity and their own resources | Authority over other owners' assets |
| Root grant | Owner-authorized effect envelope and delegation policy | Every future signature from the same key is authorized |
| Attenuation | A child grant's permitted behaviors are a semantic subset of the parent's under matched context | Smaller amount alone suffices; owner cannot later create a separate larger grant |
| Session authority | A deliberately scoped grant usable without repeated owner interaction | Full-account takeover, unlimited lifetime or unrestricted recursive delegation |
| Currentness | Validity against specified authoritative policy, time and consumption state | A simulation result remains valid forever |
| Replay protection | Rejection or idempotent handling of repeated accepted authority use under a defined identity/domain | Presence of a nonce field by itself |
| Revocation | Authoritative cessation of specified future uses at a defined ordering point | Undoing settled effects, erasing debt or deleting already learned plaintext |
| Threshold signing | Multiple shares contribute to a cryptographically valid signature under stated assumptions | Correct financial policy, guaranteed service availability or hidden metadata |
| Viewing authority | Permission/material enabling a specified observer to learn specified information | Spending, re-delegation, control or universal concealment |
| Proof-carrying authorization | A checked relation binds permitted authority to the exact candidate transition/effects | A developer-selected vacuous proof can authorize somebody else's resources |

## Proposed requirements and falsifiable scenarios

These are design proposals, not implemented or accepted product requirements. Existing financial, PCD, correspondence and actual Preview obligations remain unchanged. Per-developer product admission must not require repository campaign or human-review records.

| Proposed ID | Requirement | Positive / negative scenario |
|---|---|---|
| APSS-PERM-001 | When a developer compiles or deploys a supported program, the toolchain shall not require a maintainer, reviewer, campaign or preferred-provider approval. | New developer deploys through a supported native route / valid candidate rejected solely for missing reviewer identity reveals violation. |
| APSS-PERM-002 | When an effect changes assets or liabilities, acceptance shall verify the applicable owner authority and program/intent constraints bound to that effect. | Authorized swap accepted / signature allowing spending is reused to create unauthorized debt and rejected. |
| APSS-PERM-003 | When a grant is signed or proved, its canonical binding shall include the interpretation version, domain, scope, recipient constraints, limits, lifetime and replay identity required by its profile. | Same decoded grant displayed and enforced / changed recipient, scale, domain or profile fails. |
| APSS-PERM-004 | When a delegate creates a child grant, acceptance shall establish that the child's allowed behaviors do not exceed the parent's and that required ancestry remains bound. | Smaller same-recipient allowance / child introduces new callback, liability right or larger grant and fails. |
| APSS-PERM-005 | When multiple uses share an aggregate allowance, accepted and reserved consumption shall be accounted for under one durable identity without exceeding the authorized total. | Two fills totaling 100 / concurrent 60+60 against limit 100 cannot both consume it. |
| APSS-PERM-006 | When replay, cancellation, expiry or revocation races with a fill, acceptance shall apply an explicit authoritative ordering and preserve already incurred obligations. | Confirmed revocation blocks later use / wallet logout alone cannot cancel a finalized obligation. |
| APSS-PERM-007 | When a permission is negotiated, the returned grant shall be distinguished from the request, and any increase shall require the root authority permitted by the profile. | Owner approves revised grant / delegated wallet response silently widens authority and fails. |
| APSS-PERM-008 | When external signing is requested, the request shall bind the authorized payload and key/domain derivation context; a returned signature shall not be reported as settled effects. | Authorized bound payload signed / same hash under wrong derivation policy or mere signature labeled settled fails. |
| APSS-PERM-009 | When viewing, proving, spending, policy-change or recovery rights are granted, each shall have a distinct documented scope and no unproved privilege conversion. | Auditor observes scoped data / viewing material accepted as spending authority fails. |
| APSS-PERM-010 | If required authority interpretation or currentness cannot be established within supported bounds, the effect acceptance path shall reject that attempted effect without substituting human developer approval. | Supported bounded policy evaluated / unknown permission type is ignored or routed to maintainer approval, violating the requirement. |
| APSS-PERM-011 | When a policy or account implementation changes, existing grants shall retain their signed interpretation or require explicitly authorized migration preserving liabilities and cumulative limits. | Compatible migration preserves remaining allowance / upgrade resets limit or adds method authority and fails. |
| APSS-PERM-012 | When a report claims authorization or privacy, it shall distinguish signature/threshold assumptions, current policy enforcement, tests, proofs and actual settled observations. | Report names evidence and observers / MPC signature alone represented as verified user intent or complete privacy fails. |

The concrete schema, ledger serialization, bound values, privacy relation and proof obligations need a separate implementation specification. Unsupported cryptographic adapters may remain unsupported; permissionlessness is not a claim that every conceivable signature scheme is already implemented.
