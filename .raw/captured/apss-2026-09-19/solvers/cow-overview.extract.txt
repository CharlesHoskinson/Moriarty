What is
CoW Protocol
?
​
CoW Protocol is a fully permissionless trading protocol that leverages
fair combinatorial batch auctions
as its price finding mechanism. CoW Protocol uses fair combinatorial auctions to maximize liquidity via Coincidence of Wants (CoWs) in addition to tapping all available on-chain liquidity whenever needed.
Governance
​
CoW DAO is of its community, by its community, and for its community. CoW DAO uses a
decentralized governance model
to ensure that the community has full control over the direction of the protocol.
