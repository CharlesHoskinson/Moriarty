Guardian
Guardian makes privacy practical.
Designed for institutions to manage state, co-sign transactions, and enforce compliance, Guardian is the orchestration layer and entry point to Miden.
What Guardian enables
1
Policy & Compliance
Guardian ensures compliance on a pre-transaction basis across jurisdictions. For example, if a party tries to send funds to a known OFAC-sanctioned address, the transaction will be denied.
2
Revenue Capture
Operators running Guardians can control when revenue is collected or assessed based on transaction type. For example, transactions over a $10,000 threshold may trigger a "review fee" to ensure validity.
3
Account Orchestration
Guardian helps users keep their accounts 'up-to-state' across devices. For example, if a user is logged in to the same account on their phone and computer, Guardian will keep both in sync.
