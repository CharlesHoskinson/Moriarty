Agentic accounts let an AI agent hold funds and act through a Coinbase account or wallet. For an
x402 buyer, the account is the source of USDC that the agent uses to pay for a service.
Choose an account based on what your agent needs to do:
Agentic Wallet
Give your own agent a wallet that can discover and pay for x402 services today.
Coinbase for Agents
Connect an agent to Coinbase Advanced Trade. x402 payments are coming soon.
