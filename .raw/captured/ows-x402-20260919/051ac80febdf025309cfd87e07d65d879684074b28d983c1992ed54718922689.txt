Get Involved
There are many ways to participate in x402.
x402 is built by an open community of developers, contributors, companies, and ecosystem participants working together to advance internet-native payments.
Join us on
Slack
Connect with other community members, ask questions, share ideas, and participate in working groups shaping the future of x402.
Contribute on Github
Help improve the specification, documentation, and implementations by opening issues, submitting pull requests, and participating in technical discussions.
Participate in Working Groups
Collaborate with contributors across key areas, including technical discussions, domain discovery, identity, and tax and compliance considerations.
Meeting Calendar
The TSC and working group meetings are open to the public. If you are interested in participating and getting involved in any of our working groups, we encourage you to join one of the meetings below.
