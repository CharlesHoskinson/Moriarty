Creates a CDP-authenticated x402 facilitator client.
The returned client is pre-configured with the CDP hosted facilitator URL
and CDP JWT-based authentication. It implements the
HTTPFacilitatorClient
interface from
@x402/core/server
and can be passed directly to
x402ResourceServer
as a drop-in replacement for a self-hosted facilitator.
Credentials are resolved in order: explicit args →
CDP_API_KEY_ID
/
CDP_API_KEY_SECRET
environment variables.
