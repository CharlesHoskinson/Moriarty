## Exact audit artifacts and schema

Produce human-readable `TOOLCHAIN-INTEGRATION.md`, `PUBLIC-COMMANDS.md`, `TWO-PROGRAMS.md`, `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, and `ROADMAP-DELTA.md`. The first identifies supported routes and missing consumer edges; the second resolves executable/profile ambiguities; the third records each stage without skipping missing outputs; the fourth gives clean-builder inputs and comparisons; the fifth proposes only minimal canonical edits.

Machine outputs are `profile-command-map.json`, `target-manifest.json`, `build-inputs.json`, `stage-evidence.jsonl`, `obligations.jsonl`, and `roadmap-delta.json`. Minimal field schemas:

- Profile-command row: `{profile,executable,command,source_header,parser,lowerer,output_schema,qualification,implementation_path,evidence_ids}`.
