# Source findings

**Closure and imports.**
- Full modules are now supplied, which closes B17 condition 2 (extract fidelity).
- Import-time effects are limited to stdlib imports, constants and definitions. There is no rebinding of `Q_NULLARY`, `quint_typed_wire` or `canonical_bytes`.
- Local imports resolve only among the five modules. The stdlib imports are `argparse`, `hashlib`, `json`, `pathlib` and `copy`.

**No zip, prefix or drop.**
- `machine_states` maps every `document['states']` entry in order.
- All five equalities are `canonical_bytes` comparisons of whole lists, so differences in length and order are detected.
- An empty ITF raises at `itf['states'][-1]`, so emptiness fails closed.

**Whole-Machine equality covers the final records.**
- `states[-1]` contains the complete record vector. That includes every record's `invocation` (with `guardCosts` as rows), its raw `result` and its raw `awaitPrefix`.
- The separate `quintObservations` list is derived from the same last state.

**Alias boundary holds.**
- `_canonical_action_keys` is applied only inside `quint_record_observation`, on `result` and `awaitPrefix`.
- It runs after Snapshot state maps have been turned into rows, so user-chosen state names cannot be renamed.
- The b06 path never imports the alias function, so `actionName` stays in the raw Machine.

**Map conventions match Body 6 and Body 8.**
- `Snapshot.state` becomes rows sorted by name.
- `guardCosts` becomes integer-sorted `site`/`cost` rows, and an empty map becomes `[]`.
- `Scenario` has four keys, so it skips the Snapshot branch. Its string-keyed `state` and `inputs` stay ordinary objects, including empty `{}`.

**Nullary handling.**
- `MACHINE_NULLARY` covers the Machine-only tags: Ready, Published, NoResult, NoPrefix and NotAdvanced.
- The unit payload survives `quint_typed_wire` as `()`, so the exact-tuple check in `machine_nullary_wire` is reachable.
- Any other payload is kept and causes a mismatch rather than being erased.

**Core normalizer drops nothing.**
- Every `_row` field set is strict, and every field is either mapped or renamed.
- `_scalar` re-encodes the `type` field as the variant tag, so no information is lost.
- The full raw publications are also compared directly through `publicationsEqual`.

**H5 types are preserved.** Bool and int stay distinct end to end. UInt strings become Python `int` only through `_uint`.

**Hashes bind the bytes actually read**, and the report keeps all four raw documents, their paths and their SHAs.

# Non-blocking source notes

- **Stale comment.** In `quint_typed_wire`, the comment "guardCosts is … not compared" dates from B17. `guardCosts` is now compared as part of whole-Machine equality. The comment is wrong, but behaviour is unaffected.
- **`rawFinalRecords` is not raw.** It is a decoded view:
  - integer map keys are stringified by `json.dumps`;
  - unit tuples become `[]`;
  - a tuple-keyed map would raise, which fails closed.
  
  The authoritative raw form is `rawDocuments.quintItf` together with its pin.
- **`coreBoundariesEqual` is not independent.** It is a deterministic consequence of `publicationsEqual`.
- **Collision risk in ordinary maps.** A `Scenario` (or any other ordinary map) whose key set is exactly `{tag, value}` or `{state, balances, liabilities}` would be misread as a variant or Snapshot. That would fail closed. Root can rule it out cheaply against `scenario.json`.

# Claim limits

- **The Quint agreement is not independent semantic evidence.**
  - The model publishes runtime evidence supplied by the seed. The seed and both expected documents come from the same independent owner.
  - The genuinely independent check is Core against that owner (`publicationsEqual`).
  - `machineStatesEqual` and `quintBoundariesEqual` establish two things: the port's bookkeeping conforms to the owner's expected trace, and the seed is consistent with that trace.
  - `coreQuintBoundariesEqual` is a transitive consequence and must not be reported as a third independent vote.
- **ITF legality is a premise.** The comparison does not validate that the ITF states are legal model transitions.
  - That rests on the frozen exit-0 receipt, the ITF status `ok` and Root's six-state bookkeeping.
  - The CLI pretty-trace is truncated and has no textual summary. It is not evidence, and none is claimed.
- **Pre-existing caveats remain unchanged:**
  - `Q_NULLARY` erases payloads unconditionally, which is sound only under the typechecked-source premise;
  - the unit codec path;
  - `json.loads` accepts duplicate keys (last wins) and NaN;

  - Snapshot names are sorted by codepoint order;
  - `timeout` and `prlimit` are unpinned.
