## 3. Ranked Recommendations (Evidence, Tradeoffs, Proposed Text & Tests)

### Idea 1 (Mandatory — `UIREQ-031`, `UIREQ-035`, `B-T07`): SIEVE/Brillig-Style Arena-Segmented `WitnessPlan` with `StepInv`-Safe Liveness
- **Evidence & Applicability**: `[recommendation]` Combine SIEVE `@new`/`@delete` whole-allocation lifetime discipline ([sieve-circuit-ir.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/sieve-circuit-ir.md#L165-L188)) and Brillig SSA slot reuse ([noir-brillig.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/noir-brillig.md#L303-L308)) with Sherlock's `StepInv`. Partition `WitnessPlan` slots into reusable scratch/frame/helper pools vs. append-only `DataRow` commitments inside an A4 segment, and pin live-out wires across `advance_m` chunk cuts.
- **Tradeoffs**: Prevents soundness bugs from premature `DataRow` reuse while bounding host/prover scratch memory; adds a static liveness verification pass.
- **Proposed Spec Text** (append to Candidate 02 *Bounded runs and continuations*):
  > `WitnessPlan` (`UIREQ-031`, `B-T07`) must enforce topological SSA definition before use and partition slots by `StepInv` mutability: scratch, `value_slots`, `frames`, and `helperTasks` slots may be reclaimed after `lastUse`, whereas `DataRow` slots in `[0, activeLength)` remain append-only until a canonical A4 boundary. Any slot live across an `advance_m` stop or `ChunkStore` boundary must have `lastUse >= chunkStopStep` and appear in the chunk boundary digest.
- **Required Tests**: (1) Reject any `WitnessPlan` that reclaims a slot before `lastUse`, partially deletes a contiguous multi-wire range, or overwrites an active `DataRow` mid-segment; (2) verify `streamWitness(plan, slice)` peak live scratch slots `<= plan.slotCounts` across widths `m in {1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 16}` with bit-identical boundary digests.

### Idea 2 (Mandatory — `UIREQ-033`, `B-T09`): Truffle `MERGE_EXPLODE` Worklist Specialization with Materialized Fallback Cuts
- **Evidence & Applicability**: `[recommendation]` Apply Truffle `MERGE_EXPLODE` state-keyed merging and `@TruffleBoundary` separation ([truffle-pe.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/truffle-pe.md#L385-L535)) to `specializeStep` ([interfaces.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L282-L290)). Key specialization states on `@CompilationFinal` image fields `(pc, mode, staticMask, helperPhase)` while treating dynamic `fuel`, `work`, and `DataRow` contents as residual variables.
- **Tradeoffs**: Guarantees termination under `residualSizeLimit` and eliminates per-step tag dispatch on scalar hot paths, at the cost of explicit frame materialization on fallback edges.
- **Proposed Spec Text** (append to Candidate 02 *Reusable handwritten realization*):
  > `specializeStep(config, stepRef)` (`UIREQ-033`, `B-T09`) must merge residual basic blocks on identical static dispatch keys `(pc, mode, staticMask, helperPhase)` (`MERGE_EXPLODE`) and treat recursive helper entry, error/exhaustion transitions, and blocks exceeding `residualSizeLimit` as explicit fallback boundaries that materialize complete `MachineImage`/`StepEventC` state before invoking generic `stepReference`.
- **Required Tests**: Alive2-style bounded translation validation (`validateResidualStep`) proving `residual == stepReference` across all 39 A events and B helper microsteps, including mid-slice fallback (`residualSizeLimit = 1..8`), `Rejected(error4)`, `Exhausted(kind)`, and zero-fuel free `A80/A82/A84` publication.

### Idea 3 (Mandatory — `UIREQ-031`, `UIREQ-032`, `UIREQ-035`): Zero-Clone Borrowed Step Visitor & Rolling-Digest QA Sink
- **Evidence & Applicability**: `[recommendation]` Separate retaining test collectors (`collect_steps`) from a non-allocating `visit_step_views` path that borrows pre/post state deltas or computes a rolling transcript digest, and merge S1 (`07-doctorwatson-local-qa`) with S3 (`08-doctorwatson-evidence`) in `run_host.py`.
- **Tradeoffs**: Removes two `Snapshot` heap clones per transition on long runs while preserving exact `StepRecord` materialization when requested.
- **Proposed Spec Text** (append to Candidate 02 *Deterministic execution*):
  > `visit_steps` must not require retaining full-history `Vec<StepRecord>` sinks in bounded-memory mode; streaming QA checks must support incremental rolling-digest or borrowed-delta verification alongside exact small-step capture, and `run_host.py` `CHECK_HELPERS` must include both `host_observations.py` and `source_reference_facts.py`.
- **Required Tests**: Allocation-bounded streaming test verifying `O(1)` retained step records across `10^4` steps and exact agreement with `collect_steps` on all 96 N01 cells.

### Idea 4 (Mandatory — `UIREQ-034`, `B-T08`): Reconciled `ResourceReport` Accounting & Restored `N07/N08` Cut Suites
- **Evidence & Applicability**: `[recommendation]` Wire [`EventAccountingReport`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/helper_machine/resource_event_accounting.rs#L45-L55) into [`ResourceReport`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/helper_machine/resource_report.rs#L210-L232) and forbid wall-clock estimates from pruning exhaustive `N07/N08` cut-equivalence suites.
- **Tradeoffs**: Slightly longer CI test execution, traded for deterministic work/fuel accounting and full cut-invariance coverage.
- **Proposed Spec Text** (append to Candidate 02 *Verification and gating*):
  > Host wall-clock timing must never substitute for logical `work`/`fuel` or prune required `N07/N08` exhaustive cut suites. `ResourceReport` (`UIREQ-034`, `B-T08`) must reconcile `EventAccountingReport` (`work_delta_sum`, `helper_work_delta_sum`, `maximum_observed_logical_depth`) against `RunSummary`, keeping `Unmeasured` fields explicit until native circuit synthesis supplies measured row/gate counts.
- **Required Tests**: Assert `EventAccountingReport` sums match `RunSummary` across all `N01` (96 cells) and restored `N07/N08` cut matrices.

## 4. What Should Be Rejected or Deferred
- **Reject unconstrained loop explosion (`@ExplodeLoop` without `MERGE_EXPLODE` or `residualSizeLimit`)**: `[recommendation]` Unbounded unrolling over guest loops or recursive helpers causes residual graph blowup ([truffle-pe.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/truffle-pe.md#L555-L598), [noir-brillig.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/noir-brillig.md#L301)).
- **Reject unconstrained witness hints without circuit constraints**: `[recommendation]` Per Brillig ([noir-brillig.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/09-optimization/noir-brillig.md#L94-L97)), host witness streaming never replaces handwritten circuit constraints or `StepInv`.
- **Defer speculative JIT deoptimization cycles (`Assumption` invalidation)**: `[recommendation]` Dynamic re-specialization cycles are unnecessary for Sherlock's immutable admitted `ProgramImage`; static pre-run `specializeStep` with deterministic `genericFallbacks` suffices.
