### Rank 1 (Mandatory — S1/S3): Reconcile `run_host.py` Snapshot Closure, `source_reference_facts` Verification, and `rustc` JSON Artifacts
- **Evidence & Applicability:** In [`run_host.py`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/models/sherlock/qa/run_host.py#L54), `CHECK_HELPERS` lists only `host_observations.py`. Integrating DW2 ([01-implementation/doctorwatson-stream-review.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/01-implementation/doctorwatson-stream-review.md#L38-L40)) and DW3 ([03-blockers/doctorwatson-sourcefacts-review.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/03-blockers/doctorwatson-sourcefacts-review.md#L9-L11)) requires adding `source_reference_facts.py` to `CHECK_HELPERS` and verifying its returned 8-path/9-read inventory in `Attempt.compare`. Per [rustc-json.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/10-delivery-factory/rustc-json.md), passing `--error-format=json --json=artifacts,diagnostic-short` to `rustc` in `Attempt.compile` records structured span/artifact facts in `.stderr` without changing dep-info checks.
- **Tradeoffs:** Adds 1 file to `COMMON_PATHS` (26 attempt files for `stream`); zero new dependencies.
- **Exact Proposed Text (append to Candidate 02 *Measured sprint exits*):**
  > `run_host.py` SHALL declare `CHECK_HELPERS = ('models/sherlock/qa/host_observations.py', 'models/sherlock/qa/source_reference_facts.py')`, copy both into `self.snapshot` across `host`, `image`, and `stream`, and verify in `Attempt.compare` that `report['source_reference_facts']['files']` matches the snapshot digests of all 8 consumed corpus/serializer/transcription files (9 reads).
- **Exact Tests:** Add `test_combined_stream_and_source_facts_snapshot_closure` in `test_run_host.py` asserting preflight failure when `source_reference_facts.py` is omitted and `Attempt.compare` failure when any `source_reference_facts` digest diverges from `self.sources`.
