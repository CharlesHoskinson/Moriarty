## 3. Ranked Architectural Recommendations

### Idea 1 (Rank 1 — Mandatory): Shared Step-Spine Adapter & Sparse Write Multiplexer (`StepSpineEmitter`)
* **Evidence & Applicability**: OpenVM (Search 1) separates the **Adapter Chip** (fetch, operand bounds, state bus writes) from **Core Chips** (rule-local math) so core chips never re-constrain global ranges. Halo2 ([capture-03-halo2-gadgets-chips.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/03-handwritten-realization/capture-03-halo2-gadgets-chips.md)) uses gadgets because *"The gadget interface can provide a more convenient and stable API that abstracts away from extraneous detail."* In static `midnight-zkir-v3`, a single `StepSpineEmitter` per step realizes this by factoring out `image_ranges(program, pre, post)`, one [`emit_enabled_fetch_block`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-backend/src/sherlock_machine/enabled_fetch.rs#L74-L115), one shared 16-byte fuel decrement over `charged_enable = sum_{r in Charged36} enable_r` (`F1`), and per-lane write-mask multiplexing (`post[c] == Mux_{r in W(c)}(enable_r, val_{r,c}, pre[c])`). Replace manual `is_reserved_*_variable` lists in [`literal_complete_execution.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-backend/src/sherlock_machine/literal_complete_execution.rs#L126-L143) with a hierarchical prefix allocator (`__sherlock_s{step}_r{tag}_{var}`).
* **Tradeoffs**: Eliminates 38x redundant range checks and shrinks frame muxes per lane $c$ from 39 to its active writer set $|W(c)|$, requiring refactoring of the 4 existing standalone emitters into spine leaves.
* **Proposed Text & Test**: Add to `Candidate 02 §Component contracts (Reusable handwritten realization)`:
  `Every multi-rule step circuit MUST emit image_ranges(program, pre, post), CR0 expression fetch, and F1 16-byte fuel accounting at most once in a shared StepSpineEmitter; rule leaves emit only local guard predicates and sparse cell writes, with temporary names allocated by disjoint step/rule prefixes.`
  *Test*: `test_step_spine_single_range_and_mux`: in a 4-rule spine (`A80/A82/A84/A102`), assert `ConstrainBits` count equals 1x single-step `image_ranges` and `MockProver` rejects multi-hot `enable_r` or unmasked lane writes.

### Idea 2 (Rank 2 — Mandatory): Dual-Target `EffectPlan` Lowering (`WitnessPlan` + `ConstraintPlan`)
* **Evidence & Applicability**: Clean ([capture-02-clean-zk-circuits.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/gemini-research/03-handwritten-realization/capture-02-clean-zk-circuits.md)) separates `Operation.witness` (lowered to a Witness Generation IR in PR `#403`) from `Operation.assert`/`lookup`/`subcircuit`. Lowering Sherlock's rule descriptors and B's [`EffectPlan`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/helper_machine/shared/owner_runtime.rs#L997-L1007) into both an uncached topological `WitnessPlan` and a ZKIR `ConstraintPlan` satisfies UIREQ-031/035 and removes [`literal_witness.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-backend/src/sherlock_machine/literal_witness.rs#L27-L36) from circuit `relation_hash`.
* **Tradeoffs**: Guarantees witness/constraint lockstep and stabilizes circuit keys against witness diagnostic edits; designated owner obligations (`StepInv`, `uiValid`, `F4`, `OBL.G4`) remain explicit constraint-only gates.
* **Proposed Text & Test**: Add to `Candidate 02 §Component contracts`:
  `Each admitted A rule and B helper row MUST lower from one declarative read/write plan into both WitnessPlan and ConstraintPlan. Host witness producers MUST NOT be hashed into circuit relation_hash.`
  *Test*: `test_dual_lowering_mutation_soundness`: for each implemented rule, verify `WitnessPlan` output satisfies `ConstraintPlan`, and mutating any single written lane fails `ConstraintPlan`.

### Idea 4 (Rank 4 — Optional): Family-Specialized Step Sub-Circuits (`ResidualStepIR`)

  *Test*: `test_family_spine_equivalence`: assert specialized family macro-blocks yield identical `StateBlock` post-states and public statements as the universal spine.

## 4. Mandatory vs. Optional, Verdict & Rejections
* **Mandatory vs. Optional**: **Ideas 1, 2, and 3 are mandatory** before scaling handwritten ZKIR beyond `A102/A80/A82/A84`; **Idea 4 is optional** (Phase 6).
* **Limited Design Verdict**: **Conditionally sound direction, incomplete realization contract.** Candidate 02 rightly keeps Midnight native `ledger-9` and converges A/B via explicit adapters, but requires Ideas 1–3 to prevent a 39x ZKIR range/preservation blowup and ordering deadlocks on `MD2`/`F3–F7`.
* **Reject / Defer**: **Reject** cloning standalone per-rule `image_ranges`/preservation loops across 39 rules; **reject** STARK/Plonky3 cross-table bus migrations or `verifyProof`/`ledger-10` assumptions; **defer** Wave 3 circuit emission (`A153, A177, A181, A185, A195, A204, A214`) until designated owners freeze `MD2` and `F5–F7`.
