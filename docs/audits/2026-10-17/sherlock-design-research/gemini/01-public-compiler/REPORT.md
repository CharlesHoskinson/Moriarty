- **Primary Implementation Comparison**:
  - *Plutus (`PIR -> PLC -> UPLC/CEK`)*: Compiles mutually recursive `Datatype` and `TermBind` blocks by desugaring ADTs (Scott/SOP) and recursion (fixpoint combinators) into untyped lambda terms for a closure-allocating CEK machine. Unsuitable for Sherlock's fixed-table first-order profiles (`UIREQ-003`), where closure/fixpoint expansion inflates step/arena counts unpredictably.
  - *Lean 4 (`Elab.Inductive`, `Elab.Match`, `Compiler.LCNF`)*: Elaborates mutual inductive headers first, checks strict positivity and constructor field types, compiles pattern matrices into explicit decision trees (`MatcherApp`/case trees), and lowers to ANF-like `LCNF` with explicit constructor tags (`ctor_i`) and projections (`proj_i`). Sherlock requires the nominal tag/arity table (`TypeRowC`) and deterministic decision-tree lowering (`MatchPlan`), but replaces Lean's structural termination obligation with runtime fuel charges (`UIREQ-011`).
  - *Clap (`Clap/Compilation.lean`)*: Explicitly splits compilation into `toCs` (`ConstraintPlan`) and `toWg` (`WitnessPlan`) with an explicit failure branch (`notonic`), matching `UIREQ-031`.

## 3. Ranked Design Proposals

### Rank 1 (Mandatory — v1): Unified Grammar, Nominal `TypeDecl` Pass & First-Column `MatchPlan` Elaboration
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Bridges [`parser.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/parser.rs), [`draft_source.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/draft_source.rs), and [`shared_adt.proposed.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/models/sherlock/generic-adt-helper-source-20261016/shared_adt.proposed.rs) using two-pass header registration (`Lean.Elab.Inductive`) and fixed left-to-right first-column decision trees ([CONCEPT-ADOPTION.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/docs/research/sherlock/CONCEPT-ADOPTION.md#L15-L24)).
- **Tradeoffs**: Monomorphic nominal ADTs (`TypeId >= 4`) avoid higher-order/polymorphic erasure complexity while supporting recursive `Schedule`/`List`/`Tree` in fixed tables.
- **Proposed Spec Text**:
  > Promote `sherlock::source` out of `#[cfg(test)]`. Support `type Name = | Ctor(T1, ...) ...;`, recursive `fn name(x: T) -> R { ... }`, and `entry`/`action` bindings under one versioned parser with pre-allocation occurrence/node caps (`H1`). Elaborate declarations in two passes: (1) register nominal `TypeId` (`0..3` builtin, `>=4` user ADTs) and function signatures, rejecting duplicate type/constructor/function names and unclosed references with `AdmissionRejected`; (2) typecheck bodies and compile `match` arms via left-to-right first-column expansion into `PlanNodeRow` (`Switch`/`Bind`/`Select`/`NoMatch`) with explicit `workMap` and `NoMatch -> Rejected(error9)`.
- **Tests**: (1) Parse/elaborate mutually recursive `Schedule` ADT + recursive evaluator into `ProgramImage` with `TypeId >= 4`; (2) Non-exhaustive `match` emits `PlanNode::NoMatch` and steps to `Rejected(error9)`; (3) Duplicate `fn` or arity mismatch fails with `AdmissionRejected`.

### Rank 2 (Mandatory — v1): Single Two-Stage Admission Gate (`prepare` + `admit`) Before Execution/Lowering
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Unifies [`program_image.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/program_image.rs), [`layout_a/prepare.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/layout_a/prepare.rs), and [`owner_prepare.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/helper_machine/shared/owner_prepare.rs) behind [`interfaces.md`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L22-L45).
- **Tradeoffs**: Adds a strict pre-execution pass, but eliminates silent `ActionProjection::Unsupported` downgrade and harmonizes name-table indexing between A (`prepare_a`) and B (`OwnerEngine::admit`).
- **Proposed Spec Text**:
  > Every compiler, interpreter (`run`, `visit_steps`), and witness path MUST call `admit_program(ast, profile_id, bounds, layout) -> Result<AdmittedProgram, PreparationFailure>`. Stage 1 (`prepare`) canonicalizes the `Name` table and flat `ExprRow`/`TypeRow`/`ConstructorRow`/`PlanNodeRow` arenas. Stage 2 (`admit`) verifies `ViewCheck`, closed variables, `AdtSupportMode` (`DedicatedSorts` vs `GenericNominal`), and `Bounds.stepCap <= CounterWidth::max_value()`, returning `AdmissionRejected`, `CapacityNotAdmitted`, or `UnsupportedFeature` outside runtime `Outcome`.
- **Tests**: (1) `admit_program` rejects `GenericNominal` `TypeId >= 4` on a `DedicatedSorts`-only profile with `UnsupportedFeature`; (2) Exceeding `exprRows` or `stepCap` returns `CapacityNotAdmitted`; (3) Admitted `ProgramImage` bytes and canonical digest match across A and B preparation.

### Rank 3 (Mandatory — v1): `mori compile` Sherlock Profile Target & Split Plan Artifacts (`UIREQ-031`)
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Connects [`compile.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori/src/commands/compile.rs) and [`sherlock_machine/mod.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-backend/src/sherlock_machine/mod.rs) to Clap's `toCs`/`toWg` and Clean's `Operations.witgenJson?` separation ([papers/04/proposal.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/docs/research/sherlock/papers/04/proposal.md#L16-L42), [papers/09/proposal.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/docs/research/sherlock/papers/09/proposal.md#L15-L38)).
- **Tradeoffs**: Fails closed (`UnsupportedFeature(ruleId, locator)`) when a program uses a rule not yet lowered in `sherlock_machine`, preventing false compilation claims.
- **Proposed Spec Text**:
  > Extend `mori compile` with explicit Sherlock profile selection (`sherlock-k-a/1` or `sherlock-helper-microstep/1`). When selected, `mori compile` MUST run `admit_program` and `compileStep` to atomically emit `program-image.json`, `constraint-plan.json`, `witness-plan.json`, and `export-capabilities.json` (with per-file overwrite protection). If any reachable rule in the admitted image lacks a verified lowering in `sherlock_machine`, compilation MUST fail closed with `UnsupportedFeature(ruleId, sourceSpan)` rather than emitting non-Sherlock Core ZKIRv3 or falling back to `A102` literal emission.
- **Tests**: (1) `mori compile` on an admitted literal/return program emits all 4 artifacts with matching `programId` and `profileId`; (2) `mori compile` on a program requiring an unlowered rule returns non-zero exit with `UnsupportedFeature(ruleId, span)` and writes no partial files.

### Rank 4 (Optional — Next-Version P2): Finite Worklist Specialization (`ResidualStepIR`) over `ProgramImageView`
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Operationalizes `UIREQ-033` ([spec.md](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/specs/universal-interpreter/spec.md#L229-L233)) once uncached full39/full96 baselines pass.
- **Tradeoffs**: Reduces dispatch rows per known `pc`/`PlanId` without unrolling recursive calls, but adds a soundness obligation (`spec_sound` over reachable `(pc, planId)` states).
- **Proposed Spec Text**:
  > After Phase 0–4 uncached conformance, add an optional specialization pass `specialize_step(image: &AdmittedProgram) -> ResidualStepIR` that partially evaluates instruction fetch, static arity lookup, and `PlanNodeRow` dispatch over finite `(FunctionId, ExprId, PlanId)` keys using a memoized worklist. Recursive calls remain residual frame pushes (`Call` -> `Frame`), guaranteeing termination.
- **Tests**: Differential equivalence checking that `ResidualStepIR` produces identical `StepRecord` sequences, work charges, and `WitnessPlan` assignments as unspecialized `step` across all N01/PassT corpora.

## 4. Mandatory vs. Optional, Verdict & Reject/Defer

- **Mandatory (v1)**: Ranks 1, 2, and 3 (grammar reconciliation, unified `admit_program` gate, and `mori compile` split artifact emission with fail-closed rule support).
- **Optional (Post-Baseline P2)**: Rank 4 (`ResidualStepIR` finite worklist specialization) and compact constructor tag/field packing.
- **Limited Design Verdict**: **CONDITIONAL REVISION REQUIRED**. Candidate 02's convergence architecture is sound, but its *Program and admission* and *Public toolchain* sections must explicitly mandate: (a) promoting the recursive AST/typechecker with two-pass nominal `TypeDecl` + first-column `MatchPlan` elaboration, (b) unifying the 5 split preparation paths into one pre-execution `admit_program` gate, and (c) failing closed on unlowered rules in `mori compile` instead of writing `ActionProjection::Unsupported` alongside Core ZKIRv3.
- **Reject / Defer**:
  - **Reject** Plutus-style Scott encoding / Z-combinator recursion desugaring (violates first-order fixed-table bounds `UIREQ-003` and distorts per-step fuel accounting).
  - **Reject** Lean-style mandatory structural termination proofs for user source functions (Sherlock bounds non-termination via explicit runtime fuel `UIREQ-011` -> `Exhausted(error8)`).
  - **Reject** silent fallback from Sherlock compilation to non-Sherlock `mori_backend::compile` when `source_projection_report` reports `Unsupported`.
  - **Defer** source-level parametric polymorphism (monomorphization) and heuristic pattern-column swapping until monomorphic `Schedule` + fixed first-column `MatchPlan` pass full A/B equivalence.
