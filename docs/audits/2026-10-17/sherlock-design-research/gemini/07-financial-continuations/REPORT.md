## 1. Limited Design Verdict

**[Recommendation]** **CONDITIONAL APPROVE WITH MANDATORY SPECIFICATION AMENDMENTS.** Candidate 02's convergence direction—retaining A/B implementations behind verified profile adapters, separating proof-chunk cuts from application `Await`/commit boundaries, and assigning Owner H the complete Core/2 (`moriarty-core/2`) financial continuation map—is sound. However, before Owner H and the bounded Quint financial slice execute, Candidate 02 must explicitly specify four Core/2-to-Sherlock invariants: (1) the dual-store `(CommittedBoundary, TentativeSegment)` rollback and four-way continuation outcome relation, (2) per-statement DAG work accounting vs. tree expansion across `R6`, (3) two-bit liability state (`present`, `amount`) and `R0..R5` unvalidated-committed reporting, and (4) `PassTOutput` (`T`) oracle coverage for B financial preparation. All v1 requirements (35 UIREQ, full 39 events, B helper/ADT/financial requirements, U0–U7, M1–M17, MC/SP/G exits) and all owner, native (`0`), resource, and private boundaries remain unchanged.

## 3. Concrete Current-Design Gaps in Candidate 02

1. **Gap 1 — Missing Dual-Store `(CommittedBoundary, TentativeSegment)` Split Across Four Continuation Outcomes:** `[Repository observation]` [`execute_stage`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/runtime.rs#L878-L999) clones tentative `current` from `BoundarySnapshot` and distinguishes: (a) reaching segment-exit `Await` (charges `1` entry unit, emits no `Step`, seals `Continuation` at `site`); (b) resuming at `site` with `guard == false` (`SegmentRun::NotAdvanced` at [`runtime.rs:964-970`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/runtime.rs#L964-L970): charges `dispatch_cost = 1 + |guard_dag|` to `attempted`, leaves `c.logical.committed` and `Continuation` unchanged); and (c) mid-segment precommit failure (`Requires`/`Ensures` false, balance/discharge underflow, arithmetic overflow), which discards tentative `current` and `effects`, preserves caller `Continuation` (`C10`), and returns `StageRejection { attempted: meter.used().logical, committed: kept }`. Candidate 02 also introduces proof-chunk `TechnicalCut` boundaries, which pause mid-segment without committing a Core/2 `Await`. Candidate 02 does not specify how tentative mutations survive a `TechnicalCut` while still rolling back to `CommittedBoundary` on a subsequent `StageRejection`.
2. **Gap 2 — Core/2 Per-Statement DAG Cost vs. Sherlock Tree-Occurrence Expansion:** `[Contradiction]` [`stage::cost::statement_cost`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/cost.rs#L23-L47) charges `1 + |distinct reachable ExprId nodes|` per statement using a `seen` DAG set, whereas [`prepare_scalar_action`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/sherlock/source_adapter.rs#L14-L24) unfolds shared `ExprId` nodes into trees (`MAX_PROJECTION_OCCURRENCES = 64`). Unfolding DAGs into trees changes `logical.committed` and breaks `R6` (`logical.committed == committed_before[site]`) in [`stage::admit::resume`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/admit.rs#L337-L348).
3. **Gap 3 — Discharged Liability Retention vs. Residual Filtering, and `R0..R5` Committed Echo:** `[Repository observation]` [`Snapshot.liabilities`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/runtime.rs#L176-L185) retains discharged rows with `amount == 0` so `LiabilityPresent(name)` remains `true` (as in `F02` `grantDue`), while [`stage::residual`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/mod.rs#L146-L162) filters `amount > 0` for `StageOutcome.residual`. Also, [`stage::admit::resume`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/admit.rs#L279-L361) echoes unvalidated caller `c.logical.committed` on `R0..R5` refusals (`attempted = 0`) before `R6` checks `committed_before[site]`. Meanwhile, [`financial-prefixes.json`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/models/sherlock/oracles/financial-prefixes.json) sets `work: null` and omits multi-asset (`CREDIT`/`OPS`) and `LiabilityPresent` cases.
4. **Gap 4 — B Financial Preparation `PassTOutput` (`T`) Oracle Omission (`E15`):** `[Repository observation]` Audit [`02-evidence/REPORT.md`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/02-evidence/REPORT.md#L33-L35) records that the unmerged B financial preparation package (`E15`) serializes `PassTOutput` (`T`), but its independent oracle only derives `B` and `F`.

## 4. Ranked Ideas & Concrete Amendments

### Idea 1 (Rank 1 — MANDATORY): Dual-Store `(CommittedBoundary, TentativeSegment)` & Four-Outcome Contract
- **Evidence & Applicability:** Grounded in CosmWasm parent/child state isolation (`capture-02`), EIP-140 partial-gas rollback (`capture-03`), and [`runtime.rs:878-999`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/runtime.rs#L878-L999). Applies to Owner H, B `helper_machine`, and the Quint financial slice.
- **Tradeoffs:** Retains both `CommittedBoundary` and `TentativeSegment` during active segments, adding bounded state width to guarantee exact `C10` rollback even across mid-segment proof cuts.
- **Proposed Text (insert in *Complete financial continuation map*):**
  > Lowered Core/2 financial runs maintain `CommittedBoundary` (`Snapshot`, `site`, `CaptureSlot[0..n]`, `logical.committed`) and `TentativeSegment` (working `State`/`Balances`/`Liabilities`, `Effects`, `segment_work`). (1) Segment-exit `Await` charges `1`, emits no `Step`, promotes `TentativeSegment` to `CommittedBoundary` at `site`, and seals `Continuation` (`H25`/`R7`). (2) Resume with false guard (`NotAdvanced`) charges `dispatch_cost = 1 + |guard_dag|` to `attempted`, emits no `Step`, and returns unchanged `Continuation` and `logical.committed`. (3) Precommit failure rolls back `TentativeSegment` and `Effects` to `CommittedBoundary` and returns `StageRejection { attempted: segment_work, committed: CommittedBoundary.committed }`. (4) A proof-chunk `TechnicalCut` checkpoints `(CommittedBoundary, TentativeSegment)` without advancing `site` or emitting a Core/2 `Continuation`.
- **Proposed Tests:** In [`financial_projection.rs`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/docs/audits/2026-10-16/sherlock-financial-core2-delivery/records/source/financial_projection.rs#L276-L405), assert: (a) `F01` false-await at `site 1` yields `advanced == false`, `attempted == 2`, `committed == 33`; (b) mid-segment `Ensures` failure after tentative `Transfer`/`Discharge` preserves `CommittedBoundary` (`committed == 33`, `attempted > 0`); (c) mid-segment `TechnicalCut` + resume matches uncut `StageOutcome::Complete`.

### Idea 2 (Rank 2 — MANDATORY): Per-Statement DAG Work Lowering & Ordered `CaptureSlot` Frame Bijection (`R0..R7`)
- **Evidence & Applicability:** Grounded in [`stage::cost`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/cost.rs#L23-L110), [`CaptureSlot`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/plan.rs#L31-L53), and [`stage::admit::resume`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/admit.rs#L279-L374). Applies to Owner H Core/2 lowering.
- **Tradeoffs:** Requires per-statement `ExprId` evaluation memoization (or DAG cost tags) instead of tree duplication, preventing `R6` preflight mismatches.
- **Proposed Text:**
  > Core/2 lowering must preserve `stage::cost` per-statement DAG accounting (`1 + |distinct reachable ExprId nodes|`, eager `And`/`Or`, `1` on `Await` entry, `1 + |guard_dag|` on resume) so Sherlock work equals `committed_before[site]` at `R6`. Frame slots at `AwaitSite` map bijectively to `CaptureSlot[0..n]` in declaration order (`Input`, prior `Let`, prior `Event` binders; max 256), while `program.states` stay in `Snapshot.state`. Resume admission enforces `R0..R7` in order; `R0..R5` refusals report `attempted = 0` and echo unvalidated caller `c.logical.committed`.
- **Proposed Tests:** Add a diamond-DAG `Let` + `Await` fixture checking `logical.committed == committed_before[1]`, plus `R0..R7` order tests verifying `R4`/`R5` layout refusals return `attempted == 0` with echoed `committed` before `R6`.

### Idea 3 (Rank 3 — MANDATORY): Two-Bit Liability State (`present`, `amount`) & Unified Multi-Asset Prefix Oracle
- **Evidence & Applicability:** Grounded in [`runtime.rs:176-185`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/runtime.rs#L176-L185), [`decode.rs:204-280`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/crates/mori-core/src/stage/decode.rs#L204-L280), and [`DERIVATION.md`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/docs/audits/2026-10-16/sherlock-financial-core2-delivery/records/oracle/DERIVATION.md). Applies to B `FinanceTables`, Quint, and [`financial-prefixes.json`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-parallel-implementation-20261016/models/sherlock/oracles/financial-prefixes.json).
- **Tradeoffs:** Adds an explicit `present: bool` bit alongside `amount: UInt` and `(asset, debtor, creditor)` in `FinanceTables`/Quint.
- **Proposed Text:**
  > Financial tables and the Quint model must separate absent liabilities (`present = false, amount = 0`) from discharged liabilities (`present = true, amount = 0`): `Snapshot.liabilities` keeps `present` zero rows (`LiabilityPresent == true`), while `StageOutcome.residual` filters `present && amount > 0`. Populate non-null `work` in `financial-prefixes.json` and include full multi-asset `F01`/`F02` prefixes.
- **Proposed Tests:** Assert in `F02` that `LiabilityPresent("grantDue")` is `false` at `start`, `true` (`40`) at `site 1`, and `true` (`0`) in `final_snapshot.liabilities` with `residual == []`.

### Idea 4 (Rank 4 — OPTIONAL): Extend Independent B Financial Oracle to Derive `PassTOutput` (`T`)
- **Evidence & Applicability:** Grounded in [`02-evidence/REPORT.md`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.moriarty-dev/runtime/sherlock-review-20261017/02-evidence/REPORT.md#L33-L35) (`E15`). Applies to B financial preparation adoption.
- **Tradeoffs:** Small addition to the independent Python oracle generator to eliminate `B/F`-only partial checking.
- **Proposed Text & Tests:** Require the independent B financial expectation generator to emit `(B, F, T)` including `PassTOutput`, verified by an executable roundtrip test over `F01`, `F02`, and `R7` unsorted-boundary rejections.

## 5. What to Reject or Defer

- **Reject:** Treating a proof-chunk `TechnicalCut` or fuel exhaustion as a Core/2 `Await` commit (violates `C10` rollback and `R6`).
- **Reject:** Host-side sorting/normalization of unsorted `Balances`/`Liabilities` or pruning zero-amount created liabilities (violates `R7` and `LiabilityPresent`).
- **Defer:** Cross-contract submessage reply queues (`CosmWasm` `ReplyOn` across external contracts); Core/2's single-action `StagePlan` (`MAX_AWAITS_PER_ACTION = 16`) suffices for v1 financial targets.
