## 2. Concrete Current-Design Gaps
1. **Unfolding Capacity Blowup (`Step` -> `Canonical`)**: In `state_decode.rs:358-404`, `DecodeMode::Step` permits `ValueRef.space == false` (Constant refs) and shared DAG nodes in `Data`, whereas `DecodeMode::Canonical` and `encode_observed_a` (`state_encode.rs:253-270`) unfold every Constant ref and shared DAG node into fresh preorder Data-space trees (`space == true`). Thus `project_a` (`observation.rs:90-94`, currently `#[cfg(test)]`) can fail with `StateFailure::Capacity` on a valid Step image (`Done(ConstantRef)` or DAG-aliased `Data`) under equal Step/Canonical `StateCapacities`.
2. **Physical Pointer Identity (`Arc::ptr_eq`) vs. Persistent Identity**: B's `PreImage::bind` (`shared.rs:413-422`) enforces admission (`SI01`) solely via `Arc::ptr_eq(&admitted.body, &img.admission.body)`. Meanwhile, `admit` (`owner_prepare.rs:1001-1004`) sets all three `IdentityC` digests to `DigestC::Unselected`, and `TraceIdentity` (`compare.rs:23-26`) is an unchecked caller `&str`. Cross-process checkpoints cannot pass `Arc::ptr_eq` without structural admission re-binding.
3. **Missing Stored `seg_base` after Compaction**: `MachineImage` (`shared.rs:388-397`) tracks cumulative `AllocCounters` and physical `ActiveLengths`, but relies on `implied_seg_base` (`host_implied_prefix.rs:43-59`, hardcoded zero), which forbids technical cuts or canonical resume. When canonical projection compacts unreachable `Data` rows, `active.data < alloc.data_nodes`, breaking zero-based segment checks (`SI05`/`SI23`) unless `seg_base` is stored in the boundary.
4. **Origin Loss, Least-Equal Call-Site Conflation, and Missing Importer**: `Snapshot` (`reference_types.rs:148-161`) omits `control_origin` and `frame_origins` (kept only in test `ImageMachine` at `image_reference.rs:53-57` and optional in `CallContext` at `helper_call_adapter.rs:33-36`), so `project_a` erases origins. Also, `expression_id`/`expression_suffix` (`state_encode.rs:89-113`) map structural `ExprRef`s to the minimum matching occurrence (`old.min(start)`), conflating duplicate syntactic subexpressions across call sites; and `observed_state_bytes_a` (`state_encoding.rs:182-202`) lacks an inverse byte parser or `Machine` importer.

## 3. Ranked Design Improvements

### Idea 1 (Rank 1 — Mandatory): Dual Step/Canonical Capacity Contract & Unfolding Accounting
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Cap'n Proto canonicalization and OCaml `Marshal.No_sharing` show that canonical preorder tree serialization unfolds shared pointers/constants, expanding storage without mutating semantics. Directly governs `project_a` and B `project`.
- **Tradeoffs**: Callers must size canonical `Data`/`DataFields` bounds for unfolded trees or handle unfolding overflow as a typed boundary-tooling refusal distinct from runtime capacity failure.
- **Proposed Text**: *"In `Trace continuity, observer stops and persistent boundaries`: Canonical projection (`project_a` / B `project`) unfolds Constant-space `ValueRef`s and shared `Data` DAG subtrees into disjoint preorder Data-space trees and compacts unreachable rows. Canonical `Data`/`DataFields` occupancy may exceed Step-image `active.data` while remaining independent of cumulative `alloc`. Boundary contracts must declare `step_capacities` and `canonical_capacities`; unfolding overflow returns typed projection refusal (`StateFailure::Capacity`), never runtime exhaustion."*
- **Normal Tests**: (1) `Done(ConstantRef)` and DAG-shared tuple Step images that fit `step_capacities` but exceed `canonical_capacities.data` return `Err(StateFailure::Capacity)` under `project_a` and succeed when `canonical_capacities` covers unfolded tree size; (2) idempotence `project_a(project_a(S)) == project_a(S)`.

### Idea 2 (Rank 2 — Mandatory): Two-Phase Persistent Admission Binding
- **Evidence & Applicability**: V8 snapshot deserialization and OCaml `Marshal.Closures` verify code/layout identity at load time before binding internal heap handles. Resolves `PreImage::bind` `Arc::ptr_eq` vs. cross-process checkpoint import and unchecked `TraceIdentity`.
- **Tradeoffs**: Adds an O(program tables) verification pass on checkpoint import while keeping O(1) `Arc::ptr_eq` on hot in-memory microsteps.
- **Proposed Text**: *"In `Program and admission` & `Persistent boundaries`: Separate in-process handle identity (`Arc::ptr_eq` in `PreImage::bind`) from persistent boundary identity. Checkpoint import must verify `(ProgramBlock / ProgramView, LayoutSchemaC, BoundsC, profile_tag)` via structural equality (`views_equal` / canonical program bytes) against an admitted `Admitted` instance before minting a bound `MachineImage`. Unbound `TraceIdentity` strings and `DigestC::Unselected` fields are diagnostic labels only."*
- **Normal Tests**: Export a checkpoint, construct a fresh `Admitted` allocation (`!Arc::ptr_eq`), verify raw `PreImage::bind` fails with `SI01`, and verify `import_boundary(fresh_admitted, boundary)` succeeds on matching program/bounds and fails with `ProgramMismatch` on a 1-cell mutated `ProgramView`.

### Idea 3 (Rank 3 — Mandatory): Explicit Stored `SegmentBase` in Boundary Records
- **Evidence & Applicability**: RISC Zero segment claims (`SystemState`) and `host_implied_prefix.rs:18-20` require non-initial segments to carry their entry baseline.
- **Tradeoffs**: Adds 9 scalar counter fields (`active`, `alloc`, `consumed_work`, `helper_work`, `helper_visits`, `reference_cursor`) to the boundary envelope.
- **Proposed Text**: *"In `Trace continuity, observer stops and persistent boundaries`: A persistent boundary record stores an explicit `segment_base` capturing post-projection `active` lengths alongside cumulative `alloc`, `consumed_work`, `helper_work`, and `reference_cursor`. `implied_seg_base` (all-zero) is restricted to initial entry (`consumed_work == 0`); resumed segments check per-segment invariants (`SI05`/`SI23`) relative to stored `segment_base`."*
- **Normal Tests**: Execute chunk 1 with dead `Data` allocations, cut and canonically project (`active.data < alloc.data_nodes`), import into chunk 2 with stored `segment_base`, and verify `check_step_inv` passes and cumulative `alloc`/`consumed_work` stay monotone.

### Idea 4 (Rank 4 — Optional): Occurrence-Indexed Control/Frame Provenance in Boundary State
- **Evidence & Applicability**: Fixes least-equal structural conflation in `state_encode.rs:89-130` and origin loss in `Snapshot` (`reference_types.rs:148-161`) by retaining `SourceOccurrenceId` (`control_origin`, `frame_origins`) and frame order.
- **Tradeoffs**: Carries occurrence IDs in boundary snapshots or explicitly declares A's `Snapshot` codec origin-erasing.
- **Proposed Text**: *"In `Profile and observation adapter matrix`: Where `Snapshot` is used without occurrence IDs, `encode_observed_a` canonicalizes duplicate structural subexpressions to their least `ProgramBlock` occurrence and erases `control_origin`/`frame_origins`; adapters requiring call-site provenance (`CallContext`, B `MachineImage`) must preserve explicit `SourceOccurrenceId` fields across boundaries and declare frame-order reversal between top-first L4 `FrameSlots` and bottom-to-top `MachineImage.frames`."*
- **Normal Tests**: Program with two identical call subexpressions at distinct `ExprId` sites: verify `ImageMachine`/`MachineImage` boundary preserves distinct `frame_origins`, while A's `Snapshot` projection maps both to the least occurrence index.

## 4. Mandatory vs. Optional Classification
- **Mandatory before closing persistent boundary / chunk-resume claims**: Ideas **1**, **2**, and **3**, plus promoting `project_a` from `#[cfg(test)]` to a production API with an inverse byte decoder for `observed_state_bytes_a`.
- **Optional / Scoped Clarification**: Idea **4** (carry `SourceOccurrenceId` in the boundary carrier or explicitly label A's `Snapshot` boundary as least-occurrence/origin-erasing).

## 5. Limited Design Verdict & What to Reject / Defer
- **Verdict**: **Conditionally sound direction, incomplete boundary specification.** Candidate 02 rightly separates Step-mode Constant-space states from canonical Data-space observations and rejects in-memory loop calls as checkpoint evidence, but must adopt Ideas 1–3 so unfolding capacity, cross-process admission binding, and post-compaction `segment_base` are well-defined.
- **Reject / Defer**:
  1. **Reject** inventing a new cryptographic hash algorithm for `DigestC::Unselected` or claiming native circuit projection proof before owner C4/F3/F4/F5/G4 gates close; use canonical byte/structural equality (`observed_program_bytes_a` / `views_equal`) for host boundary binding.
  2. **Reject** treating `Arc::ptr_eq` or caller-supplied `TraceIdentity` strings as persistent checkpoint authentication.
  3. **Defer** mid-helper-microstep persistent checkpointing across process boundaries if chunk cuts are restricted to completed A-step boundaries; otherwise require full `HelperControlC`/`HelperResultC`/`tasks` canonicalization before admitting mid-helper cuts.
