## Substantive reasons for approval

**Functional architecture and scope are complete, not narrowed to fixtures.** The design fixes one reference transition relation over an admitted strict first-order grammar with explicit code, environments, values, frames, and control, and treats universal, per-program, and finite-family realizations as unmeasured alternatives over one bounded `StepInput`/`StepOutput` contract ([design.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/design.md#L15), [interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L170)). Full Moriarty scope is retained throughout: U0–U7, M1–M17, MC01–MC08, SP01–SP12, G01–G24, ZR01–ZR16, MNR01–MNR08, all 277 ACTUS fixtures and the full DeFi denominator remain open obligations, explicitly restated in [traceability.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/traceability.md#L97) and the [implementation plan](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/docs/SHERLOCK-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md#L220). The permissionless product boundary from the [product contract](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md) is preserved; maintainer review never becomes a developer compile/prove/deploy gate.

**Deterministic schedule separation is sound.** The three profiles `sherlock-k-a/1`, `sherlock-quint-a/1`, and the newly proposed `sherlock-helper-microstep/1` are named, versioned, and never conflated: A keeps unmetered helpers and the charged frame-cap `Rejected(error4)`; Quint keeps its charged terminal-return tick; B charges one unit per legacy completion plus one per explicit bounded helper event, with `W_B = W_A + H_B` stated only as an unproved completed-interval mapping target, not same-fuel equivalence ([interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L91), [interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L99)). Failure precedence — publish already-final value/error, then zero-fuel exhaustion, then frame-cap, then data/environment/helper capacity — is stated once and reused by the slice contract.

**Bounded interfaces, DAG/ADT order, and named arithmetic are properly specified as contracts.** `Bounds`, `LayoutSchema`, `MachineImage Bounds F`, partial `encode`/`decode`, and `RepresentationRelation` bound every stored index and forbid hidden whole-state decoding or recursive helper closure inside a B step ([interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L83)). Source cost and physical cost are separate `ResourceManifest` fields, with unknown never reported as zero ([design.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/design.md#L35)). The arithmetic API uses named `{low, carryOut}`, `{low, borrowOut}`, and `{low, high}` results with defining equations, little-endian limbs, full 256-bit product retention, and the carry-bit versus word-carry distinction ([interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L155)); I independently checked the three normal specimens (2³²+5, 2⁶⁴+2, 3·2⁴⁸+3) and the financial prefix arithmetic (reserve 68/57, gross debit 32/43) and the `Schedule` match example (first arm 4, structural sum 9, repeated occurrences 8) — all are arithmetically correct. The witness/constraint split is clean: typed `WitnessPlan` DAG with stable topological order and last-use data, an independent `ConstraintPlan`, capability-gated emitters returning located `UnsupportedFeature`, and no claim that constraint JSON supplies witness computation ([interfaces.md](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/d1d11108d0920ceeff402858ffeb0a51f7ea6a39/openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/interfaces.md#L221)).
