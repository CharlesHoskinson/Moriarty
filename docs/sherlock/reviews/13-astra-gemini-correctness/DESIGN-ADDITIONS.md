## D1. Independent observations and literal expectations

For every claimed domain, freeze input identity, complete initial state, complete expected rule/charge/before/after records, stop observations and field coverage before subject execution. Expectations SHALL be independent of the implementation transition, materializer, encoder, subject-produced state and actual output. Literal transcriptions may use an input-only derivation; the derivation and its read closure SHALL be retained. Internal consistency tests that call computePublishedPost remain labeled as such and SHALL NOT count as independent expectations. Run batch and streaming producers separately against the frozen expectations. Their agreement is an additional differential, since they share Machine::step.

The comparison SHALL distinguish absent layer, absent optional field, present empty collection, zero scalar and false Boolean. It SHALL check complete initial state, initial == first.before, previous.after == next.before, last.after == returned state, each charge and cumulative work, and absence of an additional semantic record after publication. For a present empty record sequence, initial == returned state still applies. Returning no records does not by itself establish termination. Reconstruction of immutable program tables, inactive rows or fixed component context SHALL be explicit and identical in every compared state; expected data SHALL NOT fill missing actual fields.

For B, retain the actual admitted context and run identity plus an owned full pre observation, raw StepEventC, and owned full post observation for each actual OwnerEngine::step. observe_bound_image returns borrowed views: the driver SHALL finish copying the pre observation before mutation and observe the post after the call. Preserve raw event origin/result/disposition/ordinal fields even where the normalized semantic projection omits them. Event-only sinks, successful metadata compilation and a body containing unimplemented! do not satisfy the complete-state differential.

Continuity, equality and transition validity are separate checks. A continuity validator need not change first_difference's equality purpose or use the illustrative Gemini signature. Comparison cost includes the sizes of compared structures, not merely the number of events.

## D2. Profiles, terminal dispatch and observation stops

The report SHALL retain separate sherlock-k-a/1, historical sherlock-quint-a/1 and sherlock-helper-microstep/1 columns. Core/2 logical work is another named source clock. Physical transitions, helper work/visits, source statement work, allocation history, observer event count, host resources, circuit work and ledger fees SHALL NOT be silently substituted for one another.

Under A, a published outcome has no next record. Pending Return with an empty frame stack publishes rule80/charge0; Pending Error publishes82/0; only other valid pending controls at fuel0 publish84/0. A Return with a remaining frame is not completed. Ordinary rules charge1; frame-cap failure charges1 to Error(4), then publishes82/0. Pending/None is outside this observation domain. An observer call with budget0 leaves state unchanged, invokes no visitor, emits no record and returns Budget, including on a terminal machine. After an emitted event, Terminal outranks Observer Break, which outranks Budget. A bounded observer SHALL NOT automatically drain a pending publication past its budget. A new positive-budget call may observe it without replenishing semantic fuel.

Private advance_m stop labels and visit_steps stop labels SHALL retain their API identity. A terminal zero-budget difference between them is a diagnostic mapping, not grounds to change either semantic event sequence. Model totalization stutters SHALL be omitted only after checking prior terminal outcome, unchanged raw semantic state and the designated zero-rule/zero-charge/no-event representation. Merely filtering every rule0 is insufficient.

B P1/P2/P3/P6/P7 SHALL follow the actual owner source. In the current implementation, free terminal events stop further execution, P6 capacity refusal is charged and directly terminal, and frame-cap failure reaches Error(4) before free error publication. Keep raw event ordinal and separate harness event position. Do not infer a collision from consumed-work ordinal without a reachable counterexample. The completed-interval relation W_B = W_A + H_B requires its stated domain and successful interval completion; it does not promise equal-fuel behavior or interrupted-helper correspondence.

eraseHelpers SHALL be an observation relation, never an operation that rolls back live B arenas, logical allocation, helper counters or pending result. Save an A-origin observation or a justified projection while preserving every raw B prefix. A helper exit and consumption of its pending result are distinct events where the owner schedule says so. No source-only scheduling proposal replaces that schedule.

## D3. Physical images, canonical projection and resume

Within a Step representation, raw adjacency SHALL use exact previous output. Across a declared canonical boundary, retain original Step image, canonical result, complete semantic decoding, identity/bounds, logical counters and projection disposition separately. Canonical rows may be renumbered, unshared or removed according to the selected layout. They need not equal Step rows byte-for-byte. Sherlock's canonical data topology is child-before-parent; a foreign format's preorder rule SHALL NOT replace it.

Canonical capacity is measured on the unfolded result under F3/F5. Record the first incremental capacity refusal and retain the original image; refusal SHALL NOT become an application outcome, semantic charge, financial commit or successful checkpoint. Current project_a has one StateCapacities argument and encode_observed_a checks prepared/data capacity agreement. A future dual-capacity API requires an explicit design decision; it SHALL NOT be described as already implemented or necessary to the current one-capacity test contract.

A technical cut and resume SHALL preserve every profile-required semantic field: control, lexical contexts, frames, helper and pending result where applicable, program/profile/layout/bounds, raw work, origins where carried, and committed/tentative financial state. They SHALL NOT refill fuel, clear a caller, discharge a duty or publish a pending result. A cut before publication leaves it pending; a terminal image can be projected for observation but has no semantic resume. In-memory continuation of the same Machine is a distinct capability from persistent byte export/import.

The current B implied SegBase is zero throughout a qualified uncut HOST run. Its eight scalar cells are active(data,dataFields,env,valueSlots), alloc(dataNodes,dataFields,environmentCells), and consumedWork. Resume requires the existing stored-segment-base work; do not extend an implied-zero helper into evidence of resume. B origins SHALL be preserved when required; A's absent origins remain absent, and choosing the least structurally equal expression does not reconstruct a live occurrence. Process-local Arc binding SHALL NOT be reported as persistent identity or rebinding. Persistent importer/admission design and F3/F4/F5, StepInv, projectA and G4 proof obligations remain with their existing owners.

Basis: S UIREQ-014/016/029; I:175–193; L:70–81,145–154; layout_a/mod.rs:1–21; layout_a/state_encode.rs:89–111,138–175,253–255; layout_a/observation.rs:88–94; helper_machine/shared.rs:388–422; shared/host_implied_prefix.rs:1–58.

## D4. Financial prefixes and source correspondence

The future financial adapter SHALL carry a committed boundary and a separate tentative segment. A committed boundary includes the complete source Snapshot, pending site, ordered captures and source committed-work field. A tentative segment includes its working state, ordered effects, current control/environment and separately counted attempted work. A technical cut snapshots both without promoting the tentative segment or creating a source continuation.

For the existing Core/2 source contract, runtime::run_segment clones the admitted boundary. Reaching an Await charges its entry1, creates no Step for that entry, and ends the segment. Successful publication through stage::admit::Admitted::finish creates Waiting(advanced=true) or Complete. Resume with a false guard returns Waiting(advanced=false, segment=None), preserves the exact input continuation and reports the attempted guard/dispatch work. A local failure returns StageRejection without effects, segment or a replacement continuation. A diagnostic recorder SHALL retain the actual input continuation/event/call context separately; it SHALL NOT invent a returned rollback snapshot. A future technical checkpoint must preserve tentative work while paused and discard only that tentative delta when an applicable local failure is observed.

Core/2 source costs charge one dispatch plus distinct reachable expression nodes per statement, with a per-statement cache and eager And/Or. Keep its plan/attempted/committed counters distinct from A or B work. A future lowering SHALL specify how Core/2 counters are preserved and when correspondence observations occur. It SHALL NOT redefine A/B fuel to equal committed_before. Source /1 tree projection does not establish a /2 finance lowering or a current R6 contradiction. Capture-slot identity/order SHALL be preserved, including prior event binders; declared program state remains in Snapshot.state. Reported early refusal counters remain raw caller echoes until the applicable validation has succeeded; this observation selects no new admission rule.

Duty state SHALL include presence, exact amount, name, asset and parties. A present zero liability differs from absence; positive residual filtering does not erase the boundary row. Preserve exact asset domains, unaffected balances and state, gross transfers, fees, net effects, supply where applicable, ordered local/aggregate effects, pending frames and duties. Keep source fees, target fees and work in separate fields.

Use F's exact normal witnesses: F01 start17, false attempt2 with committed17 unchanged, true resume18, total35, attempts across that chosen sequence37; opening/closing invoiceDue5. F02 segment costs15/15/28, committed-before[0,15,30], false attempts6/2, total58, attempts66; grantDue absent→30→18→20→present0. These literals do not replace S UIREQ-017's distinct reserve100/liability40 example. Preserve historical financial-prefixes.json/receipts and add a versioned work-aware corpus. Complete A/B/D/F/T preparation claims require independent T expectations and actual execution; B/F-only evidence and Core/2 runtime observations remain separately scoped. Do not use a same-producer round trip as independent T evidence.

## D5. Executable finite model contract

This is a sequential machine model. There is no concurrency, time, ledger, cryptography, arbitrary program generation, private Clean implementation or full39/full96/native claim. Freeze a requirement-to-field/action/invariant/case table and all independent literal expectations before running it. Mark A, B, physical-boundary and financial subinstances separately; if one is not executed, name that omission rather than claiming its invariants passed.

### A interpreter and observer instance

Subject: existing models/sherlock/quint/kProfileMachine.qnt Snapshot and pure advance. Fixed input tables: N01, N02, N04, N05 from the independent normal corpus and a second literal value, with an explicit input-only name/ExprId map. Use fuel0..28, stackLimit in {0,1,2,8}, at most8 frames,29 recorded semantic events,3 cuts and16 observer calls. Observer budgets in {0,1,2,4,7}; the7 case supports N02's exact boundary. Explicitly map entry cases for Error, Return with/without frames and Push at the frame cap; these are component entry images, not claims of whole-program genesis reachability.

State SHALL contain the entire Snapshot (program, control, environment, frames, stackLimit, remaining, initial, outcome), initial snapshot, ordered complete event records, expected-case identity, program/profile/layout/bounds, raw prefix work and observer diagnostic state (call budget/count, Break request, stop reason, call count). It also carries phase Active/Cut/ProjectionRefused/Terminal, cut count, original saved Step state, projected physical state and the declared representation relation. Immutable table references are permitted only with exact reconstruction. The model SHALL keep machine transitions separate from expected records: applying advance must not read the oracle's next state.

Proposed actions: initializeCase; beginObservation(budget); emitSemanticEvent; stopZeroBudget; stopAtBudget; stopForObserver; stopForTerminal; cut; projectBoundary; resume; probeNoNext. The scheduler may choose Break before an event, with the callback decision applied after observing that event; it may not bypass Terminal priority. emitSemanticEvent uses the actual transition and appends one record only for a real event. The other actions do not charge semantic work. cut is enabled only at the declared modeled boundary; resume consumes its saved state after a successful projection relation. probeNoNext records a diagnostic, not an event. Repeated zero-budget calls are bounded by the call count so the finite model cannot spend its entire witness budget idling.

Properties: fixed identity/bounds; full-state trace continuity; remaining+consumed=initial; prefix charge sum; D2 dispatch and stop priority; no event after publication; all non-step actions preserve semantic counters; resumed state equals the saved semantic boundary; grouped observations preserve every internal event through the earliest semantic stop. Do not compare only final outcomes. For residual/MatchPlan claims, later adapters must additionally compare supported masks and m in {1,2,4,8} at every internal prefix; unsupported Schedule cases remain reported gaps, not silent omissions or invented successful runs.

### Financial instance

Keep two nonempty stores, exact asset/account/liability identities, ordered effects/captures, pending observation, per-invocation attempted source work, committed source work, raw machine work, and a preserved unrelated balance/state flag. Use S UIREQ-017's fixed reserve100/liability40→pay30/fee2/discharge30→reserve68/liability10/gross32/net30/duty10. No native fee, DUST, mint/burn or target phase effect is invented. Carry a later tentative payment of3 and a nonempty effect list while committed reserve68/duty10 remains fixed. A technical cut must preserve both stores. A declared model-only localFailureObserved then discards that tentative delta but retains the committed store, duty10 and already recorded work. This action abstracts observation of failure; it selects no universal opcode, fault trigger or charged-failure policy. Owner review remains required before any concrete hostile/runtime failure claim.

For Core/2 correspondence, add fixed normal F01 and F02 source-operation sequences from F, with exact per-statement values/costs, captured identities and literal full states. Source operations update tentative state; sourceBoundaryObserved publishes the successful segment; falseAwaitObserved reports guard attempt and preserves the exact continuation; completeObserved publishes the final successful segment. Cut/project/resume can occur after a reached tentative effect and between committed boundaries. No transition may assign its next state from an expected outcome. All numeric domains are finite: selected literal identities only, amounts0..200, sites0..2, source statement index0..16, source attempted/committed/cumulative counters0..100, at most32 financial observations and3 technical cuts. State/counter values are exact in this finite instance, with no wraparound; larger programs and UInt128 arithmetic are outside it.

Financial properties: cut/resume preserves committed and tentative data and all counters; only explicit modeled source publication changes the committed boundary; false await changes no committed state/captures/site; local failure never restores an earlier committed prefix or erases already recorded work; present-zero duties remain present while residuals contain exactly positive duties; asset totals and unaffected balances/flags are preserved in the no-mint/burn fixtures; ordered effects and exact gross/fee/net accounting match independent literals. Core/2 attempted and committed work are not required equal after a false observation, and neither is A/B fuel.
