# Reader findings and revision decisions

Twenty independent model personas reviewed the same frozen HTML text, fifteen application-check states, seven architecture states and eleven screenshots. Reports preserve actual provider identities and process qualifications in STUDY.json. No report is a security audit or a human usability study.

All twenty requested substantive changes. The recurring failure was a gap between simple product prose and internal source-reading shorthand. The old three-box hub did not teach UTxO flow and suggested that three conditions were three proofs.

1. Replace the generic hub with named financial inputs, recipient outputs, continuing accounts and values. Use distinct financial shapes for exchange orders, issuance, lending and treasury; do not manufacture missing pool reserves, loan terms, datum layouts or signer assignments.
2. Explain three invoice checks inside one whole-execution Strand. Separate conditions, proof instances, issued claim outputs and ledger transactions. A hypothetical four-Strand decomposition needs genuine relations and a compatible consumer.
3. Draw offchain authorization/proving and onchain Issue/settlement as different stages. Show claim token mint/burn, min-ADA lock and recovery without inventing amounts.
4. Put the proposed 112 funding next to the source 110 calculation. Also preserve the actual checked-in request as its own 109/90/14 scenario. Explain fee versus duty discharge and reserve lifecycle.
5. Show real Moriarty action syntax and selector artifacts; translate the compiler path into inputs and outputs a builder can inspect. Do not substitute a fictional SDK for the absent general transaction path.
6. Deepen Groth16 and setup explanations, especially current constant predicate identity versus parameterized reuse. Count compiled circuits, not properties, users or transaction instances. Opus owns this wording.
7. Draw relationships among the seven concerns. The supplied relation lists contain reciprocal links; the visual must not silently turn them into a directed acyclic execution pipeline.
8. Make missing evidence visibly qualify the intended financial result. Keep funds and outstanding duties separate.

Suggestions to add exact pool reserves, three actual invoice proof producers, an invented owner signer, a specific wallet signing standard, or fabricated datum byte layouts are unanswered requirements, not facts to paste into the page. Their absence is preserved in source notes.
