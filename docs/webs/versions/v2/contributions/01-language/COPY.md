# Moriarty Webs · Proposed Cardano concept

## Bring the required proofs to the transition.

Make the handoff from financial requirements to concrete proofs and the consuming script explicit. Keep exact assets, recipients, authority, fees, and remaining duties in view.

A **Web** is the proposed script-bound collection of **Strands** a consuming contract requires for one transition. A **Strand** is an individual proof with its claim, statement, and binding metadata. A **claim specification** defines the reusable requirement; it does not supply its proof.

**Weaving** means composing the required proofs under an explicit consumer rule. Composition alone establishes neither aggregation, recursion, nor execution. A transaction must still invoke the applicable validators and enforce its authorized effects.

## Read the illustration

The nodes show planned proof roles: requirements for future proof instances. They are not actual Strands or evidence of independent proof producers. Removing a required role makes the illustration incomplete. Restoring it means only illustrative assembly.

A requirement, pattern, signature, UTxO, or validator alone is not a Strand. Quoted historical source identifiers containing `thread` remain unchanged; public vocabulary is **Webs and Strands**.

## Pay part. Keep the remainder visible.

In the illustrative worksheet, paying **35 CASH** plus a separate **2 CASH fee** debits escrow by **37**. Invoice duty falls from **100 to 65**; reserve duty stays **10**.

Source funding **110** leaves cash **73** against duties **75**. Proposed funding **112** leaves cash **75**, backing one partial only. Every later partial charges another fee. The supplied invoice source has no reserve-duty-return action. This worksheet teaches arithmetic; it does not execute a program or establish settlement.
