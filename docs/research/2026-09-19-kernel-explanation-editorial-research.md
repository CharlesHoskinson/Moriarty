# Writing the kernel explanation

The kernel page needs to explain why the proposed architecture is a reasonable response to the financial problem under its stated assumptions. Its previous version repeatedly interrupted that argument with tallies, slogans and compact lists. This revision treats the page as a Diátaxis explanation for a developer who understands transactions but has not studied Moriarty. The interactive agreement supplies a recurring example; the linked requirements and language pages remain the reference material.

## Research and its limits

[Diátaxis’s explanation guidance](https://diataxis.fr/explanation/) places understanding, context and the reasons behind design decisions at the center of explanation. Applied here, that means developing the relationship between signed intent, external execution and unresolved obligations before asking the reader to operate a demonstration. The example should make those relationships concrete. It should not determine the whole article’s order merely because its interface has controls.

[Diátaxis distinguishes reference from explanation](https://diataxis.fr/reference-explanation/) by the reader’s purpose: consulting exact information differs from studying how something makes sense. An accounting table is useful within our example, but a succession of tables cannot substitute for the argument about why reservations must survive an uncertain external outcome. [Its application guidance](https://diataxis.fr/how-to-use-diataxis/) also supports improving existing material incrementally; this change does not create empty documentation categories or pretend that an illustration is a complete tutorial.

[Google’s developer style guidance on tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone) favors direct, respectful language and explicitly cautions against both choppy and long-winded writing. [Its lesson on paragraphs](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/paragraphs) centers each paragraph on a topic and develops it with relevant sentences. We adopt those qualitative principles. We do not turn its illustrative length guidance into another numerical target. A technical qualification belongs with the claim whose meaning it constrains, even when retaining it makes a sentence longer.

[Jakob Nielsen’s account of website reading](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/website-reading/) describes readers moving from scanning to sustained reading when headings and layout help them locate relevant material. This supports meaningful navigation and readable paragraphs together. It does not establish that our audience wants fragments, nor that older aggregate eye-tracking measurements predict this page’s readers. We retain headings and compact controls while giving the explanation room to develop.

[Reinhart and colleagues’ empirical study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11874169/) compared human passages with continuations generated by GPT-4o and Llama 3 variants. It found grammatical and rhetorical differences, including noun-heavy structures and difficulty matching registers. These results concern the tested models, corpora and prompts; they do not establish a universal signature of AI prose or specifically diagnose this page’s short sentences. Our editorial inference is to judge writing by audience, purpose and argument, rather than trying to pass a detector through synonym replacement, artificial irregularity or word bans.

## Diagnosis of the existing page

Headings such as “Six quantities kept apart,” “Four concerns, not four steps,” and “Three mechanisms, one statement, separate assumptions” describe the arrangement of the material before explaining its significance. Several paragraph sequences repeat assertions without developing their causal relationship. The responsibility diagram is useful as a diagram, but its compact labels cannot carry the full explanation of where authority ends and evidence begins.

The previous `site/VOICE.md` also prescribed measured sentence distributions and instructed writers to add short sentences to improve conformance. That is a plausible contributor to the clipped cadence, not a demonstrated account of how every sentence was generated. Its blanket removal of hedging vocabulary could also discourage legitimate uncertainty. The replacement guidance makes reader comprehension and preservation of technical meaning the criteria for revision.

## Editorial approach

The opening establishes the coordination problem and Moriarty’s relationship to the optional kernel. The agreement then gives that problem a concrete financial form. Responsibilities follow from the authority each participant actually possesses. Partial settlement introduces the distinction between an observed external effect and acceptance under the agreement. Uncertainty explains why a timeout alone cannot safely return reserved authority while external execution remains unresolved. The evidence discussion connects each mechanism to the statement and assumptions on which an accepting destination relies. Delegated solvers and continuation histories extend that same argument. Where relevant, the explanation should show a design tradeoff: direct Midnight execution avoids federation dependencies for local agreements, while optional cross-chain coordination introduces external evidence and custody assumptions in exchange for reaching other settlement systems.

Each paragraph should develop a recognizable idea, with the next sentence giving a reason, consequence, qualification or example that the reader needs. Sentence length follows those relationships. Headings name subjects the reader might seek. Lists remain appropriate for interface choices and reference-like comparisons; they should not replace reasoning. Numerical amounts, identifiers, thresholds and actual financial constraints remain exact. Decorative counts of sections or ideas do not help explain the design.

Inkwell’s structural-editing and cadence guidance and Humanizer’s pattern review are editorial aids. They are not evidence of correctness, an authorship detector or a substitute for reading. No statistical author profile governs this rewrite. Security distinctions—especially authenticated observation versus acceptance, and a proof statement versus external truth—define the design and stay beside the relevant claims. Implementation progress belongs in its own final section. The user explicitly clarified during revision that repeated proposed/not-demonstrated qualifications undermine the explanation: the article must state what the kernel does and why, rather than turn each paragraph into a development-status report.
