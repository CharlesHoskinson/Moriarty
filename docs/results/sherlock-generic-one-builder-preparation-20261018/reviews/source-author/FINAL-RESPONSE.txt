## Exact Limitations and Unspecified Policy Seams

1. **Finance `TypedExtensionReserved` and `FinOpSrc` Own-Name Projection Seam**:
   Initial source scope is `ExtDomain::GENERIC_ABSENT` (`GrammarDomain::GenericNominalExtensionReserved`, `FinanceInput::Absent`). `FinanceInput::TypedExtensionReserved` is refused with `FeatureKind::FinancialInput`, and any non-empty `assets`/`states`/`inputs` slice or `StateRead`/`LiabilityPresent`/`LiabilityAmount`/`Fin`/`Await` expression is refused with `FeatureKind::FinancialSyntax` both prior to Pass-B capacity accounting and inside the internal `project_fin_name` callback. Which optional `ExtExpr::Fin` fields (`asset`, `from`, `to`, `liability`, `state`, `name`) project into Pass B under `TypedExtensionReserved` vs fail in Pass F is an unspecified finance policy seam and is not invented here.
2. **Global Pass-A Cross-Carrier Precedence Seam**:
   `ext_source_traversal` and `nominal_declaration_order` intentionally omit a global Pass-A feature-classification order across non-declaration carriers (`ExtExpr::Construct`, `ExtValue::Constructor`, `ExtPattern::Ctor`/`Wildcard`, signature `TypeExprSrc` under `DedicatedSorts` when `program.types` is empty). `prepare_ext_program_types` delegates `CheckedTypeDeclView::new_checked` and `PassTSession::begin` (`G-10` refusal under `DedicatedSorts`) without inventing a new cross-carrier Pass-A ordering policy.
3. **Pass-C `T3UseSite` to `ExprId` / `SourceOccurrenceId` Seam**:
   `PreparedExtProgramSource.t3_records` records walker-local `T3UseSite` ordinals (`expr`, `value`, `pattern` in PD-1 order), not `ExprId` or `SourceOccurrenceId`. Materializing the Pass-C occurrence map, Pass-P decision trees, `Admitted` tokens, and runtime `ConstructorFields` pending work remains downstream work.
4. **No Compilation, Execution, or Requirement Closure Claim**:
   `CheckedTypeDeclView` and `OwnerExtTypeBuilder` implementation bodies remain pinned and unreviewed here; no compilation, test run, runtime execution, or UIREQ-034 (`Schedule` 4 / 9 / 8) closure is claimed.
