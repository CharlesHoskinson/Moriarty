### 3. Substantive Reasoning

- **Progress vs. Verified Wait Distinction**: Read-only readiness inspection (`scripts/doctorwatson.sh --inspect`, calling [`load_readiness`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-c6-physical-host-codecs-20261019/models/doctorwatson/reconciliation.py) with post-read pin verification `if after != pins:` and exiting `return 0  # inspectable records only; never execution approval`), tool availability (`SF-15`: `"WHEN a tool or layer becomes available, availability SHALL NOT create a passing observation or complete the feature"`), or waiting on a blocked adapter is a **verified wait / explicit blocker** (`"An unavailable call is a named missing consumer or input, never an invented command or successful task"`). **Genuine progress** requires integrating source against the real receiving API and observing a discriminating check or authenticated collector receipt, bounded by `SF-14` (`"WHEN two attempts repeat the same defect class, the owner SHALL reduce the reproducer and change approach before another broad attempt; WHEN two process-only cycles or thirty administration minutes accumulate, the owner SHALL perform eligible work or record the concrete blocker and owner"`).
- **Affected Check Reuse (`SF-17`)**: `"WHEN only tests change, checks/review SHALL focus on the affected closure and reuse unchanged passing evidence unless composition or a concrete concern requires broader checks."` This avoids redundant reruns of unchanged passing prefixes via `--test-selected MODULE [MODULE...]` while mandating that `"a changed production caller refreshes its affected composition"` and post-PR integration repeats affected local checks.
- **Applicability of Review vs. Terminal `SUCCESS`**:
  1. *Provider/Process Return*: `"Provider terminal success means a response returned; source integration, observed checks and accepted behavior need their own evidence."` Likewise, `"Exit 0 plus present files is not enough; see decode_cached_current_f"` and verification requires `"its positive semantic verdict, not exit zero alone"`.
  2. *Scoped Review*: Approves only the exact inspected changed units and boundary (`"The opinion approves only the exact bounded emitter source, not a later consumer/test source, native proof, measured degree or performance"`).
  3. *Accepted Product Behavior*: Requires integrated source, digest-pinned [`load_readiness`](file:///home/charl/Moriarty/.worktrees/sherlock-c6-physical-host-codecs-20261019/models/doctorwatson/reconciliation.py), live cgroup/mount restoration probes, and independent verification.
