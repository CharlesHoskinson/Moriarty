## 3. Representation resolutions: they preserve the contract

**Configuration.** `normalize_config_data.py` only regroups the flat fields into `{bounds, layout, domain, fuel, stack}`, duplicates `bounds`, and stringifies five u128 tokens. I checked the actual configuration against that mapping field by field. The mapping leaves no freedom to fit values. `scope` is dropped, which is correct. `locator` moves to the call argument, and the actual `input.json` records `null`.

**Admission marker.** In `consumer.rs::record_boundary`, `same_as` is emitted only after typed equality `actual.admission == genesis.admission`; otherwise the admission is inlined. `compile.json#/genesis/admission` is `genesis.wire().admission`, so expanding the marker is exact. Rev01 to rev02 flips only the two affected flags. No actual, oracle or source data changed.

Qualification: the equality covers observable admission fields only. Private `ext_capacities` are excluded (gap 2).
