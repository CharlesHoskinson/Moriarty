§1 RESOURCE03 D2 PREDICATE CORRECTION (runtime statement only).
1.1 The resource03 D2 review_predicate, that REQUIRED_MASKS hides every credential path under /home/charl/.cargo in every phase where that mount is visible, is false as written for runner fc1c1c98 and for original R5 028f950b. It is withdrawn as a statement of fact and replaced for the record by 1.2. This policy makes and implies no change to source, REQUIRED_MASKS, mounts, modes, guards, numerics, quotas or provisioning.
1.2 Truthful statement. The old Cargo home /home/charl/.cargo, including any credentials, credentials.toml or token-bearing config, is masked from every Cargo, rustc, build-script and proc-macro process in every phase. It is also masked from every runner data worker in phases B, C, D, E and F. It is restored read-only only to phase A0 and A runner data workers. That exposure is pre-existing: it dates from R3 N2, is documented in the R5 README, and is unchanged by the exact16 amendment. A0/A workers run no Cargo, compiler, build script or proc-macro. They run with --unshare-net, --cap-drop ALL, seccomp network and io_uring notify-kill, and a /home/charl tmpfs. They read from the old Cargo home only the paths that staging_destination() admits for the 2,243 pinned staging rows, and no admitted class names a Cargo credentials or root config file. Receipts and logs carry paths, stats and hashes only.
1.3 Accepted exception. The A0/A visibility in 1.2 is accepted as a pre-existing exposure within the original trusted-runner boundary. The same-uid supervisor already holds unmasked home access, so this exposure adds no principal beyond the trusted runner. It is not a credential-hiding guarantee for A0/A.
1.4 Unread fact. The 20 hash-pinned git-db-metadata inputs, including git/db/.../config, are copied into the source home and are visible to Cargo in phases B through F. Their content has not been read. Nothing in this policy asserts that they are free of credential material. Making that determination is a separately authorized action. Until Root records its result, every real-run or native admission record must carry this as an open fact.
1.5 Required disclosure. Any future user Tier 1 request must state:
(a) A0/A runner data workers have read-only visibility of the whole old Cargo home.
(b) The open fact in 1.4.
(c) A0 is not stat-only. It reads and SHA-256 hashes the full raw bytes of all 2,243 inputs. The 52 git-loose-unknown containers are re-hashed as opaque bytes in A, C, D, E and, for Tier 2, F. The runner never decompresses them, but Cargo/libgit2 may inflate them in B or C.

2.3 Conditions.
(a) Test, fixture, guard and driver bytes are exactly as bound. Any change in response to a result voids this scope for the changed bytes and requires fresh review.
(b) The record discloses the oracle classes used: stdlib Git framing with published known-answer values, literal SHA-256 vectors, the owner record transcribed twice, contract text, and refusal-message substrings acting as predicate discriminators.
(c) Every outcome is recorded verbatim with the label INTERFACE_INFORMED_NOT_BLIND_R2_UNSATISFIED. That includes failures, errors, DRIVER_REFUSAL text and tripwire messages, and every attempt with none selected or omitted.
(d) Results bind only to guard fc1c1c98 and to the observed interpreter, never to 5ebe3469 or 84b0b814. If a diagnostic run against 84b0 is separately admitted, it is recorded separately.
(e) A result may not be cited as satisfying R2, as source adoption, as real-run or native admission evidence, or as C4 closure. It may be cited only as an ordinary unit result for the bound bytes.
2.4 Native boundary. Real-run or native admission of any runner requires a separate Root disposition of R2. There are two routes:
(a) Tests authored by a fresh Opus context that has not seen the implementation, reviewed, and passing against the admitted runner, with the original 22 tests (0f966d96) passing unchanged.
(b) A separately authored R2 amendment with its own fresh non-author votes.
R2 names 5ebe3469. How it applies to fc1c1c98 is unresolved and is not decided here.
