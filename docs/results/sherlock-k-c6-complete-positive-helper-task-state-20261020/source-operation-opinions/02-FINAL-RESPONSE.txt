**Required change:** preserve a clean CAPTURE_FAIL, with persisted products and validated report, as a distinct semantic/capture-mismatch terminal outcome in `ROOT-RESULT.json`. It must still stop before phase 06, but it must not be conflated with an exception or infrastructure failure.
