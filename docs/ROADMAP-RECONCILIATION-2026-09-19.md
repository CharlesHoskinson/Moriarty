## Authority and acceptance interpretation

Project maintainers may require review, budget and evidence before spending project resources or declaring their release complete. Participants may cryptographically restrict their assets and contracts. The language must not require project permission for independently authored supported programs. Required proof/ledger predicates are never waived by an internal approval, and missing internal receipts alone never reject an otherwise valid public program. Any residual older statement saying all proof commands require RP03 is superseded for public use by this contract; nested campaign instructions retain their actual internal scope.

All acceptance plans remain specified-only until exact executable evidence exists. P6/P7 do not relax original denominators, silently retire G01–G24 or promise production readiness from a scoped Preview result.
