These are different inventories, not a completion percentage. All historical lines were preserved exactly. Original normative clauses remain unchanged.

The [full roadmap audit](audit/roadmap.md) retains all 69 U/M/MC/SP/G identities and their exits. Every full exit remains open. The underlying runtime, cryptography and transitive historical result bundles were not exhaustively audited. Two source limitations remain explicit: the original direct M-table conversation was not independently recovered, and historical payload adequacy/currentness still needs its applicable review. This audit is complete for the stated source registers; it is not completed product verification.

Preview and Preprod evidence remain separate. The broader Preprod goal is paused and native authority remains zero; this audit executes neither.

./scripts/doctorwatson-utxo.sh --next
