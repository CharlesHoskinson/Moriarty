# OWS and x402 evidence reference

OWS and x402 are separate standards. The captured scope is the finite advertised current official documentation, pinned repositories, and four versioned research papers. Capture does not imply semantic reading of every page. Exact acquisition exceptions, source hashes and reading ranges are recorded below. No signing commands, payments, attacks, implementation tests or proof builds were executed.

- ows: [report](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/ows/REPORT.md), [claims](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/ows/claims.json), [reading coverage](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/ows/reading-coverage.json).
- x402: [report](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/x402/REPORT.md), [claims](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/x402/claims.json), [reading coverage](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/x402/reading-coverage.json).
- security: [report](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/security/REPORT.md), [claims](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/security/claims.json), [reading coverage](../../../deliverables/ows-x402-study-2026-09-19/security/reading-coverage.json).

OWS key custody, agent access and policy evaluation do not supply a Midnight adapter or cryptographically enforce every economic restriction. x402 facilitator and payment evidence do not establish arbitrary service delivery or independent-chain atomicity. Source-version differences and documented incomplete policy contexts are preserved in the reports. Research papers describe scoped historical experiments and proposals, not a blanket current vulnerability verdict.
