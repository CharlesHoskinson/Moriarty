# One intention across competing platforms

An owner permits spending ten units including fees to obtain a specified asset after recipient acceptance and a documentary predicate. A solver may propose partial delivery. One external leg succeeds, another remains unresolved, and cancellation races with a late result.

For each platform, identify the authenticated specification, authority scope, resource reservation, evidence predicate, completed effects and residual duties. Ask whether its proof or runtime predicate covers each fact. Then replace the document, reset the budget in another branch or present a proof for another artifact. Rejection of these traces matters as much as successful execution.

The comparison is a proposed experiment, not an executed benchmark. [How-to](how-to.md) · [Index](index.md).
