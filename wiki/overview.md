# Moriarty knowledge vault

Moriarty is a bounded financial language for Midnight. The research connects financial behavior, developer intent, contract semantics, proofs and ledger acceptance.

Start with [[wiki/index|the research index]] or the [developer introduction](../README.md). The [complete roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) identifies current capabilities and remaining acceptance requirements. [[wiki/canvases/moriarty|The language map]] connects the main research areas.

ACTUS supplies scheduled financial behaviors; the DeFi study supplies exchange, lending and composition cases. The [three-report reconciliation](../openspec/REPORT-RECONCILIATION-2026-09-07.md) integrates those financial requirements with outcome intents and proof-carrying data. Midnight constrains the compiler, proofs, private state and settlement design.

Local simulation and restricted Compact kernels exist. Financial ledger acceptance, native recursive Moriarty proofs, private witness handoff and complete conformance remain open. Historical evidence stays scoped to what was actually tested.

Use [[wiki/workflow|the vault workflow]] to ingest, query and maintain this research.
