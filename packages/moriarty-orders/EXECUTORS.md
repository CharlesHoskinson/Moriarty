# Restricted venue execution

The order owner selects a primary and a fallback client at deployment. Both have distinct nonidentity Jubjub public keys. The owner inspects the installed keys and signs the contract-specific wallet enrollment and order. The restricted fill verifier checks a second, role-specific signature from the selected client over the same order, chain, contract and revision.

The primary alone may fill before order-relative second 1100. The fallback alone may fill from 1100 through 1399. The accepted ledger clock enforces the boundary. The clients share no signing credential. Each has a separately funded B wallet. The selected client supplies at least 200 B to Bob and receives the 100 A: the primary at the order's executor account, the fallback at its own payout account fixed at deployment. Withholding a fill therefore does not let the primary collect A paid for by the fallback. This profile charges zero application fees. The test DUST sponsor pays network fees. A live venue must arrange its own inventory, fees and compensation.

The owner can cancel and reclaim residual A using the owner key while both clients are unavailable. The clients cannot cancel. A primary can withhold its signature throughout its exclusive window. A fallback or fee sponsor can also withhold service. Owner recovery still requires ledger access, fee funding and inclusion. Participation gives no fill guarantee.

Admission is specific to this deployment. The public O1 program has no client keys or service registry: any party holding the owner's fill grant can submit it. A public developer can compile Moriarty, prove a different contract or publish a different selection policy without joining the venue. This local test uses two clients operated by the maintainer. External operator participation and production liquidity need separate evidence.
