# Optimization EARS index

DRAFT / PROPOSED. These 16 clauses and 32 scenarios refine optimization work; they do not extend the original completion denominator. No clause is implemented, validated or accepted by this draft. The normative EARS/scenarios are in [spec.md](specs/sherlock-circuit-optimizations/spec.md).

| ID | EARS condition and response | Retained clauses |
|---|---|---|
| [SOPT-001](specs/sherlock-circuit-optimizations/spec.md#requirement-sopt-001-observed-recursive-baseline-before-integration) | WHEN an optimization is proposed for integration, integration SHALL NOT proceed until an actual Midnight stagenet recursive child/parent/terminal baseline with its exact source, statements, keys, target receipts and complete readback is preserved; integration means any change within the traced source/context identity membership (SOPT-010) of the native recursive child, parent or terminal path, while isolated source preparation MAY precede that observation and changes outside that membership are unaffected. | SCON-022, SCON-023, SRPOC-001, SRPOC-002, SRPOC-007 |
