# D-011: Chunk cap K_MAX = 2

- Status: accepted
- Authority: OWNER
- Legacy IDs: T20; ANC-012

## Context and Problem Statement

A call whose effects exceed one call vector runs as chunks.

## Decision Drivers

- Owner 2026-11-14 accepted the chunk cap (LOOP.md row T20).
- `L_TX` = 1,024 (OD-7).

## Considered Options

- `L_TX` sized so one call covers every measured entry; chunks a capped fallback with K_MAX = 2.
- Unbounded chunks.

## Decision Outcome

K_MAX = 2.

Authority: OWNER. Owner ruling 2026-11-14.

### Consequences

- Good: Bounded writes per call.
- Bad: Large entries are refused by the compiler (CF-007, MYC-REF-009).

## Confirmation

- outcome: T20 Quint model of the chunk cap; the chunk atomicity check of ANC-004 to ANC-006 and ANC-008 (k contract calls in one intent of one transaction). Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.
- conformance: ANC-012 (on `t20-batched`), MYC-CHK requirements; slice C BAT; slice B CLB-007. Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### `L_TX` sized so one call covers every measured entry; chunks a capped fallback with K_MAX = 2

- Good: At most two chunk contract calls in one intent of one transaction.
- Bad: An entry above 2 chunks is refused.

### Unbounded chunks

- Good: No refusal.
- Bad: Unbounded writes per call, against D-009.

## More Information

### Reconsideration

A suite entry needs more than 2 chunks.
