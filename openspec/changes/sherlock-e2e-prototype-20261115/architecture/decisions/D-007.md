# D-007: One BLS12-381 wrap over small-field STARK leaves and nodes

- Status: accepted
- Authority: OWNER
- Legacy IDs: memory rule 2026-11-11 (small-field STARK); OD-24; hard rule "one BLS12-381 wrap"

## Context and Problem Statement

Recursion in BLS12-381 circuits at k = 21 per node is too slow for an elastic prover.

## Decision Drivers

- Owner 2026-11-11: leaves and nodes are small-field STARKs, one BLS12-381 wrap; no heavy k = 21 node chains.
- STARK engine: OpenVM stark-backend v2.1.0 with its CUDA prover (TERMINOLOGY.md).

## Considered Options

- BabyBear STARK leaves and nodes with WHIR, bridge layer `L`, Fr-native `L_Fr`, one PLONK-KZG wrap.
- BLS12-381 circuits at every level.

## Decision Outcome

The first option.

Authority: OWNER. Owner ruling 2026-11-11.

### Consequences

- Good: Work grows by shards, not by circuits.
- Bad: The bridge (`L`, `L_Fr`) carries the soundness argument of the field switch.

## Confirmation

- outcome: STL-002 full chain per call. Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.
- conformance: MYC-ACC-010, MYC-KEY-010, LW-001 to LW-022; slice B and C requirements. Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### BabyBear STARK leaves and nodes with WHIR, bridge layer `L`, Fr-native `L_Fr`, one PLONK-KZG wrap

- Good: GPU leaves in seconds (T16).
- Bad: Two bridge layers.

### BLS12-381 circuits at every level

- Good: No field switch.
- Bad: Refused by the owner.
