# D-016: Stage 1 of the Compact compiler reads the stock compactc output

- Status: proposed
- Authority: LEAD PICK for T15
- Legacy IDs: CF-001; MYC-FE-001; MYC-FE-008; T18 (b)

## Context and Problem Statement

CF-001 (processor-final) taps `Lflattened` inside `compactc` and replaces `reduce-to-zkir`. The Mycroft design (T18 (b), merged at 3be9e8fd5) reads the stock ZKIR 3.1 file and `contract-info.json`.

## Decision Drivers

- AGENTS.md product boundary: maintainer tooling is no prerequisite for developers.
- MEASURED: 33 of 36 suite programs compile with stock `compactc` 0.36.0 and Mycroft compiles all 106 circuits (REPORT.md:8).
- ECC-001.

## Considered Options

- Stage 1 reads stock ZKIR; a Stage 2 pass inside `compactc` is accepted only by equality with Stage 1 (MYC-FE-008).
- Tap `Lflattened` first (CF-001).

## Decision Outcome

Stage 1 on stock ZKIR for the prototype. CF-001 describes Stage 2.

Authority: LEAD PICK for T15. LEAD PICK for T15: the T18 (b) design merge by the lead; no owner words on the tap point.

### Consequences

- Good: A developer needs only stock `compactc`.
- Bad: The pinned ZKIR version is one more pin (EUF-012).

## Confirmation

- outcome: ECC-001, ECC-010 (suite coverage). Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.
- conformance: MYC-FE-001, MYC-FE-002. Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Stage 1 reads stock ZKIR; a Stage 2 pass inside `compactc` is accepted only by equality with Stage 1 (MYC-FE-008)

- Good: No patched compiler; native transcripts are the reference.
- Bad: ZKIR has lost loops and calls (inlined), so step counts follow native.

### Tap `Lflattened` first (CF-001)

- Good: Shorter programs.
- Bad: Needs a patched `compactc`; no suite evidence.

## More Information

### Reconsideration

Stage 2 shows a measured gain and passes MYC-FE-008.
