# D-021: The anchor slot is the key constant 15

- Status: accepted
- Authority: LEAD PICK confirmed
- Legacy IDs: T12-SLOT15; ANC-001; ANC-013 to ANC-015; MYC-SEAL-005; MYC-SEAL-021

## Context and Problem Statement

A prover-chosen anchor slot lets a prover run its own program on a victim contract (PENDING-CX of Mycroft a2-transcript-fable). On the T10m keys the anchor is cell (0), which is the first field of a Compact contract.

## Decision Drivers

- ANC-013, ANC-014, ANC-015 (completion).
- T18 (c): t02 W word 3 and s01 W word 4 address root key 0.

## Considered Options

- Slot 15 as a wrap key constant; the compiler refuses a contract that uses slot 15.
- Keep cell (0).

## Decision Outcome

Slot 15.

Authority: LEAD PICK confirmed. LEAD PICK confirmed by the owner 2026-11-15 (wiki log 09:04, T12-SLOT15).

### Consequences

- Good: Compact contracts keep their native ledger layout.
- Bad: Open: no commit yet in `decode.rs`, `iface.rs`, SherlockLedger (AUDIT.md A7a).

## Confirmation

- outcome: test `t12_anchor_slot_15`; ECC-009 (T-SLOT15). Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.
- conformance: ANC-013 to ANC-015; slice B CLB and slice C SLC requirements. Status: planned unless the cited requirement reads MEASURED.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Slot 15 as a wrap key constant; the compiler refuses a contract that uses slot 15

- Good: Stock W on a deployed contract; the slot is not prover-chosen.
- Bad: A rekey; contracts lose one slot.

### Keep cell (0)

- Good: No rekey.
- Bad: Collides with the first contract field.

## More Information

### Reconsideration

The ledger changes its top-level layout.

## Merged record D-072 (slice C)

### D-072: Anchor in ledger slot 15, pinned by the wrap key

- Status: accepted
- Authority: LEAD PICK confirmed
- Legacy IDs: T12-SLOT15; MYC-SEAL-021; X1 (a)

#### Context and Problem Statement

A Mycroft contract with a user-writable field 0 could hold a forged `C_P'` in the anchor cell. A prover-chosen slot lets a prover run its own program on a victim contract (T12 PENDING-CX).

#### Decision Drivers

- WRP-007, SLC-002, ANC-013, ANC-014, ANC-015
- stock `compactc` words stay byte-equal for all-Sherlock contracts

#### Considered Options

- Slot 15 as a wrap key constant, inside the single rekey (chosen)
- Keep cell 0 with a compiler refusal only: the guard is outside the circuit

#### Decision Outcome

The anchor cell moves from 0 to 15 in `decode.rs`, `iface.rs` and SherlockLedger inside the T25 rekey: the wrap pins both the anchor read key (lane 6) and the chunk write key (lane 15) to 15 (ANC-013) and refuses an ordinary call that carries the anchor-write template (ANC-016); the Mycroft compiler refuses a contract that writes slot 15.

Authority: LEAD PICK confirmed (owner 2026-11-15, llm-wiki `wiki/log.md:4098-4107`). Shared with slice B (CLB) for `iface.rs` and slice A for the compiler refusal.

##### Consequences

- Good: the guard sits in the circuit.
- Bad: every deployed ledger and the wrap VK change; the T17 (a) ledger is not reusable.

#### Confirmation

WRP-007 by `t12_anchor_slot` (observed for forged slots on `t12-gate@90f9b4df9`) and `t12_anchor_slot_15` (planned, uncommitted); SLC-002 by a compiled-contract check (planned).

#### More Information

##### Reconsideration

A Compact release that changes ledger slot layout.
