## Trust boundaries

## Failure handling

Stop the affected campaign on missing interfaces, failed controls or resource exhaustion. Preserve original charges and failed results. Continue independent semantic work when native feasibility blocks. Do not remove required financial rows or mandatory PCD to fit an allocation.

## Migration
