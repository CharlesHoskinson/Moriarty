## Semantic definitions and bounded representation

Semantic modules distinguish syntax/program tables, recursive immutable values, environments, ordered frames, helper controls, outcomes, step and fuel-run. The semantic run decreases on finite fuel; arbitrary named source self/mutual recursion need not be structurally recursive on user data. First freeze the legacy schedule `sherlock-k-a/1`, the distinct Quint schedule `sherlock-quint-a/1`, and the proposed B schedule `sherlock-helper-microstep/1` in the rule inventory. B exposes one-record/cursor helper transitions and separately charges each helper event; no B-to-A fuel correspondence is currently established.

Representation modules supply `Bounds`, `LayoutSchema`, `MachineImage Bounds F`, partial `encode`/`decode` and `RepresentationRelation`. Fixed vectors cover code, recursive-data nodes/fields, environments, frames and helper tasks; counters, tags, active lengths and exact scalar interpretation are explicit. Recursive Lean inductives are semantic meanings, never an unbounded circuit field. Decoding DAG storage unfolds ordered structural occurrences, and saved caller environments/pending frames remain fully recoverable. A decoder is not an implicit step implementation; codec traversal and serialization work need separate reporting. The supported domain and runtime/input capacity outcomes remain explicit. The Opus-owned [profile reconciliation](security-candidate-b.md#candidate-b-profile-reconciliation-opus-55) requires a per-layout canonical codec with deterministic roots, child-before-parent topology and zeroed inactive fields. A canonical physical projection may compact storage while logical allocation-history counters remain explicit. Exact codec/projection implementation and proofs are pending.

The intended round-trip and step/run correspondence artifacts must name their admitted domain, layouts, schedules, bounds and complete observations. They are open obligations. The new generic `TypeDecl`/`CtorLayout`/`MatchPlan` adapter begins with monomorphic recursive groups and source-priority matching. Dedicated existing UInt list/tree semantics are not evidence of arbitrary declarations or nested lists. Parameterized specialization, closures and further types retain separate coverage.

## Components and common step boundary

The common `StepInput`/`StepOutput` contains only fixed bounded program/state/event fields. Full semantic `StepRecord` snapshots are produced by a separate materialization interface. Build scalar/frame/helper transition slices first, then call/bind/match/data coverage. Inventory every rule, helper visit, logical frame operation and arithmetic operation against its actual bounded component and output behavior. Missing components remain named unsupported or unimplemented entries. Interface sketches are not a complete implementable schema: Phase 0 must freeze remaining row/control payload types, widths/tags/slices, helper phases and the full outcome/error-code table. Proposed `Control.ScheduleNext` denotes scheduling; `Family.Schedule` and the example source `Schedule` ADT have separate roles.

Arithmetic components implement the independently specified `addCarry_b`, `subBorrow_b`, `mulWide_b` and `checkedU128Add/Sub/Mul` APIs. Their named carry/borrow/high results and full 256-bit intermediate product precede checked outcome selection. Public Clean WitnessIR U64 wrapping operations are not checked UInt128 semantics. Four U32 and two U64 byte groupings both have sixteen field elements in the observed public representation; packed layouts require additional adapters. No limb/row winner is selected.

The same reference step may be emitted as universal, per-program or fixed-family realization. Shared bounded inputs/outputs and independent prefix expectations are mandatory comparison interfaces, while concrete dispatch and arithmetic realization remain decisions after measurement. `ReferenceSlice` preserves every internal stop and work prefix; `ResidualStepIR` specializes immutable code/layout facts without evaluating source expressions, unrolling recursion or deleting dynamic frames/fuel/cap checks.
