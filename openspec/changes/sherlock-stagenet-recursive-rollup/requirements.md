# Experiment EARS

Status: specified, not implemented or accepted. SRPOC identifiers supplement the original Sherlock requirements; their count never inflates the original EARS completion total. Existing security-owner requirements remain controlling for recursive validity, statement binding, target authorization and rejection cases; SRPOC-002 and SRPOC-007 use the stagenet-validation predicate in the experiment's October 19 security-owner clarification.

- **SRPOC-001:** When DoctorWatson selects Sherlock implementation work, it shall prioritize actual Midnight stagenet recursive-proof validation and its concrete missing dependencies, while retaining the full Sherlock scope and permanent formal/handwritten lanes.
- **SRPOC-002:** When an experiment reports recursive-proof validation, it shall identify the executed Sherlock recursive relation, composed transitions, terminal verifier path, exact proof/statement artifacts and target receipts; literal-proof success alone shall remain a prerequisite result.
- **SRPOC-003:** When generating a Fenrir-derived workload, the experiment shall record the source commit, deterministic public seed, schema/adapter version, ordered input manifest and independently frozen expected state.
- **SRPOC-004:** When running a workload, the experiment shall retain its exact transaction count, headers, batches, recursive shape and supported operations, including failures or unsupported shapes, without relabelling a reduced fixture as the full workload.
- **SRPOC-005:** When benchmarking each proof layer, the experiment shall report actual circuit degree k, domain capacity, used and lookup row counts and column counts where the library exposes them (domain capacity is not a used-row count), proof/key bytes, build/toolchain identity and separate parameter/keygen/prove/verify measurements; unavailable measurements shall be explicit.
- **SRPOC-006:** When reporting stagenet performance, the experiment shall separate measured local cryptographic verification from submission, inclusion and settlement-readback latency, and shall identify the actual evidence for any validator verification-time claim.
- **SRPOC-007:** When reporting stagenet success, the experiment shall retain the target/network and anchor identity, public transaction receipts, complete observed effects, proof and statement identities and existing required target/rejection evidence.
- **SRPOC-008:** If execution fails, times out or reaches a retained resource limit, the experiment shall preserve the terminal result and consumed resources, and shall not silently retry, reset counters, increase k or substitute a passing profile.
- **SRPOC-009:** When publishing the feasibility conclusion, the experiment shall state the exact successful workload, sample count, observed k/resource/latency results, failures and remaining generalization gaps; a proof of concept shall not close full Sherlock requirements without their retained evidence.
