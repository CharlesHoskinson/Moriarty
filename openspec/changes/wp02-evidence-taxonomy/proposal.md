# Change: WP02 evidence and taxonomy

## Why

The 72-protocol classification drives Moriarty coverage claims. Current corpus
metrics are reproduced, but the 689 residue obligations and genuine rater study
remain incomplete.

## What Changes

- Reproduce the complete distance, clustering, composition, and residue data.
- Classify all 689 uncovered obligations with explicit rules.
- Define a genuine independent-rater protocol.
- Prevent simulated ratings from becoming agreement evidence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `evidence-taxonomy`: Produce a versioned, reproducible family, facet, and
  formal-behavior dataset for all 72 protocols.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This package controls DeFiFormal evidence, taxonomy tests, and
`wiki/defiformal-taxonomy.md`. It does not change Moriarty Core semantics.
