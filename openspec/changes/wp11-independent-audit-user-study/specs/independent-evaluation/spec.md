# Independent-evaluation specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: independent security audit

The audit SHALL cover Core, compiler, Compact generation, certificates,
verifiers, SDK signing boundary, packages, and extension capabilities.

#### Scenario: the reviewer helped author a component

- WHEN independence is materially impaired
- THEN another qualified reviewer audits that component.

### Requirement: finding closure

Every finding SHALL record severity, evidence, affected artifact, exploit path,
remediation, retest, and residual risk.

#### Scenario: a critical or high finding remains open

- WHEN the terminal decision begins
- THEN the Moriarty language option is ineligible.

### Requirement: matched user comparison

The study SHALL compare Moriarty and audited Compact libraries on matched
financial tasks with preregistered measures and participant roles.

#### Scenario: only author opinions are available

- WHEN no independent participant data exists
- THEN the package reports no measured usability advantage.

### Requirement: genuine pilot demand

At least two external pilot teams SHALL supply non-toy specifications,
integration constraints, review needs, and success criteria.

#### Scenario: a demo is invented by the Moriarty team

- WHEN no external user owns its requirements
- THEN it does not count as pilot demand.

### Requirement: fallback decision

The package SHALL recommend audited libraries or stop when Moriarty lacks a
measured assurance, review, usability, or demand advantage.

#### Scenario: outcomes are statistically or operationally inconclusive

- WHEN the preregistered threshold is not met
- THEN inconclusive does not count as language approval.
