# Sherlock co-processor animation, version 2 notes

## Summary

Working notes for version 2 of the Sherlock animation, started at the owner's request. Version 2 starts when the k = 21 recalibration, the elastic chip round and the zero-knowledge design are merged into the architecture. It uses the terminology of [[sherlock-terminology]] and depicts the design of [[k21-recalibration-brief]]. The running record is [[AnimationV2]].

## Key Claims

- Version 2 frames the eight steps as one delegation from Midnight to Sherlock and its return, and opens on the one-line definition of the co-processor.
- New story points: universal (two programs, one contract, the same keys), elastic (one run on a laptop, this host and a cluster, the same claim), zero knowledge (what the chain and each helper host see).
- Design changes to depict: node and decider at k = 21; row-core leaves as a frozen family of leaf heights (k 16, 18, 20); `b` parallel block chains plus merges; our own Sherlock prover; the GPU backend (quotient and NTT 57 to 71 percent of a node proof, measured); the zero-knowledge design; the six optimizations under review; Compact on Sherlock; ledger 9.
- Kept from version 1: the Off-chain, Crossing and On-chain location chip (plus "Off-chain: helper host"), the "what lives where" beat, addressable deterministic moments, Material 3 dark, Quint trace checks, the council step, Fable 5.2 as creative director.
- Fixed from version 1: counts that differ from the design, the carrier overlay, per-scene draw budgets, phone layouts in the first build, the on-chain green close to the outcome teal.
