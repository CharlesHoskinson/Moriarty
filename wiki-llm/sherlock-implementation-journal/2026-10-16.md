## What changed our implementation approach

**Observation: synthetic formatting was insufficient.** Actual K output exposed formatting and wrapper differences that the earlier synthetic decoder did not support. **Decision:** test the decoder against retained actual captures and keep the compile-product-to-engine-input binding visible. **Reason:** an adapter can agree with its own invented examples while failing to decode the real engine. The [correctness loop](../../docs/SHERLOCK-CORRECTNESS-LOOP.md) remains the governing cross-layer procedure.

**Observation: a final value loses important behavior.** Recursive list/tree traversal, caller-environment restoration and mutual recursion need the intermediate control, environment, frame order, depth, fuel, output and source-rule transitions. **Decision:** freeze independent complete-state expectations before producer capture, then compare every ordinary fuel prefix and the original fixture run separately. The new full-fixture corpus specifies eight original fixtures and 569 prefixes; these are specified expectations, not completed execution results. They are separate from the existing eight-program, 216-prefix corpus.

**Observation: a reviewed capture caller can still contain a manifest integration defect.** The first full-fixture attempt stopped in preflight because it looked for fixture pins in the inspected-source list, while those pins live in a separate fixture list. No compiler or interpreter child ran; all source pins remained unchanged. **Decision:** retain the failed attempt and test the corrected caller against the actual manifest shape before another subject run. A capture envelope check also remains distinct from semantic comparison.

**Observation: source review and executable closure answer different questions.** B-core preparation can be useful before the full machine is installed. **Decision:** keep the provisional types/helpers separate from admission, row selection, invariant checking and the complete consumer. Do not use preparation or a narrow compile to close B-core, general ADTs, financial semantics, circuit or target requirements.

**Observation: Core/2 continuations preserve a boundary snapshot, not the complete earlier effect history.** **Implementation direction:** compare each segment's ordered effects together with its pre/post snapshots, and retain the actual continuation and resume inputs between invocations. The next demonstration is to connect independently specified invoice and grant examples to the existing production Core/2 consumer; actual end-to-end comparison remains unrun at this entry's freeze. See the [implementation plan's financial work](../../docs/SHERLOCK-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md).
