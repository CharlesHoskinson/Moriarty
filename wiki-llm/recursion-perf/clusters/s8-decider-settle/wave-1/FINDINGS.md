# s8-decider-settle — wave 1 findings

## Verdict

**ESTIMATE — No source supports a 10× single-batch, Consumed→Finalized improvement yet.** The strongest near-term opportunities are removing repeated setup from an authenticated long-lived proving service and maintaining wallet synchronization. The structural target is a smaller decider, but it must reduce verifier/MSM work as well as pairing work. A k19 decider could approximately halve domain-dependent work; k18 plus broad GPU acceleration is an aggressive, conditional route toward 10× decider latency, not evidence of 10× settlement or whole-chain latency. Arithmetic and stopping criteria follow below.

**SOURCE — Two baseline labels conceal different workloads.** A4's 324.739 s is the successful **whole check invocation**, not a separately recorded `prove()` timer. B4's 125.375/153.118 s stock verification invokes a harness that reconstructs the leaf, parent and decider keys. Recl9 separately reports 586.4 s proving, 46.9 s PK setup and 634.8 s total; its ledger check is 0.021 s. These figures cannot be added into one measured pipeline. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/recorded-runs.md`, `local-terminal-test.md`, `local-rollup-cli.md`, `local-b4-check.md`, `recl9-report.md` (all subsequent abbreviated raw filenames are under `raw/s8-decider-settle/`).

**SOURCE — The 435 s wallet registration is predominantly sync, not an established proof benchmark.** Its log timestamps give 374.910 s sync, 16.071 s building-through-submission, and 43.241 s submitted-to-DUST-visible. There is no proof request count or proof timer. The 43.241 s interval includes observation/polling and does not establish consensus finality latency. Source: `raw/s8-decider-settle/wallet-registration-timeline.md`.

**ESTIMATE — Removing only that recorded sync interval gives an optimistic counterfactual of 435 − 374.910 = 60.090 s, or 7.24× registration workflow speedup.** It neither predicts a fresh run nor accelerates cryptography. Even removing all 16.071 s of build-through-submit would give only 435 / (435 − 16.071) = 1.038× for that cold workflow.

## What is new relative to Loki

**SOURCE — The requested `loki/10x/loki-10-integration-verification.md` was absent; the earlier study was read at `loki/nodes/loki-10-integration-verification.md` and captured in `raw/s8-decider-settle/prior-loki10.md`.** It already specifies arithmetic backend boundaries, independent CPU verification, unchanged transcript/SRS, and an illustrative 2.98× multi-kernel Amdahl model. This wave does not claim GPU offload, residue checks, or fixed-G2 precomputation as new inventions.

**SOURCE — The current local implementation already has all of the following:** residue checking integrated with the Miller loop; constant lines for `[tau]₂` and `−[1]₂`; flat Fp12 arithmetic; normalization reuse (`flat=true`, `normalize_once=true`); checked A/B subgroup assignments tied to accumulator encodings; and unit side scalars. The verified inner proof uses **Poseidon**. **Blake2b is the outer native PLONK transcript**, not an in-circuit Blake2b hash to replace. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/local-pairing.md`, `local-tower.md`, `local-flat.md`, `local-decider.md`, `local-inner.md`, `recl9-cli.md`.

**SOURCE — A finer distinction than “k20 is table-bound” matters.** Recl9 reports D advice/table rows 841,682/1,032,209 and N rows 958,095/1,032,465. However, pinned stdlib sets range width to `k−1`, and its pow2range chip loads only used tags. Its automatic sizing already searches k. Therefore the reported million-row table is a result at k20, not an invariant floor at every k; a smaller-k circuit must be resynthesized because narrower chunks also change advice work. Both D constructors already use the maximum locally allowed four range columns. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/recl9-report.md`, `stdlib-2.3.5-source.md`, `pow2range-7.2.4.md`, `local-parent.md`, `recl9-cli.md`.

**ESTIMATE — At an illustrative seven unusable rows, k19 permits 524,281 rows and k18 262,137.** Relative to 841,682, the required advice reductions are `(841682−524281)/841682 = 37.71%` and `(841682−262137)/841682 = 68.86%`; actual unusable rows and new table sizes must come from synthesis. Reducing the pairing by 10–20% is not enough by itself.

**SOURCE — A historical, different stand-in decider reports 832,015 rows, with a pairing/subgroup contribution quoted as 316,833 rows.** It attributes the remaining verifier/fold/63-base collapse work to subtraction, not a new profile of recl9. Source: `raw/s8-decider-settle/standin-decider-report.md`.

**ESTIMATE — In that stand-in only, removing half the pairing gives 832015 − 316833/2 = 673598.5 rows, still above k19; even a zero-cost pairing leaves 515182 rows before any increase from smaller range widths.** This makes verifier and MSM specialization at least as important as a smaller residue finish. Do not transplant the 316,833 count into Sherlock or recl9 as a measurement.

## Ledger-9 acceptance boundary

**SOURCE — Pinned ledger-9.1.0.0-rc.6 uses `blake2b_simd::State`, its embedded official `bls_midnight_2p14` verifier parameters and the stdlib verifier.** Recl9's k20 proof was accepted through that path. The verifier's 2^14 parameter file is not a universal k14 limit on circuit size; the source describes its degree limit in relation to public inputs. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/ledger9-rc6-proofs.md`, `recl9-report.md`.

**SOURCE — `MidnightVK::read` reconstructs the constraint system from serialized architecture and k.** `nr_pow2range_cols` is an architecture field; the local D relation validates 1–4 and currently selects 4. Arithmetic-only backend changes can preserve the same VK. Altering constraints, tables, public encoding, parent VK, or circuit layout changes the D key; the A4 test requires equality with the installed key. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/stdlib-2.3.5-source.md`, `local-decider.md`, `local-parent.md`, `local-terminal-test.md`.

**OPEN QUESTION — Treat two acceptance levels separately:** (a) unchanged installed key, feasible for backend/caching/scheduling changes; (b) a newly registered, stock-deserializable VK, potentially feasible for relation rewrites. Stock native gates can express different arithmetic without replacing ledger PLONK/KZG, but arbitrary new custom gates or lookup protocols cannot be assumed to survive ledger deserialization. No changed-key proposal is ready for the existing deployment merely because its proof verifies with a private verifier.

**SOURCE — Recl9's report records subgroup/shift hardening and shows wrong child statements/proof swaps can pass the prepare-only node but fail at D.** The local current D also binds the communication commitment using a private opening; the old recl9 report's default call inputs were a local-verifier fixture, not a chain submission. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/recl9-report.md`, `local-decider.md`, `local-terminal-test.md`.

**OPEN QUESTION — Preserve all these obligations during optimization:** canonical accumulator encoding, full-width Fiat–Shamir challenges and ordering, identity/subgroup handling, hardened shift assignment, both side scalars, pinned parent/VK/SRS, call binding and communication opening, ordered state transition/height/batch commitment, and finality/readback. Keep hints constrained; never log private witnesses or RNG/blinding material.

## DUST and state-machine accounting

**SOURCE — Current official guidance distinguishes initial registration from later fee-bearing spends, describes runtime fee estimation and declared-fee margins, and uses SPECK as the atomic unit.** A first registration may need no proof; DUST spends need proving. This is current documentation, not proof that the archived rc.8 transaction took that exact route. Sources: `raw/s8-decider-settle/manage-dust.md`, `proof-server.md`, `wallet-registration-timeline.md`.

**SOURCE — Fee timing matters even when proving is fast:** the guide distinguishes required fee from the larger declared spend and describes UTXOs locked by pending transactions. Source: `raw/s8-decider-settle/manage-dust.md`.

**OPEN QUESTION — A single sponsor/fee coin can serialize a pipeline even with idle GPU capacity.** Measure fee-input contention and transaction expiry/grace parameters before pipelining D jobs; do not shorten margins without accounting for retries and failed included transactions.

**OPEN QUESTION — Count actual DUST-spend proofs for the exact ledger-9 call.** Record declared and charged fee, margin, number of consumed fee coins, runtime parameters and logical transactions per settled batch. Resolve the registration log's “DUST” label against the raw API unit before converting amounts. Sponsorship changes who proves/pays; it does not itself eliminate the fee or all proving.

**SOURCE — The official sponsorship flow has the user prove and bind the call before the sponsor adds and proves its DUST fee payment.** This creates a dependency; overlapping those phases cannot be assumed. Source: `raw/s8-decider-settle/dust-sponsorship.md`.

**ESTIMATE — Model fee per logical transaction as `F(n)/n`, not a fixed tariff.** If a bounded batch costs `F(n)=F0+n×f`, then 16-way amortization changes a single-item cost `F0+f` to `F0/16+f`; improvement `(F0+f)/(F0/16+f)` lies between 1× and 16× for nonnegative F0,f. Proof size 6,432 B alone is insufficient to determine F(n). Fee conditions and transaction shape must be held fixed for comparison.

**OPEN QUESTION — Instrument the four owned states without collapsing them:**

| Transition | Required timing and evidence |
|---|---|
| **OPEN QUESTION:** entry to Consumed | Timestamp when the exact parent proof/public statement and its authenticated provenance are available. Record earlier wallet-sync startup separately. |
| **OPEN QUESTION:** Consumed→Decided | Split SRS/VK/PK load, synthesis/witness, MSM, NTT, quotient, transcript and independent final verification. “Prepared accumulator” is not Decided. |
| **OPEN QUESTION:** Decided→Submitted | Split contract-call preparation, fee balancing, proof-server request count/time, signing and submission acknowledgment. Preserve binding if the transaction is rebuilt. |
| **OPEN QUESTION:** Submitted→Finalized | Establish network finality and independent effects readback; report polling delay separately. Indexer DUST visibility alone is not this state. |

## Can these ideas reach 10×?

**ESTIMATE — A transparent aggressive D-only scenario is** `R = .03 + .95×.225/5 + .02 = .09275`: 3% serial baseline work, 95% domain work reduced from k20 to k18 and accelerated 5×, plus 2% added transfer overhead. This gives 10.78×, 30.14 s versus 325 s and 54.39 s versus 586.4 s. It requires the unmeasured 68.86% row reduction, small residual work, unchanged column/quotient factors and adequate GPU occupancy. It is a target to falsify, not an expected delivered result.

**ESTIMATE — With a 15% floor, the same shrink and 5× kernels plus 2% overhead gives** `.15+.85×.225/5+.02=.20825`, only 4.80×. No multiplication of a published gnark/cuZK speedup into this model is justified; the retrieved gnark code provides no benchmark, and the pairing paper's abstract says its result is instantiated for BN254. Source for workload distinction: `raw/s8-decider-settle/eprint-2024-640.md`, `gnark-sw-bls12381-pairing.md`, `prior-loki10.md`.

**ESTIMATE — For this stage, with remaining settlement time W+F, the correct speedup is** `(T_D+W+F)/(T_D×R+W'+F')`. If R=.1 and W+F is unchanged and nonzero, total speedup is strictly below 10×. None of the supplied records provides a single matched Consumed→Finalized timeline.

**ESTIMATE — s8 alone cannot make the recorded recl9 whole chain 10×.** Its serial prove-time sum is `(34.8+35.3+44.2+49.4)+867.3+586.4 = 1617.4 s`. Even deleting D entirely leaves 1031.0 s, a ceiling of 1.569× before setup/settlement. These are recorded component sums, not a measured concurrent run. Leaf/node acceleration must come from other stages. Source: `raw/s8-decider-settle/recl9-report.md`.

## What would change the verdict

- **OPEN QUESTION — A same-artifact phase trace could show that setup/sync or a removable host operation dominates.** That would promote the service/critical-path fixes above circuit changes. It must separate A4 invocation overhead from proof creation and exclude QA-only B4 reconstruction.
- **OPEN QUESTION — A stock-deserializable k19/k18 D, with explicit row/table/column counts and all negative cases rejecting, would promote structural work.** A width change that only moves cost into more columns or larger quotient domains does not qualify.
- **OPEN QUESTION — A weighted one-step arithmetic census could reject paired-line fusion immediately.** A demonstrably cheaper stock-gate sum-of-products primitive would instead justify a full prototype.
- **OPEN QUESTION — Five isolated runs of the same pinned relation, parent artifact, SRS and independent ledger verifier could establish the residual floor and GPU benefit.** Separate same-key candidates from newly registered-key candidates.
- **OPEN QUESTION — Exact rc.8 proof-request traces, measured DUST per settled batch, and a genuine Finalized/readback timeline could establish the settlement floor.** Until then neither 435 s nor 43.241 s is a wallet proof or consensus-finality measurement.
