# s5-quotient-ntt — wave 1 findings

**ESTIMATE — Verdict:** Prioritize a resident, bounded-memory quotient evaluator, with **base-domain coset tiles** when all extended columns do not fit. Do not budget a further 10× over an already accelerated quotient from faster NTT alone. For an unaccelerated quotient, 8–30× is a useful **experimental target**, not a measured forecast; at a 60% stage fraction that gives only `1/(0.4+0.6/8)=2.11×` to `1/(0.4+0.6/30)=2.38×` overall. A 10× end-to-end result needs cooperation from other stages or a measured s5 fraction above 90%.

**SOURCE — Scope/provenance:** The owned transition is LookupsArgued → Quotiented for Sherlock A102/A80 → P → D and Fenrir leaves → N → D; required protocol and host are recorded in [task-context](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/task-context.md). No GPU/prover/ledger benchmark ran in this wave. A small exact-field algebra check did run. Raw paths below are relative to repository root; hyperlinks resolve to them.

**SOURCE — Earlier-study delta:** The requested `../../wiki-llm/loki/10x/loki-03-gpu-ntt.md` was absent; the matching study was found at `../loki/nodes/loki-03-gpu-ntt.md` and captured as [earlier-loki-03](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/earlier-loki-03.md). It already proposed whole-quotient residency, fixed-column caching, expression specialization, periodic vanishing inverses and pruned FFTs. This wave adds a coset-tile derivation/check, the exact lookup degree floor, public Pipelonk artifact inspection including its timing boundary and stub, and quantitative limits on the recorded benchmarks. The earlier Pipelonk conference capture was read; a fresh [conference capture](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-conference.md) preserves its abstract.

## Baselines: what is and is not transferable

**SOURCE — cuSnark:** [cusnark.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/cusnark.md) reports `Eval_h = 1115.43 → 36.19 s` for **2^20 rows and 1,135 columns**, on **EPYC 7702, 64 cores, four RTX 3090 24 GB GPUs**. Total reported proof time is `1762.75 → 463.13 s`, or 3.81× with all listed accelerations. The separate 2^25-row, five-column aggregation workload has `Eval_h = 67.40 → 6.30 s`, or 10.70×. The page does not establish the curve and exact GPU utilization of each timed stage. Matching k20 does not match a recursive circuit's column graph.

**ESTIMATE — Amdahl audit:** Holding the first workload's non-quotient CPU work fixed leaves `1762.75−1115.43=647.32 s`. Replacing only its quotient gives `1762.75/(647.32+36.19)=2.579×`; an infinitely fast quotient caps it at `1762.75/647.32=2.723×`. Improving the reported **GPU** quotient another 10× only gives `463.13/(463.13−36.19+3.619)=1.0756×` total. At stage speedup 30.82×, 10× total requires `f≥0.9/(1−1/30.82)=93.018%`, excluding added overhead. These are calculations on the source workload, not Sherlock measurements.

**SOURCE — Useful independent cross-check:** [cusnark-quotient.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/cusnark-quotient.md) isolates polynomial-evaluation acceleration: `1069.09 → 42.13 s` and total `1690.05 → 663.66 s` for the wide proof. It describes an **opcode interpreter partitioned into kernel launches**, because giant generated kernels can take hours to compile and the live polynomial graph can exceed device memory. This supports a bounded interpreter/specialization hybrid, not an assumption that maximal fusion always wins.

**SOURCE — 1.13 ms:** [snarkify-zprize.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/snarkify-zprize.md) lists BLS12-381 NTT at 2^22 as 1.13 ms and inverse as 1.23 ms; it lists coset NTT only at 2^25 (14.45 ms). Its proof benchmark uses batches of four on an A6000 Ada and removes unused lookups. [snarkify-microbench.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/snarkify-microbench.md) shows managed arrays, initialization before measurement, an untimed warm-up, CUDA events, and 20 repetitions. The table does not separately establish the microbenchmark GPU. The code's `polynomial-divide-22` is a distinct operation, not evidence of a complete PLONK constraint quotient.

**ESTIMATE — Timing-envelope sanity:** Assuming 64 forward transforms and one inverse, the table implies only `64×1.13+1.23=73.55 ms` of those warm kernels; a further 2× saves 36.775 ms. Normalizing that saving to 36.19 s would give `36.19/(36.19−0.036775)=1.00102×`. The 64-column count is hypothetical and these two source benchmarks cannot be combined as an actual profile. This calculation explains why kernel-only milliseconds cannot price the whole stage.

## Coset tiles: the concrete extension to the earlier design

**SOURCE — Algebra hooks:** [0.8.3 domain](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/midnight-083-domain.md) sets `n=2^k`, `N=2^extended_k`, `b=N/n`, uses order-three `ZETA`, and caches b vanishing inverses. [0.8.3 evaluation](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/midnight-083-evaluation.md) accesses rotations as `(i+r*b) mod N`. Its numerator includes gates, permutation, lookups and trash. These are pinned 0.8.3 sources, distinct from mutable `main` and from an unverified Fenrir 0.8.2 build identity.

**ESTIMATE — Exact derivation, not a speed measurement:** Write `i=t+b*j`, `0≤t<b`, `0≤j<n`, `ω_n=ω_N^b`, and `s_t=ζω_N^t`. Then

```
A(ζω_N^(t+b*j)) = Σ[l=0..n−1] a_l s_t^l ω_n^(j*l).
(i+r*b) mod N = t + b*((j+r) mod n).
(ζω_N^(t+b*j))^n − 1 = s_t^n − 1.
```

**ESTIMATE — Consequence:** Each tile is a length-n NTT of rescaled coefficients; rotated reads are local wrapped reads, and vanishing division uses one constant per tile. Evaluate the complete ordered constraint accumulator for a tile, scatter `h[i]` into one N-element output, release tile intermediates, then perform the original length-N inverse NTT and inverse ζ scaling. Preserve the existing quotient truncation, splitting, blinding and commitments. This is a scheduling decomposition of the **same N points**, not a reduced evaluation domain. It assumes required argument polynomials already exist at LookupsArgued; it does not parallelize their earlier product construction.

**ESTIMATE — Memory:** With 64 live columns, n=2^20 and 32-byte scalars, full evaluations occupy `64×n×b×32 = 8 GiB (b=4)` or `16 GiB (b=8)`; one tile occupies 2 GiB. Retaining all coefficient inputs adds another 2 GiB; the final output costs 0.125/0.25 GiB, plus real twiddle/workspace/MSM reserves. With **128** live columns at b=8, full evaluations alone are 32 GiB; coefficients+one tile+output instead total `4+4+0.25=8.25 GiB` before reserves. Counts are hypothetical. At 1,135 columns even base coefficients alone are `1135/32=35.47 GiB`: coset tiling alone would still require column liveness or streaming on a 32 GB device.

**ESTIMATE — Arithmetic limit:** Replacing N-point extension by b transforms of n points changes nominal FFT butterfly counts by `log2(N)/log2(n)=22/20=1.10` or `23/20=1.15`, **before** b sets of coefficient scaling, scheduling and final scatter. These are not runtime speedups. Unlike ζ alone, `s_t` generally has no period three; tiles need powers of each shift. The pruned-DIF model in row 6 uses `(N/2)log2(n)+(b−1)n` multiplies versus `(N/2)log2(N)`, so much of the nominal saving may already exist in a pruned implementation.

**SOURCE — Check performed:** [coset-tiles-check.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/coset-tiles-check.md) records 9 small BLS12-381 scalar-field cases and 2,744 rotated-value comparisons, exact extended-evaluation and known-quotient reconstruction equality, and wrong-stride negative controls. Reproducer: [check_coset_tiles.py](check_coset_tiles.py). This establishes the tested identities only; it does not validate actual Midnight roots, CUDA arithmetic, a real circuit or ledger acceptance.

## Degree and Pipelonk: two decisions now better constrained

**SOURCE — Degree threshold:** The [pinned constructor](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/midnight-083-domain.md) chooses `N=next_power_of_two(n*(d−1))`; [constraint degree](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/midnight-083-circuit.md) is the maximum of permutation, lookup, trash, gates and configured minimum. [Lookup degree](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/midnight-083-lookup.md) is `max(4, 2+input_degree+table_degree)`. Therefore with this lookup argument present d≥4. **ESTIMATE:** d=4 or 5 both give b=4; d=6..9 gives b=8. Rewriting d=6→5 can help; d=5→4 cannot shrink N. If extra rows raise k20→k21, halving b leaves N unchanged and can erase the benefit. Removing lookup constraints or adopting main's LogUp is not an execution-only optimization.

**SOURCE — Pipelonk beyond the abstract:** The [Zenodo artifact](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-zenodo-artifact.md), published 2025-12-23, points to an `ae` branch; the repository now redirects to `zlyber/pipelonk`. [Current README](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-readme.md) specifies NTT/LDE/operator reproduction, H100/RTX 4090, CUDA 12.8 and platform-tuned host permutation threads. It says its blst CPU operator baseline is about 25–55% faster than the paper's arkworks baseline. [Exact ae operator source](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-miss-ops-exact.md) uses BLS12-381 Fr. The fetched [ae quotient file](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-github-zksnark_compute_quotient.cu.md) is an empty namespace. This is evidence for an operator toolkit, not demonstrated reproduction of a complete Midnight prover. The paper's 10.7× average/19.4× maximum remains an abstract-level result with unresolved full-prover workload/baseline details.

**SOURCE — Current LDE source is a useful adversarial benchmark:** [pipelonk-lde.md](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-lde.md), `our_lde`, materializes host input and full extended stage/output buffers, performs CPU bit reversal/gather/scatter and segmented GPU passes. It constructs buffers, index maps and workspaces **before** CUDA-event timing. `run_check` compares GPU final packing with CPU scattering of the same algorithm; it is not an independent reference-transform oracle. Multiple host thread pools and tuned counts up to 32 appear in the source. [CMake](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/pipelonk-lde-cmake.md) contains `-O0 -g`, uses FindCUDA and sets native architecture; inspect actual compilation commands on CUDA 13 rather than assuming a release configuration. `main` source is not the archived `ae` artifact.

**ESTIMATE — Decision:** Benchmark this host-staged implementation against true residency at the actual k20 extended sizes. Prefer segmentation only where it removes memory pressure or hides measured residual transfers. It is not automatically the best choice for a fitting 32 GB workload. Avoid applying its abstract speedup to the four RTX 3090 cuSnark result or multiplying the two.

**SOURCE — Other leads:** [ZKProphet](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/zkprophet.md) studies Groth16-compatible implementations and identifies NTT/transfer and integer-arithmetic limits, not a Sherlock quotient profile. [ICICLE](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/icicle-ntt.md) explicitly exposes batch, device, coset and ordering settings. [sppark](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/sppark-ntt.md) exposes `Base_dev_ptr`, whereas its high-level `LDE_aux` copies host/device and synchronizes. [Consumer-GPU author results](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/consumer-ntt.md) report negative four-step, on-the-fly twiddle and Tensor Core experiments on RTX 3060 Laptop; they are useful failure hypotheses, not independently reproduced Blackwell evidence. [cuPQC](../../../raw/s5-quotient-ntt/cupqc.md) excludes the required scalar modulus.

## What would change the verdict

- **OPEN QUESTION:** A completed, pinned P/D/N profile showing s5≥93.02% of critical-path latency would make a 30.82× stage target compatible with 10× total. Leaf throughput cannot substitute for this measurement.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** Measured spill-free resident evaluation with NTT dominating ≥80% would move backend/kernel tuning above coset tiling; a full live set exceeding the GPU budget would strengthen coset tiling/liveness scheduling.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** Actual d≥6 caused by a removable degree-setting gate, with unchanged k and acceptable key migration, would promote degree reduction. d≤5 with lookups would demote it conclusively for domain savings.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** Independent differential success for every numerator family, quotient coefficient, transcript boundary and recursive/ledger verifier is needed before any acceleration is accepted. Keep PLONK/KZG, BLS12-381, the official SRS and terminal Blake2b unchanged; research has not exercised stagenet.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** A complete Pipelonk prover artifact or full-paper workload details could alter scheduling priority. CUDA 13/SM120 build failures, register spills, extra shift scaling or host oversubscription could reverse a promising kernel result.
