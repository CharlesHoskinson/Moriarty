#### Paper 2023/033

### [Fast amortized KZG proofs](/2023/033.pdf)

Dankrad Feist, Ethereum Foundation

Dmitry Khovratovich, Ethereum Foundation

##### Abstract

In this note we explain how to compute $n$ KZG proofs for a polynomial of degree $d$ in time superlinear of $(n+d)$. Our technique is used in lookup arguments and vector commitment schemes.

**Note:** Also known as "Fast amortized Kate proofs".
A bug in section 2.2 is fixed.
