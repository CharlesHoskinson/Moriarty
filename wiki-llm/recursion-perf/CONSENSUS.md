# Speedup consensus tracker

A speedup is **agreed** only when (a) at least two independent research sources rank it top-3 with consistent bounded-gain arithmetic, and (b) a cold cross-review by Muse Wonder Full and agy Gemini 4 Argon finds no refuting evidence. Agreed items go to the wiki, then an OpenSpec change in `openspec/changes/`, then a quint-llm-kit design check, then Sherlock roadmap Phase 5, then factory implementation.

**Cross-stage reading (revised after cross-review round 1):** no speedup row is agreed. Every bound rests on an assumed stage share, and the shares assumed by C3, C4, C6, C7 and C9 add up to 216–241% of one proof, so those bounds cannot be combined (Argon, round 1). Several rows also measure different things: C5 is throughput, C1 is latency against a whole A3 check interval, C8 is cold keygen, and C10's wallet figure is a registration interval. The only agreed step is C0, the measured phase profile; it will fix the shares that every other row assumes.
