Three nested metrics must remain distinct:

1. `create_proof_ms`: external monotonic wall around complete proof creation, including synthesis, transcript finalization, conversions/transfers and completion. Host PK/SRS preparation is outside this narrow metric and its cache state is independently labelled. Do not describe it as operational proving.
2. `operational_prove_ms` and `operational_prove_verify_ms`: start at requests/witness preparation with authenticated host SRS/VK artifacts available; preserve actual per-call layout/model guards, PK construction, proof creation, required production checks and witness/instance reconstruction. External one-time SRS acquisition/generation, initial input read/decode, initial VK preparation and verifier initialization before a request are timed separately. ALL recurring parameter reload/read/decode, SRS loading, child-key/VK reconstruction, layout/model guards, and PK construction invoked by the current operational APIs remain INSIDE operational and chain timers in BOTH cold and warm modes, with separate attribution.
