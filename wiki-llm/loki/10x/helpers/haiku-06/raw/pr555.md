Contributor

Implements the fflonk PCS from a given generic underlying PCS.

This PR draws on [@irakoton](https://github.com/irakoton)'s fflonk work ([#487](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-zk/pull/487), [#511](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-zk/pull/511)), adopting his protocol choices (evaluation point `s^T_MAX`, opening at the roots of `x`, over-opening missing evaluations) in an implementation over a generic inner PCS.

This PR does not implement fflonk in-circuit yet: the verifier gadget only supports KZG for now.

### Benchmarks: fflonk vs KZG

`Fflonk<KZG, L>` for `t = 2^L ∈ {2, 4, 8, 16}` against plain KZG (criterion medians, 16-core machine).

**zswap\_output (K = 14)**

|  | KZG | t=2 | t=4 | t=8 | t=16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| prove (ms) | 503 | 802 (1.59×) | 893 (1.78×) | 1014 (2.02×) | 1579 (3.14×) |
| verify (ms) | 2.67 | 3.38 (1.27×) | 3.76 (1.41×) | 6.25 (2.34×) | 17.2 (6.42×) |
| proof (bytes) | 3808 | 3440 | **3264** | 3296 | 3424 |
| vk (bytes) | 2258 | 1586 | 1250 | 1058 | **962** |

**Synthetic circuit (`benches/plonk.rs` circuit)**

| prove (ms) | KZG | t=2 | t=4 | t=8 | t=16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| k=12 | 56.9 | 72.0 (1.27×) | 92.1 (1.62×) | 127.5 (2.24×) | 126.6 (2.23×) |
| k=14 | 207 | 245 (1.18×) | 311 (1.50×) | 452 (2.18×) | 454 (2.20×) |
| k=16 | 711 | 921 (1.29×) | 1209 (1.70×) | 1548 (2.18×) | 1549 (2.18×) |
| k=18 | 2774 | 3208 (1.16×) | 4763 (1.72×) | 5895 (2.13×) | 5919 (2.13×) |

Verification takes 1.40–1.56 ms for every scheme and k (1.02–1.11× KZG). Proof / vk bytes: 960/338 (KZG), 944/194, 896/98, 928/50, 928/50.

**Takeaways**

* fflonk shrinks the proof (best at t=4, −14% on zswap) and the vk (up to −57%), but slows down the native prover and verifier.
* Prover overhead comes from committing advice columns (×5 already at t=2, likely the Lagrange-to-coefficient conversion in `compute_g`) and from multi-open (doubling with t).
* The verifier slows down because the inner KZG sees t times more opening points. In-circuit verification, fflonk's main target, is not measured here.

`proofs: fflonk over an inner PCS`

 …

`b2cdd5a`

```
`Fflonk<PCS, T_MAX>` combines the polynomials of a `commit_many` call,
in chunks of up to `T_MAX`, into single polynomials committed to by
`PCS`. A chunk is opened at `x` by opening its combination at the
roots of `x` through `PCS`, writing to the proof the evaluations at
`x` that were not queried.

Chunks are labeled with the new `PolynomialLabel::Collection`, and the
verifier recovers them through the new `commitment_labels` method of
`PolynomialCommitmentScheme`.

The in-circuit verifier does not support fflonk yet.

Signed-off-by: ambrona <miguel.ambrona@iohk.io>
```
