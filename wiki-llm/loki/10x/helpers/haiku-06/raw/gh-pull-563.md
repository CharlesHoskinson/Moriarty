Changes
-------

* **fflonk commits from Lagrange values**: zero rows stay free in the MSM; `ParamsKZG` keeps a Lagrange basis per power-of-two size.
* **KZG multi-open divides by `∏ (X − point)` at once** (`divide_by_roots`): one pass, `X^t − x` for fflonk's roots.
* **KZG verifier evaluates interpolants barycentrically** (`eval_interpolant`): quadratic instead of cubic.
* **fflonk caches the roots of its opening points**: once per point and chunk size, evaluated without rayon.

Benchmarks: `zswap_output` (2^14 rows)
--------------------------------------

Time relative to plain KZG in the same run (criterion: 20 proofs, 100 verifications):

|  | t = 1 | t = 2 | t = 4 | t = 8 | t = 16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prove, [#555](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-zk/pull/555) | 1.48× | 1.50× | 1.50× | 1.80× | 2.51× |
| Prove, this PR | 1.03× | 1.15× | **1.16×** | 1.35× | 2.18× |
| Verify, [#555](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-zk/pull/555) | 1.14× | 1.23× | 1.04× | 1.97× | 4.83× |
| Verify, this PR | 1.06× | 0.85× | **0.67×** | 0.70× | 0.79× |

Proof and vk sizes are unchanged from [#555](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-zk/pull/555) (t = 4: proof −14%, vk −45%).

The remaining prover gap is mostly the multi-open, which grows with `t`.

Open question
-------------

The smaller Lagrange bases cost a group FFT on every params load, plain KZG included. Options: compute them lazily, or serialize them. To be discussed with the team.
