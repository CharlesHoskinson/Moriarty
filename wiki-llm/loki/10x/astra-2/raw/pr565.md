Changes
-------

* **KZG multi-open divides by `∏ (X − point)` at once**, skipping zero coefficients (`X^t − x` for fflonk's roots).
* **KZG verifier evaluates interpolants barycentrically**: quadratic instead of cubic.
* **fflonk caches the roots of its opening points**, once per point and chunk size.
* **`InterleavedCommit`**, a `PolynomialCommitmentScheme` that commits to `Σ_i X^i f_i(X^t)`. KZG implements it by committing every `f_i` against the bases of its residue `i` mod `t`. These are strided monomials in coefficient form and their Lagrange (or delta) transforms otherwise, cached lazily in `ParamsKZG`. Zero values stay free in the MSM, and nothing is converted to coefficient form.
* **fflonk commits through `InterleavedCommit`** instead of building `g`.
* **The prover commits phase 1 in `LagrangeDelta` and phase 2 in `LagrangeDoubleDelta`**, where the multiplicities' and accumulators' constant runs vanish.

`proofs: KZG implements InterleavedCommit from per-residue bases`

 …

`0abb944`

```
`InterleavedCommit` extends `PolynomialCommitmentScheme` with
`commit_interleaved`, committing to `g(X) = Σ_i X^i f_i(X^t)` for every
chunk `f_0, ..., f_{k-1}` of polynomials, with `t` the next power of two
of `k`.

KZG commits to every `f_i` of a chunk on the bases of its residue `i`
modulo `t`: the strided monomials `[s^(i + t·j)]` in coefficient form,
and the Lagrange basis built from them, `[s^i L_j(s^t)]`, in Lagrange
form or one of its delta forms. The zeros of the values of `f_i` stay
free in the commitment. `ParamsKZG` computes the bases of a residue on
first use and caches them.

Assisted-by: AI <noreply@ai-assistant>
Signed-off-by: ambrona <miguel.ambrona@iohk.io>
```
