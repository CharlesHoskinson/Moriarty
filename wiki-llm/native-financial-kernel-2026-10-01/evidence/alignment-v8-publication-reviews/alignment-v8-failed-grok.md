### Two native verifiers and finalized binding — source-path evidence

`provider.rs` `ProvingProvider::prove`:

1. Refuses absent or zero finalized ledger binding (`overwrite_binding_input` required, `Fr::from(0)` refused).
2. Enforces one-call budget and skip agreement with prior `check`.
3. Calls actual `ir.prove`.
4. `vk.verify(&pv, …)` with SRS-derived `ParamsVerifier` (`verifier_parameters` full-EOF decode of the k17 SRS bytes).
5. `vk.verify(&transient_crypto::proofs::PARAMS_VERIFIER, …)` with the official embedded verifier (`include_bytes!("../static/bls_midnight_2p14")` in pinned `transient-crypto` `proofs.rs`).
6. Only then `records.push(Record{proof, pis, skips, binding})`.

`main.rs` `prove()` after `unproven.prove` returns:

1. Requires exactly one recorded contract proof.
2. Requires recorded public inputs equal `finalcall.public_inputs(parent.binding_commitment)` and `pis.first() == Some(&rec.binding)`.
3. Writes `proof.raw`, `statement.tagged`, `skips.json`.
4. Signs the intent and `seal`s.
5. Requires public inputs unchanged after sign/seal.
6. Writes `transaction.tagged`.
7. Calls `accept()`, whose first ledger gate is `tx.well_formed(state, WellFormedStrictness::default(), tblock)`.

Default strictness is `verify_native_proofs/verify_contract_proofs/verify_signatures/enforce_balancing/enforce_limits = true`, `proof_verification_mode = Real`.

Because `proof.raw` and `transaction.tagged` exist and the process died inside `accept()` with `OutputsNotSorted`, the executed control flow reached both provider `vk.verify` calls, the post-prove public-input binding check, and the post-sign/seal public-input check. A failed provider verify would have prevented `records.push` and therefore `proof.raw`. A changed statement at sign/seal would have refused before `transaction.tagged`.

This is **same-process, same-VK, source-path** evidence. It is not the independent `verify` CLI (`verify.rs`), which additionally mutates public inputs/proof/call fields, checks suffix-refusing decoders, refuses a stripped ownership signature, and re-runs `accept()`.
