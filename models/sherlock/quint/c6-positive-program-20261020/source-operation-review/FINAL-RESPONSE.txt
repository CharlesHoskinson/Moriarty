No mandatory fix in the six author modules, within the positive domain.

- Header and grammar agree: magic `18918541878711089`, version `1`, program tag `1347571527`/`0x50524F47`, entry at lane 3, then exactly 22 count-prefixed tables in `ProgramSnapshot` field order. Zero tables still emit a count. Rows, repeats, order, raw slices, options, booleans, marker bits, and raw `u128` values are preserved without deduplication or empty-slice normalization.
- Row encoders and decoder widths agree variant by variant. This includes expressions (widths 2/2/4/4/3/4/4/6/7 by tag), patterns (1/2/3/5), plans (8/6/3/2), and constants (3/3/2/4/4/4/6/2). - Table selection is by the fixed public 22-table index only. Neither the encoder, positive decoder, nor observer contains a case-id or fixture selector. The two literal inputs exercise all 22 empty tables and all 22 populated tables, including repeated rows and repeated/duplicated text.
- Declaration encoding matches the Rust order: declaration name, parameter count and names, constructor count, then per constructor its name, field count, and per field its name and one complete type expression.
- Type reading is sound on the positive premise. The `pending` fold starts at 1, decrements per completed token, and adds an `Apply` arity. Each token consumes at least one lane, so iterating over the supplied lane-stream length bounds the fold without adding a production clock, cap, or guard. It is not a malformed-stream validator: out-of-domain tags fall through to last variants in several readers, and that remains outside this positive task.
- Text is handled by byte length, not character count. Empty text consumes only its length lane. The supplied alphabet instances (`""`, `A`, `é` as `[195,169]`, `AB`, `C`, `F`) are consistently represented in the Rust form, byte-wire form, Quint inputs, and projection document.
- Text spans are emitted in encoder/reader call order, with `Names` budget for the names table and `Template` budget for declaration and type-name text. Declaration, parameter, constructor, field, and type-name paths match the observation interface, including the preorder token index `t` for `Named`/`Param`/`Apply` name spans.
- Table, row, and final cursors are coherent. `rows_start = count_lane + 1`; an empty table has `rows_end = rows_start`; row spans are contiguous; the last table's `rows_end` is required to equal `final_cursor = encoded_lanes.length()` in the continuity predicate.
- `c6PObserve` exposes exactly the six required fields: `encoded_lanes`, `decoded_program`, `table_counts`, `table_spans`, `text_spans`, `final_cursor`. Additional roundtrip, count, and exact-consumption checks in `c6PWholeObservationsExact` are conjuncts, not substitutes for whole-observation equality.
- The Quint model does not use `caps`. That is consistent only because cap satisfaction, 64-bit host fit, cumulative name/template byte budgets, type-node budget, and arity limits are external positive premises.
