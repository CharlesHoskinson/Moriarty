# Candidate custody contract

Status: proposed. Scope: authorize local implementation preparation for the fixed atomic loan and swap financial integration milestone. This is not a public submission, native-history, full-language, private-handoff or release admission.

Use distinct role capabilities checked in the circuit by persistentHash of private secret material. Constructor enrollment seals each role commitment and its recoverable payout address. Enrollment is explicitly trusted for this bounded campaign; it does not prove a legal identity or universal wallet-signature compatibility. Never use ownPublicKey, a witness boolean or an unchecked private argument as authorization. Keep role secrets outside tracked files and public evidence, and preserve recoverability. Do not create or replace any wallet identity until its separate local preparation packet describes custody and recovery. Never print seeds or secret capabilities.

Use the existing Preview wallet in the borrower/trader roles. A later admitted identity-preparation task supplies distinct recoverable lender/provider identities. One operator may control all test identities; evidence must say so. This tests role separation and real asset movement, not independent institutional custody.

Role possession authorizes only the fixed action and constrained current state revision under the exact program, network, deployment, token identifiers and units. It must not grant arbitrary recipients, arbitrary minting, arbitrary debt or unbounded asset withdrawal. Constructor bindings and circuit assertions bind the role to the payout address and action-specific constraints. Every state transition consumes currentness and advances the revision. Duplicate execution, wrong revision, repeat initialization, wrong role, arbitrary recipient, wrong asset/unit and out-of-phase action reject. All initialization/mint paths have a one-shot guard; a role secret alone must not bypass it.

Complete financial state/effects must match the accepted fixed loan/swap design. Nominal outstanding loan duties survive the end of this milestone. The loan settlement transfers the exact specified amount and does not claim the residual principal has been discharged. The swap uses the pinned input/fee/output arithmetic, gross movements and reserve constraints. Show pre/post balances, nominal principal/accrual/duties, full transfer recipients/amounts/assets and fees. Evaluate gross debits and cumulative signed limits independently from net balance changes. Do not infer accounting from a successful call alone.

receiveUnshielded constrains receipt to the contract but does not itself name or authenticate the payer. The circuit must not claim borrower debit from mere receipt. Where a milestone claims who funded an input, bind that claim to the actual signed transaction offer and consumed UTXO ownership in the independent receipt verifier. Third-party funding must remain explicitly third-party funding unless the required payer evidence exists. Contract-enforced funding amount/color and receipt-level payer attribution are separate predicates. A payer-attribution failure blocks that financial claim even if the transaction succeeds.

Unshielded balance queries describe entry balances, so do not assume they include preceding intra-call receives or mints. Use explicit, bounded arithmetic for in-call deltas and sufficient entry reserve checks for spending. Wrong secrets, forged witness authorization, repeat initialization, wrong role/action/revision, wrong recipient/color/units, missing funding, insufficient reserves, gross-versus-net errors, third-party payer misclassification and financial rollback are required local rejection controls. Distinguish rollback of financial state from transaction/proof fees.

The competing option is action-specific in-circuit signatures using wallet identities. The inspected evidence has not established a complete compatible signing path. Do not pretend a signature helper name establishes this route. This option remains a separate compatibility investigation; role capabilities in the bounded milestone do not establish final general intent signing or replace mandatory proof-carrying acceptance.

Acceptance criterion: this proposal may authorize only a new bounded local implementation/test packet. That packet must specify exact source, role commitment and payout encoding, public-state disclosure, secret custody/recovery, one-shot initialization, action/revision binding, entry-balance semantics, complete financial comparisons and all controls. Compact proof-enabled public financial tests require their own source/resource/result admission after local checks and independent review. Preview inclusion/finality must come from the actual network, and every actual transaction ID must be reported. No transaction is authorized by this decision artifact alone.

Open work remains full mandatory native history acceptance, general signing/display semantics, private handoff, full financial coverage and release. No host-computed success flag discharges these claims.
