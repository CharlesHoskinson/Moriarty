# Historical UTXO publication scope

This archive preserves the selected public research and validation history captured from the October 12–14 work. Original paths, byte counts, content hashes, compressed objects, exclusions and public-text extraction provenance are recorded in its manifests. Historical failures and rejected reviews remain failures and rejected reviews.

The source417 packet and source383 refer to their original historical Node implementation. Their inclusion does not establish compatibility with the current Rust language, current semantics, Groth16/PCD prototype, or a current target. Newly created Groth16, cata and graph experiment directories, keys, witnesses and provider traces are outside this frozen capture. The archived graph is the original historical graph, not output from the subsequently updated Graphify package.

The main archive and the public-source restoration supplement have separate policy attestations, manifests and reconstruction checks. The restoration supplement recovers exact public Git commit blobs and two official documents that the old parser conservatively withheld. Its independently pinned profile and curator receipt establish provenance permission; content filtering and reconstruction remain separate checks. The original main exclusions and the cross-index explain each restoration without relabeling the old result.

Private keys, authentication material, private witnesses, raw private provider events, runtime state and dependency/cache payloads are withheld. Public model output is selected only through the recorded strict event-field rules. Dependency manifests, pinned source provenance and exclusion hashes support reacquisition where appropriate. A withheld original is not represented as a downloadable raw file.

Scoped helper reviews and passing reconstruction checks do not approve Moriarty source behavior, native execution, financial outcomes, target deployment or PCD. Consult the exact manifests, recorded commands and actual exits for publication evidence; no source or native approval is claimed here.

Reconstruction uses only the downloaded archive and Python's standard library. Original absolute paths in provenance receipts describe the capture environment; they are not required to restore archived files. Replaying every original capture/filter step is a different operation: it requires the pinned acquisition inputs and, for withheld raw logs, the locally retained originals. Public archive verification does not require access to those private originals.

Provider labels are conservative classifications. Withheld entries can include source or fixture syntax that the classifier cannot establish as an admitted public schema. The restoration cross-index and separately classified direct artifacts identify the public files recovered under their exact permissions. A legacy provider label does not establish that every such original was a real provider log.
