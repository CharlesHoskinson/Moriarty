## Independent source-based expectation derivation

At scale2,0.75 USD is75 atomic units;3 is a scalar; the quoted amount is75×3=225 atomic units. The tight limit2.10 USD is210; the loose limit2.50 USD is250. Therefore `min(225,210)=210` chooses operand b, while `min(225,250)=225` chooses operand a. `atoms(asset:USD,value:0)` is a typed zero quantity, not a decimal rescaling operation. Gross=value+0; fee cap0; net floor=value. These small exact integer expectations were authored directly, without invoking/importing the parser, checker, expander, Core evaluator or TypeScript lowerer.

The separate provisional Source/6 contract specifies the complete ordered transfer vector and that a fee Credit is present iff fee>0 ([experiments/moriarty-language/spec/successor/financial-agreement-source-v6.md:34](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/74a909d69639df8823a527a4d608a7516ccd69a2/experiments/moriarty-language/spec/successor/financial-agreement-source-v6.md#L34)). Thus each expected source proposes Debit Owner v, Credit Recipient v, UseAllowance Owner v, UseReplay nC, AdvanceHead c0→c1, with **no** Credit Fee0. The supplied third opening balance is nevertheless Fee7. The adopted public interface requires three ordered balance cells even at zero fee and raw Source/6 stdout with the existing final newline ([docs/BETA-SOURCE6-EXPANSION.md:23](https://github.com/CharlesHoskinson/Moriarty/blob/74a909d69639df8823a527a4d608a7516ccd69a2/docs/BETA-SOURCE6-EXPANSION.md#L23)). The EBNF independently fixes field order and the optional credit production. `INDEPENDENT-SOURCE-SHAPE.json` freezes these source facts, all local cells and the complete expected proposal shape.
