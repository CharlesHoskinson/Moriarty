# Proposed bounded ordinary requirements

These clauses are proposed extensions, not adopted EARS or current behavior. Current normative sources remain in `basis/`; source pins identify the complete subjects. The SP10 five-operator list and all original acceptance predicates are preserved.

**SS01 — Owned shared head.** WHEN a caller opens the proposed local shared Session with one admitted `/2` program, two distinct selected actions, canonical complete opening and fixed slot/bound inputs, THEN the Session SHALL own one complete opening/current financial Snapshot, two explicit slot statuses/frames and zero source work; it SHALL NOT construct the head by merging independent snapshots.

**SS02 — Explicit interleaving.** WHEN start/resume explicitly selects one slot, THEN exactly that source segment SHALL execute against the current head through the existing admission/scalar path, preserving the other slot's status/captures/cap/committed work. Source statement boundaries SHALL NOT become implicit interleaving points. The selected local segment's success SHALL NOT imply whole-session atomic synchronization or ledger acceptance.

**SS03 — Frozen locals, current state.** WHEN a pending slot resumes after another slot changes the head, THEN its saved inputs/lets/prior events SHALL remain exact and un-captured state reads SHALL use the current head. Its history entry SHALL retain frame-origin and actual entry/exit revisions. Ordinary standalone continuations and disjoint Pair semantics SHALL remain unchanged.

**SS04 — Complete frame and duties.** WHEN a positive segment publishes a head, THEN every declared state, explicit balance and source-registered duty SHALL remain present except for the exact source changes. Both selected actions' existing canonical-boundary predicates SHALL remain satisfied. Every positive liability SHALL appear in the current residual list; completion SHALL NOT discharge a duty implicitly.

**SS05 — Complete observations.** WHEN the Session/public consumer reports a prefix, THEN it SHALL preserve the complete current head, both statuses/frames, all original stage envelopes and complete Steps/values/snapshots/effects, all cumulative ordered effects, and all work counters. Historical boundaries SHALL NOT be rewritten as current projections. Whole expected comparisons SHALL precede any possible encoding transform. The first proposed wire uses complete arrays, not a new reference codec.

**SS06 — Finite cumulative work.** WHEN a source invocation is considered, THEN its per-slot stored cap SHALL remain fixed and its cumulative attempted/accounting policy SHALL follow adopted SI4, with no reset/borrowing. Positive selected costs [5,18] per action SHALL be preserved independently of order. The descriptive cap sum SHALL NOT substitute for actual cumulative attempt accounting. Design numbers confer no resource adequacy or execution authority.

**SS07 — Failure distinction, owner-dependent.** WHEN a false guard/refusal occurs, THEN financial head/frame preservation, actual attempted work and original rejection data SHALL remain distinguishable. SI4 accounting on failure, exact codes/wire, authority/head-lifetime binding and designated resource/security decisions SHALL be resolved before implementation. No hostile or false/error input test is authored in this positive source proposal.

**SS08 — Real existing consumer.** WHEN the proposed shared profile is adopted, THEN existing `mori test` SHALL dispatch a separate thin consumer that loads source via actual `stage::load`, drives public Session calls and compares all complete independently frozen observations. It SHALL NOT return success from a second evaluator, authored expected values, a mock or native substitute. Existing disjoint consumer/profile/feature/caps/output/fixtures SHALL remain unchanged.

**SS09 — Evidence and operator boundaries.** WHEN any local shared example passes in future, THEN its exact source/feature/commands/results and independent expectation qualifications SHALL be reported separately from native acceptance. Sequential, disjoint parallel, shared-state interleaving, atomic synchronization and asynchronous messaging SHALL retain separate dispositions. Missing head/proof/ledger/authority/confidentiality/resource requirements SHALL remain open.
