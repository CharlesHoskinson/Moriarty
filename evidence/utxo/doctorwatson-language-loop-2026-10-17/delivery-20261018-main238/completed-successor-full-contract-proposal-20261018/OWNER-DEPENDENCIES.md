# Required designated-owner contracts, not ordinary-author rules

This proposal defines the ordinary successful dataflow, whole-frame observation and arithmetic. It does not decide the following source/admission/identity/error/security/resource rules. The designated Opus or explicitly authorized Gemini/Muse owner must supply the applicable contracts and judgments. No hostile tests, security conclusions, error codes, commitment scheme, admission waivers or resource approval are authored here. Existing clauses remain verbatim in the pinned basis, including their original scenarios.

| Dependency | Concrete interface requiring owner input | Undecided question and required handoff |

Which local duty registry identities may be projected, including discharge-only incoming duties and await/R7 boundaries? Current predicates are not silently weakened. Supply exact admission ordering and supported domain; ordinary SC2 projection is not an admission proof. |

The normal-path records include the complete data these future rules must bind; they do not replace those rules with optional report fields. Production integration depends on applicable owner contracts and ordinary consequential-design votes. This is a concrete dependency handoff, not a new maintainer permission requirement on public developers. No provider dispatch or owner review occurred in this task.
