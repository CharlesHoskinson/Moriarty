**Tier 3 — structural.**

## Problem
Both crates lower `mori_core::Program` to ZKIR independently:
- `mori-backend`: `Builder` and `lower_action` in `src/lib.rs`, plus `stage/` for `moriarty/2`.
- `mori-ledger`: `relation/compiler.rs`, `relation/arithmetic.rs`, `ops.rs`, `layout.rs`.

Each has its own range, equality and 64-bit-limb multiplication helpers. #74 made `mori-ledger` depend on `mori-backend` and share the Schnorr verifier, the pinned SRS hashes and `hex`. The two lowerings and the two SRS loaders (`OfflineParameters` in the backend, `PinnedCallParameters` in the ledger) are still separate.

## Suggested fix
1. Inventory what each lowering emits (local relation constraints vs native ledger transcript ops) and where the differences are deliberate.
2. Move the shared gadgets (range checks, equality, limb multiplication, field encoding) into one module in `mori-backend`, or a small `mori-zkir` crate, used by both.
3. Merge the SRS loaders into one that takes a byte cap.
4. Keep the outputs byte-identical. The existing compile and relation tests, plus the pinned example artifacts, are the oracle.

## Needs input
- Are the backend's local proofs and the ledger's native calls meant to stay separate products? If yes, share only the gadgets; if no, pick the lowering that survives.
- A new `mori-zkir` crate, or grow `mori-backend`?

## Done when
One implementation of each gadget and one SRS loader, with every existing artifact test passing unchanged.
