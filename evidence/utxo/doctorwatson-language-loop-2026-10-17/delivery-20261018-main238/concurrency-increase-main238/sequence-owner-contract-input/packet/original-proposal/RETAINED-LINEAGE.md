## Meaning of complete frame here

Current Core/2 Snapshot contains state, balances and liabilities, and its scalar effects contain State/Transfer/Fee/Liability/Emit. The proposal preserves **every field of that current ordinary profile**. Snapshot does not encode signed intention, affine authority, consumed receipts, authenticated domains, external custody/mint/burn, pending settlement, expiry or proof artifacts. No opaque/untyped placeholder field is presented as implementing them. OD2/OD4–OD8 must specify those complete carriers/bindings and their integration before any corresponding full MPLR/native/accepted-history claim. A future profile adding fields must explicitly extend projection/lift/accounting/independent expectations; silently projecting them away is not this contract.
