# Proposed ordinary recovery EARS and finite predicates

Status: draft clauses for review; not edits to canonical requirements, not adopted normative authority and not a new completion denominator. Original MPLR-005/010/017 and all related authenticated/ledger obligations retain their full scope. These local clauses cannot replace them.

| Proposed ID | Ordinary language clause | First finite discriminator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DREC-01 Source-bound recovery | WHEN the new profile declares a recovery arm on an Await, the local recovery invocation SHALL select only that declared arm for the supplied pending action/site and SHALL use its actual boundary/captures and typed handler inputs. An unrelated ordinary action SHALL NOT retire that pending value. | A's pending deliver value reaches source-bound cancel and closes only through recover; a separately run financial action supplies no Closed result for it. This is a control/API predicate, not an authority judgment. |
| DREC-02 Explicit local closure | WHEN a true recovery guard and finite body complete normally, the returned normal control SHALL be RecoveryComplete with the source kind/site/arm, no pending successor, full new segment and complete residual liabilities. | A returns refund-complete with warranty9; B returns compensate-complete with remedy30. Neither reports economically settled. |
| DREC-03 Guard remains unresolved | WHILE the declared recovery guard is false, the local result SHALL remain pending with identical boundary/site/captures, no new committed effects/segment and unchanged committed work, while adding actual guard attempts. Timeout alone SHALL NOT imply a successful recovery transition. | C: deadline true/outcome false, attempted4, committed7, total attempts11, remedy50. The authenticity/reliability of any outcome input remains unassessed. |
| DREC-04 Preserve committed history | WHEN recovery succeeds or fails, local output SHALL preserve all earlier committed ordered effects and fees, append only genuinely successful new effects and never reconstruct history by netting a refund against earlier gross debit. | A preserves original fee2 and alice gross62 despite refund60; B preserves merchant50 and adds insurer debit20. |
| DREC-05 Explicit duties | WHEN the handler changes financial state, its explicit ordinary statements SHALL determine each transfer/fee/liability transition; mode annotation and control closure SHALL NOT automatically discharge or delete duties. Separate typed evolution equations remain visible. |
| DREC-06 Complete new trace | WHEN a recovery body completes, its trace SHALL identify the Recovery route, declared gate Step and every body Statement/ExprId/value/snapshot/delta in route-local order; the new execution effects SHALL equal the ordered concatenation of its Step deltas. | A gate0/body1..3; false C has no public gate Step. | DREC-07 No work reset | WHEN any normal/recovery invocation is attempted, its reported cumulative attempts SHALL add that invocation's attempts; committed work SHALL increase only on successful segment commitment. | DREC-09 Profile and qualification | WHEN the new source/profile is reported, its separate versioned schemas SHALL be used and local-reference-stage/authentication:none/consumption:untracked SHALL remain explicit. Existing `/1` and `/2` bytes/semantics SHALL NOT change, and unsupported compiler/target paths SHALL remain explicit. | Independently derived full Program3/Plan3/report for A/B/C, with old fixture bytes retained; no inference of native, security or proof result. |

**Single first useful feature predicate after adoption:** On the complete A specimen, actual public load/start/recover on its actual pending value with cancel_local:true returns the independently specified RecoveryComplete refund report: prefix effects retained, refund60 appended, fee2 retained, gross debits alice62/escrow60, return_due present-zero, warranty9 residual, committed15/attempted_total15 and closed returned normal control. A separate pre-run expectation must cover exact Program/plan/trace/DTO bytes. This would establish one finite local child capability only.

Follow with B/C to distinguish genuine compensation and unresolved timeout, and D only once complete ordinary error premises are reviewed. A financial refund transfer through existing start, normal multi-condition tests or a host deleting a continuation cannot satisfy the first predicate. Full formal, authenticated, race, native, ledger and product evidence remain open.
