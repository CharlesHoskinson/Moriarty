## S01: constrained genesis and counterfeit identity

One-shot mint policy requires spending configured seed out-reference exactly once. Mint exactly one asset `(policyHash, tokenName=instanceId)` and place it at the registered validator, with constrained initial ordinary/financial state, network/genesis domain, source/Core/toolchain manifest, revision0, lifecycle Live and empty used outcome nonce set. Bind full address including staking credential according to manifest. Genesis 100 Lender Cash +10 Borrower Cash and the unrelated7 Token follows the supplied financial fixture. Native funding accounts independently for fee/minADA; no claim that Cash is ADA.

Negative controls independently follow initiality: intermediate revision1 at genesis, outstanding debt without declared origination, duplicate quantity2, seed only referenced, wrong policy with same name, extra mint, wrong script/staking credential, changed program/network, fake same-address datum without genuine token, and a second mint after seed consumption. Same-address counterfeit outputs may legitimately be created by arbitrary parties: the oracle rejects their use as an authenticated instance, not their mere existence. Genuine live token may not be burned outside declared terminal branch. Mint policy and spend validator must agree on terminal burn authority.

Exact positive projection: one authenticated live head, revision0, genuine quantity1, no additional heads; fixture economics/authority/work unchanged. Real ledger predicate includes phase1 validity, phase2 success, seed consumed, mint policy enforced, and independently decoded output datum. Script success alone does not prove initialization across mint policy.

Transaction spends exact authenticated head H0 and creates exactly one H1 at registered address with token quantity1, mint0, revision1, exact source result and complete fee payout allocation. Every payout/effect carries unambiguous input/instance attribution.

Controls: missing successor, two successors, token moved to attacker address, H0 only in referenceInputs, valid economics attached to unrelated consumed head, altered post work/allowance/used IDs/obligation list, stale revision, reused fee ID. Reference-read transaction cannot perform state extension: reference inputs do not consume H0 or authority. Oracle does not reject ordinary harmless read-only transactions.

## S03: split/join and nonce-budget-obligation conservation

Minimal supported backend should reject Split/Join explicitly until lineage protocol exists. `unsupported-split.mori` is a concrete source rejection probe, not an executable feature. Keep both positive target context and negative mutations in cases.json so this coverage does not vanish. Do not duplicate the singleton token and advertise sharding.

Extension discriminator uses deterministic child tokens derived from parent out-reference + child index, authorized mint/burn transformation, plus one instance authority UTXO A carrying consumed outcome nonce N7. Split spends parent H and A; burns parent identity, creates exactly child0/child1, recreates A. Each step consuming outcome authority must spend A, so this design is intentionally serialized. It does not claim cross-head parallelism. A referenced registry is insufficient. A nonce remains globally consumed within that instance; there is no across-instance guarantee.

Concrete oracle: parent gross remaining100/spent20 partitions child0 60/12 and child1 40/8. Parent work remaining200/spent30/reserve20, declared Split work debit1, partitions child0 119/19/reserve12 and child1 80/12/reserve8. Sums: remaining199, spent31, reserve20. Parent owes O1 principal80 + O2 principal20; assign whole O1 to child0 and whole O2 to child1, preserving creditor/debtor/terms/history. Each duty has exactly one owner; no copied or dropped duty. Parent Cash100 partitions60/40. Used nonce registry remains {N7}; existing operation-ID history remains available globally or with disjoint declared namespaces. Positive Join consumes both children and A, burns child tokens and creates one authenticated parent: gross100/20, work198/32/reserve20 after one declared Join debit, Cash100, O1/O2 unchanged and nonce {N7}. Future implementation must specify descriptor/work semantics; this debit is a proposed profile rule, not today's runtime.

Controls reject duplicate child index/token, reference-only child in join, missing child, allowance copies100+100, dropped O2, duplicated O1, work restored200, closureReserve restored independently to each child, N7 usable on either child, join discarding spent IDs. No array-sum check alone can establish unique ownership/provenance.

## S04: migration and termination with residual duty

Migration must be a declared signed transition to an allowlisted new validator/program manifest, spending old head and authority state. New head revision+1 preserves instance/genesis, nonce history, role bindings, liabilities, remaining/spent gross, work/reserve and complete native asset state, with only declared migration debit. Immutable token policy must permit the declared target; otherwise migration is rejected, not silently enabled. Wrong target/new program, backward revision, reset nonce, substituted role, lost fee/closure funds or omitted debt rejects.

Concrete proposed envelope: old revision5, outstanding O1=80, gross remaining80/spent30, work remaining100/spent60/reserve16, nonce {N7}; migrate to revision6 with work99/spent61 and every other field exactly preserved. Termination with outstanding80 rejects even if thread burn and native value conservation succeed. Legitimate terminal transition requires all liabilities0, final disbursement allocation, authorized burn exactly-1 and no live successor. Closed audit/nonce record persists by declared tombstone/history rule; deleting a live datum cannot restore outcome authority. A transfer of nominal debt to a duty output is a different declared transition, not ordinary debt-free termination.

Full settlement is representable in existing lifecycle operations, but Migration, identity burn, lifecycle status and durable outcome nonce storage are absent. This design does not request replacing existing financial semantics. A backend closed manifest may initially admit only live Extend and debt-free Terminate; fail closed for other lifecycle actions.
