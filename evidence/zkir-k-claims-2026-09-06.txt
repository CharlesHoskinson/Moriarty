Claims that did not prove: none in the final run. One earlier formulation of
spec_compiled_observable, which sequenced a marker after job(P, Pre) in the <k>
cell to package (status, outputs, pis) into one term, stopped after 11.1 s
with "backend terminated because the configuration cannot be rewritten": the
job rule of ZKIR-VM rewrites the whole <k> cell, so nothing can follow it. The
template now states the triple over the <status>, <outputs> and <pi> cells.

What the claims cover: symbolic native inputs in [0, r) on fixed program terms;
the arithmetic claims also state that every emitted gate holds on the witness.
transient_hash proves with poseidonHash left uninterpreted (its defining rule is
[concrete]); in the second iteration the same claim did not finish in 420 s.
Not covered: symbolic programs, non-native types, curve arithmetic, inv, the
transcript instructions with symbolic guards, and the extension surface.
