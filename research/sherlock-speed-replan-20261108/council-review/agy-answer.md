- id: 1.2
- item: 1
- severity: OBJECT
- location: research/tree.md:157-159, 174 (§1 F8 constraints)
- problem: Gap in the **F8 constraints** for the three-slot tree node. F8 specifies `acc = collapse(accumulate([scale(proof_L, has_L), scale(carried_L, has_L), scale(proof_R, has_R), scale(carried_R, has_R), scale(leaf_acc, has_leaf)]))` with only a single outer `collapse` after `accumulate`. As demonstrated by `SHKIVC05` (`code/ivc.rs:354-361` and `qa/ivc/REPORT.md:13-17` `CEX-0`), `AssignedAccumulator::scale_by_bit` zeroes the scalars of a disabled slot's accumulator while keeping the variable-base points parsed from its dummy witness proof, and `Accumulator::accumulate` hashes the full encoding (including variable-base points and base counts) of every input accumulator to derive the Poseidon folding challenge `r`. Passing zero-scaled slot accumulators uncollapsed into `accumulate` makes the in-circuit folding challenge `r` depend on dummy witness bases and diverge from off-circuit `next_acc` on every disabled tree slot (`has_L = 0`, `has_R = 0`, or `has_leaf = 0`).
- proposed change: In the **F8 constraints** (`research/tree.md:157-159, 174`), require every disabled slot's accumulator inputs to reduce to `trivial_acc` *before* `accumulate`: collapse `scale(proof_L, has_L)`, `scale(proof_R, has_R)`, and `scale(leaf_acc, has_leaf)` before `accumulate` (or in `next_acc` mirror the exact fixed base count), and for `carried_L` and `carried_R` either constrain their public-input lanes to `trivial_acc` when `has_L = 0` / `has_R = 0` (realization (a)) or scale and collapse them before `accumulate` (realization (b)).
- evidence: `research/tree.md:157-159` (`acc = collapse(accumulate([scale(proof_L, has_L), scale(carried_L, has_L), ...]))`); `code/ivc.rs:354-361` (`Scaled by zero at genesis, the uncollapsed accumulator still carries the bases parsed from the (absent) previous proof, so the challenge would depend on them and could not match next_acc`).
