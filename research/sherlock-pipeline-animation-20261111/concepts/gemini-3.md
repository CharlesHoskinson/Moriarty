# Visual Concept 3: The Single Take

## 1. Concept in one paragraph

The workbench spans 96 world units in one unbroken line (`X = -6` to `X = +90`), divided into eight `12`-unit stations, with one camera on a parallel rail and a four-socket token carrier riding a front-edge groove beside the lens from start to finish. Nothing spawns in mid-air and nothing vanishes between scenes: scene `N` ends with its output object resting on the shared handoff plate at `X = 12N - 6`, scene `N + 1` begins from that exact pose, and a scrub across any timestamp reconstructs one continuous mechanical chain. A retractable copy-bridge on the front carrier clones `prog_d` and `input_d` into each station that checks them while the master tokens stay in plain sight at the front lip. The image a viewer remembers is the final pullback from Station 8: the whole bench in one frame, warm paper stations 1 to 5 on the left, the threshold at Station 6, dark slate stations 7 and 8 on the right, and the same `prog_d` and `input_d` minted at `t = 20 s` sitting beside `outcome_d` and `n_steps` inside the settled Midnight claim.

### 2.3 The `5 x 5` digest dot face and equality comparison

Each digest token carries `25` circular recesses (`5` rows by `5` columns, pitch `0.052` units) on a `ceramicAlabaster` hexagon. At build time, a deterministic 32-bit FNV-1a hash of the token's canonical content tag (for example, `"prog:image_1"`, `"val:uint_24"`, `"claim:run_1"`) sets bits `0..24`; filled bits place an `ink` (`0x14171f`) micro-disc flush with the surface, while zero bits show a shallow `ceramicEdge` dimple. No hex string ever appears.

To compare two digests for equality, both tokens tilt to the camera plane (`0.2 s`), sit `0.80` units apart for `0.2 s` so the viewer reads both `5 x 5` grids side by side, and then glide onto the same `(x, z)` center over `0.6 s` (`easeInOutCubic`). Token B descends the last `0.02` units onto Token A: matching `ink` dots land on `ink` dots with zero visual chatter, the two rims merge into one outline, and the rim flashes `goldBright` for `0.4 s`. When two tokens differ (Scene 6 tampered value), mismatched `ink` dots land inside empty dimples, triggering Refusal.

## 3. Motion sheet

### 3.1 Deterministic `seek(t)` and stepped-mode sampling

Every scene implements `seek(t)` (`0 <= t <= D_scene`) with zero retained frame state. Each beat `b` (`[t_0, t_1]`, duration `D_b = t_1 - t_0` in `14..22 s`) divides into three deterministic windows:

1. **Camera move `[0, d_cam]`**: `d_cam = 1.8 s` when shot pose changes (`0.0 s` if shot holds). Camera position and target interpolate via `easeInOutCubic(t_local / d_cam)`. All station parts hold Beat `b - 1`'s final pose.
2. **Key motion `[t_k0, t_k1]`**: starts at `t_k0 <= 2.0 s` (`1.8 s` after a camera move, `0.4 s` on a held camera; `0.5 s` on `Follow` shots where camera and object move together) and finishes at `t_k1 <= D_b - 2.5 s`. Composed of pure sub-tracks `u_i = easeInOutCubic(clamp((t_local - a_i) / dur_i, 0, 1))`.
3. **Still hold `[t_k1, D_b]`**: at least `2.5 s` where every matrix, color, and label is constant.

**Stepped reduced-motion mode:** `shell.js` maps any scene time `t` inside beat `b` to `t_step = t_1 - 1.0` before calling `scene.seek(t_step)` and `cameraRig.seek(t_step)`. Because `t_1 - 1.0` always lies inside the `[t_k1, D_b]` hold window, stepped mode and screenshot capture sample the exact same timeline with cuts and zero extra state logic.

## 5. Scale devices

Large quantities never use object counts; each uses a dedicated physical gauge:

1. **`16` steps against 2<sup>64</sup>:** Station 2 and Station 3 place a literal `16`-frame slice (`7.68` units) directly beneath a `3.2`-unit titanium ruler. Its left end marks `0..16` at true 1:1 frame pitch, followed immediately by a sharp double-chevron break notch (`//`) and an end-stop engraved 2<sup>64</sup> `- 1` coupled to a 64-bit odometer drum.
2. **`8` cells against 2<sup>66</sup>:** Station 3's `MEM` window holds `8` physical ceramic tiles (`1.36` units wide) above an address ruler showing `0..7` at tile scale, a double-chevron break notch, `3 x` 2<sup>64</sup> as a shaded safe-reach tick, and the hard wall at 2<sup>66</sup> (`2^63` segments of `8` cells), making visually immediate why `3` appends per step never exhaust memory before the 64-bit counter ends.
3. **Depth `63` (`TOP = 63`):** The memory tree spire is `2.10` units tall: `3` literal hash-node levels above the `8`-cell segment base, a machined zig-zag break collar in the middle shaft, `3` literal hash-node levels below the `gold` root token, and a vertical depth scale ticked `0, 1, 2 // 61, 62, 63`. During a flush or cold read, the `heat` cursor traverses the lower `3`, zips across the break collar, and traverses the upper `3`.
4. **`120` rules:** Drawn at exact 1:1 count in Station 2 as one `InstancedMesh` of `10 x 12 = 120` low-profile ceramic keycaps (`0.16 x 0.03 x 0.14`). Exactly `119` stay matte `ceramicAlabaster` while `1` lights `heatBright` per step.
5. **"Same size at any height":** Stations 4 and 5 mount a rigid brushed-titanium vernier caliper locked to `1.60` units wide. Whether a slab proves `16` steps (leaf), `32` steps (height-1 node), or `h_hi` near 2<sup>64</sup>, every slab slides between the same fixed jaws with zero clearance change, and folding adds radial grooves to the single `0.24`-radius `gold` ring rather than adding rings.
6. **"Linear work":** Station 4 mounts a `2.8`-unit horizontal ratio bar above the parallel cradles: a bottom step-slice bar (`2, 4, 8` slices) and a top node-work bar (`1, 3, 7` merges) coupled by a straight `45 deg` mechanical pantograph arm that rises along a ruled diagonal line (`65 s` per node unit).

## 6. Build notes

- **Renderer & TSL dual-path (`WebGPU` + `?webgl`):** Import `THREE` from `three/webgpu` and node helpers from `three/tsl`. Use `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` with `ceramicGrainSingleton`, `createBrushedMetalTexture`, and `studioEnvMapSingleton` (`environmentIntensity = 0.68`).
  - *Risky technique 1 — Verification sweep & Refusal hatch:* Custom TSL fragment masks can diverge in precision on older WebGL2 drivers. **Primary / Fallback:** Drive Verification with an actual `0.03`-wide `goldBright` bar mesh translated across the target bounding box (`visible = true` only inside the `0.4 s` window), and drive Refusal with a pre-built `InstancedMesh` of `12` thin `errorRed` diagonal slats (`y + 0.003`) scaled/toggled by `seek(t)`. Zero custom shader risk; identical on WebGPU and `?webgl`.
  - *Risky technique 2 — Paper-to-slate bench & fog transition:* The bench top is two static meshes meeting at `X = 60.0` (`paper` left, `graphite` right) separated by a `titanium` bar—no blended ground shader needed. `shell.js` sets `scene.background.lerpColors(PAPER, GRAPHITE, u)` and `scene.fog.color.copy(scene.background)` from `u = smoothstep(57.0, 63.0, camTarget.x)` inside `seek(t)`. During Beat `8.4` (`t >= 888 s`), `scene.fog.density` lerps from `0.014` to `0.0025` so all `96` units remain sharp.
  - *Risky technique 3 — Single shadow-casting light over 96 units:* One `DirectionalLight` (`2048 x 2048` PCF, `1024` on mobile) translates its `X` and `target.X` with `camTarget.x` (`frustum +/- 7.5` units), updating `shadow.needsUpdate = true` only when active motion occurs. On Beat `8.4`, widen shadow frustum to `+/- 50` units (or fade shadow intensity to `0.25` ambient contact slabs).
- **Expected draw calls & instance counts:** Only the active scene (plus adjacent handoff plate during transitions) is visible (`group.visible` gated by camera rail `X`). Per active scene: `18` to `34` draw calls (`55` in Beat `8.4` wide shot using simplified LOD shells for non-adjacent stations). Peak `InstancedMesh` counts: Scene 2 (`120` rule tiles + `32` tape frames + `600` micro-cells = `3` draw calls); Scene 3 (`16` slots + `64` register pairs + `48` memory/cache tiles + `400` digest dots = `6` draw calls); Scene 4 (`12` slabs + `8` rings + `200` dots = `4` draw calls); Scene 7 (`24` cabinet drawers = `1` draw call).
- **What to cut first if frame time exceeds `16.6 ms`:** (1) Drop `currentDpr` via existing DRS from `2.0` to `1.25`; (2) freeze `keyLight.shadow.autoUpdate` during static `Inspect` holds (`> 70%` of runtime); (3) replace `5 x 5` `InstancedMesh` dot cylinders on background tokens with a single cached `256 x 128` `CanvasTexture` dot-atlas; (4) swap non-active stations in Beat `8.4` to single-mesh baked-color silhouettes (`8` draw calls total).
- **Parallel builder isolation & viewport adaptation:** `shell.js` owns the bench slab, wall rail, handoff plate pedestals, camera rig, and Token Carrier (`setCarrierState({ prog, input, outcome, steps, copyPulse, docked })`). Each `sceneN.js` builds inside a local `THREE.Group` at `X_N = 12(N - 1)` and never references `sceneM`. Both `sceneN` (`t = end`) and `scene(N+1)` (`t = 0`) instantiate the shared `createHandoffPayload(N)` factory from `theme.js` at `(+6, 0, 0)` and `(-6, 0, 0)`. On wide viewports (`>= 960 px`), the narration panel takes the right `34%` and `camera.setViewOffset(W, H, +0.17 * W, 0, W, H)` centers the bench in the clear left `66%`. On narrow viewports (`< 960 px`), the panel docks to the bottom `36%`, `setViewOffset(W, H, 0, +0.18 * H, W, H)` centers the bench in the upper `64%`, and camera rail distance scales by `1.22x`.

## 7. Three risks

1. **Token Carrier clones vs. master tokens confuse provenance.** When `prog_d` is simultaneously visible in the front Token Carrier and inside Station 3's `INIT` or Station 4's `MERGE`, a viewer scrubbing mid-beat can wonder why two `prog_d` tokens exist. *Repair:* Keep the retractable `gold` pantograph copy-bridge arm faintly etched as a thin hairline trace on the bench surface whenever a clone is active in the station, so even on a paused hold frame the clone visibly tethers back to its master socket in the front Carrier.
2. **Scene 3 module density crowds `Inspect` readability on narrow screens.** Fourteen beats over `12` leaf modules risk overlapping DOM labels or shrinking `5 x 5` dot faces below legibility. *Repair:* Sub-frame Scene 3's `Inspect` camera onto three tight `3.4 x 2.6`-unit quadrants (`CORE + DEC`, `BUS + ALU + MEM + Spire`, `STACKS + BOUNDARY + INIT`) rather than holding one wide board shot for all `240 s`, keeping at most five labels on screen and inactive quadrants dimmed by `ceramicEdge` contrast.
3. **The final `96`-unit wide pullback (`Beat 8.4`) dwarfs the four tokens in Station 8.** At `56` units camera distance, `0.22`-radius hexagonal tokens span only a few pixels, weakening the visual payoff of carrying them for `15` minutes. *Repair:* Mount a crisp `gold` optical reticle ring (`createReticleTexture`) and a four-chip DOM callout anchored directly to the docked Carrier at Station 8 during Beat `8.4`, while lighting the `0.06`-wide `gold` centerline trace from Station 1 (`X = 0`) straight into that reticle (`X = 84`).
