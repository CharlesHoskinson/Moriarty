# Visual Concept 2: The Drawing

## 1. Concept in one paragraph

The workbench is an architectural drafting table where all eight stations sit as precision 2.5D plates in shallow bas-relief: recessed rule lines, corner registration marks, calibrated scale bars, and flat hardware trays at a uniform `0.06`-unit height. Vertical elevation (`+Y`) appears only where height carries mathematical meaning: depth `63` of the memory tree, stack chain links, and recursion rank. Most beats use square-on `Inspect` views aligned to a three-zone reading grid—entry at left, mechanism at center, output at right, persistent tokens on the front datum rail, pinned keys on the rear rail—so every still pose reads in five seconds like a textbook figure. Side-by-side comparisons share one split plate with aligned hairlines rather than sequential cuts. The one image a viewer remembers is the scale lock at the center of the bench: a `64`-bit logarithmic ruler broken by zig-zag cut marks (`16` steps against 2<sup>64</sup>) feeding `k = 20` titanium proof slabs whose caliper jaws hold the exact same width at `16` steps and near 2<sup>64</sup> steps, while the four public tokens on the front rail never leave alignment.

## 2. Object sheet

One world unit equals `10 cm` on the bench. Station centers sit at `X_s = (s - 1) * 11.0` along `+X`, depth `z` in `[-4.2, +4.2]`, bench top at `y = 0`. All plates carry `0.008`-unit 45-degree chamfers so the warm key light (`(6.0, 10.5, 5.4)`) catches crisp drafting edges. Materials use `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` with cached procedural ceramic and brushed-metal grain textures from the stage theme.

### 2.1 Canonical data objects

- **Field element, register, cell.** Rounded cube (`BoxGeometry(0.18, 0.08, 0.18)`, `0.02` bevel) in matte `ceramicAlabaster` (`0xebe5da`) with an `inkSoft` (`0x333c4d`) top border. Instanced per station in one `InstancedMesh` with per-instance color. *Idle*: flat `ceramicAlabaster`. *Active*: lifts `+0.03` in `Y`, rim `heat` (`0xea580c`), emissive `heatBright` (`0xf97316`). *Passed*: `0.25 s` `goldBright` (`0xfbbf24`) flash settling to `ceramicAlabaster` with a `gold` (`0xd4af37`) corner pip. *Refused*: halts, recoils `-0.04`, overlaid with a 45-degree `errorRed` (`0xe11d48`) stripe hatch.
- **Digest token and `5 x 5` dot face.** Regular hexagonal prism (`CylinderGeometry(0.28, 0.28, 0.08, 6)`, rotated 30 degrees so two edges align with `X`) with a `0.03` raised rim in its family hue: `gateViolet` (`0x818cf8`) for `prog_d` and `prog_root`; `steel` (`0x0284c7`) for `input_d`; `resolve` (`0x0d9488`) for `outcome_d`; `gold` (`0xd4af37`) for `pre_d`, `post_d`, `body_d`, `rules_d`, `deps_d`, `claim_d`, `set_root`, `reg_root`, and `shape_d`. Its recessed face (`ceramicAlabaster` on paper, `titaniumSilver` `0xe2e7f0` on slate) holds a `5 x 5` grid of circular pips (`CylinderGeometry(0.024, 0.024, 0.012, 12)`, `0.062` pitch) rendered by one shared `InstancedMesh`. Content hashes deterministically to the `25` binary positions: `1` sets a raised `ink` (`0x14171f`) pip, `0` sets a shallow `ceramicEdge` (`0xd5cec0`) dimple, and a top-left rim notch fixes orientation. **Comparing equal faces**: two tokens pause `0.80` units apart inside a twin bracket crossed by five `inkSoft` registration lines through rows `1` to `5`, then slide into coincidence at the midpoint; identical pips land dead-on without ghosting and the merged rim flashes `goldBright`.
- **Poseidon hash block.** Brushed `titanium` (`0xd8dde6`) block (`BoxGeometry(0.76, 0.14, 0.52)`) with a recessed `gold` (`0xd4af37`) channel from `-X` to `+X` and three top permutation ribs. Instanced where multiple sponges appear together. *Idle*: matte `titanium` with `gold` channel. *Active*: ribs light left-to-right in `goldBright`. *Passed*: exit collar holds `gold`. *Refused*: exit bar closes with an `errorRed` hatch.
- **Bus tuple.** Five-slot `titaniumSilver` bar (`BoxGeometry(1.04, 0.09, 0.24)`) carrying five `0.18` cubes `(chip, key, a, b, c)`. The first cube carries `1` to `4` engraved `ink` bars on top to mark `1` ALU, `2` MEM, `3` HASH, `4` ROM without texture text. *Active*: bar rim lights `heat` (`0xea580c`) in transit; returns to `titanium` once matched.
- **Memory record and 8-cell segment tray.** Square `ceramicAlabaster` tile (`BoxGeometry(0.22, 0.05, 0.22)`) scored with four `inkSoft` grooves. Eight tiles fill one recessed `ceramicEdge` segment tray (`BoxGeometry(1.96, 0.06, 0.30)`). Unwritten tail slots hold flat `paperWarm` (`0xebe5da`) pad plugs. *Active*: touched tiles tint `heat`.
- **Memory tree (`TOP = 63`).** Vertical column (`1.96` wide, `2.80` high in `+Y`) behind the segment tray: bottom `Tree8` bracket over `8` cells, tree levels `1` and `2`, a double zig-zag break collar (`—╱╲—`), and top levels `61`, `62`, `63` (`root`) beside a vertical `ink` depth ruler.
- **Machine step frame and witness tape.** Flat `paperWarm` plate (`BoxGeometry(0.46, 0.04, 0.92)`) bordered in `ceramicEdge` with three bands: rule id, state before, state after. Sixteen frames butt along `+X` (`7.36` units) per slice. *Active*: firing frame outlines in `heatBright`.
- **Stack (`FRAMES`, `TASKS`).** Stepped track (`1.80 x 0.36`) with a front cached-top register tray and a chain of linked `gold` hexagonal tokens stepping `+0.03` in `Y` down to an engraved `EMPTY` pin.
- **Proof slab.** Brushed `titanium` slab (`BoxGeometry(1.60, 0.12, 1.05)`) with a `0.02` `gold` edge. Every leaf slab, node slab, and decider seal shares this exact `1.60 x 1.05` footprint. Public lanes protrude on the `+Z` edge as `titaniumSilver` pins: `7` on a leaf slab, `5` on a node slab, `4` on the decider seal. *Passed*: `goldBright` edge sweep settling to `gold`. *Refused*: `errorRed` hatch on top face.
- **Accumulator ring.** Single `gold` torus (`TorusGeometry(0.24, 0.032, 12, 48)`) seated on a node slab (`y = 0.13`). Each folded deferred pairing check adds an instanced radial `ink` tick mark on the ring; a second ring never appears. In Scene 5, two jaws clamp the ring into a solid `gold` disc.
- **Verifier key rack.** Rear ledge (`z = -3.65, y = 0.16`) holding three flat `gold`-rimmed `titanium` cards (`leaf`, `node`, `decider`). Unchanged across all programs.
- **Claim and `runs` cabinet.** `claim_d` is a `gold`-rimmed token. The contract's `runs` map is a shallow slate cabinet (`BoxGeometry(2.60, 1.80, 0.32)`, `graphiteLight` `0x2a3446` over `wellSlate` `0x161d29`) of horizontal drawers with a vertical break mark. Each occupied drawer shows `claim_d` on its tab and holds `outcome_d` and `n_steps` inside, beside an empty crossed-out square showing that no state digest is stored.
- **Transaction envelope.** Flat `graphiteLight` folio (`BoxGeometry(2.30, 0.10, 1.48)`) with `gold` call-binding traces joining four value sockets, a `1.60 x 1.05` seal pocket, a `DUST` fee recess, and a `SherlockRun.acceptRun` tab.

### 2.2 Changes to the system

- **Change to the system 1 — Caliper, Break-Mark, and Row-Budget Kit.** Adds three `inkSoft` and `titanium` drafting instruments: `FixedCaliper` (`1.60` inner span straddling slabs across scenes), `BreakRule` (axis cut by a `—╱╲—` zig-zag mark for 2<sup>64</sup>, 2<sup>66</sup>, and depth `63`), and `RowBudgetBar` (linear gauge scaled to 2<sup>16</sup> or 2<sup>20</sup> rows showing exact shares such as `8,821` and `4,761`). *Reason*: Shows exponential scale and row budgets without counting objects or baking text textures.
- **Change to the system 2 — 8-Byte Counter Collar for `64`-bit heights and `n_steps`.** Draws `h_pre`, `h_lo`, `h_hi`, and `n_steps` as `8` contiguous `0.09` rounded cubes inside one `0.80 x 0.12` `titanium` collar (`8` bytes = `64` bits). *Reason*: Shows why 2<sup>64</sup> is the range of an `8`-byte counter rather than a circuit size, and visualizes Scene 5's `8`-byte decomposition of `n_steps`.
- **Change to the system 3 — Opaque Privacy Shutter.** Adds a sliding `titanium` cover (`BoxGeometry(2.80, 0.04, 1.60)`) scored with 45-degree `inkSoft` section lines. *Reason*: Covering internal state in Scene 5 while four public pins protrude past the shutter edge demonstrates cryptographic hiding, whereas fading meshes out looks like deletion.
- **Change to the system 4 — Split-Track Comparison Plate.** Divides comparison stations along `z = 0` into Track A (`z < 0`) and Track B (`z > 0`) feeding a shared right-hand coincidence socket. *Reason*: Places two programs, two tape cuts, two equal-value runs, and replay cuts side by side in one still pose.

### 2.3 Station housings and shared rails

Stations `1` to `5` use `paper` (`0xf4f0e8`) plates with `ceramicEdge` score lines and `inkSoft` corner marks at `(±5.1, 0.005, ±3.3)`. Station `6` splits at `x = 0` from `paper` to `graphite` (`0x1c2331`) across a `titanium` threshold bar. Stations `7` and `8` use `graphite` plates with `wellSlate` (`0x161d29`) wells and `titanium` corner marks. The shell owns the **Token Carrier** (`z = +3.65, y = 0.08`) and **Handoff Plates** (`x = ±5.50, z = 0`).

## 3. Motion sheet

Every pose in `seek(t)` is a pure function of `u = clamp((t - t0) / dur, 0, 1)` and `easeInOutCubic(u)`, ending in a zero-motion hold of at least `2.0 s`.

- **Poseidon hash (`dur = 1.00 s`).** `0.00–0.30 s`: ordered input cells lift `+0.03` in `Y` along a `-X` feeder line with `heat` rims; all other objects hold still. `0.30–0.75 s`: inputs slide `+X` (`easeInOutCubic`) into the `gold` channel, flattening inside as the three top ribs sweep `goldBright`. `0.75–1.00 s`: one hexagonal digest token exits `+0.55` units to `+X`, flashes `goldBright`, and settles with its `5 x 5` dot face upward.
- **Equality (`dur = 0.80 s`).** `0.00–0.25 s`: five `inkSoft` datum hairlines join rows `1` to `5` of the two tokens (`0.80` apart). `0.25–0.65 s`: both tokens slide symmetrically (`easeInOutCubic`) to the midpoint; identical `5 x 5` pips coincide cleanly. `0.65–0.80 s`: merged rim flashes `goldBright` and locks into one outline.
- **Verification (`dur = 0.40 s`).** `0.00–0.28 s`: a `0.03`-wide `goldBright` scan bar sweeps `-X` to `+X` across the top surface, wired by a hairline to the active rear key card. `0.28–0.40 s`: perimeter rim flashes `goldBright` and settles to `gold`.
- **Refusal (`dur = 0.45 s`).** `0.00–0.22 s`: moving token or scan bar advances to the gate (`u = 0.65`) and stops dead. `0.22–0.45 s`: recoils `-0.12` (`easeInOutCubic`) while a 45-degree `errorRed` hatch steps to full opacity across the face and freezes.
- **Station handoff (`dur = 1.60 s`).** Each scene closes on an `Establish` pose with its output resting at local `(+5.50, y, 0)`, identical in world space to the next scene's opening `Establish` pose at `(-5.50, y, 0)`. A `Follow` move (`1.60 s`, `easeInOutCubic`) tracks the object into the next station's entry bay (`-3.60`) alongside the Token Carrier.
- **Added diagram motions.** **Caliper lock (`0.50 s`)**: jaws close onto the `±0.80` edges of a `1.60` slab. **Shutter close (`0.70 s`)**: hatched `titanium` cover slides `+Z` over internal cells up to the public pin bar. **Row-bar step (`0.50 s`)**: fill bar scales `+X` to `rows / 2^k` inside `RowBudgetBar`.

## 4. Scene by scene, 1 to 8

**Framing rule:** Wide screens (`>= 1024 px`) give the right `34%` to the DOM narration panel and shift the frustum into the left `66%` via `camera.setViewOffset`. Narrow screens (`< 1024 px`) dock the panel below the canvas (`42%` height), clear the offset, and scale camera distance by `1.18`. Each scene opens and closes on `Establish` (`pos (0, 7.0, 8.5)` -> `(0, 0.1, 0)`, `fov 25°`). All diagram beats use `Inspect` at `85°` pitch (`z_cam = z_target + 0.85, y_cam = 10.2, fov 16°`), avoiding `lookAt` gimbal lock while keeping foreshortening below `1.5%`. Coordinates are station-local `(x, y, z)`.

### Scene 1: The program (Station 1, `X = 0`, paper)

**Layout:** Rear Key Rack `(0, 0.16, -3.65)` (`leaf`, `node`, `decider`). Track A (`z = -1.3`): source `(-3.8)`, ROM code tables `(-1.2)`, Poseidon `H_prog` `(+1.2)`, `prog_d` `(+2.6)`. Center (`z = +0.2`): input tiles `(-2.8)`, `sherlock-value/1` sponge `(+0.4)`, `input_d` `(+2.6)`. Track B (`z = +1.7`): second program source and ROM tray.

1. **Beat 1.1 — Source to ROM image (`Establish`).** Cam `(0, 7.0, 8.5)` -> `(0, 0.1, 0)`. Source sheet, ROM rack, 3-key rack, Carrier. *Motion* (`0.8 s, 1.1 s`): `gateViolet` source bars extrude and slide `+X` into ROM code tables. *Mechanism*: source compiles to a program image in ROM. *Still*: source wired by arrow rule to filled ROM rack. *Labels*: `Moriarty source`, `Program image (ROM)`, `Three verifier keys`.
2. **Beat 1.2 — `prog_d` (`Inspect`).** Cam `(0.2, 10.2, -0.45)` -> `(0.2, 0.06, -1.3)`. ROM rack, `H_prog`, `prog_d`, Carrier. *Motion* (`0.6 s, 1.0 s`): Poseidon hash mints `gateViolet` `prog_d` (face A) and copies it to Carrier slot 1 (`1.8 s, 0.8 s`). *Mechanism*: program image hashes to `prog_d`. *Still*: ROM -> `H_prog` -> `prog_d` on plate and Carrier. *Labels*: `Program image (ROM)`, `Poseidon hash`, `prog_d`.
3. **Beat 1.3 — `input_d` (`Inspect`).** Cam `(0, 10.2, 1.05)` -> `(0, 0.06, 0.2)`. `steel` input cells, `sherlock-value/1` sponge, `input_d`, Carrier. *Motion* (`0.6 s, 1.0 s`): Poseidon hash under `sherlock-value/1` mints `steel` `input_d` (face B) into Carrier slot 2 (`1.8 s, 0.8 s`). *Mechanism*: input digests to `input_d` under `sherlock-value/1`. *Still*: input -> sponge -> `input_d` beside `prog_d`. *Labels*: `Input value`, `sherlock-value/1`, `input_d`, `prog_d`.
4. **Beat 1.4 — One key set (`Inspect`, closing on `Establish`).** Cam `(0, 10.8, 0.85)` -> `(0, 0.08, 0)`, `fov 18°`, pulling to `Establish` `(0, 7.0, 8.5)` as ROM and tokens settle on `(+5.5, 0.04, 0)`. *Motion* (`0.6 s, 1.0 s`): Track B hashes to distinct `prog_d` face C while datum lines drop from the unchanged `leaf`, `node`, `decider` keys across both tracks. *Mechanism*: program is data, not circuit; one key set serves every program. *Still*: ROM rack and tokens on right handoff plate under unchanged keys. *Labels*: `Program A: prog_d`, `Program B: prog_d`, `Same three keys`, `Program is data`.
- **Transition 1 → 2 (`Follow`, `1.6 s`):** ROM rack and tokens glide from `(+5.5, 0.04, 0)` into Station 2.

## 6. Build notes

- **Materials and `?webgl` parity.** Built with `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` and `MeshBasicNodeMaterial` (`three/webgpu` r175) and cached procedural grain/env maps. Verification sweeps and `45°` `errorRed` refusal hatches use `three/tsl` fragment mix nodes driven by `uniform(float)` values set in `seek(t)`, compiling to WGSL on WebGPU and GLSL on `?webgl` (`forceWebGL: true`). *Fallback*: replace the TSL scan with a `0.03`-wide moving `MeshBasicNodeMaterial` bar mesh and the TSL hatch with a cached `64 x 64` striped `CanvasTexture` decal plane (`+0.002 Y`).
- **Stateless `seek(t)`.** Each scene precomputes static beat tables and `5 x 5` dot bitmasks at build time; `seek(t)` binary-searches the beat, writes all `InstancedMesh` matrices and colors from pure `easeInOutCubic` functions of `t`, and projects DOM anchors. Stepped reduced-motion mode seeks directly to `beat.endTime - 0.1`.
- **Expected budget (integrated GPU 60 fps, 1 shadow-casting directional light at `2048²`, `shadow.autoUpdate = false`):**
  - Shell: `6` draw calls, `112` instances.
  - Scenes 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7: `12` to `18` draw calls, `110` to `260` instances each.
  - Scene 3 (densest): `28` draw calls, `380` instances (registers, limbs, tray tiles, `5 x 5` pips).
  - Scene 8 (with self-owned merged low-LOD bench proxy for Beat 8.3 so no scene touches another scene's meshes): `34` draw calls, `320` instances.
- **Cut order if slow:** (1) lower `devicePixelRatio` cap from `2.0` to `1.35` during camera moves; (2) refresh directional shadow map only on end-of-beat holds; (3) merge static `inkSoft` rules per station into one `LineSegments` mesh.

## 7. Three risks

1. **Shallow `0.06` relief washes out on light `paper` in `85°` `Inspect` shots.** *Repair*: surround every `ceramicAlabaster` plate with a `0.015` `ceramicEdge` moat and `inkSoft` `LineSegments` outline under grazing key light (`(6.0, 10.5, 5.4)`).
2. **Scene 3's thirteen modules crowd the frame.** *Repair*: hold non-participating modules in flat `paperWarm` / `ceramicEdge` line-art state with zero labels; apply `titanium`, `heat`, `gold`, and DOM labels only to the `2` or `3` modules active in that beat.
3. **Viewers misread `—╱╲—` break marks as small linear gaps.** *Repair*: compress logarithmic decade ticks (`16`, `2^8`, `2^16`, `2^32`) visibly before every break cut and pair the far stop with the `8`-byte (`64`-bit) collar.
