## 1. The theorem

```lean
theorem bridge_sound (P : Premises) {pub : List Nat} {prf : P.LedgerProof}
    (h : P.LedgerAccepts pub prf) : P.Run pub
```

`pub` is the wrap's whole public input, and it is the statement (the claim). `P.Run pub` says the reference machine makes that claim true. Under option E (OD-24) the wrap verifies `L_Fr`, and `L_Fr` verifies `L`. Proof route (in `Bridge.lean`):

1. A1 gives a wrap witness `w` with `WrapRel X B LFrAccept vkLFr pub w`.
2. `wrap_refines` (a theorem) gives: the reference verifier of `L_Fr` accepts `(pub, w.pv, w.π)` (native Fr checks `LFrAccept` and the Fr transcript run, `runFr_refines`); the wrap table `w.T` has exactly `B` entries; every entry is well-formed (canonical values) and `Valid`; `w.pv = pvOf vkLFr.pvLen w.T`.
3. A2' (`lfrSound`, with A2b and `vkLFr_ok` discharged) gives an `L` proof `πL` that the reference verifier of `L` accepts, with the same statement and public values: `VerifyL X vkL pub w.pv πL`.
4. (Not a step of the proof; a supporting theorem.) `lfrSound_of_emul` shows how an `L_Fr` meets A2': if it enforces the Fr-cell emulation `EmulL` of `L`'s verifier, `emulL_refines` (with `check_refines`, `run_refines`, `arith_refines` and the packing and digit lemmas) turns it into `VerifyL`.
5. A2, with its three hypotheses discharged (`packed_hash_inj` from A2b; `pvFirstT` and `ProvenRegime` from the static key check, both computed from the key's transcript program), gives a root acceptance with some digest `d`.
6. A3 gives a carrier log tree `t` with `pvOf vkL.pvLen (pad B (flatten t)) = w.pv` and digest `d` (`vkLFr.pvLen = vkL.pvLen` by `vkLFr_ok`).
7. `pvOf_inj` (from `encode_append_inj`) gives `pad B (flatten t) = w.T`, so every entry of `flatten t` is valid.
8. A4 gives a carrier tree `t'` with digest `d` and `RunWith (flatten t') pub`.
9. `digest_inj` (a theorem from A5) gives `t' = t`.
10. `runWith_all_valid` turns the run into one whose Fr operations are all valid (deferred ones from step 7, emulated ones from the leaves); A6 closes.

Companion theorem for the zero-knowledge boundary:

```lean
theorem wrap_view_only_claim (P : Premises) {pub : List Nat} {w₁ w₂ : WrapWit}
    (h₁ : WrapRel P.X P.B P.LFrAccept P.vkLFr pub w₁)
    (h₂ : WrapRel P.X P.B P.LFrAccept P.vkLFr pub w₂) :
    P.Indist (P.prove pub w₁) (P.prove pub w₂)
```

Two witnesses for the same claim (different logs, used-entry counts, splits, proofs of `L_Fr` and so of `L`) give indistinguishable ledger proofs.

### 2.1 Gate G4: the unique decoding parameter check

`ProvenRegime vk = powShapeOK vk.whir vk.transcript && oodShapeOK vk.whir vk.transcript && vk.whir.gate efSize (powVecOf vk.whir vk.transcript)`. `UDSet` holds the declared analysis parameters (log blowup `b`, polynomial size `2^m` with `m = n_stack + l_skip`, `k_whir`, the per-round query vector, `w_stack`, the interaction bound, the frozen LogUp PoW, the radius slack `sigma`); `PowVec` holds the proof-of-work values and OOD sample counts, read from the transcript program. `|F| = |EF| = p^4`. Each term is an exact rational `num / den`; "at least `B` bits" is the Nat fact `2^B num <= den`, and "reaches `B` bits" adds `den < 2^(B+1) num`.

Per-fold proof of work (OD-1 to OD-3, kept under OD-19 with value 0): `PowKind.fold i s` tags the PoW step of fold `s` of round `i`, `PowKind.query i` the query PoW of round `i`. `foldSteps` and `querySteps` list them in transcript order. `powShapeOK` requires exactly one folding PoW step per fold position, in protocol order (`foldSlots M k`: round-major, folds `1 .. k`), and one query PoW step per round in round order. `powVecOf` takes the folding vector and the query vector from those steps, so the bits of fold `(i, s)` in the gate are the bits of that fold's own step. The weakest `mu` and LogUp step give the `mu` and LogUp values. With required fold PoW 0 a gate that only took a minimum would pass a transcript with no folding PoW step at all; the position check refuses it (`lKey_missing_folds_not_proven`).

OOD samples: the transcript of `L` has two OOD operations, `TOp.oodPoint i dsts` (a challenge squeezed as by `squeeze`, 5 base-`p` digits) and `TOp.oodAnswer i vars` (the prover's answer, BabyBear words absorbed as by `absorbBB`). `VerifyL` runs them in `runRef`; the emulation gadgets in `runGadget` (inside `EmulL`) are the digit gadget and the packing gadget, and `run_refines` covers both. `oodShapeOK` requires, in each round `1 .. M - 1`, one answer per point and every point squeezed before the first answer of the round (`oodPointsFirst`): the order in which WHIR Lemma 4.25 bounds `s` samples drawn together (G5-PAPER-MAP section 5, OD-1). `powVecOf` counts the samples of each round from the `oodPoint` steps, and the gate computes the OOD and `gamma` terms with that count. The OD-19 set has one sample per round (`lTranscriptUD`); the two-sample transcript `lTranscriptUD2` (`z_0, z_1`, then `y_0, y_1`) also passes (`lKey2_ok`, OOD 216 and 224 bits). `ood_interleaved_rejected` shows `z_0, y_0, z_1, y_1` fails the order check, `lKey_no_ood_not_proven` that a transcript without OOD steps fails the gate. `ood_point_digits`: in an accepted run the digits of each OOD point are those of the sponge output on everything absorbed before it. `ood_batch_refines`: the emulated check of the batched claim `gamma * poly_v(gamma) = c` (WHIR Construction 5.1, decision 2c, over the OOD answers and shift values) gives the reference value mod `p`.

Limits of this modeling that the lead should know:

- Under option E the BabyBear emulation (bounded-quotient arithmetic, packing, digit gadget) is not in the wrap. It is the relation `EmulL`, a sufficient relation for `L_Fr` to enforce, and A2' is stated on `VerifyL` as the lead specified. So the refinement lemmas of `L`'s checks and transcript (`check_refines`, `run_refines`, `arith_refines`, `squeeze_digits_sound`, `packChunksR_eq`) are supporting under option E: `lfrSound_of_emul` uses them to show that an `L_Fr` enforcing `EmulL` meets A2', and they leave the path of `bridge_sound` with the old wrap of `L`. The "On path" column of the rows above marks them for that sufficient route. The wrap itself is native in Fr, and its gadget for `L_Fr`'s checks other than the transcript is the reference by definition, since `LFrAccept` is abstract.
- The arithmetic gadget in `EmulL` is `ArithGadget` (unsigned values, `c + q p` with `0 <= q < 2^223`). The plan's signed `ReduceRel` is formalized and proved (`reduce_sound`) but is not the gadget that `EmulL` uses; an `L_Fr` that uses signed lazy reduction needs `ArithGadget` replaced by `ReduceRel`.
- The transcript of `L` is a program in the key (`KeyL.transcript : List TOp`: absorb BabyBear words, absorb Fr elements, squeeze, proof of work, OOD point, OOD answer), and the verifier `VerifyL` runs it (`runRef`). So `pvFirstT`, the proof-of-work values and the OOD counts of `ProvenRegime` are derived from what the verifier executes, not declared. What stays inside A2 is that this `TOp` program is the transcript of the real stark-backend verifier of `L`; that link is a property of the code that freezes the key, outside Lean (gate G3).
- Only the transcript start carries a use code in the model (`trStart 3` for `L`, `trStart tTag` for `L_Fr`). The Merkle leaf and node hashes of `L` (codes 1 and 2) and of `L_Fr` are untagged `frSponge` calls inside `Check.hash` and `merkleRoot` or inside `LFrAccept`; their domain separation is the capacity rule of PROPOSAL section 3.6, outside the model. `tags_separated` covers every code and length in the capacity cell, so it applies to them once the hashes carry their code.
- Only the WHIR analysis parameters stay key metadata (`UDSet`: blowup, polynomial size, `k_whir`, query vector, `w_stack`, interaction bound, frozen LogUp PoW, radius slack). Reason: they are not verifier operations that the model can read off; the blowup and the polynomial size occur in the verifier only through the domain constants of the `arith` checks, and this model has no Reed-Solomon code to tie those constants to (core Lean, no mathlib). The query count per round is the number of Merkle openings, which the model does not count from the checks. Deriving them would need a formal WHIR verifier with its code, which is outside this lane. `ProvenRegime` computes every WHIR and `mu` error term from them, and the theorems that bound the errors stay inside A2. That the declared set equals the set the key-generation code freezes is gate G1, outside Lean.
- `coset_fold_refines`, `whir_round_refines` and `pv_eq_refines` are specific lemmas about named expression shapes. `bridge_sound` does not need them: every such check is an `arith` check of the key, and `arith_refines` covers all of them at once. The specific lemmas state what the reference relation is for each shape.
- Lemma 23 is proved over the prime field `Z/q` (BabyBear). The binding challenge of `L` lies in the extension field `EF`; the same induction applies over any field, but this lane proves the prime-field case, and the bound enters `bridge_sound` only through A2.

## 4. Statements changed from the plan (CANDIDATE.md section 11)

1. `Params` removed. `p` and `r` are fixed literals (`pBB`, `rFr`), with `w = 30`, `l = 9`, base `2^31`, 8 words per Fr element, and every bound checked by `decide`. Reason: the plan's `hr : 2^248 < r` is too weak for the lazy reduction. `ReduceRel` with `b <= 252` needs `|x - (q p + t)| < 2^252 + 2^253 + 2^31 < r`, which needs `r > 2^254`. True for BLS12-381 Fr (`r_big`, `reduce_bits_ok`). No design change.
2. `castP`, `castR` and `|.|` replaced by `%` on `Nat`/`Int` and two-sided bounds; `ReduceRel` takes explicit bounds `X`, `Q` with the static condition `X + Q p + 2^31 <= r`. `lift_eq` is stated in difference form (`-r < x - y < r`). Reason: no mathlib absolute value; the explicit form is what the wrap's static bound check verifies.
3. `verifyL` returns `Prop`, not `Bool`, and is defined (`VerifyL` over `Check` and the transcript program), not a `Premises` field. `WrapRel`, `RunWith`, `Carrier` are also definitions. Reason: as opaque fields, `wrap_refines`, `encode_inj` and `digest_inj` cannot be proved and none of the soundness points would be in the theorem.
4. `RunWith` is defined from a new field `RunOps` (the leaves' relation over the run's whole Fr operation sequence) and a split into deferred and emulated operations. `oracleElim` (A6) is stated on `RunOps`. Reason: makes lemmas 20 and 21 provable and puts the overflow path in the proof.
5. A2 (`lastLayerSound`) has three hypotheses: `PackedHashInj X FrDom`, `pvFirstT vkL.nStmt vkL.pvLen vkL.transcript = true` and `ProvenRegime vkL = true`. Reason: these are the conditions under which the soundness of `L` holds; stating them puts packing, transcript order and the proven parameter regime on the formal path. All three are discharged inside `bridge_sound`. The proximity and batching theorems themselves stay inside A2: core Lean has no Reed-Solomon codes, so the formal content is that the key's parameters meet the hypotheses of the cited results and that their error terms, computed exactly, reach the bit gate.
6. New premises A2b (`frSpongeInj`, Fr hash) and A7 (`wrapZK`), and the static field `vkL_ok`. ## 5. Design findings

1. Empty onion must be a hash output (amendment 1, applied). With `d_0 = TAG_EMPTY` as a raw constant (section 3.1 of the candidate), tree-digest injectivity needs a preimage assumption (`p2` never outputs `TAG_EMPTY`) that collision resistance does not give: an empty segment could equal a `TAG_NODE` merge. The model uses `d_0 = P2([TAG_EMPTY])` (`onion_empty`), at one extra permutation per empty segment.
2. The quotient bound is a hard requirement, not a cost choice (amendment 2, in the model as `ArithGadget`). Without `q < 2^223` (any bound with `c + q p < r`), a prover can claim a false product: for any claimed residue `c`, `q = (a b - c) p^(-1) mod r` satisfies the Fr equation (`unbounded_quotient_unsound` is the instance `1 * 1 = 0`). Every bounded-quotient gadget that emulates BabyBear in Fr (in `L_Fr` under option E) must range-check `q`. The static bound `< r` of every lazily evaluated expression must be checked when the key is frozen (`vkL_ok`).
3. Packing needs both checks the candidate names: equal (key-fixed) absorb lengths and words below `2^31`; each alone is not enough (`pack_length_check_needed`, `pack_range_check_needed`). In the transcript of `L` the absorb lengths are fixed by the key (`TOp.absorbBB` lists its variables), so the length check is static.
4. The squeeze needs a canonical check on the recomposition (amendment 3, in the model as `SqueezeGadget` inside `runGadget`; `squeeze_alias`), the same `v` against `v + r` issue as for logged values.
5. The `pv` length is fixed per key (amendment 4, applied as `pvLen` and `pvOf`). Sizing input for M9: `pvLen` must be at least the encoded length of `B` entries of the longest arity, or `SPONGE(n)` slots need a bound on `n`.
6. Pigeonhole: hash premises in Lean must be carrier-guarded with an abstract domain. Stating the small-field digest premise as "injective on all BabyBear vectors" would be false and make the theorem vacuous. The formalization follows `Aggregation.lean`.
7. Under OD-19 (unique decoding) every WHIR and `mu` term of `L` and of the internal layer is computed in Lean from the theorem statements and reaches the gate (`lUD_bits`, `intUD_bits`); the integer parts equal the D3 calculator's on every term. The gate reads the PoW of each kind from the transcript program, so a key with `mu` PoW 5, query PoW 19 or LogUp PoW 17 fails `vkL_ok` and `bridge_sound` does not apply to it. A transcript with no PoW of a kind earns no PoW credit (the minimum is `0`). The weakest term of `L` is `mu` (101.07 bits); the margin rule (101 bits on grindable terms) holds with 0.07 bits to spare.
8. The transcript must absorb all of `pv` before the first challenge or proof of work; absorbing a part first is rejected (`partial_pv_rejected`). The stark-backend preamble order (commitments, then public values, then challenges) passes (`lKey_ok`).
9. No design change found for: the fixed capacity with spill (`spill_equiv`, `discharge_sound` hold for any split), the ZK boundary (only `pub` is public), and the case-A digest form `PoseidonFr(tag, pack d2)` (`public_digest_binds`).
