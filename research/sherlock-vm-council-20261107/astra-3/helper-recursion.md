## Conditions that cannot be removed

1. **Bind the actual relation and statement.** Preserve program and semantic version, authority and intent, domain, predecessor state/history, observations, complete effects and bounds. A prover-selected predicate or key cannot stand in for this relation (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md:25–31`, `:37–45`). The recursive wrapper only establishes the relation its leaf key encodes. A correct wrapper cannot repair an incomplete leaf.
2. **Constrain continuity, genesis and termination.** Preserve program identity and predecessor equality across every child. Constrain the genesis case. Bind the final state to the required result or effects. In the fixed parent, only the anchor ties the predecessor root and height to its current state; the parent explicitly leaves initialization and broader interpreter obligations open (`crates/mori-recursion/src/parent.rs:10–22`). The general node and run decider have explicit genesis-link and halted-result constraints (`crates/mori-recursion/src/ivc.rs:303–320`; `crates/mori-recursion/src/run_decider.rs:278–288`).
3. **Discharge deferred cryptographic obligations.** Carrying an accumulator is not terminal verification. Preserve its binding to the proof, key and SRS, point/subgroup conditions and final pairing. Deleting the terminal check would change what is proved (`crates/mori-recursion/src/decider.rs:179–226`; `crates/mori-recursion/src/run_decider.rs:201–276`).
4. **Verify retained public artifacts independently.** Preserve fresh decoding, exact identities, transcript exhaustion, full statement correspondence and hostile mutations. The independent verifier reconstructs the child accumulator, checks transcript exhaustion and carried pairing, verifies the installed key, and mutates public inputs and proof bytes (`crates/mori-recursion/examples/verify_chain.rs:197–245`, `:261–289`).
5. **Preserve the ledger boundary.** Local proof acceptance, installed-key verification, inclusion and finalized effects are separate predicates. A4 does not imply submission or finality (`openspec/changes/recursion-phase-profile/specs/recursion-phase-profile/spec.md:157–165`). Target acceptance needs independently read complete effects and replay/predecessor rejection, as specified by A7–A10 (`qa/sherlock/predicates.yaml:61–95`).
6. **Keep permissionless public use.** An external developer must author a new supported program, prove it and submit through the developer's own Midnight environment. Fixed loan/swap examples and internal review records are insufficient. Maintainer tooling must not enter public validity (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md:5`, `:51–59`, `:72–76`; `AGENTS.md:37–38`).

## Cuts and sequencing

**Remove retired process dependencies immediately.** MC03's named review and campaign gates are historical. The current charter explicitly retires those earlier rules and directs work through factory predicates. They are neither public product requirements nor present blockers (`AGENTS.md:8–25`; compare `openspec/changes/mc03-native-recursive-proof/tasks.md:65–73`). Preserve the underlying semantic and verification obligations.

**Defer throughput infrastructure until one execution is complete.** GPU kernels, persistent workers, proof markets, concurrent heavy proofs and multiple aggregation topologies are optimization candidates. None has an agreed speedup in the supplied tracker (`wiki-llm/recursion-perf/CONSENSUS.md:7–19`). Keep the official native proof system and target interface while making the smallest useful execution work. The September native-recursion study treats a foreign recursion layer as a later alternative and explicitly separates Rust recursion from ledger integration (`docs/research/2026-09-06-midnight-native-recursion.md:5–16`, `:80–95`).

**Defer additional workloads, without deleting the language promise.** A first release can expose a precise bounded fragment and reject unsupported programs. Multi-parent privacy, additional financial libraries and longer histories remain scope to deliver. The product contract explicitly retains native recursive compliance and private multi-parent composition; current limitations do not erase them (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md:11`, `:15`, `:49`). One fixed literal or stand-in relation cannot become the public language's completion criterion (`:72–76`).

A minimum useful sequence is therefore:

1. Select one bounded financial operation sequence with independently frozen input, expected complete effects and explicit authority/bounds. MC03's accrual then due settlement is an existing candidate, rather than another literal demonstration (`openspec/changes/mc03-native-recursive-proof/specs/mc03-native-recursive-proof/spec.md:19–25`).
2. Establish that the real leaf constrains those operations. Compose at least two proved transitions or chunks under pinned keys. The general interface includes `steps`. Chunking can preserve the same node boundary if the leaf proves every included step (`crates/mori-recursion/src/ivc.rs:58–62`, `:354–369`). The chunking benefit remains to be measured.
3. Produce and independently verify the terminal artifact, including context, key, predecessor and malformed-proof controls. Rehearse ledger acceptance and complete effects before relying on stagenet receipts (`qa/sherlock/predicates.yaml:42–68`).
4. Establish a finalized call and independent effects readback for that exact candidate. Then extend to the specified 16-transaction batch and 2/4/6-batch prefixes. Eight transactions require a separate adapter and cannot silently replace the full workload (`openspec/changes/sherlock-stagenet-recursive-rollup/tasks.md:17–23`; `docs/sherlock/stagenet-recursive-rollup-experiment.md:25–27`).

A direct fused terminal is a useful **proposed alternative for a bounded computation**. It can remove recursive-verifier work when one prover holds both witnesses. It does not establish recursive composition and cannot replace that requirement under the same claim. Its proposed k15 shape is unsynthesized, its gain depends on the unmeasured remainder, and an enlarged fused domain can lose the gain (`wiki-llm/recursion-perf/CONSENSUS.md:8`).
