# Draft gate and recovery corrections

These are comments on the files being assembled, before the formal DRAFT v1 broadcast. They do not cast a vote.

1. **Universal soundness claims need a premise and an honest evidence label.** `assurance.md:O2` says every satisfying witness decodes to a valid transition and allows hostile raw-witness tests to close it. State the `StepInv(pre)` and committed-profile/bounds premises explicitly. Tests can close a finite campaign acceptance gate, not the universal mathematical claim. Keep the universal obligation open unless a checkable proof discharges it; a declared release gate can still require specified finite evidence with this limitation. The same distinction applies to O3's universal completeness claim and its P1-P3 tests. This is required by the document's own SAS-009/SAS-012 and UISEC-012 evidence policy.

2. **Keep invariant induction distinct from refusing arbitrary unreachable rows.** The SVM-002 scenario permits either rejection or a checked unreachability argument, but the requirements table says every raw hostile case must be refused by the leaf key. Synchronize them. The obligation is initialization plus preservation on its declared domain; a bad pre-state outside `StepInv` is not automatically a full-run exploit. Conversely, naming `StepInv` without checking its initialization and every relied-on preservation premise is insufficient. Source: `openspec/changes/sherlock-universal-interpreter-design/security-candidate-b.md:A4`; `crates/mori-backend/src/sherlock_machine/c6_admin_publish_step.rs:88-127`; SAS-010.

3. **Recovery must account for the old on-chain contract.** `workflow.md:7` currently sends an unsafe old key to redeploy-and-retire, and SMN-007 labels every immutable deployment disposable. A fixture change and a local retired label neither revoke an accepted on-chain key nor move funds, duties, pending continuations, or consumed statement IDs. Limit discard/redeploy to demonstrably empty/disposable instances. For active instances require an explicit preservation/migration policy, the old address's residual authorization status, and tests of the chosen mechanism. If no safe recovery mechanism exists, record that limit; do not claim rollback completed. Immutable deployments remain permitted without compulsory maintenance authority. The Preview rehearsal must cover the declared mode and verify old-key rejection when revocation is claimed. Source: existing ZR15, SND-005/006, and `wiki/midnight-ledger-9-hardfork.md` maintenance/#2375 notes.

4. **Required security gates cannot be left open at change exit.** The proposal's exit language must distinguish required gates for the claimed profile/target (must pass) from deferred profiles and conditional mainnet work (may remain open and explicitly unclaimed). An open mandatory soundness obligation cannot be converted into a passing gate by documentation. This follows SAS-006/SAS-012 and UISEC-010/012.
