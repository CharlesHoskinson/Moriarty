Capacity SHALL NOT depend on the key."* Introducing a key-baked $2^{35}$-cell ceiling below $2^{64}$ narrows `GP-1` and `GP-001`: an allocating run of $2^{36}$ steps (`< 2^64`) cannot complete as one run under that key set.
     2. **Zero Practical Prover Saving**: Moving from `TOP = 32` ($2^{35}$ cells) to **`TOP = 61` ($2^{64}$ cells, full 64-bit address width)** adds $(61 - 32) \times 2 \times 70 = 4{,}060$ rows per shared-path flush (`8,120` rows at `W_flush = 2`) and $(61 - 32) \times 70 = 2{,}030$ rows per `PAGE` opening (`crates/mori-chip/src/poseidon.rs:52-61`). By Experiment `E2` (`research/sherlock-recursion-design-review-20261110/experiments/e2-e4.md:25-35`), `k_leaf = 15 -> 16` adds **`+4` rows (`0.0005%`) to the `k = 20` node**, **`+0` rows to the decider**, and **`~1–2 s` `ESTIMATE` to a leaf sitting beside a `63 s` node** (`< 3%` wall impact). #### 1. Why `2^64` Exists in the Spec and What "Unbounded" Honestly Means
- **Why `< 2^64` is in `SRC-004` and `GP-001`**: In a prime field of order $r_{\text{BLS12-381}} \approx 2^{255}$, unconstrained addition `h = prev_h + steps` wraps modulo $r$ (`(r - 1) + 1 == 0 mod r`), allowing a mid-chain leaf at `prev_h = r - 1` to masquerade as `h = 0` (`is_genesis = 1`) or forge arbitrary `n_steps` (`clean/fork/Clean/Sherlock/Aggregation.lean:1208-1213`, `crates/mori-recursion/src/ivc.rs:359-376`).
