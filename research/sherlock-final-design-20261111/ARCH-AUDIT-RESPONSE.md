Limits of "fixed" (stated so that the table is not read for more than it says):

- The fixes are design text. Nothing was built, tested, modelled or proved for this revision. Every new mechanism is marked as proposed by revision B2 in `A:` section 7.3.
- Three fixes leave one sub-item to the owner because the governing specification itself leaves it open: the input-admission form (finding 3, AD-17), the one-arena text of SRC-021 (finding 3, AD-18), and the normative full-language reference (finding 14, AD-21).
