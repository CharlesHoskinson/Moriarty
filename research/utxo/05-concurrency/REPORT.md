## Split/join design constraints for Moriarty

These are compatibility inferences, not implemented acceptance:

- Authenticate all live shards by genesis/minting policy, instance/program/network, lineage and exact out-reference. Split consumes the parent; creates precisely named children and partitions authority, remaining work and every residual duty. Copying a cap or debt across children is unsound. Lowering must reject an unsupported split rather than silently duplicating datums.
- Shared mutable budget/nonce UTXO is simple and safe but a serialization point. To obtain parallelism, partition quota into nonoverlapping, nonduplicable tickets, or allocate nonce namespace/ownership deterministically to shards. Two branches with copies of the same nonce set can each accept the same outcome signature. A read-only reference input does not consume authority or synchronize writes.
- Join consumes all specified child heads and commits exact lineage/participant set; rejects replay, omitted liabilities, premature closure and overlapping authority tickets. Migration must carry these invariants to allowed successor program identities. Thread-token conservation alone does not prove lifecycle/financial conservation.
- Loan installments, partial swaps and handoffs keep residual liabilities explicit. Completion of a message or consumption of a parent is not payment of all future dues. Asynchronous messages introduce pending obligations, expiry, reclaim and executors; atomic composition consumes all participating states in one transaction and faces transaction size/execution budgets.
- Work bounds prevent runaway computation; they do not provide executor willingness, oracle availability, timely ACTUS settlement, sufficient minimum ADA, collateral or closing resources. Define a funded retry/close/reclaim route before claiming liveness.
