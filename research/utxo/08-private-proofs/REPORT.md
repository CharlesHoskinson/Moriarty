# Private proof verification and certificates for Moriarty on Cardano

Research pass: 2026-10-12. Read-only Moriarty inspection and public primary-source acquisition; no downloaded code, prover, verifier, test, estimator, or ledger transaction was executed. All numerical costs below are producer reports, not reproduced measurements. Sources and immutable acquisition receipts are in `sources.json`. Three repository snapshots were acquired; only the files named here were inspected, not whole repositories.

## Assessment

**A Cardano BLS12-381 Groth16 private-transition verifier is a credible experiment, not demonstrated Moriarty feasibility.** Existing verifiers expose the required pairing primitives and report roughly 2–3 billion execution steps for small statements. The complete Moriarty relation, its integer semantics, statement binding, private witness lifecycle, key governance, and transaction-wide settlement projection have not been ported or measured. Constant-size Groth16 proofs do not imply that constructing or auditing the complete relation is easy.

**The amended public ledger-anchored core is materially easier than recursive PCD.** Moriarty's `openspec/PCD-INTEGRATION-2026-09-11.md`, “Decision adopted” and “Stage graph”, and `openspec/changes/pcd-ledger-anchored-acceptance/design.md` select constrained genesis, immutable audited operation keys, fused step relations, and same-head read/write discipline. On Cardano, consuming the authenticated head output can supply predecessor linearity, subject to unique genesis and exact successor validation. Every accepted public step can be checked directly by Plutus; privacy is then a separate mode. A private mode replaces checks over secret state with a bound ZK relation and retains ledger checks over public effects and head consumption. Neither mode removes liabilities, nonce consumption, deployment audit, oracle policy, or liveness obligations.

**Off-ledger certificates remain a separate feasibility gate.** A batch of independent proofs is not a sequential-history certificate. A hash anchor is not proof verification. The inspected slim Nova route does not check a complete commitment-opening/PCD relation on-chain. IOG reports a recursive Halo2 prototype split across 18 transactions after exceeding resource limits; this does not establish an atomic complete Moriarty certificate import. Current native MSM/expMod improvements justify remeasurement rather than copying 2025 impossibility or headroom conclusions.

## Standards and cryptographic boundary

Reused read-only evidence: `../02-standards/cips` at its recorded commit `b4a593c960f2751fef2ddc8df28bec7b22c68eb5`, and `../02-standards/plutus-core-spec.pdf` (title date 2026-09-14; receipt retained there).

- [CIP-381](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/blob/b4a593c960f2751fef2ddc8df28bec7b22c68eb5/CIP-0381/README.md), frontmatter and “Path to Active”, is **Active** and says included in Chang #1. It supplies opaque G1/G2/Miller-loop types, scalar multiplication, Miller loops and final verification. It is not a SNARK protocol or privacy layer.
- [Plutus Core specification](https://plutus.cardano.intersectmbo.org/resources/plutus-core-spec.pdf), §4.3.6, Notes 4–5, printed pp44–45, specifies G1 48-byte and G2 96-byte compressed encodings; wrong length, unsupported flag/coordinate encoding, non-curve or non-subgroup points fail uncompression. Identity points have supported encodings. Whether a proof/key protocol rejects infinity is an additional policy; infinity rejection is not supplied by merely requesting a valid group element.
- `../02-standards/REPORT.md`, “Protocol and transaction semantics”, documents PV11/batch6 MSM and expMod in the inspected ledger API. The older [CIP-133](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/blob/b4a593c960f2751fef2ddc8df28bec7b22c68eb5/CIP-0133/README.md) still has **Proposed** frontmatter. Distinguish proposal status, implementation/version tables, actual network protocol and the cost model used. The acquired 2026 specification includes MSM, and the inspected Midnight-compatible verifier calls it; do not infer target-chain activation from code alone.
- Jens Groth's [2016 original paper](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1501201/1/Groth_SNARK9.pdf), §3.2, defines field-valued statements, randomized proving, a relation-dependent CRS, and three proof group elements. The paper's mathematical statement is not application integer semantics. Production circuit setup and range/encoding constraints are part of the application obligation. Its verification-cost discussion can precompute the fixed-key pairing; inspected Aiken implementations perform four Miller loops.

## Three pinned repositories: actual code and evidence limits

### R1 — Cardano Foundation BLS

[Repository](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/bls/tree/d0128d81c43665a3d7cafe89a0b379e8e1cdeb09), commit `d0128d81c43665a3d7cafe89a0b379e8e1cdeb09`. Receipt `cf-bls.receipt.json`.

Inspected `aiken/groth16/lib/groth16/verifier.ak` lines120–190 and tests197–306, `aiken/groth16/lib/groth16/batch.ak`, `aiken/groth16/README.md` “Aiken On-Chain Verification Cost Analysis”, `groth16-prover/README.md` implementations6/7/11, and `aiken/nova/{README.md,lib/nova/verifier.ak,lib/nova/types.ak}`.

Observation: `verify` decompresses proof and VK points and evaluates the ordinary Groth16 pairing equation. Public input commitment is `Σ input[i]*IC[i]`, including an explicitly supplied constant wire. `do_compute_v` stops when `remaining<=0`, so extra public inputs are ignored; a missing prefix returns the identity rather than an explicit arity failure. The constant wire is not independently fixed to1 by this API. `scalar.from_int` is used without an explicit canonical input-range check in this function. Do not treat the function's comment that malformed lengths cause pairing failure as a general proof: a circuit/key for a zero commitment may satisfy that equation. A Moriarty wrapper needs exact schema/arity, canonical scalar encoding, fixed constant wire and an immutable approved key.

Producer cost: README reports SimpleExample `1,998,214,009` CPU and `15,293` memory. Its 14M memory denominator and public-input slope are documentation figures, not a current network query or worst-case whole-transaction measurement. Tests include wrong public output, valid-point proof tampering and substituted alpha/beta/IC; malformed lengths/field aliases and full Moriarty effects are not demonstrated by those tests. The deterministic fixture publishes toxic waste and uses zero randomizers: pedagogical parity fixtures, unsuitable as production soundness/privacy evidence. README contains stale cross-file statements (e.g. a Groth16-prover note says batch is off-chain while actual Aiken `batch.ak` exists), so code locators take precedence.

Batch observation: `batch.ak` checks nonempty proof list, outer list count and `r>=2`, then uses powers of a Blake2b challenge and N+3 Miller loops. Its challenge payload contains compressed proofs and alpha/beta/gamma/delta, **not public statements, IC, n_public or an application/domain identifier** (lines45–77). This is a concrete transcript-completeness review obligation; no exploit or security theorem was executed or established here. Batching remains O(N) proof bytes/group work and does not itself bind chronology, identical program semantics, heads, effects or nonce uniqueness.

Critical counterevidence: Nova README and `types.ak` explicitly leave HashPC opening proofs off-chain. `verify_slim_fs` (`verifier.ak` lines143–148) checks sumcheck claim count/round arithmetic and terminal equality, but no commitment opening or bound evaluation of the underlying relation. `verify_predicate_gate` lines197–205 separately compares supplied shape and supplied `final_instance.x` with policy. A **static accepting-path candidate**, not executed: empty `sumcheck_polys`, `sumcheck_claims=[0]`, `claimed_product_at_r=0`, matching `circuit_params`, arbitrary other proof fields, and attacker-chosen `final_instance.x=[issuer_pk_u,issuer_pk_v,current_year,country_root,1]`. Zero rounds return True and the public policy comparison passes; the inspected path never establishes a signed credential witness. Even without this candidate, missing commitment openings prevent treating this as complete on-chain Nova/PCD soundness. It is not acceptable evidence for Moriarty certificates.

## Required binding and privacy obligations — compatibility inference

1. Fix verifier code, key, statement schema, trusted setup provenance, target protocol/cost model, compiler and program digest at genesis; forbid caller-supplied arbitrary keys/configuration. Bind migration to forward-declared approved targets while conserving liabilities, authority and nonce state. A VK hash alone does not audit a circuit or universal property certificate.
2. Public statement must bind instance/network, old authenticated head out-reference/headId/revision/commitment, exact successor commitment/lifecycle/program digest, action and domain, authorization mode/signature digest, outcome nonce consumption, oracle window and **complete effects commitment**. Validator recomputes public effects from actual native transaction inputs/outputs/mint/fees and checks that digest. A prover-provided effect hash detached from ScriptContext proves nothing about settlement.
3. Gross authority must cover entire protected wallet inputs, not just economic transfer amounts net of change. Include sponsored fees/minimum ADA/collateral policy explicitly. Commit liabilities, work bounds, rounding and residual debt into the proved successor. Field arithmetic needs integer/no-wraparound constraints and bounds; bytes/hash packing must be injective over the declared schema, not an unchecked256-bit hash modulo Fr.
4. Require exact list and byte lengths, canonical scalars and permitted points; reject missing/extra inputs, negative values, x+Fr aliases, wrong curves/endianness, infinity where prohibited, substituted keys/SRS/circuit metadata and malformed/trailing proof material. Establish decompilation/semantic correspondence separately from cryptographic verification.
5. Consuming a state UTXO prevents exact-head double spend; cross-head outcome signatures require durable consumed-nonce state/nullifiers. Reference reads do not consume authority. Split/join/import paths cannot reset nonces or obligations; certificates bind both endpoints and the specific relation/key/domain and bounded segment length.
6. Commitments hide values only under their scheme and sufficient secret randomness; public amounts/assets/addresses/timing still leak. Groth16 ZK protects the circuit witness, not all native Cardano settlement. Recipient-keyed encrypted openings, witness/nonce/obligation delivery and availability acknowledgments are separate requirements. Losing witness data can make a valid head unspendable; proving knowledge today does not prove future counterparty availability or permission to see it. A remote prover learns secrets unless its trust/isolation protocol prevents that.
