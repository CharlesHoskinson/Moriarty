## Main assessment

**Compatibility inference:** Cardano eUTXO is a credible semantic target for Moriarty’s latest **ledger-anchored certified state machine**. The strongest foundation is not the vague claim that eUTXO supports smart contracts. It is the explicit construction of initialized, uniquely threaded state machines, and the extraction of their ledger traces into abstract machine traces. This fits constrained genesis plus consumption of a particular head and validation of the successor. It does **not** establish Moriarty compilation correctness, native proof verification feasibility, resource fit, private witness/history confidentiality, or release readiness.

**Repository observation:** The September 11 amendment selects a fused authorization/transition/effect/intent/invariant relation, immutable audited operation keys, and history by induction over accepted steps. Recursion is reserved for bounded off-ledger certificates/imports. The amendment and design remain specified-only, with their second Charter reviewer pending. Consequently a UTXO backend comparison should target this design, not require per-step recursive proofs based on stale README/wiki diagrams. Sources: local `openspec/PCD-INTEGRATION-2026-09-11.md`, sections Decision adopted, Stage graph, Follow-ups outside this revision, Review record; `openspec/changes/pcd-ledger-anchored-acceptance/design.md`, Interfaces and Trust boundaries.

## Mapping to the current Moriarty design

**Compatibility inference, specified-only:** A consuming state UTXO can represent a head. Its datum contains the versioned head record and commitments; its unique policy/token identifies the initialized instance. A validator derived from the fused relation checks authority, transition, all financial effects, intent caps/goals, residual obligations, work bounds and exactly the permitted continuation. The thread-token policy constrains genesis and final token disposition. Script identity plus immutable policy rules play a role analogous to audited immutable operation keys; migration requires a forward-declared allowed script/program digest and explicit liability/nonce transfer.

This mapping needs separate rules for split/join and multiple heads. Spending one head does not consume an outcome-intent nonce across other heads: an instance-wide authority state or another enforceable nonce design is required. A script hash binds validator bytes, but a Moriarty program digest also binds Core, bounds, certificates and toolchain; correspondence between them must be established. NFT presence alone does not prove the complete head datum or financial effects. Neither historical token-provenance theorems nor native assets provide a recursive verifier, private state, private handoff or authenticated off-ledger certificate import.

## Prioritized experiments — all specified-only, none run

1. **Initialized state-thread relation:** Formally instantiate one bounded loan CEM with thread policy, constrained initial debt/authority, complete repayment effects and residual liabilities. Prove/extract invariant preservation under the target era’s validity relation. Reject initial intermediate states, mint duplicates, recreated/burned live threads, wrong program digest and uncontrolled migration.
2. **Consumption and replay matrix:** Construct two transactions consuming the same head, then verify at most one successful transition; replay a signed outcome against another head and after split/join; forge the same datum without the thread token. Require revision, instance, deployment and network bindings; preserve instance-wide consumed nonce history.
3. **Accounting correspondence:** Compare source expected effects to target ledger inputs/outputs/mint/fees. Include the existing 100,000-A input / 10,000-A swap / 90,000-A change case; whole input remains gross debit. Add ADA fees, collateral-only failure, minimum-output funding, token policy substitution, partial loan repayment and residual obligations. Verify no second contract can reuse one payment output to satisfy a separate obligation without explicit attribution.
4. **Compilation and resource boundary:** Pin current era, Plutus language/cost model, compiler and parameter snapshot. Measure fused validator and any required cryptographic proof verifier independently, with malformed/tampered proof controls. No proof support or resource feasibility follows from these papers alone.
5. **Privacy and certificate scope:** Document which head/effect/history data becomes public and what remains committed. Specify availability and import trust assumptions. Reject any claim that datum hashes themselves supply confidential ledger semantics.
