- **BENCH-PASS**: a gate passed on a bench circuit, a stand-in contract or a separate revision. It closes no freeze row.
- **INTEGRATED-PASS**: the done-when test passes on the integration branch, with a `scripts/doctorwatson check` receipt where an OpenSpec task exists.
- **OWNER**: the row waits on an owner ruling listed below. The loop does not pick.
