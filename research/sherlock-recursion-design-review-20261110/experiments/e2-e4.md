- On the C6 digest leaf the check adds 27,736 rows: 2,637,481 to 2,665,217, k=22 in both.
- Standalone: the inputs alone are 28,141 rows (k=15); with the check, 55,877 rows (k=16).
- Every pre lane over four programs (`fact3`, `literal`, `add`, `list123`) is classified: 27,734 lanes, of which 27,723 are constant zero and 11 are non-zero; none are unexplained.
- Fuel changes pre lanes 26 and 27 and prefix lane 211; the stack limit changes pre lane 22 and prefix lane 213. The check therefore binds both.
- Non-proving checks: the canonical genesis at `h = 0` is accepted, a forged genesis at `h = 0` is refused, and a forged state at `h = 3` is accepted (the check is gated on `h_pre == 0` by design).
- Genesis prefix lengths differ by program (271, 290, 403 and 439 lanes), so carriers are not padded to `Bounds` (a GP-1 gap, unchanged).
