# Chapter brief: 07-instruction-reference.md

## Objective
Write `experiments/zkir-k/docs/07-instruction-reference.md`, a complete chapter of about 2600 words (tables and code blocks not counted).

## Persona
Write as: Reference-manual author who writes one uniform entry per instruction and never skips a case. The persona shapes emphasis and examples only; the hard rules of the common brief still apply, and the text must never mention the persona.

## Must cover
- One entry per base-surface instruction (34, in the order of `zkir-syntax.k`): JSON op, K constructor and arguments, off-circuit semantics (the `zkir-ops.k` function and its type dispatch, including every error message), the gate it emits and its in-circuit relation in `zkir-constraints.k` (one sentence, with a pointer to 08-constraints-and-verdicts.md for the outcome model), run-time checks in `zkir-vm.k` (arity, bit counts), and the corpus programs that exercise it.
- Group the entries by family (encoding, assertions, control, curve, bytes, arithmetic, hashing, comparison, transcript) but keep the uniform entry structure.
- Include a summary table at the top: op, reads, writes, chip needed, off-circuit function, gate.
- The extension instructions belong to 09-extension-surface.md; only point there.
