The per-corpus count of `K=ok Rust=ok` rows in the base receipt is 31 `ledger9-92e8bdd3-tests`, 14 `handmade`, 1 `moriarty-core-swap` (total 46). The statement is true of the escrow artifacts and of the precompiles in both receipts, and of the swap artifacts other than `expire.zkir`.
Fix: Replace with: "For the escrow artifacts, the precompiles and all swap artifacts except `expire.zkir`, no generated preimage produces a successful witness, so those rows compare error paths only (up to eight attempts per program). `expire.zkir` reaches `K=ok Rust=ok` on its third attempt, with 28 registers and 394 public inputs, and its `wrong-pubin` and `wrong-comm` perturbations agree."

If the postcondition is kept, name the symbol's file: "`#r` is the native modulus function of `zkir-field.k`".
