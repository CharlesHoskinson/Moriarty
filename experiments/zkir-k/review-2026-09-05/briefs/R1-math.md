# Review R1: fields, curves, values, encodings and hashes

Files: semantics/zkir-field.k, zkir-curves.k, zkir-values.k, zkir-constants.k
(generated by tools/gen_constants.py), zkir-hash.k, zkir-sha512-constants.k;
tests tools/unit_values.py, tools/unit_hash.py, tools/zkir_values.py.

Questions to answer:
- Are the moduli, curve constants, generators and the Poseidon / SVDW constants
  exactly those of midnight-curves 0.3.1 and midnight-circuits 7.2.4? Check at
  least one constant of each kind against the source, not against the tests.
- Is every group-law formula complete on its domain (Edwards addition with the
  chosen d, Weierstrass doubling with y = 0, identity handling in ecMul)?
- Do encodeValue / decodeValue implement `encode_offcircuit` and
  `from_public_input` exactly, including the limb batching for LOG2_BASE 64 with
  4 limbs and 51 with 5 limbs, the identity encoding of Weierstrass points, the
  Jubjub scalar 252-bit rule, and the panics of the Bytes32 decoder?
- Is Tonelli-Shanks correct for all inputs, and is the parity convention of
  jubjubFromXY the one JubjubAffine::from_bytes uses (including the ZIP 216
  rule for u = 0)?
- Poseidon: does the plain-round implementation match permutation_cpu with its
  round-skip batching for every input length, including 0, 1 and > 2 elements?
  Does the variable-length sponge in hashToCurve add the padding element and
  squeeze twice as the Rust does?
- SHA-256, SHA-512 and Keccak-256: padding, endianness, multi-block messages,
  empty message.
- Anything that fails for adversarial but well-formed inputs (values >= modulus,
  non-canonical encodings, empty lists).
