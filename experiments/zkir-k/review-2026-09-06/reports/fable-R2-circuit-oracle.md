# R2 review: the circuit oracle and the comparison table

VERDICT: WARNING — the oracle builds the circuit the crate proves and the two 2026-09-06c receipts are sound as run, but the comparison table and the taint walk name outcomes the crate does not produce on paths the corpus never reaches, one cell is permissive enough to hide a mis-ordered verdict, and the prove-and-verify receipt is presented without the three residuals that separate it from ledger acceptance.

## Findings

### 1. Major — D3 names constraint failures where the crate panics or misaligns

Files: `experiments/zkir-k/plan-iter3/circuit-comparison-table.md:141-147` (the `C` bullet of D3); `experiments/zkir-k/tools/circuit_compare.py:512-523` (`bytes32_from_low_high` in `Walk.step`), `:363-366` (`inv`), `:376-381` (`less_than`).

What the harness says. D3's `C` bullet lists "`less_than` operands above the padded bound, `bytes32_from_low_high` with byte 31 set or a high above 255, ... or an `inv` whose operand became 0" as constraint failures. The walk records a `C` violation for a high operand at or above 256 (`circuit_compare.py:518-521`) and returns `C` at the end of the walk; for `less_than` and `inv` it raises `Undecided`, so only the table text is wrong there.

What the crate does (verified by running the oracle on hand-built programs with `--inject`, see section 5):

- `bytes32_from_low_high` with high = 256: `std.convert(layouter, &high)` to `AssignedByte` (`ir_vm.rs:1148`) panics in the known-witness hint, "Trying to convert Fq(0x...100) to AssignedByte in-circuit". Outcome `X`, not `C`. With low = 2^248 (byte 31 set) the outcome is `C` as the table says.
- `less_than` with an operand at or above 2^padded: `std.lower_than` (`ir_vm.rs:966`) panics, "Trying to convert Fq(0x10) to an AssignedBounded less than ...". Outcome `X`.
- `inv` of 0: `inv_incircuit` assigns the hint's inverse-or-zero and `mem_insert` (`ir_vm.rs:761-781`) compares it with the witness inverse. Outcome `W`, not `C`.

Why it matters. The walk's `C` for a high above 255 is a wrong prediction: a corpus program that feeds a declared Native input to `bytes32_from_low_high` as the high operand, injected past 255, would be reported as a blocking "comparison outside the table" although the crate behaves as the model intends (the relation is unsatisfiable; K reports `violated`, the crate aborts the prover). Today no injection reaches these paths because every injection is `old + 1` (finding 3), so the receipts do not show it.

Fix. In `Walk.step` return `f'X: {at}: high operand {v} above 255, the byte conversion hint panics'` for `bytes32_from_low_high` (and give `predict_injection` a message class "AssignedByte"); decide `less_than` above the padded bound as `X` with class "AssignedBounded" and `inv` of 0 as `W`; correct the three entries of D3, and add the two panic classes to the "What the crate cannot distinguish" list next to D1c.

### 2. Major — the `ok/violated` cell and the "first non-holding verdict" rule are too permissive

Files: `circuit-comparison-table.md:35-38, 69, 78-107`; `circuit_compare.py:98-103` (`first_bad`), `:207-212` (`expected_preimage_cell`, generic `violated` → `{C, W, X}` with no message class).

What the harness says. The row is decided by the first non-holding verdict in emission order "because `MockProver::run` stops at the first instruction that fails", and a `violated` that is neither `commGate` nor `fromCoordinates` accepts any of `C`, `W`, `X`, with no message check.

What the crate does. A `violated` realised as a constraint failure does not stop synthesis; only a `mem_insert`/`pi_push` misalignment or a panic does. Every K `synthErr` (missing arm, rejected type, alignment option, missing chip, width) is witness-independent and fires inside `optimal_k`'s unknown-witness synthesis (`midnight-zk-stdlib-2.3.5/src/lib.rs:2130-2148`, `cost_model` → `DevAssembly::run(..).unwrap()`) before `MockProver::run` is ever called. So a run whose verdicts are `violated` at instruction 3 (say `constrain_eq`) and `synthErr` at instruction 7 (say an `option` alignment) is row `ok/violated`, expected `{C, W, X}`, and the crate's `X` from the alignment option is counted as agreement although the violated relation was never exercised and the panic has nothing to do with it. Symmetrically, an `X` with an unrelated message (a chip panic on a program the K chip check missed) would pass any `violated` row.

Why it matters. This is the one cell that lets a real disagreement pass as agreement. It is latent: both receipts report `oracle 2: 0 successful K runs with a non-holding gate` (`evidence/zkir-k-circuit-differential-92e8bdd3-2026-09-06c.txt:832`, `...ext-2ffe2d1-2026-09-06c.txt:919`), so only the divergence cases, which have one non-holding verdict each, reach the row.

Fix. In `expected_preimage_cell`, take a `synthErr` anywhere in `all_verdicts` before any `violated` (they always win, witness-independently), and keep its message class. For `violated`, derive the realisation from the gate: `commGate` → `C`; `fromCoordinates` → the existing decompression logic; `bytes32_from_low_high` high → `X` class "AssignedByte", low → `C`; `less_than` → `X` class "AssignedBounded"; `inv` → `W`; `constrain_*`, `assert`, `piGate`/`guardGate` booleanity → `C`; everything else `W`. Reserve the three-way `{C, W, X}` for gates the table has not classified and print those as `N/C` rather than `AGREE`.

### 3. Major — the injection columns never exercise the guarded inputs or any boundary of the walk

Files: `circuit_compare.py:591-639` (`choose_injections`): `inject-input` takes `doc['inputs']` only (`:614`); the computed list skips `public_input`/`private_input` outputs (`:618-619`); every value is `(old + 1) % r` (`:605, :630`). `circuit-comparison-table.md:126-129` claims D3 covers "declared input, or `public_input` / `private_input` output".

What this leaves untested. (a) A guard-on `public_input`/`private_input` register is never injected, although it is the second of the two places the circuit reads the witness (`ir_vm.rs:978-1001`) and the one every Moriarty circuit is made of (`%t.*` ledger reads). I ran one by hand (`handmade/transcripts.zkir`, `%p1` 2 → 3): the walk predicts `W` at `pi_push` of the following `impact` and the oracle agrees; the harness should produce that row itself. (b) With `old + 1` no injection reaches a range, booleanity or hint branch: `constrain_bits`, `constrain_to_boolean`, `reconstitute_field` bounds, `bytes32_from_low_high` byte 31, `less_than` bound, native `from_coordinates` parity, `inv` of 0, non-boolean guard or bit. The receipts' `inject: C x4` rows are all commitment or `constrain_eq` (`...92e8bdd3-2026-09-06c.txt:14, 253, 262, 270`) and the `inject: A` rows are all "no consumer". The claimed D1 realisations for `from_coordinates` (`W` on a parity flip, `C` on the same parity, `X` on a bad `y`) and the walk's `C` for `constrain_bits`/`constrain_to_boolean` are right (verified by hand, section 5) but rest on no receipt.

Fix. Add the guard-on transcript registers to the `inject-input` candidates (a fourth kind `inject-transcript`), and for each chosen register try a second value chosen by the first consumer the walk meets: `1 << bits` for `constrain_bits`/`div_mod`/`reconstitute`, `2` for a boolean consumer, `256` and `1 << 248` for `bytes32_from_low_high`, `x + 1` and `x + 2` for `from_coordinates`, `0` for `inv`. Record the extra rows under D3 so the cells `inject: X` and the range sub-cells are hit at least once per surface.

### 4. Major — the prove-and-verify receipt is not ledger acceptance, and the residuals are not stated

Files: `tools/circuit-oracle/ledger-92e8bdd3/src/main.rs:442-471` (`--prove` verifies `pis` returned by `Zkir::prove`); `experiments/zkir-k/docs/16-compilation-target-contract.md:31, 117`; `evidence/zkir-k-provability-2026-09-06b.txt:1-16`; `experiments/zkir-k/plan-iter3/m4-contexts.md:120-127`.

What the oracle does. `--prove` runs `keygen` with the file provider, `prove` with a fixed seed, then `VerifierKey::verify(&PARAMS_VERIFIER, &proof, pis)` where `pis` is `preproc.pis` handed back by `prove` (`ir.rs:82-103`). That is the same call the ledger makes (`ledger/src/structure.rs:503` → `transient-crypto/src/proofs.rs:554-566` → `midnight_zk_stdlib::verify` with the `nb_public_inputs` check at `lib.rs:2030`), with the same embedded verifier parameters.

What the ledger does differently. (a) The statement is not the prover's: `ContractCall::public_inputs` (`ledger/src/verify.rs:1956-1970`) is `[binding_input(address, entry point, transcripts, binding commitment), communication_commitment, field_repr of the guaranteed transcript, field_repr of the fallible transcript]`. The proof is accepted only if the circuit's pushes reproduce that vector. For the generated preimages the harness computes `public_transcript_inputs` from the K generation run, so the equality with a runtime transcript is never tested; for the seven M4 contexts it is, through `Public transcript input mismatch` (m4-contexts.md:100-103), except for the binding input, which is 0. (b) The ledger pushes the communication commitment unconditionally, so a program with `do_communications_commitment: false` can never satisfy a ledger statement, whatever its proof; every crate test program in the "prove-and-verify" section of the receipt (lines 184-189) is such a program, and chapter 16 records `commitment` as `info` only. (c) The verifier key is regenerated by the oracle; the deployed key of a contract is the one compactc produced, and no key files exist under `experiments/moriarty-*/output` to compare against. `vk_match` (`main.rs:404`) only checks `keygen_vk` against `keygen` in the same process.

Why it matters. Chapter 16 says "keygen, proving and verification with real parameters are the receipts of `tools/provability.py`" and the plan's M5b says "prove provability rather than assume it". A reader will take "13 proved and verified" for "13 proofs the ledger accepts". The proof-system part is the same; the statement, the commitment requirement and the key identity are not checked.

Fix. State the three residuals in the receipt header and in chapter 16 tier three; make `commitment` a failed tier-one obligation (or a distinct `ledger.commitment` fact) for a program presented as a contract circuit without the commitment; if the compactc keys of the two Moriarty contracts can be produced, add a `--vk FILE` comparison of the serialised verifier key to `--keygen`.

### 5. Minor — `--pis` and `--instance` can produce an `accepted` the real verifier would reject, and a shortened `--pis` is `C`, not `W`

Files: `main.rs:19-28` (doc comment), `:346-351`, `:520-543`; spike receipt `evidence/zkir-k-circuit-oracle-spike-2026-09-06.txt` finding 5 ("Perturbing pis or binding_input in Preprocessed is always caught earlier by pi_push").

Verified on `corpus/divergence/f03_guard_uncoupled.zkir` (pis = `[42]`): `--instance '["42","5"]'` and `--pis '["42","5"]'` are `accepted` (the extra instance cell is constrained by nothing; `pi_push` compares only while `idx < preproc.pis.len()`, `ir_vm.rs:791`), whereas `midnight_zk_stdlib::verify` rejects a statement whose length differs from `vk.nb_public_inputs`; `--pis '[]'` is `constraint-failure` (the instance cell is `Padding`, read as 0, `midnight-proofs-0.8.2/src/dev/mod.rs:826-840`), not a witness-consistency error. Neither harness uses these flags, so no receipt is affected. Fix: after `MockProver::run`, compare `instance.len()` with the number of pushes (`pre.pis.len()` on an unperturbed witness) and report `instance-length-mismatch`; correct the doc comment and spike finding 5.

### 6. Minor — D1(b) and `_native_from_coordinates_cell` are wrong for the foreign Edwards chip

### 7. Minor — the `panic` row accepts any panic

File: `circuit_compare.py:193-194` (`Expect({X}, None, 'panic: X (D10)')`).

K's `panic` status has two message classes (short transcript, Bytes32 decoder assertion, D10) but the cell checks none, so a K `panic` on a preimage where the crate's `preprocess` succeeds and a chip panics in synthesis would agree. Oracle 1 (`diff_test.compare`) does compare the message class on the same row, which is why this is minor. Fix: carry "out of range" / "assertion `left == right` failed" as the message class, as D2 does.

### 8. Minor — three undecided walk cases can be decided, and one is decided wrongly in the docs

Files: `circuit_compare.py:316-321` (`bool_check`), `:416-418` (tainted `y`), `:493-497` (non-boolean guard); receipts `...92e8bdd3-2026-09-06c.txt:837`, `...ext-2ffe2d1-2026-09-06c.txt:924-925` (three `N/C` rows, all `cond_select` with `%bit` 1 → 2, oracle `C`).

Observed: `cond_select`, `not` and `impact` with a tainted bit or guard of 2 are all `constraint-failure` with two equality failures and no misalignment, so the `convert` hint maps the non-boolean value to the honest branch and only the booleanity constraint fails. A tainted `y` into native `from_coordinates` is decided by the walk's own `jubjub_from_xy(x, y')`: `None` → `X` ("Affine coordinates must satisfy Jubjub equation", verified twice), `Some(p)` → `W` because `p` differs from the witness point. Deciding these removes the only `N/C` rows of both receipts. For the boolean cases confirm the hint's mapping in `midnight-circuits` `convert` before relying on it.

## Answers to the brief's questions, where not covered above

1. Same circuit, same instance. `MidnightCircuit::new(ir, known(instance), known(pre), Some(k))` with `k = optimal_k(ir)` (`main.rs:516, 543`) and `MockProver::run(&circuit, vec![vec![], instance])` (`:548`) match `midnight_zk_stdlib::prove` (`lib.rs:1984-2013`: `&[com_inst, &pi]`, `Some(pk.k)`), `used_chips` enters through `params()` (`lib.rs:1853-1855`) and the lookup tables are loaded at the end of `synthesize` (`lib.rs:1900-1940`). `Relation::circuit` ignores `_instance` (`ir_vm.rs:709`), so `--instance` changes only the column MockProver checks, as documented. `--inject` writes `Preprocessed.memory` only (`main.rs:338`), the same struct `prove_unchecked` takes (`ir.rs:993-1010`); `--binding-input` is caught at `pi_push` index 0 (verified). `k`: `optimal_k` on both the MockProver path and `Zkir::k` (`ir.rs:105-106`), `setup_vk` takes `params.max_k()` of the file `bls_midnight_2p{k}`, and the receipt checks `pk_k == k` on all 122 keyed programs.
2. The six outcomes. The `witness-consistency-error` class rests on the tracing ERROR event whose text only `mem_insert` and `pi_push` emit (`ir_vm.rs:773, 791`; the crate imports `tracing::error`), captured by a global layer; any other `MockProver::run` error is `synthesis-error`; `verify()` returns `Err(Vec<VerifyFailure>)` and never panics on a constraint (`dev/mod.rs:923`). `MockProver::run` synthesises the known witness twice (`RowSizer::min_k` at `dev/mod.rs:824`, then the real run), which is harmless. `catch_unwind` hides nothing: the release profile unwinds (`panic = "abort"` is set only under `[profile.wasm]`, `ledger-92e8bdd3/Cargo.toml:61-66`), a panic in a rayon worker is re-raised in the caller, and the hook stores the first message. Panics before the hook is installed (program or preimage loading) exit 101 and the harness reports `load-error`, which disagrees with every expectation, so nothing is absorbed.
3. Not-comparable declarations. D8 (`unsupported`) → `None` → `N/C` is justified: no relation is modelled. D7 (`unknown`) → empty set → every outcome `DISAGREE` is correctly strict. `undecided (D3)` → `N/C` is honest, and the three rows it produced are decidable (finding 8).
4. The taint walk. Verified by hand on: declared input into arithmetic (`W`), into the commitment (`C`), into `constrain_eq` (`C`), into `constrain_bits` and `constrain_to_boolean` (`C`), into `div_mod_power_of_two` (`W`), into `jubjub_scalar_from_native` with `s + r_J` (`A`, the circuit reduces with `div_rem`, `ir_instructions/encode.rs:227-240`, and the walk's `% RJ` is right), into `assert` as 2 (`A`, finding 2 of the review), guard 1 → 0 of an `impact` (`W` at `pi_push`), guard-on `public_input` into an `impact` (`W`), native `from_coordinates` `x + 1` (`W`), `x + 2` (`C`), `y + 1` (`X`), `bytes32_from_low_high` low with byte 31 (`C`). The four bypass paths are as stated (`ir_vm.rs:1123, 1129, 1137-1138, 1149`, `memory.insert`), and at 2ffe2d1 `reverse` joins them while `slice`, `nth`, `concat`, `load_constant`, `and`, `or`, `xor` use `mem_insert`, matching `BYPASS`. Jubjub `from_xy` decompresses from `y` and the parity of `x` and checks only `v == y` (`midnight-circuits-7.2.4/src/ecc/curves.rs:120-138`), and `into_subgroup` panics rather than clearing the cofactor (`:69-71`), so `jubjub_from_xy` is a faithful model. The one wrong prediction is finding 1.
5. Keygen and prove. `--keygen` and `--prove` use the same provider the crate's tests use, `k` is `optimal_k` by construction on both sides, `pk_k` and `vk_match` are checked; the residuals to ledger acceptance are finding 4.

## Coverage gaps

- Guard-on `public_input`/`private_input` registers: never injected (finding 3).
- Boundary values: no injection reaches a range constraint, a booleanity constraint, a parity or decompression hint, an inverse of zero, or a hash chunk overflow (`check_alignment`, `circuit_compare.py:530-549`); the cells `inject: X` (D1c) and the `W`/`C` sub-cells of D5 are hit by no receipt.
- Rows `ok/violated` and `ok/synthErr` are reached only by the divergence cases, each with one non-holding verdict; the ordering rule of finding 2 has no test.
- The extension-only instructions carry Bool, Byte and Bytes values, so no Native injection taints them; the walk's `Undecided` for `slice`/`nth`/`concat` is therefore never exercised either way.
- `--pis`, `--binding-input` and a length-changing `--instance` are used by no tool (finding 5).
- Programs without a commitment are proved and verified but can never be ledger statements (finding 4b); no Moriarty circuit is proved against its deployed key.
- `wrong-pubin` and `wrong-comm` land in `P` on every corpus program; there is no perturbation that keeps `preprocess` happy and reaches `C` on a real program other than `--instance`, so the `commGate` `C` realisation is tested by f05 alone.
