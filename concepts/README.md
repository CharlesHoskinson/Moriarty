# concepts

Language and system designs held for deeper review before they become controlling.

A concept here is **specified-only**. It has not been accepted by a milestone, no evidence
closes any U predicate on its behalf, and the controlling documents remain
[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md), [docs/MORIARTY-CONSOLIDATED-DESIGN.md](../docs/MORIARTY-CONSOLIDATED-DESIGN.md)
and [docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md](../docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md). A concept
graduates by being reviewed, repaired and then adopted into those documents — not by
sitting here.

`deliverables/` holds requested outputs and dated reports; `concepts/` holds designs that
are still being argued with.
