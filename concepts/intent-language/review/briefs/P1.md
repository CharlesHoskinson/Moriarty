
## YOUR LENS: P1 — type system and semantics

You own the type layer and the metatheory. Attack it.

- **The type system.** `Asset` with domain/issuer/symbol/repr; `Qty<A>` unsigned vs `Signed<A>` for positions; `Price<B,Q,s>`; `Share<P>`; `Obligation` with principal/accrued/outstanding and a `ClaimClass`; `Encumbrance`; linear `Receipt`; `Instant`/`Duration`/`Window` domain-qualified by clock. Are these the right sorts? Is the unsigned-balance / signed-position split coherent, or does it leak? Does `Share<P>` need a pool identity in the type, and is `mulDiv` with a rounding role enough to make vault and LP accounting well-typed? What breaks when two pools share an asset?
- **Φ, the predicate language.** Totality, decidability, termination, and the claim that every clause is statically costed. Is the grammar in §4 actually total — consider `mulDiv` by zero, `min`/`max` over empty sets, `fresh` when `observedAt` is in the future, comparisons across different assets or domains. Is the deliberate absence of negation and unbounded quantification enough for decidability, and does it cost expressiveness the categories need? Is Φ closed under the substitution that hole-filling performs?
- **The anchoring rule as a typing rule.** `Evidence ∈ {anchored, imported of Policy, attested of (k,n)}`, where a position requiring `anchored` rejects the others at compile time. Is this a subtyping relation, an effect system, or an indexed type? Is it sound — can an imported fact launder itself into an anchored position through a hole, through `min`/`max`, through an escrow's residual, or through an Episode edge? This is the design's central honesty claim; try to break it.
- **Monotone completion.** §7 claims the compiler can check that guarantee clauses are antitone in every hole by a polarity check. Is that true for the grammar as given? Consider a hole under `mulDiv`, under `k_of_n`, under `fresh`, on both sides of a cross-multiplied comparison, and in an escrow `refund` predicate. Give a counterexample if you can find one — a filling that satisfies the syntactic check and weakens a guarantee.
- **Linearity and consumption.** Receipts are linear, spending permission affine, encumbrances linear-and-seizable. Is there a coherent substructural story, or three ad-hoc rules? How is linearity enforced across an Episode's DAG, including a join where both branches consume the same receipt?
- **Conservation and the liability/supply separation.** Per `(domain, asset)`, Σ balance deltas = supply delta. Does the type system actually prevent debt from being counted as supply? Does the obligation roll-forward compose with the encumbrance rules under `enforce`?
- **Metatheory to demand.** State the theorems this design would need — preservation, progress, conservation as an invariant, monotonicity of completion, non-laundering of evidence — and say which are plausibly provable in K, which need something stronger, and which are false as stated.

Where the design gives a rule informally, write the rule formally enough to check it, and say whether it holds.
