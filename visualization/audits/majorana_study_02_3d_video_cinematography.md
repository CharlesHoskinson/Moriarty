## 1. Multi-Scale Camera Dive Choreography (`Macro Package` $\rightarrow$ `Meso Interposer` $\rightarrow$ `Micro Topological Core`)

In Microsoft’s *"Introducing Majorana 1"* (`Q4xCR20Dh1E`) and the *"How the Majorana 1 chip works"* sequence, the central cinematographic challenge is identical to Sherlock’s: **how to transport the viewer across orders of magnitude—from a palm-sized monolithic processor carrier down into a recessed central silicon well and right up to sub-millimeter active structures—in a single continuous camera move without disorienting perspective distortion or geometry clipping.**

### 1.1 The Three Spatial Tiers of the Majorana 1 Dive

1. **Tier 1 — Macro Package (Palm-Sized Cryogenic Carrier, $\sim 35\text{–}45\text{ mm}$):**
   - **Framing & Lens:** 3/4 studio hero perspective (`FOV 30°–34°`, equivalent to a 55mm–65mm full-frame macro lens) with the chip resting on a quiet stage.
   - **Visual Anchors:** Perimeter gold contact pads, front call tiles, and the outer boundary of the multi-layer interposer establish the outer silhouette.
2. **Tier 2 — Meso Interposer & Radial Gold Trace Fanout ($\sim 4\text{–}12\text{ mm}$ Bore Approach):**
   - **Optical Leading Lines:** As the camera initiates its dive, it glides over the radial trace fanout toward the central recessed die well.
   - **Crane-Over-the-Lip Trajectory:** The camera converges horizontally inside the central bore opening before descending past the 45° diamond-cut aperture chamfer.
3. **Tier 3 — Micro Topological Core (Recessed Die Well & Split-Arc / Wafer Chamber):**
   - **Gallery Framing:** Once inside the aperture rim, the camera settles inside the hollow 3D chamber (`pos: [0.38, -0.44, 0.42]`, `lookAt: [0.0, -1.15, 0.0]`) clear of the wafer bevel lips while framing the continuation wafers, open-bore `Control` stator, and 39-rule floor.

## 2. Pacing, Camera Inertia & Lighting Reveals

1. **Smooth Damped Camera Transitions**:
   - Spherical interpolation with target-lead orientation turns the camera gaze into the central well as it descends into the hollow chamber.
2. **Low Fill-to-Key Contrast (`HemisphereLight 0.90` vs `KeyLight 2.60`)**:
   - Crisp `2048×2048` shadow terminators (`d = 2.8`) across the recessed well lip and 12 split-arc rings.
3. **Selective Emissive Hierarchy (Dark Field, Luminous Active Frontier)**:
   - Dark sapphire-slate well and channels (`0x161d29` / `0x18202c`) make the active frontier half-arc (`frames[0]`), 10-sector `Control` stator tooth, and rising Unwind Result Orb pop with zero visual clutter.
