# Sherlock Visualization & Performance EARS Requirements Index

All 40 atomic EARS clauses (`SVP-001`–`SVP-040`) and their `WHEN`/`THEN` scenarios are normative within this OpenSpec package at [spec.md](specs/sherlock-visualization-and-performance/spec.md). Every optimization requirement is subject to the global invariant that **visual, material, lighting, shadow, and CEK semantic trace fidelity SHALL remain 100% indistinguishable from or superior to the unoptimized baseline**. Existing `UIREQ-001`–`035` and `SCON-001`–`025` interpreter requirements remain unchanged.
