# Soundness Review Report (Round 4): E5 Wrap In-Circuit D-9 Decoder (`lane-src/decode.rs`)

## Executive Summary

This report documents the Round 4 repair and re-verification of the Quint formal model (`qa/gemini-quint/e5-decode-r4/decode.qnt` and `qa/gemini-quint/e5-decode-r4/decode_test.qnt`) for the Sherlock co-processor E5 wrap in-circuit D-9 decoder (`decode::decode` in `lane-src/decode.rs`, unchanged since Round 3) against the stock native reference (`iface::decode` in `lane-src/iface.rs`) over the BLS12-381 scalar field $\mathbb{F}_r$ ($\lceil \log_2 r \rceil = 255$).

### What Changed from Round 3 to Round 4

1. **Field Modulus and Limb Parameters (`base-lead/decode.qnt`):**
   - Round 3 used `FR_MOD = 359`, which was below `SLOT_FIELD_LIM = 512` ($2^9$). Consequently, `in_bound(x, 512)` accepted every element of $\mathbb{F}_{359}$, making the Step 7 active-slot offset range checks `range(d1, 2^9)` and `range(d2, 2^9)` (`decode.rs:582, 585`) vacuous: a negative difference such as $t - \text{poff} = -1 \equiv 358 \pmod{359}$ passed `in_bound(358, 512)`.
   - Building on `base-lead/decode.qnt`, Round 4 uses the prime `FR_MOD = 2053` (more than twice the largest range bound `SLOT_FIELD_LIM = 512`), `LIMB_BASE = 16` ($16 < \text{BB\_P} = 17$ and $16^3 = 4096 > \text{FR\_MOD}$, matching $2^{30} < P_{\text{BB}}$ and $2^{270} > r$), `R1_LIMBS = [4, 0, 8]` ($4 + 0 \cdot 16 + 8 \cdot 256 = 2052 = \text{FR\_MOD} - 1$), and 12-bit modular exponentiation in `pow_mod`.
2. **Resolution of Mutant 1 (`mut_free_mult`):**
   - In Round 3, the counterexample `PW_BAD_FREE_MULT` / `WIT_BAD_FREE_MULT` passed `mut_free_mult` only because `FR_MOD = 359 < 512` allowed slot 6 to hold entry 6 (`PATH(1)` with $\text{poff}_6 = 7$), giving $d_1 = 6 - 7 = -1 \equiv 358 < 512$.
   - In the repaired field (`FR_MOD = 2053`), `mut_free_mult(PW_BAD_FREE_MULT, WIT_BAD_FREE_MULT)` is `false` (and becomes `true` only when `check_m_range` is also disabled).
   - As proved in Section 3 below and verified by an exhaustive pure check over the finite slot-assignment attack domain in `decode_test.qnt`, **`mut_free_mult` is an equivalent mutant when `check_m_range` and all other constraints are kept**: subset membership from the slot-to-entry lookup together with `0 <= t - poff < perms` (`check_m_range`) and the disjoint partition $\bigsqcup_{\text{perms}_i > 0} [\text{poff}_i, \text{poff}_i + \text{perms}_i) = [0, \text{np})$ already uniquely pins every active slot $t < \text{np}$ to its honest entry $i$ and forces each claimable entry $i$ to appear on exactly $\text{perms}_i$ active slots. Thus the fixed multiplicities $\text{perms}_i$ in `r2` (`decode.rs:738-741`) and the range checks `0 <= m < perms` (`decode.rs:580-585`) provide redundant defense-in-depth on multiplicities.
   - Accordingly, `SOUND_mut_free_mult` is retained as a state invariant that **holds** (cannot be violated) and is classified as an **equivalent mutant**, not counted as caught. The other 6 mutants (`mut_no_m_range`, `mut_no_inactive_zero`, `mut_no_has1`, `mut_no_chain`, `mut_first_cap_wrong`, `mut_ungated_y1`) are all **caught** in the repaired field without any wrapped values.

**Overall Circuit Verdict: SOUND and COMPLETE** (up to the deliberate `pw[0] == sh.pre_hash` pin on the `L` verifying-key pre-hash word and the negligible Schwartz-Zippel soundness/completeness error $\le \frac{7660}{r} < 2^{-241}$ of the Fiat-Shamir log-derivative lookups).

## 2. Detailed Answers and Mathematical Evidence for the 7 Questions

### Question 1: Slot Placement and Tuple Packing
**Verdict: SOUND** *(Verdict unchanged from Round 3; model repaired with `FR_MOD = 2053`)*

1. **Active slot prefix $\text{act}_t = [t < \text{np}]$ (`lane-src/decode.rs:382-383, 547-557, 653-654`):**
   - Step 3 enforces $\text{cap}_p = P - \text{np} \in [0, 2^{17})$ (`decode.rs:382-383`). Since each entry $i \in \{0, \dots, B - 1\}$ has $\text{perms}_i \in [0, \max(V/2, \text{PATH\_MAX})] = [0, 128]$, over $\mathbb{Z}$ we have $0 \le \text{np} = \sum_{i=0}^{B-1} \text{perms}_i \le P = 256 \ll r$.
   - For each slot $t \in \{0, \dots, P - 1\}$, $\text{act}_t \in \{0, 1\}$ (`wit_bit`), $\text{act}_{t-1} \cdot \text{act}_t = \text{act}_t$ for $t \ge 1$ (`decode.rs:550-554`), and $\sum_{t=0}^{P-1} \text{act}_t \equiv \text{np} \pmod r$ (`decode.rs:653-654`).
   - Because $\text{act}_t = 1 \implies \text{act}_{t-1} = 1$, the bit vector $(\text{act}_0, \dots, \text{act}_{P-1})$ is non-increasing ($1, \dots, 1, 0, \dots, 0$), and since $P < r$ ($8 < 2053$ in the model), the sum in the field equals the integer count of $1$s. Thus $\text{act}_t = [t < \text{np}]$ is uniquely forced for all $t \in \{0, \dots, P - 1\}$.
   - On every inactive slot ($t \ge \text{np}$, so $1 - \text{act}_t = 1$), the circuit enforces $(1 - \text{act}_t) \cdot \text{pk}_t = 0$ (`decode.rs:574-577`), forcing $\text{pk}_t = 0$.
2. **Injectivity of `tup` on claimable rows and witness tuples (`lane-src/decode.rs:188-191, 518-530, 560-573`):**
   - The packing map is $\text{tup}(b_0, \dots, b_4, \text{param}, o, \text{poff}, \text{prm}) = \sum_{k=0}^4 2^k b_k + 2^5 \text{param} + 2^{13} o + 2^{22} \text{poff} + 2^{31} \text{prm}$.
   - On every slot $t$, witness fields are constrained to $b_k \in \{0, 1\}$, $\text{param} \in [0, 2^8)$, and $o, \text{poff}, \text{prm} \in [0, 2^9)$ (`decode.rs:561-572`). Since the bit fields $[0, 5), [5, 13), [13, 22), [22, 31), [31, 40)$ are pairwise disjoint and $2^{40} < r$, `tup` is a bijection from $\{0, 1\}^5 \times [0, 2^8) \times [0, 2^9)^3$ into $[0, 2^{40}) \subset \mathbb{F}_r$. In particular, $\text{pk}_t = 0 \iff b_0 = \dots = b_4 = \text{param}_t = o_t = \text{poff}_t = \text{prm}_t = 0$.
   - Every entry $i$ with $\text{perms}_i > 0$ has $\text{op}_i \in \{\text{PERM}, \text{H2}, \text{SPONGE}, \text{PATH}, \text{DIGEST}\}$, so $\text{param}_i \in [0, 255] \subset [0, 2^8)$ and $o_i, \text{poff}_i, \text{perms}_i \in [0, 256] \subset [0, 2^9)$. Thus `tup` is strictly injective on all claimable rows.
3. **Inability to claim a $\text{perms}_i = 0$ row (`PUB`, `NOP`) or an overflowing `PUB` row (`lane-src/decode.rs:724-742`):**
   - In $\sum_{t=0}^{P-1} \frac{\text{act}_t}{\alpha - \text{pk}_t} = \sum_{i=0}^{B-1} \frac{\text{perms}_i}{\alpha - \text{row}_i}$, any `NOP` or `PUB` entry has $\text{perms}_i = 0$, so its RHS term vanishes identically (and independently, any active slot satisfies $0 \le t - \text{poff}_t \le \text{prm}_t - 1$, forcing $\text{prm}_t \ge 1$).
4. **Unique placement at $t = \text{poff}_i + m$ (`lane-src/decode.rs:579-585`):**
   - By Schwartz-Zippel (soundness loss $\le \frac{P + B}{r} = \frac{512}{r} < 2^{-245}$), every active slot $t \in [0, \text{np})$ has $\text{pk}_t = \text{row}_{i(t)}$ for an entry $i(t)$ with $\text{perms}_{i(t)} > 0$.
   - On every active slot $t \in [0, \text{np})$, `range(d1, 2^9)` and `range(d2, 2^9)` enforce $d_1 = t - \text{poff}_{i(t)} \in [0, 2^9)$ and $d_2 = \text{perms}_{i(t)} - 1 - (t - \text{poff}_{i(t)}) \in [0, 2^9)$. Over $\mathbb{F}_r$ (and over $\mathbb{F}_{2053}$ in the repaired model), negative values wrap above $2^9$, so this holds iff $\text{poff}_{i(t)} \le t < \text{poff}_{i(t)} + \text{perms}_{i(t)}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$.
   - Because the intervals $I_i = [\text{poff}_i, \text{poff}_i + \text{perms}_i)$ for entries with $\text{perms}_i > 0$ form a disjoint partition of $[0, \text{np})$, each active slot $t \in [0, \text{np})$ belongs to a unique interval $I_i$, uniquely forcing $i(t) = i$ and $m = t - \text{poff}_i \in \{0, \dots, \text{perms}_i - 1\}$.

### Question 2: Sponge Chaining
**Verdict: SOUND** *(Unchanged from Round 3)*

- By Step 3 (`decode.rs:358-366`), a `SPONGE(n)` entry ($n \in [1, V - 1]$) has $\text{par} = n \bmod 2$ and $\text{perms} = q = \lceil n / 2 \rceil \ge 1$, occupying contiguous slots $t = \text{poff} + m$ for $m \in \{0, \dots, q - 1\}$.
- By `is_zero` (`decode.rs:586-593`), $\text{first} = [m == 0]$, $\text{nf} = [m > 0]$, $\text{last} = [m == q - 1]$, and $\text{has1} = 1 - \text{last} \cdot \text{par}$, which equals $0$ iff $m = q - 1$ and $n$ is odd.

## 5. What the Model Does Not Cover (Scope and Limitations)

1. **Reduced-Scale Parameters vs. `Dims::FULL`:**
   - The Quint model uses reduced parameters (`FR_MOD = 2053`, `BB_P = 17`, `WORD_BASE = 18`, `PACK_K = 2`, `LIMBS = 3`, `LIMB_BASE = 16`, `B_CAP = 8`, `V_CAP = 18`, `P_CAP = 8`, `LANES = 4`, `LANE_PAIRS = 3`, `PATH_MAX = 4`) so that state-machine steps and unit tests evaluate rapidly. Full-scale bounds ($r \approx 2^{255}$, $P_{\text{BB}} = 2^{31} - 2^{27} + 1$, $B = V = P = 256$, $L_{\text{TX}} = 1024$) are verified analytically in Sections 1 and 2.
2. **Symbolic Multiset Lookup vs. Probabilistic Fiat-Shamir Rational Evaluation:**
   - `check_lookup` (`decode.qnt:709-735`) and `check_slot_lookup` (`decode.qnt:739-768`) model the log-derivative equality $\sum_q \frac{g_q}{\alpha - (i_q + \beta v_q)} = \sum_t \frac{m_t}{\alpha - (k_t + \beta u_t)}$ and $\sum_t \frac{\text{act}_t}{\alpha - \text{pk}_t} = \sum_i \frac{\text{perms}_i}{\alpha - \text{row}_i}$ symbolically as exact field-weight equality per key/value pair rather than sampling random challenges $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{F}_r$. The soundness error $\le \frac{7660}{r} < 2^{-241}$ and completeness pole-collision probability $\le \frac{1794}{r} < 2^{-243}$ over $\mathbb{F}_r$, as well as transcript observation order (`decode.rs:681-691`), are established analytically in Question 6 and Question 7.
3. **Uninterpreted 3-Tuple Permutation Table (`PERM_TABLE`):**
   - Instead of computing the full Poseidon permutation over $\mathbb{F}_r$, `decode.qnt` models `permute` as an uninterpreted injective 3-tuple function (`PERM_TABLE` + default `[300, 301, 302]`, verified injective by `perm_table_injective_test`). Correctness of the underlying Midnight `ZkStdLib` Poseidon chip (`self.std.poseidon_chip().permutation`) and range-check chip (`assert_lower_than_fixed`) is assumed as part of the `Alg` backend (`lane-src/alg.rs`).
4. **Non-Replayed `PATH` Merkle Siblings and Unpinned Statement Head Words:**
   - Neither stock `iface::decode` nor wrap `decode::decode` replays `PATH` entries (which require wrap-private Merkle sibling witnesses outside `pv`); both check only that `PATH` entries consume $3$ logged values and $\text{param} \in [1, \text{PATH\_MAX}]$ permutation slots.
   - As noted in `decode.rs:31-32`, `pw[0]` is pinned to `sh.pre_hash` (whereas `iface::decode` ignores `lfr_words[0]`), and the non-anchor statement head words (`root_stmt`, `run_tag`, `layout_d`, `X`) are only range-checked to `< BB_P` in `decode` (their semantics are enforced by other wrap components).
