3. Entries: does the one-hot over the 7 admitted codes with `sum c = 1` and `sum code * c = op` force the op word to an admitted code? 4. Zero padding: does the monotone bit vector g with `sum g = V - off_n` force g_j = [j >= off_n], so that every value after off_n is zero?
5. Lane map, in stock order: is the skip of a pair (0, 0) exact (the `is_zero` on `lane + lanes * ent` with z u = 0, z = 1 - u inv)? Can a prover skip a pair that is not (0, 0), or not skip (0, 0)?
