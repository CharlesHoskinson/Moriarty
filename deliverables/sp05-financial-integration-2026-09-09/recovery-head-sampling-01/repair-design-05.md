# Bounded recovery snapshot sampling

Actual recovery02 failed RECOVERY_INDEXER_FINALITY after readiness and before private creation. All services were independently observed contained at +112.849 seconds. The exact failed hash pair was not retained. An actual-SDK/full-preflight fixture demonstrates one matching mechanism: one ordinary head advance after indexedTip and before the next finalized-head call. The unchanged fixture went RED to GREEN after this correction; transport remains synthetic.

Run the historical receipt watch once. Then sample current public state at most six times within one shared min(caller deadline, sampler start +60 seconds) decision deadline, with one-second spacing after a detected forward movement. This is at most six snapshot attempts, not six HTTP requests: each readiness reader has its existing bounded polling.

Only locally detected, well-formed forward head/coverage movement causes another sample. Require the earlier hash still canonical and remember a monotonically nondecreasing finalized-height floor. Malformed samples, same-height conflicting hashes, backward finality, different/missing native state, identity/canonicality errors and provider errors fail immediately. Every accepted sample retains exact indexed/finalized bracketing, separate node head/header observations and whole historical/current native-state equality. Original initial-tip freshness and two-block bounds remain conservative; they can stop earlier than six samples.

The actual preflight caller passes the shortened deadline to both waitForLocalTip and its local RPC factory, so their internal requests/polling cannot inherit the longer wallet deadline. Native SDK queryContractState lacks a cancellation hook here: timeout fails without resampling or private work, but underlying read-only SDK I/O cancellation is not claimed. Existing process and independent service limits still apply.

On sample exhaustion, a closed public recoveryObservation gives samples, elapsed time, reason and last validated before/after hashes and heights. No arbitrary SDK or private object is retained. Existing identity, constructor, metadata-copy, wallet and submission gates are unchanged; no compiler/deployment/reset/wallet retry is introduced.
