# Four-paper security study: authorization, service delivery and delegated spending

Research draft, 2026-09-19. The four supplied identifiers resolve to the intended titles. This track acquired versioned primary HTML and PDFs with Scrapling and rendered all 70 PDF pages with PixelRAG's pixelshot. Selected substantive pages were read; rendering is not reading. Exact hashes, page coverage and source claims accompany this report. No attacks, deployments, payments, proof builds or vault edits were performed.

## What each paper establishes within its scope

**Five Attacks on x402 Agentic Payment Protocol**, arXiv:2605.11781v1, separates settlement timing, caller preemption, repeated service grants, HTTP handling and discovery manipulation. Its reorg percentages are controlled/model-based estimates, not measured public-chain reorg frequencies. The tested header ambiguity is not itself an end-to-end bypass. Its discovery experiments emulate retrieval over a catalog; they do not establish that every agent or registry behaves similarly. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.11781v1), pp9–14.

**When HTTP 402 Meets the Blockchain: Risks on Emerging x402 Payments**, arXiv:2607.19545v1, derives eight facilitator rules and distinguishes authorization, settlement and sponsored execution. It studies faulty or noncompliant trusted facilitators, excluding fully malicious/colluding facilitators from its principal model. The July publication reports January disclosures and fixes acknowledged by February 6; its findings must not be presented as a September vulnerability inventory. High-impact validation was deliberately bounded. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19545v1), pp5–7,16–17.

**402Pilot: An x402 Decision Layer for Autonomous Agent Micropayments**, arXiv:2608.01341v1, evaluates buying decisions under finite funds, not protocol safety. Its 823-task frozen replay abstracts payment execution; a separate local integration witness contributes none of the benchmark measurements. Its formal component analysis explicitly does not prove a complete regret theorem for the full replay system. Learning provider utility is distinct from enforcing the owner's spending authority. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.01341v1), pp2–5,7,11–12,15.

**A402: Binding Cryptocurrency Payments to Service Execution for Agentic Commerce**, arXiv:2603.01179v2, combines TEE-bound execution with adaptor signatures and channel settlement. Its model trusts hardware isolation and vendor attestation, excludes TEE-specific vulnerabilities, and assumes eventual delivery/inclusion for liveness. Atomic-exchange success is defined as the **vault** obtaining the result and the service obtaining payment. Client delivery is a subsequent operation. The performance setup uses a fixed simulated service delay. This is not a proof of arbitrary service quality or unconditional endpoint delivery. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.01179v2), pp5,8–11.

## Implications for a permissionless Moriarty kernel

The following are proposed research obligations, not claims established by the papers or implemented in Midnight.

1. **Separate choice from authority.** An agent may rank providers with a learned policy, but acceptance must independently check owner-signed purposes, recipients, assets, per-call bounds, aggregate bounds, expiry and delegation scope. A high predicted utility cannot enlarge authority. Counterexample: a manipulated listing offers an attractive service outside the owner's approved data-use or recipient policy.
2. **Reserve across concurrent work.** Maintain spent, reserved and residual liabilities across outstanding stages. Prove conservation when authorizing, settling, retrying, failing or releasing a reservation. Two agents each seeing a $10 balance cannot both reserve $8. A per-request cap without cumulative accounting does not establish a wallet budget. Include sponsored gas and retained fees, not only merchant payments.
3. **Use distinct settlement judgments.** `Authorized`, `Reserved`, `Executed`, `PaymentAccepted`, `PaymentFinal`, `ResultRecoverable` and `DeliveredToRecipient` must have explicit evidence and principals. A provider's receipt or facilitator success flag cannot silently discharge another stage. For delayed delivery, retain a recovery obligation, evidence location and authorized continuation. Payment confirmation cannot retract an already disclosed response; failures need specified compensation or debt, not invented global rollback.
4. **Bind the whole obligation.** A payment proof must bind its relevant service/request, result predicate, price, destination, chain/domain, nonce, stage, authorized actor set and semantic version. Where permissionless submission is intended, recovery must recognize a valid already-settled authorization without granting the service twice. Caller binding is one design option, not the only possible reconciliation protocol.
5. **Make release and replay policy explicit.** A single-use chain nonce does not by itself provide single-use service delivery. Distinguish repeated retrieval of the same paid result from repeated billable execution. An HTTP cache, retry or failover must preserve that distinction and the authorized recipient. Prove idempotency against the logical obligation, not merely a byte string whose encoding can vary.
6. **Keep source truth and execution correctness separate.** A correct circuit or attested program can process false input, return a misleading answer, or satisfy the wrong predicate. Documentary evidence needs a declared issuer, freshness and acceptance relation. Spending authorization needs correspondence to the owner's canonical intention, not only valid signatures over an agent-generated transaction.

## ZK, MPC, TEE and PCD are different guarantees

A federated kernel should give each evidence type a typed assumption set. A ZK proof can show satisfaction of a specified relation without exposing its witness; it does not supply data availability, source truth or ledger finality. MPC can distribute witness processing or signing under a specified corruption threshold; threshold approval alone does not prove the requested service predicate. TEE attestation can bind measured code and protected execution to a hardware trust root; it does not remove hardware, freshness, rollback, availability or implementation assumptions. A402's TEE committee is a useful architectural comparison, not evidence of a Midnight ZK/MPC implementation.

The recursive acceptance judgment should bind policy/program/circuit versions, domain, predecessor commitment, current public inputs, stage authority, budget deltas and remaining obligations. It needs a legitimate base state and well-founded predecessor evidence. It must reject a proof valid for another request, an old balance reused by concurrent branches, and a witness satisfying a circuit that omits a mandatory condition. Actual ledger consumption or equivalent nullifier rules must prevent duplicated spending; recursive proof validity alone is insufficient.

A migration or federation change must preserve unresolved delivery/refund obligations and the owner-approved trust policy. An unavailable threshold quorum must not be silently replaced with a weaker one. Recovery is an authorized transition with explicit outcomes, not an assumption that progress will occur. Confidentiality claims must specify the observer and permitted leakage—including counterparties, timing, amounts, retries and exceptional dispute paths.

## Worked conceptual trace

An owner delegates a $10 workflow budget. The agent selects a $3 service, with up to $1 of authorized execution fees. Acceptance reserves $4 and binds the service predicate, result recipient and evidence profile. The provider executes and produces acceptable evidence. The next stage records the $3 payment and actual fee; result recovery remains an obligation until the authorized recipient can obtain it. A timeout cannot erase already-paid value or already-disclosed information. The signed policy decides whether timeout opens a refund claim, a compensated failure or continued recovery. A retry consumes the existing logical request identity and reservation; it cannot create fresh spending authority. Every amount here is illustrative, not a measurement or proposed syntax.

## Remaining proof boundaries

No paper in this set establishes Moriarty-to-ZKIRv3 correspondence. The kernel research must prove both accepted-witness soundness and useful valid executions under explicit premises. Empirical routing quality, an honest witness generator, a successful simulator, an attestation signature, and a threshold signature each leave different obligations open. The candidates in MPLR-candidates.json refine existing authority, staged-settlement, resource and certified-lowering requirements; no new canonical IDs are allocated.
