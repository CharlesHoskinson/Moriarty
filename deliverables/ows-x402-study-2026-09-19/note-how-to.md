# Evaluate a wallet and payment integration

Pin the wallet, payment scheme, adapter, verifier and target versions. Identify who can recover each key. Determine whether policy enforcement survives a malicious solver or copied token. Compare parsed effects with authenticated state and actual execution, including failure effects.

Model request, reservation, authorization, submission, finality and result delivery separately. Check duplicate requests, concurrent budgets, expired evidence, missing effects, foreign domains, amount substitution, cancellation races and service failure after payment. Preserve unavailable or unresolved outcomes. Record each cryptographic, hardware, federation and chain assumption.

Demonstrate a valid independent solver and permitted recovery as positive witnesses. Prove the selected relation and its correspondence to Midnight ZKIRv3 before labeling the integration certified. [Reference](reference.md) · [Index](index.md).
