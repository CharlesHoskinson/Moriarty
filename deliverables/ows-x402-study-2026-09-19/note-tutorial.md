# A solver buys evidence under a bounded intention

This is a conceptual trace, not executable Moriarty syntax.

1. The owner delegates a $10 total budget for a specified result, including at most $1 in associated fees.
2. A solver finds a $3 service. The kernel reserves the permitted payment and fee exposure before concurrent work can use it.
3. The request binds the service predicate, payee, chain, expiry, result recipient and recovery policy.
4. A compatible wallet or signer supplies authorization within its declared trust boundary. The proof binds the exact material effects.
5. Payment authorization, payment finality, result recoverability and recipient delivery advance only with their required evidence.
6. A retry refers to the same logical request. Unknown payment or delivery retains its reservation or residual duty until resolved.

Change the recipient, reuse the budget in a parallel branch, or replace missing effects with an empty list. Each must fail at the specified enforcement boundary. A legitimate alternative solver and authorized recovery must remain possible. [Explanation](explanation.md) · [Index](index.md).
