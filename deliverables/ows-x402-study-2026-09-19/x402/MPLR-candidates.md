# Unnumbered research candidates from x402

These are research proposals, not implemented guarantees. Root should merge/refine existing MPLRs and allocate IDs only after comparison. Evidence identifiers resolve in `claims.json`.

## Stage-indexed settlement outcomes

**Need:** A language must distinguish authorization checked, escrow established, commitment stored, onchain claim recorded, value transferred, resource executed and recipient delivery attested. “Settle success” cannot collapse those states. **Evidence:** X402-03–04,14–15. **Open theory:** indexed effect types, epistemic transition systems or refinement-indexed receipts; explicit observation authority and finality policy. **Adversary:** a solver supplies successful batch commitment as evidence that a merchant was paid, or treats pending HTTP500 as nonpayment. **Positive witness:** a confirmed transfer advances only the financial stage while a separately authenticated delivery condition remains pending. **PCD obligation:** each successor proves the exact stage and preserves unresolved effects; no invented global atomicity.

## Authorization lifetime and channel reincarnation

**Need:** Funding a channel again must not silently resurrect authority the user intended to retire. Explicitly model epochs, retained voucher ceilings and capability revocation. **Evidence:** X402-16–18; May audit PDF, printed pages8–9. **Open theory:** affine capabilities with monotonic epochs versus finite expiry plus history commitments. **Adversary:** drain and top up the same channel, then redeem remaining headroom from an old signature; a key rotates while old submitter obligations remain. **Positive witness:** deliberate same-relationship top-up under a still-authorized ceiling succeeds, whereas a new epoch rejects the old voucher. **PCD obligation:** carry outstanding authority and residual obligations across funding, withdrawal and migration; a zero balance is not proof of revocation.

## Atomic consumption across financial and service effects

**Need:** Prevent duplicate delivery and duplicate spending independently, including multi-replica retry and process crashes. **Evidence:** X402-05–06,21,26–27. **Open theory:** linear request capabilities, effect journals, authenticated nonmembership and bounded retention policies; application-specific idempotence. **Adversary:** duplicate payment submitted to two replicas, conflicting resource under the same ID, or a crash after side effect but before receipt persistence. **Positive witness:** identical retry returns the same result without rerunning effects; a changed request is rejected. **PCD obligation:** prove request/effect binding and completeness of the consumed-state frame; consensus or atomic storage must enforce uniqueness between concurrent valid proofs.

## Local consent checks versus ledger-enforced lifecycle authority

**Need:** An offchain signature check cannot be represented as equivalent to an atomic onchain precondition. Distinguish held value, capture authority, refund consent and refund funding authority. **Evidence:** X402-10–13. **Open theory:** rely/guarantee interfaces describing who enforces each predicate, compare-and-swap state refinements, bounded composable evidence algebra. **Adversary:** replay a signed balance snapshot in the check/inclusion race; use a merchant refund signature to spend facilitator liquidity; complete capture but fail void. **Positive witness:** a matching ledger-checked snapshot permits one partial capture and carries the remainder's release duty. **PCD obligation:** transitions consume the relevant authority once, preserve residual duties, and do not claim policy execution from reserved-future specifications.

## Liveness assumptions for contested exit and redemption

**Need:** Record the assumption that a receiver can land a claim before payer withdrawal; distinguish enabled recovery from successful funds delivery. **Evidence:** X402-17–19 and May audit. **Open theory:** timed games, bounded synchrony assumptions, fairness conditions and explicit conditional progress theorems. **Adversary:** censor receiver claims for the whole withdrawal window; blacklist a fixed payout address; repeatedly submit tiny refunds to reset an exit timer. **Positive witness:** claim inclusion before expiry protects that recorded obligation, while partial refund preserves the original exit deadline. **PCD obligation:** prove safety independent of liveness where possible; never manufacture availability or treat timer expiry as knowledge of all external effects.

## Bounded agent authority and truthful evidence

**Need:** A solver may discover services and suggest calls, but spending follows signed purpose, per-call/aggregate budgets, approved evidence semantics and finite resource bounds. **Evidence:** X402-02,22–25,29. **Open theory:** budget-aware effect types, dimensional accounting and terminating plan combinators; decidable verification of bounded evidence predicates. **Adversary:** prompt-injected service metadata induces repeated individually affordable payments above an aggregate limit; a valid receipt key falsely attests delivery; mutually recursive proof histories lack a legitimate base. **Positive witness:** an authorized paid query fits the remaining budget and returns authenticated evidence of exactly its claimed proposition. **PCD obligation:** induct authority, conservation, complete effect/fee frames and residual duties over well-founded lineage. Recursive proofs do not make attester statements true, create cross-chain atomicity, or guarantee completion. Preserve the permissionless all-Midnight-DeFi language and ZKIRv3 target.
