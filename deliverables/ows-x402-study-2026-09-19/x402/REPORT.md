## Explanation: payment state is not one boolean

`/verify` is a read-only validity check, not a reservation or finality certificate. `/settle` durably commits whatever the selected scheme defines: transfer, hold, recorded charge, or stored commitment. Its generic success terminology must be refined by binding and stage. HTTP transport response, payment authorization, chain inclusion, application execution, client receipt and adjudicated delivery are distinct facts. No studied source supplies atomicity across arbitrary service effects and a payment ledger, much less independent chains. [X402-03–06,14–15]

| Scheme | Authorization and effect | Material limit |
|---|---|---|
| exact | Fixed amount; authorization flow runs resource before settlement; upfront settles first | Handler execution can precede failed settlement. Upfront handler failure leaves payment committed; no scheme refund. |
| upto | Signed ceiling, single metered request, at most one settlement; zero actual charge allowed | A smaller charge is not repeated partial settlement. Actual usage comes from server accounting, not a general computation proof. |
| auth-capture | Escrow hold then capture/void; repeated partial capture/refund; payer expiry reclaim | Delegated consent is facilitator-enforced. Refund needs liquidity. Custom policies operate outside facilitator validation. |
| batch-settlement | Request commitment and immediate access; later redeem/claim/settle | Stored voucher or credit-backed acceptance is not value received. Claim inclusion and intermediary honesty/liveness remain assumptions. |

These descriptions require network refinements. SVM upto escrows a one-request ceiling before resource execution and later seals/distributes. Its receiver authorizer signs nonzero charges, while the facilitator holds closure authority and can seal early at the current watermark. Its validAfter policy is offchain and voucher expiry is not bound by the client's open signature. EVM upto and the generic properties must not be silently substituted for this binding. Cloudflare batch is identity/credit-backed billing, not onchain escrow. [X402-07–08,20]

Auth-capture is the closest direct precedent for conditional settlement: funds can wait in a hold, and a custom operator may require arbitration, roles, time locks or other conditions before release. It does **not** standardize a general accumulator of document, proof and recipient-signature predicates. A deferred finalize is a timing choice, not proof that the user's chosen condition was met. The future policy appendix is not a deployed capability claim. [X402-09–10]

## How to: interpret failure and recovery evidence

Preserve the original authorization, scheme, network, version, request identity and known effects. `settlement_pending` means broadcast occurred but confirmation was not established. Both Foundation and CDP docs describe one retry of the identical request, reconciling a stored transaction rather than issuing a fresh authorization. Direct API users must implement that reconciliation themselves. A missing receipt or HTTP 500 with `success:false` does not establish nonpayment. Current docs explicitly warn against charging again while the original outcome remains unknown. [X402-04,26–27]

The default pending store is in-memory: it does not provide shared state across replicas or survive process loss. Platform timeouts can kill a receipt wait before the transaction hash reaches the caller. The payment-identifier extension supports normalized request fingerprints, scoped caching and 409 on conflicting reuse, but role adoption is optional. Chain nonce consumption protects spending, not automatically duplicate resource delivery. Client-submitted exact proofs therefore require atomic single-use claims bound to the request, finality checks and defined retention. [X402-05–06,21,26]

Recovery must preserve residual duties. Auth-capture signs both capturable and refundable snapshots because one balance can recur. Its delegated check is expressly best-effort: state can change between an offchain check and inclusion. Capture-and-void may be two transactions; a success can represent capture alone when the remaining hold disappeared. Refund liquidity comes from the operator, so a receiver signature alone cannot authorize spending the facilitator's funds. Key rotation must preserve outstanding submitter duties through refund deadlines. [X402-11–13]

EVM batch keeps offchain charged amounts, onchain claimed amounts and actually settled amounts separate. Outstanding unclaimed vouchers do not reserve value onchain. A payer withdrawal can win if the receiver cannot land a claim during the delay; watcher infrastructure mitigates this race but cannot make inclusion unconditional. Partial refunds reduce pending withdrawal amounts without restarting the timer. Refund nonces can advance even when zero tokens move. Vouchers have no expiry and unused ceilings can remain relevant across later top-ups of the same channel: emptying escrow is not cryptographic revocation. [X402-15–18]

The May Cantina report documents the withdrawal race, acknowledgement of voucher lifetime exposure and token-blacklist recovery limits. Its “fixed” classification for the withdrawal issue refers to documentation/operational mitigations, not elimination of the race. Historical audit fixes and source recommendations are not evidence that the current pin or deployed addresses have been formally verified.

## Tutorial: follow an agent payment without overstating its proof

An MCP client receives structured payment requirements in a tool error, constructs payment, retries with x402 metadata, and receives result plus payment metadata. A2A correlates payment with an input-required task. The chatbot example puts payment in the host application and auto-approves through a hook: the model only sees tool definitions/results. This is useful solver plumbing, not a solver correctness theorem or owner-intention proof. [X402-23–25]

A Moriarty integration can let an untrusted solver propose a bounded operation and payment plan, then check the authenticated formal intention before signing. That intention must identify allowed services, units, per-operation and aggregate ceilings, fee authority, validity stages, replay domain, condition evidence, and recovery duties. CDP spend controls are implementation-side client policy; core x402 does not supply a universal budget invariant. Discovery and payment receipts remain untrusted inputs until authenticated and semantically checked. [X402-02,22,25,29]

An offer/receipt signature proves which authorized service key made a statement, under the verified key-authorization history. It does not independently prove that data was useful, work was correct, or a remote effect occurred. The extension itself distinguishes valid signatures from authority and recommends temporal authorization evidence after key rotation. [X402-22]

## Research inference: requirements for Moriarty

Retain a permissionless language for all Midnight DeFi developers that targets pinned ZKIRv3 and carries proof of authenticated signed formal intention. Protocol-wide project admission gates do not follow from a particular facilitator's local operator allowlist. Applications may still choose counterparties, attesters and evidence policies.

Treat the user's definition literally: submit the destination request, withhold settlement until the configured combination of signatures, documents, proofs, recipient actions or other supported conditions is satisfied. Funded programmable escrow is one implementation; unfunded conditional requests also need representation. Distinguish partial evidence, partial fills, partial resource execution, partial financial capture and asynchronous completion.

A restricted language plus proof-carrying data can make local transitions and recursively accumulated obligations checkable. It needs authenticated base provenance, bounded canonical inputs, explicit economic units, an acyclic/well-founded history, ledger-enforced uniqueness and sound external-evidence rules. It cannot infer external truth, finality or liveness from a valid signature or a recursive proof. The candidate research questions in `MPLR-candidates.md` include negative and positive witnesses; no requirement IDs were allocated and no vault files changed.
