## Controlling design

Moriarty is a permissionless language of provable intention for all Midnight DeFi developers. Supported programs need no project deployment permission. Native protocol validity, cryptographic owner authority and mandatory correctness evidence remain objective requirements. The [product contract](../../docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md) and [revised roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) supersede conflicting managed-service or administrative deployment requirements.

The target is **ZKIRv3 executing on Midnight**. Compact can be an intermediate compiler route; an intermediate source file is not evidence of completed compilation or ledger execution. Formal intention means the authenticated specification and declared assumptions, with no claim to prove an unexpressed wish.

Signed outcomes bind complete effects, exact assets or permitted substitution predicates, recipients, fees, replay use and residual liabilities. Successful fulfillment satisfies its net outcome. Permitted partial/failure transitions satisfy separate signed phase limits, including retained guaranteed effects and fees. Token supply/transfer equations and debt evolution are distinct.
