## Agreed direction proposed

Borrow concepts. Implement a Moriarty-native, off-chain advisory layer around existing source/Core interfaces. Do not import Aeon's runtime or verifier as authority. Do not change admitted source grammar, runtime semantics, Compact lowering or release gates in these increments.

**NOW A0 — Trust and obligation inventory.** Add a versioned, machine-readable action report and CLI consumer. Enumerate mandatory assumption categories and use explicit unsupported/incomplete status. Bind source, profile, compiler/Core, schema, kernel, snapshots/oracles, solver/encoder where present, and proof/ledger correspondence evidence. Static claims, runtime enforcement, finite conformance and actual ledger evidence remain separate classes. Known absent proof/ledger evidence is `unestablished`, not a claimed verified fact. A missing required category fails report completeness. A trust inventory is not new assurance and must not claim completeness beyond its declared extraction coverage.

**NOW A1 — Bounded advisory VC pilot, after A0.** First freeze the exact judgment and arithmetic fragment. Implement only existing pure Boolean/UInt64/UInt128 expression constructors needed by a small predeclared corpus. Initial operations: literals, variables, checked add/subtract, comparisons, Boolean short-circuit and conditional. Constant multiplication and division belong only after their exact intermediate bounds, denominator, rounding and rejection rules are reviewed and covered. Exclude state mutation, emitted effects, dynamic observations, arbitrary reflection, new ADTs, general recursion, quantity conversions and quantifiers from the first supported fragment. Unsupported surrounding actions must be explicit. No unsupported node can be silently dropped.

Distinguish (i) successful-execution safety, (ii) guaranteed defined execution within stated bounds, and (iii) assumption feasibility. Result status is one of `encoding-established`, `counterexample-validated`, `unsupported`, `timeout`, `unknown`, `checker-error`. Carry a separate feasibility status (`satisfiable`, `inconsistent`, `unknown`, `not-checked`) and judgment kind. Inconsistent assumptions yield a vacuity diagnostic and cannot be presented as a useful established guarantee. Retain raw solver answer and timing cause. `encoding-established` means only that the bound formula was discharged under named encoding/solver assumptions. It does not erase runtime checks or prove semantic correspondence.

Freeze 12 positive and 12 negative/unsupported obligation fixtures before implementation. Include zero, maximal values, checked overflow, underflow, dead-branch division, short-circuit behavior, false postcondition, inconsistent assumptions, timeout/unknown injection, unsupported nodes and changed input hashes. Each supported encoding rule has a deliberate mutation control. Finite controls expose defects but are not a proof of encoding adequacy. A1 passes its functional pilot only when every named fixture returns its expected status and all required mutants are detected. Its usefulness gate separately records supported obligation coverage, time and diagnostic usefulness. It cannot silently weaken predicates to improve coverage.

**NEXT A2 — Counterexample diagnostics and resource-use diagnostics.** CLI first. A solver model becomes an executable counterexample only after replay through the pinned evaluator confirms the stated violated obligation or definedness failure. Successful-evaluation safety counterexamples can be successful outputs violating Q. They need not all be runtime rejection. A replay mismatch is a checker error. No model, incomplete snapshot or ledger-unreachable state is not an executable attack claim. LSP presentation is deferred until the CLI has an accepted consumer.

Resource-use analysis is a separate later increment based on reviewed operation signatures. Classify consumable funding receipts, affine optional authority and persistent liabilities separately. Do not force exactly-once linearity onto revocable or unused permissions, or treat debts as disposable handles. An unresolved alias/use remains an explicit diagnostic, not a permissive inference. Kernel and ledger uniqueness/replay checks remain mandatory.

**CONDITIONAL LATER A3 — Restricted pure-expression synthesis.** No implementation until A1/A2 meet their acceptance and a frozen authoring corpus demonstrates a useful missing capability. The corpus must contain at least ten independently specified pure tasks, including negative/unsatisfiable tasks, and compare against manual templates or deterministic completion. Predeclare budgets and success criteria before running the comparison. Begin with bounded enumeration or SMT completion over an allowlisted grammar and context. Holes exist only in external draft artifacts. Emit hole-free ordinary `.mori` and reparse/recheck the complete program with the existing frontend and all applicable obligations/bounds. Never change specifications, preconditions or postconditions to make a candidate pass. Unknown, timeout, unsupported required obligations or unresolved holes cannot authorize a synthesis candidate. Such a result does not revoke a manually authored runtime-valid program's existing status. Fitness may rank validated candidates only and cannot soften fees, debit limits, obligations or work reserve.

**DEFER:** qualifier inference, broader synthesis, GP/LLM proposal backends, IDE integration and general language extensions. General ADTs/polymorphism may be revisited for independently justified Moriarty requirements, but are rejected as Aeon-driven additions in this enhancement set.

**REJECT for this integration:** unchecked financial FFI, Aeon as runtime/compiler substrate, Aeon as a second authoritative verifier, optional termination as a replacement for Moriarty bounds, and effectful synthesis before independently accepted source/kernel/backend correspondence. An external Aeon proposer is outside the selected architecture. Reopening requires a separately reviewed scope amendment and measured need.
