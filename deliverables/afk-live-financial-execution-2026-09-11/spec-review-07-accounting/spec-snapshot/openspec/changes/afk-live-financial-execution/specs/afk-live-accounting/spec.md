Receipts is a list of contained immutable `{path,sha256}` references to actual retained loan/related-operation outcomes. Each outcomes entry SHALL contain exactly `receiptSha256,defectClass,outcome,resolvedBy`, where outcome is exactly `failed`, `unknown` or `verified-success`, and resolvedBy is a list of explicit same-defect resolution receipt digests (empty means unresolved). Each eventBindings entry SHALL contain exactly `repository,requirement,capability,eventIds`, identifying already reconciled canonical-store rows. Verify those rows and receipt contents; unknown outcomes are not zero failures. Every referenced failed/unknown outcome must remain represented in history until an explicit applicable resolution is verified. Empty history/receipt/event mappings or mismatched checkout lineage deny the live action. Observer SHALL contain exactly `entryPath,entrySha256,receiptPath,maxAgeSeconds,preflightSeconds,preflightAttempts,lockPath`. The mutable observer receipt SHALL contain exactly `schema,allocationId,preflightChargeId,sourceSha256,observedAt,syncTime,network,roleIdentity,amounts,unitNames,finalityRef,pendingUseDispositionSha256,canonicalInputsUnchanged,childrenStopped`; schema `moriarty.i2-funding-observation/1`. Amounts SHALL be exact nonnegative integer decimal strings by selected asset identity, and unitNames an equal-key map of explicit units. Bind allocation/source/role/network to the context and actual final plan; validate timestamps, independent pending-use receipt and both true unchanged/stop facts against retained observer evidence rather than trust caller-written booleans. The producer SHALL create final correspondence only from its own successful bounded observer result, rejecting a preexisting arbitrary receipt.

ObservedAt and syncTime SHALL be UTC ISO-8601 strings; network SHALL contain exactly `networkId,genesisHash,protocolVersion` and roleIdentity exactly `publicKey,address`, all matched to the final plan and genuine observations. FinalityRef SHALL be a contained immutable `{path,sha256}` reference to the actual public state/finality evidence. PendingUseDispositionSha256 SHALL identify one of the exact reviewed history receipts, not an unrelated caller digest. CanonicalInputsUnchanged and childrenStopped SHALL be booleans, checked against the actual bounded observer's retained execution/containment evidence.

The mutable correspondence SHALL contain exactly `schema,allocationId,executionContextSha256,masterSha256,projectionSha256,observationSha256,preflightChargeId,runtimeChargeId,reservationId`, schema `moriarty.i2-current-correspondence/1`. RuntimeChargeId/reservationId may be null before the financial debit/reservation. Under the existing master lock, the consumer SHALL verify every current digest, exact ownership and allowed transition; the final live check requires matching non-null runtime IDs. Observation refresh updates only mutable receipts/correspondence, not immutable context, runner digest, binding or a prior charge.

### Requirement: Separately charged bounded read-only preflight
The exact resource projection and both votes SHALL include the context's 125-second one-attempt observer allowance within G and its single resource-operation use within N, separately from the financial runtime allowance. The producer may initialize the approved grant, acquire the declared exclusive wallet-use lock and commit this preflight-only charge before wallet funding observation exists, but only after source/resource/context/canonical-history authorization and non-funding preflight checks pass. This bounded read-only authority permits no financial submission, proving, wallet persistence or runtime debit. No extra clock/grant is inferred from the desire to observe funding.

New debit rows SHALL use purpose exactly `funding-preflight` or `financial-runtime`. Both count toward S/n and preserve master dispatch history; a funding-preflight charge reserves exactly 125 seconds before that observer child and consumes its one approved preflight attempt even on failure. Its one-shot master claim binds the exact observer source/argv/bound digest and canonical ownership; it is executed as the explicitly authorized bounded sub-operation of existing cmd_run, not a new public campaign/action. The financial runtime later uses the existing plugin reservation plus master claim sequence. A funding-preflight is not loan-case attempt 3 because it cannot submit a financial transaction; only the financial-runtime debit commits that cumulative attempt. If the observer fails or fresh evidence expires, retain the preflight charge and deny runtime; no second preflight is authorized by this one-attempt context. A further observer allocation requires new explicit reviewed resource authority, not a free refresh.

Retain the exclusive current wallet-use lock across observation and final runtime handoff so intervening known wallet use cannot invalidate the observed unreserved funds. A failure before handoff must release only this invocation's owned lock after child containment, retaining unresolved ownership as a blocker; a successful handoff transfers the exact ownership token to the admitted executor, whose cleanup releases it. Source tests SHALL cover same-owner handoff, competitor rejection, observer timeout/stop failure, missing lock and attempted free retry.

#### Scenario: Funding observation without a circular grant dependency
- **WHEN** exact source/resource votes authorize G/N including one 125-second read-only preflight, but current funding is not yet observed
- **THEN** only that charged exclusive preflight may run; its successful current receipt can satisfy later funding admission, while failure consumes its allowance without starting or charging financial attempt 3.

### Requirement: Authenticated MC02 envelope on the preserved MC01 master

Schema is `moriarty.mc02-successor-projection/1`; predecessor is exactly `{path,sha256}` for the original external master; owner values are exactly `MC01` and `MC02`; historicalDispositions is a list of distinct contained `{path,sha256}` references to explicit historical closure/deficit/authority dispositions. Campaign/action/envelope identity and executionContextSha256 SHALL exactly match the current admitted scope and committed execution context. Concrete predecessor digest and historical dispositions remain subject to separate resource votes.

Before first application, verify the predecessor digest against the locked actual master and retain those exact predecessor bytes in immutable existing admission evidence. Subsequent validations SHALL authenticate that retained predecessor by its committed digest and current master/projection correspondence; they SHALL not require the legitimately advanced master to keep its old digest or permit replay of the first application. Historical disposition evidence SHALL reference the exact retained predecessor path/digest so that U, M and preserved fields remain independently recomputable after mutation.

Let G=grantSeconds and N=grantDispatches, positive integers; C=closureSeconds and D=closureAdjustmentSeconds, nonnegative integers. Preserve predecessor `package=MC01`, `charges`, `planning_charge_seconds`, `planning_overhead_reserved_seconds`, `package_limit_seconds`, `worker_dispatches` and `worker_dispatch_limit` unchanged. Preserve every existing external row's owner and bytes. Do not move old rows or relabel top-level MC01 52/52 as MC02 usage.

Current master used therefore equals U+C+G regardless of S. All old residual reserves/envelopes are nonspendable under the reviewed closure disposition; no old reserve or master rounding slack supplements the selected grant. Top-level externalWorkerDispatches SHALL equal its predecessor value plus n, and master_worker_dispatch_limit its predecessor value plus N. ### Requirement: Single allocation, all-kind limits and prepaid boundary
For the live action, its resource allocation SHALL occur once in `resourceAmendment`; binding `resources.allocationSeconds` SHALL be absent or zero and the same grant SHALL not recur in `successorAmendments`. A new resource vote must expressly extend exhausted aggregate 83/83 or package ceilings where applicable; it cannot reset the retained 52/52 MC01 counter.

At the limit a genuinely prepaid invocation may claim and launch once. The exact current charge of c seconds is already included in S and the external charges. For a predicate expressed as available-for-this-invocation versus c, define A=G-S+c only for that exact previously committed charge and require A>=c; after accounting for that invocation the remainder is A-c=G-S. This is a validation credit for its already reserved execution, not new spendable grant or a refund; every other caller sees only G-S. Retain master nonnegative slack validation without requiring the fully reserved G to appear a second time as unreserved master headroom. The current reader's final nonnegative-remainder contract may equivalently use G-S directly in the prepaid branch and SHALL NOT subtract c again.
