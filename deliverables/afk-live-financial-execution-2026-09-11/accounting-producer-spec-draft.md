## A1. Closed accounting representation and exact grant

The implementation SHALL retain `moriarty.supervised-accounting/1` and its existing closed top-level keys. `currentAccounting` SHALL retain only `schema`, `path`, `passBinding`, `passLedger`. `ACCOUNTING_KEYS` permits `charges`, `reserved`, `externalPackageCharges`, `successorEnvelopes`, planning charges, package/master limits and dispatch counters; it does not permit new top-level observation, closure or identity fields. The original `authority_sha256` SHALL remain `f6b7f17d1bda437d5f83abc656ccf4a0d9c7a57a83995668387c29bd0328f492`. A new grant SHALL point to and validate its actual amendment/votes in existing admission evidence, not rewrite that original authority or pretend the historical top-level amendment pointer proves the later suffix.

Proposed representation: preserve every historical ledger and known charge row; append one explicit `reserved` row for closure of otherwise spendable historical seconds and one new `successorEnvelopes` entry for the current grant. Existing list rows are open to provenance fields but the producer SHALL parse its new rows against exact closed field sets. Closure rows SHALL have a unique ID, nonnegative integer `seconds`, original/master digest and reviewed amendment reference; unknown historical actual cost SHALL remain in the immutable provenance/disposition evidence, never be encoded as a fabricated zero cost. Existing `reserved` rows are reservations, not paid cost. Every old envelope SHALL be nonspendable in the current view, with its original bytes preserved.

The current master has fractional planning seconds and approximately 6.95267 represented seconds remaining. A conservative integer closure reservation may use the ceiling (7 seconds for that exact reviewed snapshot), making the new grant slightly less spendable; it SHALL NOT refund the rounding difference or assert that 7 was an actual historical cost. If the locked source changes, recompute the closure from its exact represented values and revalidate the applicable disposition. Any historical deficit SHALL consume capacity or require an explicit reviewed disposition; it is not erased.

The concrete resource proposal SHALL fix grant seconds, runtime/audit/cleanup partitions, master and selected-package dispatch ceilings, one current envelope identifier and exact fee/submission bounds before votes. No numeric grant is selected by this drafting task. The represented master suffix's disputed/unestablished ancestry (197630/71 baseline; two later direct delegated amendments lacking majority votes) SHALL receive explicit dispositions: preserved historical observations, no implied retrospective approval, and no spendable remainder transferred to this grant. The September 7 authority permits only a separately reviewed bounded successor, with two substantive agreeing votes on its exact bytes. Missing votes mean no grant.

The selected MC02 projection SHALL preserve package ownership when projecting the old master whose `package` is MC01. Old MC01 charges cannot become MC02 charges or disappear. `charges` must contain selected-package charges and `externalPackageCharges` the other owners' charges, with original rows and source identities recoverable. Planning/overhead and worker counters require an explicit scoped projection definition; lifetime MC02 counts are not established by the inspected old master. The implementation SHALL not relabel an MC01 snapshot as MC02 or report a new-grant counter as a lifetime counter. If a truthful selected-package projection cannot be expressed through existing fields and reviewed scoped interpretation, this is a specific record-interface blocker; no shape-valid placeholder may pass.

Independent acceptance: a supplied exact grant yields no credit above that grant; old envelopes/residuals yield none; duplicate grant application, missing disposition, unreviewed amendment, wrong package, noninteger/boolean/negative/nonfinite row values and omitted external costs reject. Record the narrower active-grant reconciliation scope; do not claim quantified lifetime-global costs.

## A2. Single counting and all-kind dispatch limits

The new live allocation SHALL appear once in `resourceAmendment`; set its binding's `resources.allocationSeconds` absent or zero and do not repeat that grant in `successorAmendments`. The source implementation SHALL reject a grant identity appearing in multiple additive locations, rather than trust `_verify_resource`'s current summed value. `workerSeconds` or the applicable verification bound may describe action cost without granting another allocation.

The producer SHALL enforce the selected package, aggregate master and current envelope dispatch ceilings for every live kind, including verify/review/reproduce. The old master is observed at package MC01 52/52 and aggregate 83/83; this does not authorize a next action. The exact concrete amendment must name any increment, preserve the old counters and define the new MC02 envelope's counter. For a one-invocation grant, its envelope starts with zero uses because that grant is new, has a limit of one, and becomes consumed at debit commit. That new-envelope count SHALL never be described as resetting historic attempts or master counts. The genuinely prepaid final invocation may reach its ceiling and launch once; another action cannot use the prepaid exception.

Acceptance: a last-credit/last-dispatch race lets at most one reservation proceed; registering the same live command under another kind cannot escape ceilings; repeated allocation identity never grants twice; the exact prepaid boundary passes once while the next caller fails.

## A3. Actual atomicity, canonical store and one-shot launch

Keep the authoritative current budget at its existing external location and use its existing `budget.lock`; write the consumed projection only under `.moriarty-dev/runtime/current-accounting.json`. Do not weaken `records._contained_file` to follow the external master. The producer is the explicit owner of external-master access; the reader consumes a contained projection and verifies its current correspondence evidence.

Under the existing lock, preparation SHALL validate frozen source/runner/resource approval, read the exact master, verify its expected digest/generation and absence of conflicting charge/claim, and durably commit the new charge/counters before any child launch. Write temporary files, fsync each file, replace, and fsync parent directories. Since master and projection are separate files, do not claim their two replacements are one atomic transaction. Store their correspondence digest and commit disposition in the existing admission/reconciliation evidence; interrupted publication is unavailable until explicitly reconciled. Mutable projection/observation evidence stays outside candidate, binding inputs and runner-plan hashes.

Use the existing master charge row to bind charge ID, action ID, candidate hash, runner digest, canonical in-store identity token and reservation ID, with a monotone launch-claim disposition. These are newly specified fields of a new charge row, not changes to old rows or new top-level accounting keys. After `store.reserve` atomically obtains its existing unique SQLite charge claim, `claim_live_launch` SHALL validate matching ownership under `budget.lock` and transition that master charge from unlaunched to launch-claimed exactly once, durably before calling `execute`. Launch requires both claims and matching identities. The master transition prevents replay through a same-identity pre-claim SQLite backup. A failed claimed invocation remains consumed; removing rows or recreating a store does not repair it.

`canonical-store-before-source.json` records the authorized store path `/home/charl/Moriarty/.git/moriarty-dev/state.sqlite3`, 603 events at its observation, and no persisted identity token. Its only nonempty lineage is SP01.6/loan-swap-subset report/design checks. Before live application, a separately reviewed canonical adoption/history reconciliation must bind the original store inventory, establish its persisted token without discarding rows, and disposition external loan failure receipts. A token merely created in an arbitrary store or unrelated seeded events SHALL not establish loan history. This is not task 1.1a's isolated repair.

Required actual-code fault tests, against isolated master/lock/projection/SQLite: crash before debit (no launch); after debit before projection (no launch, charge retained); between file replacements (stale credit denied); after SQLite claim before master claim (no launch, both histories retained); after master claim before child start (consumed, no automatic retry); restored same-token SQLite backup (master claim rejects); other clone or replaced store (identity rejects); conflicting candidate/runner/reservation (reject); normal path (exact one child, exact one debit). No test touches the real lock or store.

The inspected historical `preview-loan-exit-01/execute-once.py` asserts four submissions; this is a source precedent, not authorization for the new run. Native DUST reservations/`DustSpend.vFee` are SPECK quantities, token transfers use their explicit raw token domain, and paid indexer fee units remain separate. Acceptance: four allowed calls cannot become five via retry/restart; cross-allocation reservation reuse fails; a charged unlaunched attempt cannot reset; native/indexer unit mismatch remains unavailable; refunds do not lower gross debit and fees reduce net goals.

## A5. Nonpersisting observations: concrete SDK path and remaining validation

The inspected `ledger/financial-rpc.mjs::createPreviewRpc` is a real read-only primitive: it permits only finalized-head/header/block-hash/contract-state queries, with one request, no redirects/retries and a 64 KiB response bound. `ledger/contract-balances.mjs` decodes only public unshielded contract balances; `indexed-owner.mjs` handles public owner encodings. Proposed observer location is `experiments/moriarty-midnight-financial/ledger/observe-preview-funding.mjs`, invoked as a bounded child by accounting preflight and returning only public evidence. Reuse those primitives for contract identity/finality, but do not claim they expose fee-payer spendable DUST or complete wallet funding.

`funding-observer-source-diagnosis.md` identifies a concrete narrower source: pinned `DustWallet(configuration).restore(savedDust)` and `UnshieldedWallet(configuration).restore(savedUnshielded)` child APIs, direct `start` and `waitForSyncedState(0n)`, then `stop`. Use NoOp transaction history; do not initialize the full facade's default submission/proving/pending services. Reuse the strict original-snapshot/derived-identity checks from `preview-bootstrap.mjs:76–89`, not its surrounding launch function. The observer privately reads only the existing seed and dust/unshielded snapshots needed for original identity restoration; no fresh wallet creation/fallback, role secrets, password/contract store, balancing, signing, proof, submission, serialization or persistence calls. Test filesystem permissions/symlink refusal and before/after snapshot/seed/marker digests; leave those bytes unchanged. Public output contains only aggregate selected-asset funding and identity/freshness facts.

This proposed refinement permits synchronization of a disposable in-memory restored copy while forbidding changes to canonical wallet files, identities, keys, contract state or chain state. It must receive explicit specification approval before that observer is implemented or run; the frozen phrase “non-mutating” is not silently treated as permission to change the live wallet. An exclusive wallet-use check and bounded outer child lifetime (propose 90 seconds plus 5 seconds stop grace) are required. Stop every partially created child on success, timeout and failure; an uncontained background sync yields unavailable observation.

For DUST use the sum of `DustWalletState.availableCoins[*].generatedNow` evaluated at the native sync time, excluding pending entries, not `balance(Date)` or hypothetical generation. For unshielded funding use `UnshieldedWalletState.balances`/available coins for the exact selected token and derived public identity. Check connected/strictly-complete progress, native sync-time age and canonical network/genesis/protocol; `waitForSyncedState(0n)` alone is not a timestamp guarantee. Crucially, SDK restore resets `pendingDust` to empty: existing allocation/finality/persistence history must independently rule out unresolved older spends. Empty restored pending state SHALL NOT imply unreserved funds. Missing that history remains `funding-observation-unavailable`. The diagnosis pins actual SDK entry hashes; the final source candidate must pin the complete used closure and prove forbidden APIs are absent before a separately admitted observation.

Every admitted observation SHALL include public role/network/asset identity, exact amount and unit, finalized state reference where available, source identity and `observedAt`, with age no more than 120 seconds at actual launch. Reject future timestamps, missing fields, pending/ambiguous resources and unknown units. Store only public observation output outside immutable runner/binding/candidate inputs, bound by current digest in admission evidence. No secret-path weakening, renamed private snapshots or synthetic balance rows.
