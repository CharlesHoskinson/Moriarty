### Requirement: Authenticated MC02 envelope on the preserved MC01 master

For `finalized-financial-settlement`, the producer SHALL obtain the selected campaign from the canonical campaign store and read that campaign's contained `resourceAmendment`. Its exact bytes SHALL be committed by the already verified campaign binding `inputs` map. Within the amendment's existing `resources` object, `projection` SHALL contain exactly `path,sha256`; `votes` SHALL contain exactly two distinct `{path,sha256}` references. These references SHALL resolve contained immutable files. Each substantive approving vote SHALL bind the same projection-file digest and applicable grant scope; no caller-supplied override or unbound envelope selection is permitted. The projection file SHALL commit the predecessor master path/digest, ledger owner `MC01`, selected owner `MC02`, current envelope ID, G, N, C, D, historical closure disposition and applicable current campaign/action scope. Referenced historical-disposition files SHALL carry exact path/digest references. The two votes approve this projection and exact grant; specification votes are not resource votes. The final amendment can contain these vote references because votes bind the immutable projection, not the final amendment's own digest. The binding is frozen after the amendment; no self-hash is required.

G and N SHALL be positive integer new seconds/dispatch allowances. C and D SHALL be nonnegative integer explicit closure-only allowances. The predecessor's original `package`, `charges`, `planning_charge_seconds`, `planning_overhead_reserved_seconds`, `package_limit_seconds`, `worker_dispatches`, and `worker_dispatch_limit` SHALL remain unchanged. In this owner mode, `package` SHALL remain MC01. Existing external rows SHALL preserve original `package` ownership; new MC02 debits SHALL append only to `externalPackageCharges` with package MC02 and exact grant/charge/action/candidate/runner identifiers. Existing rows SHALL not be moved or rewritten. Top-level MC01 worker52/52 SHALL not be relabeled as MC02 usage. A missing ownership/disposition commitment SHALL reject `resource-package-projection-unresolved`.

Let M be predecessor `master_limit_seconds` and U the sum of its planning, overhead, all ordinary charges, all external charges and all reserved rows. Historical closure SHALL use C=ceil(max(0,M-U)) and the specified closure row. Let D=max(0,ceil(U+C)-M). The exact reviewed projection SHALL authorize D separately as nonspendable master-closure reconciliation, not new user spendable credit, and set new master limit M+D+G. A pre-existing deficit SHALL be explicitly dispositioned; the producer SHALL not infer authorization from the formula. If D or deficit disposition is omitted or unapproved, a fully reserved G SHALL reject rather than silently increasing the ceiling. All spendable credit SHALL remain bounded by G even if integer ceiling conversion leaves master slack.

The new envelope SHALL record selected owner MC02 and immutable projection/amendment identities, `remainingReservedSeconds=G-S`, `workerDispatches=n`, `workerDispatchLimit=N`, where S and n count only committed new-grant usage. This active MC02 package limit is G, not a lifetime MC02 total. All old envelopes and residual reserves SHALL be unavailable to current actions under the reviewed historical closure disposition. A new reserved row for this envelope SHALL start at G; each debit of c SHALL atomically decrease it by c and append a same-c MC02 external charge. Thus master used after transfer SHALL equal U+C+G, independent of S, and selected available allowance SHALL equal G-S. No historical reserve or master rounding slack may supplement it. Unknown historical costs SHALL remain unknown in immutable dispositions; they cannot create credit. Unresolved applicability of old costs to the active envelope SHALL deny launch.

Top-level `externalWorkerDispatches` SHALL equal its predecessor value plus n; `master_worker_dispatch_limit` SHALL equal predecessor aggregate limit plus N. In the inspected predecessor these are31+n and83+N; aggregate usage is52+31+n. New-grant dispatch count SHALL include every admitted live kind specified by this requirement, while preserving the historical meaning and value of earlier counters. Before a new charge, require S+c<=G, n+1<=N and aggregate usage plusone<=aggregate limit, plus all source/history/attempt/financial bounds. Partial initialization, failure and interruption SHALL not refund charges or decrement committed counts.

For this authenticated owner mode, `records._remaining_from_current` SHALL retain full master summation and call `accounting` current-projection validation before selecting package semantics. Its existing `package not in _owners_of(record.owner)` check SHALL compare the authenticated selected envelope owner MC02 to the campaign owner, while independently requiring the preserved ledger owner MC01. Its existing `package_used=planning+overhead+charges` and top-level package/worker eligibility checks SHALL NOT apply MC01's limits to this MC02 action. They SHALL be replaced only in this mode by selected grant usage S/G and n/N, with aggregate master validation. Legacy profiles SHALL retain existing behavior. Merely setting an envelope owner field or the action profile SHALL not activate this mode without verified amendment/projection/votes.

Final existing runner validation occurs after the producer commits a debit/reserve transfer. A matching prepaid invocation MAY proceed at S=G or n=N only when its exact debit exists, all resulting totals are within limits, and its plugin reservation/master one-shot claim are unconsumed and identity-bound. The reader SHALL not subtract c again from already-debited master or grant totals. Prepaid status SHALL never authorize a second debit or replay. The final limit equality exception SHALL require the exact matching charge/action/candidate/runner, not merely an exhausted envelope with a success-shaped record.
