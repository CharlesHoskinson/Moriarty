# L3 proposal: numeric profile and U0 exit path

## 1. Goal

U0 should give U1 and U2 a versioned, internally consistent contract for the first signed financial stage: exact units, price direction, rounding and remainder ownership, source/Core meaning, failure behavior, and a current Midnight target tuple. It should also show which parts are executable today and which are specified only. U1 then certifies the arithmetic used by that stage; U2 proves and settles it. The roadmap assigns those distinct exits to [U0, U1 and U2](/home/charl/Moriarty/ROADMAP.md:19).

## 2. Findings

**The numeric policy has a useful base.** The owner decision fixes base-per-quote prices, obligation `ceil`, receipt `floor`, exact `none`, domain-qualified integer amounts and checked widths ([decision](/home/charl/Moriarty/docs/decisions/u0-numeric-profile-decision.md:5)). The [profile](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/numeric-profile.json:49) inventories 17 current primitives. I ran `check_u0_numeric_profile.py`: it reported 17 primitives and six open conformance gaps. That result checks the record’s consistency; it does not demonstrate reserve accounting or native financial behavior.

**“Protocol-reserve dust” needs an economic definition before it can be frozen.** The decision assigns every rounding remainder to a protocol reserve ([D2](/home/charl/Moriarty/docs/decisions/u0-numeric-profile-decision.md:11)), while `$.reserveMechanism.status` is `absent` ([profile](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/numeric-profile.json:55)). The sharpest conflict is `prorata-principal-share`: its profile row calls the division a receipt with a reserve beneficiary, but says the truncated principal share stays in accrued discharge ([profile](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/numeric-profile.json:272)). The code confirms `dA = n - dP`; no settlement amount is left to post elsewhere ([lifecycle](/home/charl/Moriarty/experiments/moriarty-language/src/successor/financial-lifecycle.ts:1412)). Crediting the reserve as well could double count. Accrual and price-conversion remainders also need an explicit unit, precision and funded posting rule before “reserve beneficiary” has executable meaning. The current checker checks cited declarations, not whether division posts a remainder ([checker](/home/charl/Moriarty/scripts/check_u0_numeric_profile.py:18)).

**Rounding direction is recorded but still author selectable at material sites.** The profile marks accrual, both expression-division roles, and origination/repayment conversion as open gaps ([profile](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/numeric-profile.json:60)). Origination and repayment even share `convertNominal` while requiring opposite directions ([profile](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/numeric-profile.json:258)). U0 must state how result role is derived and bound; merely recording that an author chose `floor` or `ceil` cannot enforce the policy.

**Aeon identifies a source contract gap, not a demonstrated ledger exploit.** `/5` `repay` requires nonnegative nominal and positive payment, then writes phase and paid counters and emits transfer/repay operations ([source](/home/charl/Moriarty/experiments/moriarty-language/spec/successor/examples/loan-lifecycle.mori:118)). The uncommitted [Aeon proposal](/home/charl/Moriarty/openspec/changes/aeon-refinement-integration/proposal.md:10) reports an SMT model satisfying those written guards with negative principal. Its retained [model](/home/charl/Moriarty/openspec/changes/aeon-refinement-integration/evidence/verification-discharge/counterexample-repay.txt:7) supports that narrower claim. The callable lifecycle kernel separately rejects insufficient balance, excess outstanding and component over-allocation ([transfer guard](/home/charl/Moriarty/experiments/moriarty-language/src/successor/financial-lifecycle.ts:1458), [repay guards](/home/charl/Moriarty/experiments/moriarty-language/src/successor/financial-lifecycle.ts:1564)). The SMT model has not been shown to settle or to pass that whole path. Aeon itself labels `/6` specified only and says its handwritten repay model is not a proof about the TypeScript kernel ([README](/home/charl/Moriarty/openspec/changes/aeon-refinement-integration/README.md:1), [limitation](/home/charl/Moriarty/openspec/changes/aeon-refinement-integration/requirements/refinement-types.md:803)).

**The present “exit” is a record check.** I ran `check_u0_exit_gate.py`; it reported “contract recorded, capabilities open.” The [receipt](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/EXIT-GATE.md:3) lists 1/84 present source/Core leaves, 0/84 native enforcement, 0/23 covered K rows, and historical pins with no compatible tuple. Those numbers should remain visible. The guarded CLI `status --json` could not open its SQLite store in this read-only environment, so I make no live guarded-status claim.

## 3. Proposed work

1. **Resolve the numeric contract.** Output a successor [numeric decision](/home/charl/Moriarty/docs/decisions/u0-numeric-profile-decision.md:1) and versioned profile, for example `docs/decisions/u0-numeric-profile-decision-v2.md` and `deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-v2/numeric-profile.json`; update the numeric schema, `scripts/check_u0_numeric_profile.py`, and `tests/test_u0_numeric_profile.py`. For each of the 17 rows, state input/output dimensions, result role, directed equation, remainder unit, actual beneficiary and failure on overflow. Decide explicitly whether ProRata’s residual remains accrued or is economically reallocated; require conservation either way. Add independently calculated fractional, exact and overflow vectors. **Exit:** no contradictory beneficiary rule, and every still unimplemented rounded primitive is marked unavailable to the first slice. **Dependency:** owner policy decision D2; **effort: M**. Preserve the September 23 receipt as historical evidence.

2. **Turn repay’s counterexample into a bounded semantic requirement.** Output `deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-v2/repay-obligations.json` and a law-versus-fixture classification alongside the successor source specification. Extend `experiments/moriarty-language/tests/loan-lifecycle.test.mjs` with callable adversarial cases derived from the SMT witness, and add a U0 check that the obligation inventory matches the source. **Exit:** record the evaluator’s actual reject/result and complete state; specify outstanding, payer-balance, allowance, receiver-overflow, counter-overflow and invariant preconditions without treating fixture constants as general laws. Do not claim the SMT model was replayed until that test runs. **Dependency:** task 1’s exact numeric meaning; **effort: M**.

3. **Publish a slice-ready U0 matrix.** Output versioned successors of `source-core-embeddings.json`, `judgments.json`, `k-reconciliation.json`, `target-pins.json`, `enforcement-map.json` and `trust-premises.json`, plus `deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-v2/EXIT-READINESS.md`. Extend the existing `check_u0_*.py` checks and `tests/test_u0_exit_gate.py`; run a source/K differential case for the admitted exact repayment slice, including rejection. **Exit:** all 84 fields have a normative meaning and an implementation placement or an explicit later owner; the admitted slice has source/Core and failure meaning; an inspected, compatible compiler/ZKIRv3/verifier/key/ledger tuple is recorded. Unresolved interfaces remain blockers for dependent claims. **Dependency:** tasks 1–2; **effort: L**.

4. **U1: certify only the arithmetic the first stage uses.** Start with a one-asset, principal-only repayment at exact 1:1 settlement: checked amount construction, comparison, addition/subtraction and range checks, with no price division or ProRata. Output `deliverables/u1-minimum-basis/exact-repay-certificate.json` and retained native artifacts; add `tests/test_u1_exact_basis.py` with valid and adversarial witnesses. **Exit:** native completeness and invalid-witness exclusion under the pinned tuple, call-site preconditions and measured cost, as [U1 requires](/home/charl/Moriarty/ROADMAP.md:22). **Dependency:** slice-ready U0; **effort: L**.

5. **U2: connect that stage to a signed public path.** Output a newly authored repayment program, a structurally contrasting exact transfer program and retained ZKIRv3, proof and complete-effect readback under `deliverables/u2-signed-stage/`; add `tests/test_u2_signed_stage.py` and actual Preview observations. Sign gross debit, fee cap and denomination, recipient, asset/domain, liability discharge, phase/failure policy and replay authority. **Exit:** both programs use the same supported compiler path; valid alternatives succeed and tampered intent, fees, state, effects or replay fail at the bound native/ledger boundary, consistent with [U2’s exit](/home/charl/Moriarty/ROADMAP.md:23). **Dependency:** task 4; **effort: L**.

## 4. Exit definition and U3 handoff

Use two explicit labels: **U0 contract frozen** for a coherent versioned semantic/profile matrix, and **U0 slice ready** for the additional executable reference controls and current compatible target tuple in task 3. Call **“U0 done” only when both hold**. Six rounded-primitive gaps may remain open if the initial grammar excludes them and U1/U2 own their qualification. U0 does not require 84/84 native enforcement, a native certificate, or Preview settlement; those are U1/U2 and later claims. It does require a real target tuple rather than calling historical pins “current.” Feasibility experiments may help resolve a blocked tuple without being counted as U1 acceptance.

The exact repayment stage creates a short path to useful signed behavior while preserving the next discriminator. U3 must then exercise the [11 A gross cap, including at most 1 A fees, for at least 20 B](/home/charl/Moriarty/ROADMAP.md:36), exact recipient/domain and partial residual duty. Its cross-asset conversion and fee denomination need the resolved U0 numeric semantics and U1 certificates; current `/5` same-name unit/settlement restrictions cannot be presented as that two-asset capability ([alignment](/home/charl/Moriarty/docs/MORIARTY-LANGUAGE-REQUIREMENTS-ALIGNMENT.md:7)).

## 5. Risks, exclusions and disagreements

Do not make the full Aeon `/6` parser, SMT discharge system, linear rights or gadget deletion a U0 prerequisite. Retain its repay finding as an obligation and negative control; implement refinements when the supported encoding, evaluator replay and correspondence are available. A solver verdict is not a native or ledger proof. Do not credit fractional dust to an integer reserve without a representation and conservation rule, silently treat ProRata reallocation as reserve income, or use exact 1:1 repayment to claim rounded conversion support.

I disagree with treating the current passing U0 checker as an exit gate: its own success text says capabilities are open, and the tuple is unresolved ([receipt](/home/charl/Moriarty/deliverables/u0-semantic-contract-2026-09-23/EXIT-GATE.md:16)). I also disagree with reading Aeon’s negative SMT model as proof of an accepted negative balance; the runtime guards and ledger path need separate tests. Conversely, requiring all 84 fields to be natively enforced before U1 would move U2 and later work into U0. The right U0 requirement is complete, honest field disposition plus demonstrated readiness for one admitted slice.

## 6. Ranked recommendations

1. Resolve the ProRata/reserve contradiction and version the numeric contract.
2. Reproduce repay’s adversarial cases through the callable evaluator, then freeze the first exact signed slice and its exclusions.
3. Establish a current compatible target tuple and a separate slice-readiness gate before claiming U0 completion.
