# MIL/2 research questions from audit

These are **open repository questions**, not primary-source facts and not resolved conclusions. Their design locators are included so the deep-research sprint can check them.

1. **Escrow solver-class question.** MIL/2 §7 calls its authoring check a difference-logic decision procedure (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:218`), while §4.4 says sums, `min`/`max`, and `k_of_n` lift to QF-LIA (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:160–165`). Which exact generated formula fragment does the check decide, and is its claimed logic class correct for every admitted Φ₀ escrow form?
2. **Branch footprint question.** The sample transfer sends asset A from escrow E to `counterparty` (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:313`), while the sample footprint lists writes to E and `balance(midnight.preview, owner, B)` (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:316–317`). Does footprint derivation include every branch's counterparty-A and owner-A balance changes, and what does §9's derived-set checker compute for this sample (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:239–250`)?
3. **Unfilled-hole denotation question.** MIL/2 gives `Accepted(I[σ]) ⊆ Accepted(I)` per concrete filling (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:228–234`) and says unfilled-hole footprint cells evaluate to unknown (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:249`). What is the exact denotation of `Accepted(I)` while holes remain unfilled, and how is that denotation used in a finite circuit check?
4. **ZKIR shape-premise question.** The Agda README states statement soundness from a satisfying witness with a `WShape` premise (`source-text/zkir-formal-readme.md:38–48`) and describes a canonical-witness faithfulness theorem under run-shape facts (`:27–37`). Which shape assumptions are needed to validate the planned MIL/2-to-ZKIR correspondence claim?
5. **Timeout/nonreceipt question.** IBC ICS-004 uses proof of packet-receipt absence on unordered timeout paths (`source-text/ibc-ics004.md:1600–1617`), distinguishes timeout-receipt checks for ordered-allow-timeout channels (`:1621–1632`), and says timeout can substitute for packet confirmation with relayer participation (`:915–919`). Which authenticated absence, timeout, and liveness assumptions are required for MIL/2 recovery viability?

**Evidence labels.** Items 1–3 and the framing of item 4 are repository observations/questions. The protocol mechanisms cited in item 5 are source facts. Every proposed mapping from those mechanisms to a MIL/2 obligation remains an inference pending further research.
