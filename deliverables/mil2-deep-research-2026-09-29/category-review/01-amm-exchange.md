# MIL/2 category review 01 — AMMs and exchanges

**Date:** 2026-09-29. **Status:** source review of a specified-only proposal. No MIL/2 implementation, native proof, or Midnight settlement is established by this report. The current [MIL/2 design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md) explicitly says that nothing in it is implemented or milestone accepted (lines 1–4). Its [proposed semantics](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex) is likewise a research draft (lines 36–42). The [MIL/1 category map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md) is superseded and used here only to identify claims that need re-derivation (lines 1–6; [execution summary](../../../concepts/intent-language/EXECUTION-SUMMARY.md), lines 18–22).

**Scope.** Trader intent, constant product and related pool execution, liquidity provision, routing, RFQ, order books and batch clearing. “Covered” below means that the proposed MIL/2 language states a bounded need with a plausible acceptance carrier; it does **not** mean that compilation or ledger enforcement has been demonstrated. “Partial” means that only some necessary semantics are present. “Open” means that the needed relation or constructor is unspecified. “Out of scope” means an explicit MIL/2 product cut. The previous [R1 review](../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R1-amm-exchange.md) supplies the category requirement inventory, especially lines 81–126 and 167–196; its verdicts describe the older language and must not be carried forward unchanged.

Φ₀ cannot state `x'·y' ≥ x·y`; the limb rule and bounded-width proof must be discharged for Φ₁ (lines 167–171). ## Corrections to the MIL/1 category map

1. **Pool invariant:** MIL/1 called `x·y ≥ k` expressible through cross-multiplication ([map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), line 30). MIL/2’s Φ₀ permits multiplication by *literals*; variable products and pool math are Φ₁ ([design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 135–163). `pre`/`post` fixes the missing state boundary but does not make the invariant U0-ready.
2. **LP shares and fees:** MIL/1 treated LP issue/redeem as expressible and fee tiers as library work ([map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), lines 32–33). MIL/2 supplies a better share vocabulary and an `issue` right, yet share arithmetic is deferred and the pool operation, share supply mapping, fee tier and LP accrual remain undefined ([design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 80–89, 189–193).
3. **Route completion:** MIL/1 relied on a false monotonicity claim ([map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), line 34). MIL/2 replaces it with per-filling acceptance refinement and limits route holes to effect grammar ([design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 154–156, 221–234). The [semantics draft](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), lines 108–127, gives the unfilled intent a trace denotation, but native refinement is still an obligation.
4. **Clearing:** MIL/1 left a disjoint-footprint episode join as a possible batch mechanism ([map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), lines 35–39). MIL/2 expressly says shared pool writes make that construction wrong, reserves signer-set arity, and defers a real n-party clearing relation ([design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 330–355).
5. **Escrow:** MIL/1’s release/refund sketch was rejected by its own meta-rule. MIL/2 makes `pending` legal and resolves overlapping guards by signed priority and one terminal tombstone ([design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 195–219). The current showcase still has a faulty footprint ([semantics draft](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), lines 208–227).

## Category judgment and next evidence

**Repository fact:** The [roadmap](../../../ROADMAP.md), lines 21–27 and 44–48, assigns U0 the semantic contract, U1 certified primitives, U2 a general native stage, U3 conditional settlement, U4 advanced evidence/Φ₁ in the MIL/2 plan, U5 coordinated solvers, and U6 AMM library conformance. The [MIL/2 design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), lines 340–365, adds its own milestone placement and six unproved freeze obligations. No category-level “covered in production” verdict follows from either plan.

**Inference:** MIL/2 is a credible *trader-intent envelope* for a bounded spot swap: it can bind who pays, what they receive, fees, time, authority, evidence and recovery policy. It is **partial** as an AMM language and **open** as a general exchange language. The decisive missing semantic artifact is a reusable pool transition that reads authenticated reserves, computes exact-input and exact-output amounts under a declared numeric policy, writes the complete two-asset effects, and preserves a stage-bound pool invariant. A separate multi-signer relation is required for order books and auctions.

**Minimum reviewable evidence sequence:** (1) correct the `AcquireB` footprint and define transfer/alias derivation; (2) write a two-asset constant-product library contract with a bootstrap LP share rule, fee and remainder beneficiary, exact-input and exact-output vectors, and malformed-reserve negative cases; (3) map every effect, pool invariant and intent clause to source/Core/K/native/ledger checks; (4) demonstrate an accepted Midnight stage and hostile mutations before claiming settlement coverage; (5) specify and then test n-party clearing separately. These are recommendations, not accepted milestone changes. The [proposed semantics](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), lines 239–257, already lists the cross-layer proof obligations that the first four steps must discharge.
