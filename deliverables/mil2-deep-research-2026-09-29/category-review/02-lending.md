# MIL/2 category review 02 — lending and borrowing

**Date:** 2026-09-29. **Status:** source review of a specified-only proposal; no MIL/2 implementation or U milestone acceptance is claimed. **Verdict vocabulary:** **covered** means the proposed grammar and relation give a coherent carrier for the stated bounded case; **partial** means a carrier exists but a necessary rule is missing or deferred; **open** means no sufficient carrier is specified; **out of scope** means MIL/2 expressly excludes the case. All verdicts are *design coverage*, not executable or native coverage.

**Evidence and method.** I read [MIL/2 design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), the [execution summary](../../../concepts/intent-language/EXECUTION-SUMMARY.md), the [proposed semantics PDF source](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), [roadmap](../../../ROADMAP.md), the predecessor [MIL/1 category map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), and the earlier [R2 lending review](../../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R2-lending.md). The earlier R2 review identifies the DeFi taxonomy and existing Moriarty implementation gaps; this report re-evaluates its needs against MIL/2. I ran the required guarded `status --json`: capability `SP01.6 loan-swap-subset`, implementation/repair blocked by stale binding/candidate input, missing current accounting, unavailable resource state, and unresolved operational history; there were no pending transactions. I ran no tests, builds, proofs, or Preview transactions.

## Category judgment

MIL/2 gives a plausible **single-domain, collateralized, fixed-rate loan** a typed obligation, an encumbrance, debtor consent, evidence-conditioned guards, third-party enforcement authority, and persistent stage linkage. This is a meaningful design advance over the present source/Core loan evaluator, whose existing obligation lifecycle is the one lending path supported by retained K evidence ([R2 §3.1 K10](../../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R2-lending.md)). The proposal remains specified-only ([design lines 1–4](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md)); its six freeze obligations are unproved ([lines 363–365](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md)). The PDF adds a candidate roll-forward equation and lock invariant, but expressly labels them proposed and leaves native correspondence open ([PDF source lines 171–189, 229–257](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex)).

For **pooled, variable-rate, isolated, undercollateralized, and impaired-debt markets**, the design gives some vocabulary but does not yet specify the full transitions, risk policies, or cross-position invariants. A DeFi category is covered only when the specific family and its controls can be stated and enforced; an `Obligation` type alone does not establish a lending market.

## Corrections to the MIL/1 lending map

The [MIL/1 lending example and verdicts at lines 41–75](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md) are historical, not a MIL/2 coverage register. Its `refund never` and `anchored(price)` forms are invalid in MIL/2: `never` was removed, `deadline none` is legal, and anchored evidence is a source-set typing condition bound to an authenticated read [design 126–156, 173–187, 195–219](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md). Its liquidation guard uses `mulDiv`, which is Φ₁ and deferred [design 80–89, 158–165](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md). Its “bad debt, backstop, seniority expressible” verdict conflates `rank` with an actual waterfall; the latter remains unspecified. Its claim that simple interest is expressible by the current evaluator is an existing implementation observation, not MIL/2 acceptance. Its claim that default is defined lacks a default predicate or transition in MIL/2. Flash loans are explicitly excluded as a product decision, not a logical consequence of atomic stages [design 330–333](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md).

## Priority obligations for lending

1. **Freeze one bounded loan relation:** origination with consent and funded transfer; exact principal/accrued/outstanding invariant; partial repayment; full discharge; collateral lock/release; one fresh-price threshold; one fixed-bonus partial seizure; explicit residual debt. State numeric units, rounding, opening/maintenance thresholds, and failure effects.
2. **Prove the six MIL/2 freeze obligations on that relation:** total rejection, source-set preservation, aggregate lock safety, completion refinement, recovery viability, and derived-footprint containment [design 363–365](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md). Use the PDF's more precise lock and native correspondence predicates [PDF 229–257](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex).
3. **Bind the full stage to native acceptance:** exact signed intent and `ConsentRef`, price provenance, opening/closing liability, payment/seizure effects, authenticated locks, authority budget, predecessor, failure policy and terminal/replay state. The canonical stage statement requires these classes of data [consolidated design 40–48](../../../docs/MORIARTY-CONSOLIDATED-DESIGN.md), while U0–U2 and U6 still require actual enforcement and conformance evidence [roadmap 19–28](../../../ROADMAP.md).
4. **Scope later lending families explicitly:** Φ₁ share math and rate/index policies; market isolation; undercollateralized obligor/recourse; impairment and loss waterfall; and future flash traces. Do not count these families under a generic loan pass. U6 owns all retained DeFi rows and held-out behavior, with separate semantic, native, local and Preview evidence [roadmap 27, 32, 48](../../../ROADMAP.md).

**Inference boundary.** The scenario designs and “plausible in Φ₀” judgments above are my derivations from the cited types and atoms. They are not claims that the current parser accepts those examples, that a compiled circuit enforces them, or that Midnight has settled them. The current MIL/2 design explicitly says no milestone has accepted it [design 1–4](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md).
