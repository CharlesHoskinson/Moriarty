# MIL/2 DeFi category 6: governance and protocol administration

**Review status:** source review of a specified-only proposal, 2026-09-29. No MIL/2 program, compiler, proof, or Midnight settlement was executed for this review. The verdicts describe what the *written design* can specify; none means demonstrated native coverage.

**Sources and notation.** `D` means [DESIGN-MIL2.md](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), `M` means the [MIL/1 category map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md), `S` means the [MIL/2 execution summary](../../../concepts/intent-language/EXECUTION-SUMMARY.md), `P` means the [proposed semantics PDF source](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), and `R6` means the [earlier governance review](../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R6-governance.md). Line references below are to these checked-in files. `M` is legacy: its governance rows were written against MIL/1 and `S:134-138` explicitly calls for re-derivation. `R6` assessed the earlier U0 design and implementation, so its absence findings cannot be carried forward without checking MIL/2.

## Category judgment

**Repository observation:** MIL/2 adds eight scoped authority rights, including `amend`, `issue`, and `enforce`; bounded budgets, windows, delegability, and revocation (`D:189-193`). It adds `policy(id)` and `allowance(authId)` to the cell vocabulary (`D:236-250`), time and threshold predicates (`D:139-149`), signed completion bounds (`D:221-234`), and ledger-linked history (`D:31-46`). These are meaningful carriers for governance *effects* on financial state. The product contract requires per-contract claim and verifier semantics and explicit scope for upgrades and application governance (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md:62`).

**Inference:** The defensible boundary is: MIL/2 can aim to check a bounded administrative transition and its effect on existing claims; choosing governors, conducting deliberation, and authenticating the outcome of an external vote remain application or trust assumptions. This reconciles the taxonomy's statement that governance is outside Core (`wiki/defiformal-taxonomy.md:179-192`) with the product contract's in-scope rule-change effects. The repository has not yet adopted this boundary as a single controlling rule. `M:164-165` states it, but `M` is legacy.

**Overall verdict: partial specified coverage.** The authority vocabulary fixes the earlier “one spending right only” defect. A policy cell, `amend` right, and `after` predicate do **not** yet define a general policy transition, freeze, upgrade, timelock queue, or in-flight claim-preservation rule. The proposed stage relation names `AuthorityFresh`, `HistoryLink`, and `FailurePolicy` (`P:129-154`), but its native path is explicitly open (`P:156-169,239-257`).

## Needs, scenarios, and limits

Verdicts: **covered** = a bounded behavior has a complete written carrier and rule at this abstraction; **partial** = useful syntax or rule exists but a decisive semantic clause is missing; **open** = no adequate MIL/2 carrier/rule; **out of scope** = an external governance process or social guarantee. Even “covered” here would be *specified-only*, not implemented.

## Corrections to the earlier coverage claims

1. `R6:333-350` said Moriarty had no authority vocabulary for rule change or transition suppression. MIL/2 now has `amend`, `issue`, `enforce`, `recover`, windows, delegation, and revocation (`D:189-193`). That changes the *design* verdict for right kinds. It does not change the earlier report's implementation or native-enforcement observations; MIL/2 is explicitly specified-only (`D:1-4`; `S:1-4`).
2. `R6:287,293` said there was no time or threshold carrier. `after(Instant)` and `k_of_n` plus threshold escrow custody are present (`D:139-149,199-212`). A queue-to-execution timelock and verified multisignature authority remain open.
3. `M:155-164` says amendment, in-flight policy protection, timelock, and proposal lifecycle are expressible. The first has a right name, but the others require semantic rules or library definitions absent from `D`. In particular, `policy(id)` is a cell, not yet the claimed signed policy-version commitment (`D:239-243`; `P:108-127`).
4. `R6:291-292` remains accurate about missing token voting and snapshot semantics. `M:161` must be downgraded from “Library” as a coverage verdict to “possible application design, library unbuilt.”
5. `R6:295` said there is no residual claimant. MIL/2 adds a named recipient for a specific escrow (`D:210,315`) and reserve accounts to conservation (`D:256`). It still lacks a general protocol surplus rule.

## What would establish demonstrated coverage

**Semantic obligations:** define a typed administrative effect and policy-version state transition; state the authority source and exact grant/revoke/delegate semantics; bind each existing position to a policy or obtain applicable consent for change; specify pause scope and allowed recovery; define queue/veto/execute if timelocks are claimed; define a residual-claimant rule if treasury coverage is claimed. Add hostile traces for stale grants, threshold replay, a beneficiary-changing upgrade, paused repayment, and a queued action whose approval was revoked. These are recommendations inferred from the gaps above, not existing MIL/2 rules.

**Native obligations:** discharge source-to-Core/K/native correspondence and O1–O6 (`P:239-257`), including the exact signed template, authority and revocation state, predecessor/policy head, complete effects and fees, and persistent liabilities. `ROADMAP.md:21-28` gives U0/U2/U3/U5/U6 acceptance owners; only U5 explicitly owns delegation and migration. A governance fixture must pass an actual proof and Midnight readback with adversarial mutations before any row is called demonstrated. No such result is claimed here.
