# MIL/2 category review 04: derivatives

**Status:** specified-only review, 2026-09-29. This is a derivation from the current MIL/2 design, not evidence of a compiler, proof, or Midnight acceptance. The earlier [R4 review](../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R4-derivatives.md) supplies requirements D1–D15; it assessed the older source and stage contracts. [CATEGORY-MAP.md](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md#4-derivatives) assesses MIL/1 and is historical input, not a MIL/2 verdict. The [proposed semantics PDF source](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex) is a later research candidate, not an accepted implementation. The [roadmap](../../../ROADMAP.md) keeps all U milestones specified-only until demonstrated (lines 7–11).

## Category result

**Repository observation.** MIL/2 supplies useful carriers for a bounded derivative: `Instrument` and signed `Position<I>` (DESIGN-MIL2.md:53–76), clock-indexed time (lines 69–73), an observation with provenance and freshness (lines 173–187), collateral encumbrances (lines 91–102), guarded escrow exits (lines 195–219), and domain-local conservation (lines 252–256). These are language ingredients. It has no derivative position transition, margin account, funding schedule, settlement fixing rule, payoff formula, loss waterfall, or claim split/merge relation. The Φ₀ grammar expressly excludes variable products and the design defers Φ₁ (lines 126–165). The entire intent design is a specified-only proposal (line 4).

**Inference.** A simple, fully collateralized, cash-settled option *could be designed* as a finite sequence of stages if a library specifies the missing instrument state, payoff, fixing, and exercise rules within the admitted arithmetic. A perpetual with cross margin, funding, liquidation and bankruptcy needs more primitives and policy than MIL/2 currently defines. Neither is presently demonstrated. This report uses **covered** for a need whose semantic carrier is stated well enough at the specified level, **partial** for a carrier with missing category rules, **open** for a missing carrier or unresolved design decision, and **out of scope** for an explicit product cut. No verdict here means implemented.

## Worked design pressure cases

**Covered call with physical delivery, inference.** The writer escrows one ETH; the buyer pays a premium. Before expiry, a valid exercise should move strike payment to the writer and ETH to the holder in one accepted local stage; after expiry, a final no-exercise outcome returns ETH. MIL/2 gives typed time, custody and guarded terminal transitions (DESIGN-MIL2.md:69–73, 195–219), but the design's `exercised(opt)` atom has no effect that sets it, and two independent escrows do not establish atomic exchange across them. One must specify a shared stage and write footprint covering ETH, strike asset, premium, exercise state and receipts; prove one terminal branch and preserve fee/gross limits. The MIL/1 example at CATEGORY-MAP.md:103–112 uses `refund never`, `Signed<ETH>` and `equity`, which MIL/2 removes or replaces (DESIGN-MIL2.md:76, 154–156, 195–219). It cannot be reused as a well-typed MIL/2 program.

**Perpetual margin failure, inference.** A mark moves against a short. A signed margin policy must compute equity from spendable collateral, unrealized P&L and owed funding, select a permitted liquidation, and allocate any shortfall in stated order. MIL/2 has position, encumbrance, authority and evidence carriers; the valuation and waterfall are missing. `fresh(mark, Δ)` cannot establish that a sale clears at the mark or within Δ. A closeout promise needs market-depth and actor assumptions as well as a safety rule.

**Cross-domain option fixing, inference.** A fixing on another domain may be imported only under a signed policy and linked to a later accepted stage (DESIGN-MIL2.md:173–187, 252–256). The local stage must not represent foreign payment as its own effect. A refund after a deadline requires evidence of the relevant negative outcome or an explicit trust policy; time alone cannot prove the foreign exercise did not happen (RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md:39–45).

## Corrections to the MIL/1 derivatives map

1. The old `Signed<A>` representation merged supply/balance deltas and instrument exposure. MIL/2 uses `Position<I>` and `Delta<A>` (DESIGN-MIL2.md:65–76). This repairs identity, not position lifecycle or P&L.
2. The old `CoveredCall` text is not a valid MIL/2 example: `never`, `equity` and `collateral_value` are absent; `refund never` is replaced by a legal pending state and explicit transition policy (CATEGORY-MAP.md:103–112; DESIGN-MIL2.md:154–156, 195–219).
3. An `Encumbrance` plus `enforce` does not itself define maintenance margin or liquidation. MIL/2 states a debtor-consented enforcement policy and lock sum invariant, with the latter still an obligation (DESIGN-MIL2.md:91–102, 189–193).
4. A ledger-linked Episode is not a periodic funding ratchet. A period index, authenticated mark, funding calculation and no-skip/no-replay rules must be added; the old map's “Expressible” row is too strong (CATEGORY-MAP.md:120; DESIGN-MIL2.md:31–46).
5. `ClaimClass` and a signed order over seizes do not define ADL, an insurance fund or socialized loss. The old map's “Expressible” row is too strong (CATEGORY-MAP.md:121–123; DESIGN-MIL2.md:91–102, 189–193).
6. `fresh` constrains observation age, while a liquidation latency promise is conditional on inclusion, witness and keeper availability. MIL/2 correctly treats recovery viability as an obligation under named assumptions (CATEGORY-MAP.md:124; DESIGN-MIL2.md:219).

## Evidence and next obligations

**Repository observation.** The design itself lists six pre-freeze obligations: total Φ evaluation, source-set preservation, encumbrance sum safety, acceptance refinement, recovery viability and footprint containment (DESIGN-MIL2.md:363–365). The research draft adds a precise candidate interpretation for unfilled-template acceptance and a stronger native-to-source correspondence goal (MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:108–127). It also finds that the general Φ₀ escrow check needs more than difference logic and that the showcase footprint omits balance writes (RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md:7–29). These gaps affect derivative guard, exercise and liquidation proofs directly.

**Recommendation, inference.** The first derivatives acceptance slice should be a single-domain, fully collateralized, cash-settled European call with one authenticated fixing and no dynamic margin. Define the instrument and exercise cells, fixing policy, positive-part payoff with explicit units and rounding, premium/claim/collateral effects, branch priority, unique exercise and redemption marks, and complete write footprints. Then require source/Core/K differential traces and native readback with wrong fixing, wrong recipient, double exercise, early exercise, unpaid premium and missing collateral as invalid controls. Follow with a separately scoped period-indexed funding and liquidation slice. U6 is the library/conformance milestone; U0–U4 supply semantic, arithmetic, settlement and history prerequisites (ROADMAP.md:19–27, 44–48). No category family should be counted accepted from this report.
