# MIL/2 category review 03 — stablecoins and synthetic assets

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** design review, specified only. No compiler, prover, K rule, or ledger execution was tested for this report.  
**Verdict scope:** “covered” means MIL/2 states a usable carrier at the proposal level. It does not mean implemented or demonstrated. “Partial” means a carrier exists but a required rule is absent or deferred. “Open” means the carrier itself is missing. “Out of scope” is a declared product cut.

## Category boundary and overall finding

Stablecoins require authorized creation and destruction of token supply, exact backing and liability accounting, redemption, and a response to failing collateral or reserves. Synthetic assets add payoff, hedge, and sometimes shared debt pool rules. Fiat backed tokens additionally depend on external custody and issuer obligations. These are distinct use cases; a generic `supply` field alone does not establish any of them. The prior DeFi review identifies those needs as R1–R25 in `deliverables/u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R3-stablecoins.md:298-324`, with the reference requirements at `:112-229`.

**Finding (repository observation and inference):** MIL/2 substantially repairs the *language carrier* for a collateralized issuance stage: nominal asset identity, an `issue` right, domain qualified supply state, conservation, encumbrance state, typed price evidence, and guarded escrow are specified (`concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md:50-102,126-149,173-193,195-219,252-258`). It does **not** yet cover the stablecoin or synthetic category as a whole. No category operation has a complete typed effect grammar and executable semantics, and the U0 profile has no accepted issuance slice. The latest proposed formalization explicitly leaves the source/Core/K/native correspondence open (`deliverables/mil2-deep-research-2026-09-29/MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:156-169`). U6 owns complete DeFi conformance (`ROADMAP.md:27`); all U milestones remain specified only (`ROADMAP.md:11`).

## Need-by-need coverage

| Core need and realistic scenario | MIL/2 carrier, with exact source lines | Specified coverage and remaining obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Authorized mint and burn.** A vault locks 150 units of collateral and issues 100 units of USDm; redemption burns USDm. | `AssetId` is nominal and `Asset.repr` can be synthetic (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:50-61`); `Delta<A>` represents supply and balance change (`:63-76`); `supply(d,asset)` is a Φ read (`:126-137`); `issue` is scoped to `(domain, asset)` (`:189-193`); conservation is `Σ balance deltas = declared supply delta` (`:252-256`). The proposed stage relation makes supply and the signed `issue` right conjuncts (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:129-150,171-178`). | **Partial.** The separate supply right and general law repair old R1–R4 at the design level. A concrete `mint`/`burn` effect form, issue grant/revoke/consumption transitions, exact refusal code, and source/Core/K/native lowering are still missing. Existing supply leaves were previously `NOT_ENFORCED` (`R3-stablecoins.md:323-324`); this proposal supplies no executed replacement. |
| **Collateralized issuance and debt accounting.** A borrower opens a CDP; issuance must create a debtor liability and prevent the same collateral from backing an unbounded second mint. | Obligations carry debtor, creditor, principal, accrued, outstanding, rank, and debtor consent; encumbrances name a *set* of obligations (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:91-102`). Φ reads `outstanding`, `locked`, `pre` and `post` state (`:126-154`). The lock invariant is `Σ active locks ≤ balance` (`:102`); the proposed formalization includes liability roll-forward, funded AccrualFirst repayment, and reserved-lock safety (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:178-187,229-237`). | **Partial.** An issuer could state a guard linking an issuance delta, new obligation and locked collateral; that composite operation is an inference, not a supplied MIL/2 rule. Prove the lock sum invariant, consent binding, complete effect capture, and funded debt/supply correspondence. `DESIGN-MIL2.md:365` lists these freeze obligations as unproved. |
| **Collateral health and oracle price.** A CDP may mint only if the collateral value exceeds a signed threshold at a recent price. | `Price<B,Q,s>`, typed comparisons, `pre/post`, `obs.value`, `fresh`, `final`, and `attested` (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:63-74,104-149`); evidence class and domain are type indices, with source sets intended to prevent laundering (`:173-187`). | **Partial.** A fixed, literal-coefficient ratio can be expressed if units and the required price conversion are pinned. MIL/2 has no complete collateral-value expression or variable-times-variable arithmetic in Φ₀; Φ₁ reserves it for U4 (`:156-171,340-350`). Native anchoring to an authenticated read and non-laundering are obligations (`:185,365`). The proposal’s claim that all Φ₀ escrow patterns use difference logic is too broad: the separate semantics paper flags the general guard fragment as wider (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:37,102-106`). |
| **Supply caps and debt ceilings.** A protocol limits minting per vault, collateral class and whole asset. | `supply(d,asset)`, `pre/post`, typed inequalities, signed authority budgets, and the complete cell vocabulary (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:126-149,189-193,236-249`). | **Partial.** A single asset cap such as `post(supply(d,USDm)) ≤ cap` is expressible in the proposal. Per-collateral-class and global debt ceilings need named, authenticated aggregate cells and atomic reservation across concurrent stages; neither is specified. Quantity widths and `u128` overflow must reject (`:122-124,167-171`). |
| **Redemption and peg support.** A holder burns 100 USDm and receives 100 reserve units within a signed window; a delayed request remains pending. | Escrow has legitimate pending state, guarded release/refund, priority, and tombstone (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:195-219`); `delivered` and time predicates are in Φ (`:139-149`); stage effects are domain local (`:252-256`). | **Partial.** This can express a conditional local redemption template. The mint/burn request → accepted → externally settled lifecycle, reserve ownership, par conversion and rounding are not defined as a library or typed transition. A deadline is not proof of external nonexecution (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:191-205`). Stability fees, holder savings rates and arbitrage windows are also protocol rules still to specify; the prior review found no complete carrier (`R3-stablecoins.md:311-312,315`). |
| **Liquidation and loss allocation.** A price drop permits an authorized keeper to seize locked collateral, repay some debt, and record remaining bad debt against a backstop. | `enforce` is bounded by a debtor-consented policy; competing seizes need a signed total order (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:189-193`). `seized(enc)`, outstanding debt, priority, and encumbrance sets are available (`:91-102,126-149`). The stage relation carries liabilities and residual duties (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:129-150,178-189`). | **Partial.** A narrow seizure guard and debt roll-forward can be proposed. The liquidation transition, proceeds pricing, auction/bonus rounding, seniority payout, residual claimant and bad-debt socialization are not specified. The prior R3 report identifies loss allocation and backstop as separate requirements (`R3-stablecoins.md:181-189,226-228`). Need an explicit loss waterfall and hostile controls for under-collateralized states. |
| **Reserve attestations and issuer identity.** A fiat issuer presents a dated reserve report from an independent attester before honoring redemption. | Asset metadata includes issuer, and nominal IDs separate same-ticker deployments (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:50-61`). `Obs<T,ε,d>` carries anchored/imported/attested evidence with freshness and source-set propagation (`:173-187`); `attested(obs,k,n)` is a proposition (`:139-149`). | **Partial.** MIL/2 can name an issuer and an attestation premise. It does not define reserve portfolio state, legal obligor or jurisdiction, attestation subject/assurance/scope/periodicity, attester independence, or recourse. An attestation cannot prove an off-chain reserve balance by itself. The reference inventory requires those discriminators (`R3-stablecoins.md:197-202,213-217,255-263`). Imported evidence remains a named trust premise until U4 (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:187,350`). |
| **Depeg, reserve shortfall and emergency shutdown.** A reserve falls below liabilities; issuance pauses and all holders acquire a defined pro-rata claim. | `policy(id)` and `supply` are state cells; authority has revocation; escrow terminal outcomes and `recover` exist (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:189-219,236-256`). | **Open.** These pieces do not define a system-wide pause, complete holder/liability enumeration, fixed shutdown price, pro-rata allocation, or claimant priority. A bounded stage can update named cells, but it cannot infer that it has covered *all* claims. Authenticated completeness and native history would be needed. The prior review records this gap (`R3-stablecoins.md:317,461-470`). |
| **Rebasing and elastic supply.** A token applies a global index change so every nominal account balance changes while fixed shares remain. | MIL/2 has `Share<P,C>` and pool conversion terms, but `sharesFor`/`assetsFor` are Φ₁ (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:80-89,158-162`). Its general conservation law accounts for explicit balance and supply deltas (`:252-256`). | **Open.** No global balance index, conversion adapter or rebase transition is defined. Treating rebasing as ordinary explicit per-account supply deltas may be impossible to enumerate in a bounded stage. The older R3 report warns that this is a distinct accounting model (`R3-stablecoins.md:204-207,313,450-459`). |
| **Synthetic issuance and backing.** A synthetic dollar is minted against collateral while a hedge and shared debt pool maintain target exposure. | Synthetic representation and `Position<I>` are typed (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:50-76`); issue, locks, obligations, and observation evidence provide component carriers (`:91-102,173-193`). | **Partial for simple collateralized synthetic issuance; open for a shared debt pool and maintained hedge.** No pool-level debt distribution, hedge state, funding payment, off-exchange custody mirror, revenue capture or maintained-neutrality invariant is given. The reference study explicitly calls those missing elements (`R3-stablecoins.md:264-267,319`). Φ₁ is required for many ratio and share calculations (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:158-165,350`). |
| **Circular or reflexive backing.** A protocol token backs a stable asset whose demand in turn supports that protocol token. | Nominal `AssetId`, representation links, and typed `balance`/`supply` cells let a designer identify both assets (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:50-61,126-137,236-243`). | **Open.** No explicit asset-indexed `backs` relation or cycle test exists. The older kernel analysis says an element-only graph cannot expose this dependency (`R3-stablecoins.md:219-224,318`). Naming both tokens is insufficient to assess backing reflexivity. |
| **Cross-domain burn and mint.** A holder burns token on domain A and receives a canonical claim on domain B. | One stage executes on one domain; a foreign credit is imported evidence for a later accepted stage, and the Episode links stages by authenticated commitments (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:31-46,252-256`). | **Partial.** Two domain-local supply transitions can be represented as an Episode, with a later proof of destination receipt. There is no cross-domain supply equality/form rule for lock-mint versus burn-mint, nor a demonstrated bridge verifier. Unknown destination outcomes cannot be treated as failed and refunded (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:191-205`). Global rollback is outside the product scope (`EXECUTION-SUMMARY.md:86-91`). |
| **Netting cannot hide issuance.** Several redemptions are settled together; the batch must not mint unreported units. | General conservation and complete effects are stated (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:252-258`; `MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:129-150,171-178`). A stage has a shared write set, but U0 admits one signer (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:330-351`). | **Partial.** A transfer-only lane could require `Δsupply = 0`; MIL/2 does not yet specify the lane rule or the multi-signer clearing relation. A join over disjoint footprints is unsuitable when all orders write a pool, as MIL/2 itself explains (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:336`). |

## Corrections to the MIL/1 category map

`concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md:77-98` says the old design “closes the whole category.” That conclusion is superseded. The example at `:79-85` uses `collateral_ratio_ok`, `delta`, `debt_ceiling`, a `supply` effect and an issue policy without a fully specified MIL/2 formation or effect rule. MIL/2 supplies component types and the general conservation law, but does not make that example a checked program. A signed `issue` right must authorize the exact `(domain, asset)` change; a solvency guard must use typed, provenance-preserving observations and checked arithmetic (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:104-124,173-193,252-256`).

The map's “redemption at par” row (`CATEGORY-MAP.md:93`) is partial: `delivered` is a guard atom, not proof that a burn occurred or a fiat transfer settled. Its “peg arbitrage, stability fee, savings rate” library row (`:94`) names work still requiring rate, distribution, and settlement semantics. Its “emergency shutdown” row (`:96`) needs authenticated completeness over all claims; an ordinary escrow terminal transition is too local. Its asset identity row (`:95`) improves token identity but does not establish reserve/issuer legal facts. These are **inferences from the MIL/2 grammar and old category requirements**, not runtime observations.

## Blocking proof and native work

1. **Freeze a checkable issuance profile.** Specify typed `mint`/`burn` effect constructors, issue grants and revocation, negative refusals, supply/custody accounting, and a smallest valid CDP and redemption example. Reconcile the U0 S0 exclusion of nonempty supply changes (`R3-stablecoins.md:321`) with MIL/2's U0 type/authority/representation work (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:340-350`). This is a scope and milestone reconciliation; it does not assert that issuance belongs in S0.
2. **Discharge MIL/2's six stated obligations.** Total evaluation, non-laundering, lock sums, completion refinement, recovery viability, and footprint containment are expressly unproved (`DESIGN-MIL2.md:363-365`). The companion semantics paper also finds the showcased escrow footprint incomplete (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:208-227`), which matters to reserve and liquidation custody writes.
3. **Prove complete effects and native binding.** The native statement must bind the signed template, issuer right, collateral and supply state, observations, all effect and fee lines, liabilities, predecessor head, and tombstone. The proposed semantics lists these fields and says the native path is open (`MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex:239-252`). A host-computed health flag or imported reserve assertion cannot substitute for the required constraints (`:129-154`).
4. **Specify protocol families at U6.** CDP caps and liquidation, reserve-backed request/confirm redemption, peg modules, synthetic shared pools, rebasing, and shutdown each need independent source expectations and semantic/native/local/Preview evidence. U6 is the complete DeFi conformance milestone (`ROADMAP.md:27,48`), with earlier U0–U4 prerequisites (`:21-25`).

No category need above is demonstrated. The report evaluates the proposed design and the written semantics only. Its protocol scenarios are explanatory examples; they are not accepted MIL/2 source programs.
