# MIL/2 category review 08 — staking, restaking, and yield

**2026-09-29. Status: source review of a specified-only design.** “Covered” below means that MIL/2 states a usable semantic carrier for the narrow need, not that a compiler, proof, or ledger result exists. The design itself says nothing is implemented or milestone accepted ([MIL/2 lines 1–4](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L1-L4)); the roadmap likewise leaves full DeFi conformance at U6 ([roadmap lines 19–28](../../../ROADMAP.md#L19-L28)). The previous [R8 review](../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R8-staking-yield.md) supplies the K1–K19 needs, while the [MIL/1 category map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md#L194-L208) is a claim to recheck, not MIL/2 evidence. No evaluator, proof, or network run was performed for this review.

## How a product would use the design

**Share vault.** A depositor sends 100 units of asset A to a pool and receives class C shares. `Share<P,C>`, `totalShares`, `totalAssets`, `sharesFor`, and `assetsFor` give a typed expression for the conversion, including an explicitly selected zero-supply rule and pool-retained rounding residue ([MIL/2 lines 80–89](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L80-L89)). Pool, share, balance, and supply cells can be read and written in one bounded stage ([lines 236–256](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L236-L256)). **Inference:** a vault library could express a deposit or redemption once it defines mint/burn effects, the conversion's exact pre/post snapshot and four method-specific rounding rules. Those are not supplied by the type signatures. The conversion terms are Φ₁, expressly deferred from U0 ([lines 158–165](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L158-L165)). ERC-4626 distinguishes fee-free, caller-independent `convertTo*` from fee-inclusive previews and specifies directional preview bounds ([captured standard lines 124–166](../source-text/erc4626.md#L124-L166), [210–220](../source-text/erc4626.md#L210-L220), [293–303](../source-text/erc4626.md#L293-L303), [374–384](../source-text/erc4626.md#L374-L384)); MIL/2 does not yet define that interface contract.

**Liquid staking and unbonding.** A user deposits A, receives a pool claim, then requests exit while a validator withdrawal is pending. MIL/2 can carry the claim and represent a pending, time-guarded escrow in ledger-linked stages ([lines 31–46](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L31-L46), [195–219](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L195-L219)). **Inference:** a library can build a request, finalize, and claim workflow from these pieces. The design has no named validator event, queue request object, request ordering, liquidity rule, or rate-freeze point. `deadline` alone cannot prove an exit will be available; recovery viability remains an explicit obligation ([lines 217–219](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L217-L219)).

**Restaking and slash.** A user's stake secures two services. One encumbrance can name multiple obligations and priorities; `Σ active locks ≤ balance` forbids duplicate collateral allocation ([lines 91–102](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L91-L102)). `enforce` is bounded by prior debtor consent, and competing seizes of one encumbrance require a signed total order ([lines 189–193](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L189-L193)). **Inference:** this is a credible liability and authority substrate for restaking. It does not specify operator allocation, AVS adjudication, correlated slash budgets, a loss waterfall across claim classes, or how an external validator verdict becomes authenticated evidence. The encumbrance sum rule has not been proved ([lines 363–365](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L363-L365)).

**Yield strategy.** A manager could run bounded stages that move funds and create continuing obligations under scoped authority; the stage and episode machinery carries budgets and history ([lines 31–46](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L31-L46), [189–193](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L189-L193)). **Inference:** harvest, reinvest, and unwind can be library workflows only after a strategy mandate states allowed venues, allocation limits, loss treatment, fee basis, and persistent debt. No policy-over-mechanisms or strategy constructor is defined; the roadmap names vault libraries but does not give that semantic contract ([roadmap lines 44–48](../../../ROADMAP.md#L44-L48)).

## Correction to the MIL/1 coverage claim

The old map calls vault operations, share price, bootstrap, reward accrual, slashing, unbonding, and delegation “Expressible” and leaves only competing slash order partly open ([MIL/1 lines 194–208](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md#L194-L208)). MIL/2 repairs specific mistakes: share identity includes pool and class, conversion has both asset and share totals, `pre/post` relates stage states, encumbrance `against` is a set, and competing seizes require signed order ([MIL/2 lines 63–102](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L63-L102), [126–154](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L126-L154), [189–193](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L189-L193)). Those repairs justify **carriers**, not full category expressibility: Φ₁ conversion is deferred, the four vault method rules are not stated, and the lifecycle and strategy rules above are absent. The summary explicitly instructs re-derivation of the old map ([execution summary lines 130–139](../../../concepts/intent-language/EXECUTION-SUMMARY.md#L130-L139)).

## Gates to demonstrated coverage

1. **Freeze semantic rules:** define vault operation transitions and conversion snapshots; round each direction; specify rewards versus emissions, slash waterfall, delegated liability, queue/finalize/claim rate freeze, and strategy mandates. Resolve share-supply conservation and external validator evidence. These are category rules inferred from the K inventory and ERC-4626; they are not existing MIL/2 rules.
2. **Discharge MIL/2's own six obligations:** Φ totality, evidence source-set preservation, encumbrance sum, acceptance refinement, recovery viability, and derived-footprint containment ([MIL/2 lines 363–365](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L363-L365)). For this category, encumbrance sum, footprint containment, and recovery viability are especially load-bearing.
3. **Build and measure the native path:** Φ₁ arithmetic and wide-product limb lowering must have feasible valid witnesses and hostile invalid witnesses. Its costs motivated deferral ([MIL/2 lines 158–171](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L158-L171)); the proposed caps have not been measured ([lines 268–285](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md#L268-L285)). Then demonstrate source/Core/ZKIR/ledger correspondence and retained DeFi row behavior under U1, U2, and U6 acceptance criteria ([roadmap lines 19–28](../../../ROADMAP.md#L19-L28)).

**Category judgment:** MIL/2 is a substantially better substrate for vaults and restaking than MIL/1, but this category is **partial at the design level and unproved at the product level**. No K item has demonstrated native or ledger coverage, and the retained staking/restaking crosswalk rows remain conformance work, not exclusions.
