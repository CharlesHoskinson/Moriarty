# Category 5 — oracles and observations under MIL/2

**Status:** specified-only design review, 2026-09-29. No compiler, K, native proof, or Preview result is claimed. “Covered” below means the MIL/2 proposal states an adequate core rule for the narrow need; it does not mean implemented. “Partial” means a carrier exists but a material rule or binding is missing. “Open” means MIL/2 does not yet define the needed construct. This review uses [MIL/2 design](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md), the companion [proposed semantics](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex), [roadmap](../../../ROADMAP.md), and the earlier [R5 oracle audit](../../u0-study-2026-09-28/defi-coverage/R5-oracles.md). The [MIL/1 category map](../../../concepts/intent-language/CATEGORY-MAP.md) is historical and is re-evaluated here.

## The core transaction

A borrower pledges 150 A and requests 50 B. The signed policy names feed `A/B`, an allowed evidence class and domain, an observation age of at most five minutes, and the conversion and rounding rule. The stage accepts only if the authenticated feed value, its observed-at time, and the resulting collateral calculation satisfy the lender's ratio. A missing, future-dated, stale, wrong-unit, wrong-feed, or unverified value rejects with a distinct reason. The accepted stage binds the actual observation identity/value and its complete debt and balance effects. This is a *target scenario*, inferred from the kernel comparison in R5 §§2.1–2.2 and MIL/2's constructs; MIL/2 has not demonstrated the full transaction.

## Corrections to the MIL/1 category map

The historical `CATEGORY-MAP.md` §5, lines 128–149 says multi-source, fallback, aggregation/TWAP and circuit breakers are “expressible.” MIL/2 does **not** inherit that verdict. Its §16 item 6 explicitly leaves bounded observation collections open. `attested` and `k_of_n` permit bounded threshold *predicates* but do not define unique sources, aggregation or signed fallback. A comparison can express a simple deviation condition only after both observations have authenticated value, unit, source and time. The old example's `anchored(price)` proposition is also obsolete: MIL/2 makes anchoring a typing side condition (`DESIGN-MIL2.md` §5, lines 181–185). The map's “oracle truth out of scope” conclusion still holds, under the architecture's external trust premise.

## Acceptance work for this category

1. **U0 semantic freeze:** add observation value, unit/scale, feed identity, observed-at time and enforcement locus to the stage statement; specify formation, `fresh`, `final`, `attested`, missing/wrong-unit/wrong-feed rejection and per-operation read derivation. Reconcile the current five-string observation schema and `source/5` regression. This is a recommendation based on `ROADMAP.md:21` and R5 §5 G1–G8; no roadmap row yet explicitly assigns all oracle work.
2. **Source and K evidence:** execute positive and adversarial cases for future/stale/missing/wrong-unit/wrong-feed data, provenance laundering, skipped guards, undeclared reads, and a guard replaced with `true`. Discharge MIL/2 totality and source-set obligations (`DESIGN-MIL2.md` §17, lines 363–365; proposed semantics O1/O2/O6, lines 247–252).
3. **Native and ledger evidence:** bind exact feed/value/time/policy to the signed template, circuit public inputs or authenticated state, complete effects and verifier key; mutate each field and show refusal. The proposed semantics lists the required public statement fields and an open source-to-ledger path at lines 156–169 and 239–257. U2's general path and U3's conditional workflow remain dependent on actual native and Preview evidence (`ROADMAP.md:23–24,38–40`).
4. **Later profile/library work:** define bounded collection, aggregation, distinct-source quorum, signed fallback and oracle-specific circuit breaker policy before claiming full U6 DeFi conformance (`ROADMAP.md:27,48`; `DESIGN-MIL2.md` §16 item 6).
