Five fresh read-only Claude Code sessions ran **sequentially** with requested and returned canonical model `claude-opus-5-5`. Their recorded terminal reasons are all `completed`; aggregate CLI-reported cost is USD 5.0801664. Each session saw the common brief, its own lens and the prior [AMM coverage review](../category-review/01-amm-exchange.md), but no other Opus recommendation. ## Strong agreement

1. **Separate the trader promise from the pool rule.** The signed intent can remain Φ₀: input cap, output floor, fee cap, recipient and validity. A constant-product pool invariant is a **program transition property** evaluated for one concrete witness. All five reviewers reject waiting for general Φ₁ at U4 merely to make the first CPMM stage possible. They recommend a narrow certified operation at U1 and one accepted stage at U2, with the U0 profile reserving its syntax, widths and public inputs. This is a recommendation that changes the MIL/2 placement of pool arithmetic; it must be evaluated against the repo's numeric and native cost gates.
2. **Complete contract and effect acceptance.** The current proposed [stage formula](../MIL2-PROPOSED-SEMANTICS.tex) lacks an explicit conjunct that the selected pool program's transition holds. A stage must check both the trader's signed constraints and the program's own prepared effects/ensures, then require exact correspondence to actual balance and supply writes. This prevents a prover from giving a trader an acceptable output by draining LP reserves.
3. **Use one canonical custody model.** Reserve amounts must be tied to authenticated pool custody balances or to a separately proved reserve-cache invariant. `escrow(E)` cannot silently stand for arbitrary `balance` cells. Complete derived footprints resolve aliases after hole completion; a stale pool version or unexpected recipient rejects.
4. **Account for fees and rounding explicitly.** LP fees retained in reserves still count for the signed fee policy; exact-input output rounds toward the pool, exact-output input rounds against the trader, with named remainder/beneficiary rules. The comparative [Uniswap v2 pair](https://github.com/Uniswap/v2-core/blob/master/contracts/UniswapV2Pair.sol) checks a fee-adjusted post-state invariant and has a separate initial-liquidity path. [ERC-4626](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626) specifies distinct directions for its four vault operations; its one-asset standard is an analogy for LP share accounting, not a two-asset pool specification.
5. **Keep the first slice small.** One domain, one signer, one pre-existing pool, exact input, fixed fee tier, one hop, no imported evidence and no LP mint/burn. Read authenticated reserves at a current pool head, produce four complete balance deltas, check a fee-aware pool property and the user's gross/fee/net conditions, then read back accepted effects. Use a well-formed one-unit over-delivery and a stale-reserve head as hostile controls. An envelope-only rejection does not demonstrate the pool rule.

## Proposed amendment order

1. **U0 semantic boundary:** add `ProgramValid` to the stage relation, classify pool custody/share assets, make complete effects and alias-closed footprints normative, and stratify `pre`/`post` so branch guards cannot depend on the branch's own writes.
2. **U0 numeric/economic profile:** define exact fee line semantics, role-derived rounding, narrow pool width candidate and a real signed surplus policy. Mark any unmeasured field-width or target-interface premise open.
3. **U1 native basis:** certify one exact-input CPMM operation, its integer-width bounds and native constraints for valid and hostile witnesses. Choose quotient/remainder or inequality-plus-tightness only after equivalence and cost analysis.
4. **U2 discriminator:** accept one fresh, single-hop pool swap with authenticated reserves, current version, exact signed budget/recipient, complete four-cell effects and actual ledger readback. Reject one-unit over-delivery, stale version, missing footprint cell, wrong recipient, hidden LP fee and product-invariant violation with otherwise valid envelopes.
5. **U3 and later:** define bounded partial fills and signed intermediate outcomes; add LP mint/burn and route semantics with separate evidence. Reserve shared-write multi-signer clearing for a later profile. Weighted and concentrated liquidity remain separate, higher-cost pool families; [Balancer weighted math](https://github.com/balancer/balancer-v2-monorepo/blob/master/pkg/pool-weighted/contracts/WeightedMath.sol) and the [Uniswap v3 whitepaper](https://app.uniswap.org/whitepaper-v3.pdf) illustrate their additional arithmetic and state obligations.

The five reviews agree on the first-slice direction but do **not** establish its feasibility. The existing [MIL/2 six pre-freeze obligations](../../../concepts/intent-language/DESIGN-MIL2.md) and the repo's actual source/Core/ZKIR/ledger correspondence gates remain open. No test, proof, compilation or Preview transaction was run in these reviews.
