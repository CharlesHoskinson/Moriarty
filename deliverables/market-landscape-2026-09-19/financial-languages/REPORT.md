# Financial languages and platforms versus Moriarty

As of **2026-09-19**. Independent bounded research by GPT-6 Astra, medium. Fifteen official-source URL attempts produced fourteen captures and one 404. Twenty search queries across three research rounds were used. Exact URLs, hashes, source dates, claim classifications and reading ranges are in `sources.json`; the eight-platform matrix is `comparison.json`. No payments, deployments, formal proof runs or vault/product changes occurred. No PDFs were selected. Four same-day Daml source-bank claims were reused with verified hashes.

## Conclusion and strongest counterargument

**Moriarty's ingredients are not individually new.** The strongest argument against building another language is that Daml/Canton already supplies private multiparty financial workflows, Marlowe already supplies a financial DSL with staged onchain semantics, Simplicity already supplies finite programmable spending on a production chain, and Stacks already enforces owner-signed asset constraints. Optional verification tools on Move are also substantially more than type safety. A narrow requirement such as “express a conditional financial agreement safely” already has plausible existing solutions.

The bounded sample did **not establish** a product delivering Moriarty's whole proposed conjunction: permissionless broad DeFi authorship, authenticated signed formal intention, mandatory semantic proof acceptance tied to complete effects and recursive residual duties, private composition, and actual Midnight ZKIRv3 compilation/execution. This is a bounded negative finding, not a novelty theorem. Specifying Midnight as the target distinguishes an implementation requirement; it does not by itself establish an inventive financial-language concept. Moriarty remains a research/design target in this comparison and must meet the same evidence standard applied to competitors. [FL15]

## Existing products and meaningful limits

**Daml/Canton is the strongest practical substitute for private institutional workflows.** Authorization and selective visibility are part of its model; current tooling includes AI-assisted Daml development. The reused settlement study establishes approval/allocation/DvP patterns and explicit cancellation. Transactions execute atomically within a declared synchronizer; relocation and external effects remain separate. This should defeat any claim that multiparty consent, conditional financial workflows or privacy-aware composition originate with Moriarty. It does not demonstrate general mandatory PCD or Midnight compilation. Private synchronizer admission also differs from unrestricted public program deployment. [FL01–02; DS01, DS11, DS14, DS15]

**Marlowe is the closest financial DSL counterexample.** Runtime is officially available on Cardano mainnet. Contract continuations and transaction validation express financial stages directly. However, static analysis is not universally mandatory: warning-producing contracts are allowed, and insufficient account funds can yield partial payment. Offchain actors still initiate progress. Finite contract structure must not be promoted into unconditional liveness or a theorem that every specified payment will occur. [FL03–05]

**Move is an existing verification ecosystem, not merely safer syntax.** Aptos documents explicit developer invocation of its Prover, solver dependencies and vacuity diagnosis. Sui's official announcement describes property verification used during development and audits, alongside resource-oriented typing. Neither selected source establishes that every network transaction must carry a proof of the owner's complete business intention. Sui's cryptographic and agent tools are composable products with distinct meanings; their presence is not a theorem about private financial execution. [FL06–08]

**Scilla is important historical design precedent, with a current availability warning.** Its communicating transitions separate state changes from interactions. But Zilliqa's September 2, 2026 update reports disabled legacy transactions and retirement of non-EVM infrastructure. Do not reuse old “Scilla supported on Zilliqa 2.0” statements as an unqualified current deployment recommendation. Planned mid-September migration milestones were not independently confirmed in this bounded pass. [FL09–10]

**Pact deserves serious prior-art treatment but not a current-mainnet label.** Its legacy reference combines finite computation, key/capability authorization and sequential multi-step pacts with explicit cancellation. Yet the official organization reports cessation, archival and a halted mainnet in 2025. The captured language reference is historical and cannot establish Pact 5 verification parity or a live successor service. Reusable design and available source are different from an operational product. [FL11–12]

**Simplicity is deployed, not merely a paper.** Blockstream reports Liquid availability since July 2025, working tools and real post-quantum signature transactions. Its program-budget orientation is a strong counterexample to novelty claims about bounded verifiable financial computation. The same update distinguishes upcoming wallet manifests/registry work from shipped tools. Executing an authorized spending predicate is not automatically carrying a proof against a separate signed business specification. [FL13]

**Stacks is the sharpest signed-intent counterexample.** Protocol-enforced postconditions are part of signed transactions and constrain asset activity. Their documented scope does not generally establish final ownership or all economic obligations; modes also change what unspecified activity remains allowed. This is nevertheless concrete prior art against claiming that signatures cannot bind user-declared financial limits. The older Clarity overview returned 404, so current decidability/version details remain qualified. [FL14]

## What the comparison means for requirements

Keep four layers separate: (1) rejection of malformed or ill-typed programs, (2) execution-time authorization and financial assertions, (3) offline verification of chosen program properties, and (4) mandatory proof-bearing acceptance of a complete signed intention and its history. A product may be strong at one without claiming all four. An ordinary transaction witness or signature is not necessarily layer four; conversely, a runtime-enforced predicate is a real guarantee even without a separate general-purpose proof artifact.

The precise conditional-settlement requirement is also already approachable in existing state-machine languages: a request or funded position can wait for selected authorized conditions before release. The unsettled questions are the **evidence semantics and complete guarantee**: which signatures bind which stage, whether document attestations establish the required proposition, which partial effects remain after failure, who supplies refund liquidity, and how cancellation competes with late completion. Neither a timeout nor an offline proof grants chain inclusion or establishes external truth.

AI integration is a weak novelty claim by itself. Existing ecosystems already advertise code assistants, agent skills and RPC/MCP tooling. A stronger obligation is keeping an untrusted solver inside a signed authority and aggregate budget across concurrent requests and recovery. A model-generated program that passes a narrow checker must not be labeled safe beyond that checker's specified relation.

## Tests that could defeat the proposed differentiation

1. Ask Daml/Canton or Marlowe implementers to model the same conditional purchase with partial fulfillment, recipient acceptance, late result, competing cancellation and preserved liabilities. Compare required trust and developer effort rather than terminology.
2. Ask a Stacks implementation to bind the same signed financial constraints. Separate constraints directly expressible as postconditions from final ownership, gross liabilities and evidence requirements that need additional application logic.
3. Ask Simplicity or Move tooling to prove the same functional and resource properties for a concrete application. Determine whether certificates are optional build evidence or enforced by the destination's actual acceptance path.
4. For Moriarty, demonstrate a newly authored contract outside fixed fixtures, exact ZKIRv3 correspondence, a valid positive trace and adversarial witness rejection. Include concurrent overspend, wrong-recipient evidence, omitted fees/liability, cyclic proof history and unknown external settlement. Until then, the claimed combined advantage is a hypothesis.

## Evidence boundaries

Market availability here means the cited official evidence supports a usable tool/platform or explicitly reports interruption; it is not a liveness measurement, adoption survey or commercial-support commitment. Unknown cells in `comparison.json` are not negative capability claims. Undated documents are current retrieval observations, not proof of when a feature shipped. No dated narrative source after September 19, 2026 was used. ZK-native languages, intent middleware and confidential-computing platforms require the sibling research tracks before any market-wide conclusion.
