1: # Proposed composition of expression descriptors with the funded kernel
2: 
3: Status: **PROPOSAL**, not registered. No runtime implements this document, no
4: profile digest includes it, and no evidence record cites it. It closes one named
5: gap: the [expression contract](semantic-contract.md) ends at `ExpressionPrepared`
6: with typed operation descriptors, and the [funded repayment kernel](repayment-kernel.md)
7: begins at a closed packet of state and actions. Neither document states how a
8: descriptor becomes an action. This proposal states a mapping, one composition-layer
9: rejection code and a precedence order, and nothing else: it invents no financial
10: behavior, no new effect and no authority. Every financial rule remains the
11: kernel's.
12: 
13: ## Domains
14: 
15: From the expression contract: a successful action yields
16: $\mathsf{ExpressionPrepared}(\mathit{post}_e,\ D,\ w_r)$ where $\mathit{post}_e$
17: is the pre-state overridden by staged ordinary writes, $D = d_1 \cdots d_n$ is
18: the descriptor list in emission order, and $w_r$ is the expression work remaining
19: after the statements and the Ensure suffix. This document also names $w_s$, the
20: expression work remaining after the last ordinary statement and before the
21: suffix; $w_0$ is the initial budget.
22: Each $d_i = \mathsf{Operation}_{O_i}(r_i)$ carries a declared operation name
23: $O_i$ and an operand record $r_i : \mathrm{Record}\langle R_{O_i}\rangle$.
24: 
25: From the kernel: an input is $\langle S, \vec a \rangle$ with $S$ the closed
26: `RepaymentState` (balances, allowances, obligations, used ids, work) and
27: $\vec a$ a nonempty action list of at most 128 `Transfer` or `Repay` actions.
28: The kernel returns $\mathsf{Prepared}(S', E)$ with post-state and one effect per
29: action, or $\mathsf{Rejected}(\mathit{code}, i)$ with $i$ the failing action
30: index or null.
31: 
32: Financial-class fields belong to the field registry $F$ like ordinary fields:
33: they are readable through `ReadPre` and only their writes are refused, and the
34: expression contract retains every unwritten field, financial records included,
35: in $\mathit{pre} \oplus W$. The two post-states therefore overlap wherever a
36: financial field exists in $\mathit{pre}$. In the post view of the composition,
37: $S'$ takes precedence: write $(\mathit{pre} \oplus W) \lhd S'$ for the record
38: whose ordinary fields come from $\mathit{pre} \oplus W$ and whose financial-class
39: fields are replaced by their projection from $S'$. The projection from the
40: kernel's balance, allowance and obligation records onto declared financial-class
41: fields is not defined by either component and is listed as open below.
42: 
43: ## Descriptor mapping
44: 
45: Let $\mu$ map one descriptor to one kernel action. It is defined exactly on the
46: two operation names whose operand records match the kernel's closed action
47: shapes field for field, and undefined elsewhere.
48: 
49: ```math
50: \begin{array}{rcl}
51: \mu\bigl(\mathsf{Operation}_{\mathsf{Transfer}}\{\mathit{id},\mathit{from},\mathit{to},\mathit{asset},\mathit{amount}\}\bigr)
52:  & = & \mathsf{Transfer}(\mathit{id},\mathit{from},\mathit{to},\mathit{asset},\mathit{amount}) \\[6pt]
53: \mu\bigl(\mathsf{Operation}_{\mathsf{Repay}}\{\mathit{allocationId},\mathit{transferId},\mathit{obligationId},\mathit{payer},\mathit{nominalAmount}\}\bigr)
54:  & = & \mathsf{Repay}(\mathit{allocationId},\mathit{transferId},\mathit{obligationId},\mathit{payer},\mathit{nominalAmount}) \\[6pt]
55: \mu(d) & = & \bot \qquad \text{otherwise}
56: \end{array}
57: ```
58: 
59: Field values pass through unchanged. The Core has no identifier value type, so
60: the record fields that the kernel reads as identifiers (`id`, `from`, `to`,
61: `asset`, `allocationId`, `transferId`, `obligationId`, `payer`) must be declared
62: as `Text` whose decoded value satisfies the kernel's identifier syntax
63: `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_]{0,63}`; a `Text` value outside that syntax reaches the
64: kernel and rejects `INVALID_IDENTIFIER` with null index. Whether a dedicated
65: identifier type should be added to the Core instead is listed as open below.
66: `amount` and `nominalAmount` are `UInt128` and pass as canonical decimal text. The operand record types $R_{\mathsf{Transfer}}$
67: and $R_{\mathsf{Repay}}$ must be declared in the trusted schema with exactly
68: those field names and with the types the kernel admits; a schema that declares
69: an operation of either name with a different record shape makes $\mu$ undefined
70: for it. $\mu$ extends pointwise to lists and is undefined if any element is.
71: 
72: ## Composition rule
73: 
74: The combined judgment runs the expression statements, hands the mapped
75: descriptors to the kernel, then runs the Ensure suffix against the combined
76: post-state. $\sigma_s$ is the frame after the last ordinary statement and
77: $\vec q$ the Ensure suffix.
78: $\sigma_s[\mathit{post} := X]$ abbreviates the frame in which $\mathsf{ReadPre}(\mathit{post}, f)$
79: reads $X[f]$; $\sigma$ has no separate post component, so the bracket names
80: only what the Ensure suffix sees.
81: 
82: ```math
83: \frac{
84: \begin{array}{c}
85: \langle s_1;\ldots;s_m,\ \sigma_0,\ w_0\rangle \to^{*} \langle \varepsilon,\ \sigma_s,\ w_s\rangle
86: \qquad \sigma_s = \langle \mathit{pre}, W, L, A, O, D\rangle
87: \qquad \mu(D) = \vec a \neq \varepsilon \\[4pt]
88: \mathsf{kernel}(S, \vec a) = \mathsf{Prepared}(S', E)
89: \qquad \langle \vec q,\ \sigma_s[\mathit{post} := (\mathit{pre} \oplus W) \lhd S'],\ w_s\rangle \to^{*} \langle \varepsilon,\ \_,\ w_r\rangle
90: \end{array}
91: }{
92: \mathsf{Compose}(\mathit{pre}, S, \mathcal{A}) \to \mathsf{Prepared}\bigl((\mathit{pre} \oplus W) \lhd S',\ E,\ w_r\bigr)
93: }\ \text{(COMPOSE)}
94: ```
95: 
96: ```math
97: \frac{
98: \begin{array}{c}
99: \langle s_1;\ldots;s_m,\ \sigma_0,\ w_0\rangle \to^{*} \langle \varepsilon,\ \sigma_s,\ w_s\rangle
100: \qquad D = \varepsilon \\[4pt]
101: \langle \vec q,\ \sigma_s[\mathit{post} := \mathit{pre} \oplus W],\ w_s\rangle \to^{*} \langle \varepsilon,\ \_,\ w_r\rangle
102: \end{array}
103: }{
104: \mathsf{Compose}(\mathit{pre}, S, \mathcal{A}) \to \mathsf{ExpressionPrepared}(\mathit{pre} \oplus W,\ \varepsilon,\ w_r)}\ \text{(COMPOSE-PURE)}
105: ```
106: 
107: An action that emits nothing never reaches the kernel and keeps today's
108: `ExpressionPrepared` result, with its Ensure suffix run exactly as the expression
109: contract specifies; the kernel's nonempty-actions rule is not violated by an
110: empty list because the kernel is not called. The kernel's post-state $S'$ carries
111: its own updated `work` record; the composed result adds no separate work field.
112: 
113: **Ordering.** Ordinary statements run first, in lexical order, exactly as the
114: expression contract specifies. Descriptors reach the kernel in emission order and
115: the kernel executes them in that order under its own transition rules. Only
116: after the kernel returns does the Ensure suffix run, and it sees a post view in
117: which ordinary fields come from $\mathit{pre} \oplus W$ and financial-class fields
118: from $S'$. This resolves the postcondition-visibility question the expression contract
119: leaves open: an Ensure can observe the financial outcome, but through `ReadPre`
120: with the `post` view only, so it still cannot write. Nothing is visible outside
121: the candidate at any intermediate point.
122: 
123: **Work.** Two budgets are charged separately and neither converts into the other.
124: Expression work is charged by E-ENTER as the contract specifies: $w_s$ remains
125: after the statements and $w_r$ after the Ensure suffix, which is entered with
126: $w_s$. Kernel work is charged
127: by the kernel's own rule: exactly one action-work unit per mapped action, taken
128: from `work.remaining` and added to `work.spent`, with `closureReserve` untouched.
129: A descriptor therefore costs one expression unit at its Emit occurrence plus one
130: kernel unit at its action, and the two never net against each other.
131: 
132: **Rejection precedence.** Failures resolve in this order, and every failure
133: publishes nothing: no post-state, no effects, no descriptors.
134: 
135: ```math
136: \begin{array}{ll}
137: \langle s_1;\ldots;s_m,\ \sigma_0,\ w_0\rangle \to^{*} \mathsf{Rejected}(c, \mathit{sp}, p, w) & \text{(C-EXPR)} \\
138: \quad \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf{Compose} \to \mathsf{Rejected}(c, \mathit{sp}, p, w) & \\[6pt]
139: \mu(d_i) = \bot & \text{(C-UNMAPPED)} \\
140: \quad \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf{Compose} \to \mathsf{Rejected}(\mathtt{UNMAPPED\_OPERATION},\ \mathrm{span}(d_i),\ \mathrm{path}(d_i),\ w_0 - w_s) & \\[6pt]
141: \mathsf{kernel}(S, \vec a) = \mathsf{Rejected}(c, i) & \text{(C-KERNEL)} \\
142: \quad \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf{Compose} \to \mathsf{Rejected}(c,\ \mathrm{span}(d_i),\ \mathrm{path}(d_i),\ w_0 - w_s) & \\[6pt]
143: \langle \vec q,\ \ldots,\ w_s\rangle \to^{*} \mathsf{Rejected}(c, \mathit{sp}, p, w) & \text{(C-ENSURE)} \\
144: \quad \Longrightarrow\ \mathsf{Compose} \to \mathsf{Rejected}(c, \mathit{sp}, p, w) &
145: \end{array}
146: ```
147: 
148: An expression rejection is reported before any kernel work, so a statically or
149: dynamically failing action never charges kernel work. An unmapped descriptor is
150: found before the kernel is called; its provenance is the Emit occurrence. A
151: kernel rejection with action index $i$ is attributed to the $i$-th descriptor's
152: Emit occurrence, so the source diagnostic points at the emission that produced
153: the failing action; a kernel rejection with null index, which the kernel gives to
154: input, state, work and action-list admission failures, is attributed to the
155: action root. Any rejection raised while the Ensure suffix reduces, whether
156: `ENSURES_FAILED`, `WORK_EXHAUSTED`, an arithmetic or index code or a bound,
157: keeps its own code and provenance and discards the kernel's tentative post-state
158: and effects exactly as it discards staged writes today.
159: 
160: `UNMAPPED_OPERATION` is the only new code and belongs to this composition layer,
161: not to either component. Kernel codes keep their kernel meaning and kernel index.
162: 
163: ## Worked shape
164: 
165: A source action that stages one ordinary write, emits one Transfer descriptor
166: and then emits one Repay descriptor referencing that transfer's id maps to the
167: kernel's two-action funded repayment packet in exactly the order the
168: [K definition](../../formal/k/README.md) executes: Transfer then Repay. Its Ensure
169: suffix can then require, for example, that the obligation's outstanding amount
170: in the post view equals the expected remainder. That Ensure reads the kernel's
171: result; it does not recompute the discharge.

1: # Proposed static semantics and expression rules
2: 
3: Status: **PROPOSAL**, expression contract/1 companion to [semantic-contract.md](semantic-contract.md).
4: Rules apply to the complete 40-constructor expression inventory, not to a newly
5: accepted execution profile. Financial operation equations and full signing
6: remain outside this contract. Rule IDs below are stable references for the
7: [machine-readable signatures](expression-signatures.json).
8: 
9: ## S0 — judgments and admission order
10: 
11: Σ is the bounded acyclic schema registry from V. Γ contains four disjoint
12: namespaces: immutable locals L, typed arguments A, typed observations O and
13: persistent fields F. Names are identifiers; no implicit fallback across
14: namespaces. T includes the finite ground and recursively constructed types in V.
15: Unit is a statement-result marker, excluded from T and every Σ data schema,
16: input, field, record member, option and collection element. Asking for Unit as a
17: value type (for example ConstructNone(Unit)) rejects TYPE_NAME. It cannot be
18: introduced by a read or projection.
19: 
20: Exact type equality includes every asset, holder, vault, scale, unit vector,
21: record/enum identity and collection capacity. No subtyping or implicit casts.
22: 
23: `Σ;Γ;phase ⊢ e:T` is total as an algorithm on the admitted finite syntax:
24: visit constructor operands in their named/source order, recursively check all
25: children (including both Boolean branches), then apply the listed rule. If no
26: listed overload applies, reject TYPE_MISMATCH. Unknown tags reject
27: TYPE_CONSTRUCTOR. Unrecognized metadata/fields reject INPUT_SCHEMA. Bounds or
28: malformed spans reject INPUT_BOUND/INPUT_SPAN before typing. Malformed literal
29: values reject TYPE_LITERAL. Missing names/types/fields reject TYPE_NAME.
30: 
31: Admission precedes reduction:
32: 
33: 1. Validate closed structure, duplicate-key freedom, canonical scalar metadata,
34:    raw aggregate JSON byte bounds, schema JSON-tree bounds, Core AST bounds and
35:    spans in preorder, first defect wins. Type-dependent individual W/value-node
36:    bounds wait until types are resolved; no undefined type-size function is called. Structural
37:    parsing itself is an implementation obligation; these rules do not claim a
38:    supplied hostile object is already safe.
39: 2. Validate Σ in identifier order: acyclic declared references and bounded shapes;
40:    validate action parameter/field/observation declarations. Field registries,
41:    not callers, assign ordinary/financial write classes.
42: 3. Typecheck the complete action in lexical order, including unexecuted statements.
43:    At each statement check its placement/target/binder before its expression,
44:    then check expression operands recursively before its result constraint.
45: 4. Validate snapshots in order Pre, Args, Obs, each in identifier order. Missing/
46:    extra names or a value tree differing from its resolved type shape rejects
47:    INPUT_SCHEMA. Then check individual W and typed aggregate node/depth bounds
48:    (INPUT_BOUND), then mathematical value domains (INPUT_VALUE). In each stage
49:    inspect children in the type-defined field/list order. No missing read can
50:    surface after a preceding guard. This is before all reduction, not a late read.
51: 5. Start work-metered reduction. The exact shape, size, annotation-versus-value distinction and admission
52:    phase are fixed by [representation.md](representation.md). Static checks consume no semantic work; bounded
53:    validation overhead still exists and is not a native-cost estimate.
54: 
55: Thus a later static error precedes an earlier *runtime* guard failure. Lexical
56: first-failure means first in the relevant admission/static/runtime phase, not
57: an excuse to skip checking later source. No diagnostics list is authoritative
58: for deciding which side effects to commit: all rejection paths publish none.
59: 
60: Actions are statement lists `Require|Let|NextWrite|Emit` followed by zero or more
61: Ensure. Those five nodes cannot nest as expression operands. A pure expression
62: never returns Unit or mutates a frame. Statement results below are Unit, including
63: Let (it binds a typed value but is not an expression returning that value).
64: This corrects the earlier candidate's ambiguous Let result T and is a new proposal.
65: 
66: ## L — literals
67: 
68: All literals take metadata only, have no evaluated child and cost one on entry.
69: The indicated type is determined statically; literal domain validity is checked
70: there after structural admission. Wrong JSON scalar kinds fail INPUT_SCHEMA
71: earlier; in particular LitBool(1) cannot reach TYPE_LITERAL. Only true/false
72: have a structurally valid Boolean literal representation.
73: 
74: | Rule | Constructor metadata | Type and exact value |
75: | --- | --- | --- |
76: | L-U | LitUInt(width,value) | width 64 or 128; type UInt64/UInt128; value in that unsigned range |
77: | L-I | LitSInt(value) | SInt128, exact signed integer |
78: | L-B | LitBool(value) | Bool, literal true/false only |
79: | L-T | LitText(value) | Text, exact decoded bounded scalar string |
80: | L-A | LitAmount(asset,value) | Amount<asset>, declared asset, UInt128 quanta |
81: | L-Q | LitQuantity(units,scale,mantissa) | Quantity<units,scale>, canonical declared units and SInt128 mantissa |
82: | L-S | LitShares(vault,holder,value) | Shares<vault,holder>, declared identities, UInt128 quanta |
83: | L-R | LitRate(scale,mantissa) | Rate<scale>, scale 0..18, SInt128 mantissa |
84: | L-P | LitPrice(base,quote,scale,mantissa) | Price<base,quote,scale>, distinct declared assets, UInt128 mantissa |
85: 
86: There is no LitDebt. A financial record read from a declared schema is not a
87: primitive numeric amount. No arithmetic overload operates on a Debt record.
88: 
89: ## READ — immutable reads
90: 
91: ReadLocal(name), ReadArg(name) and ReadObs(name) return exactly L[name], A[name]
92: and O[name], with their declared types. Names must exist at that lexical point.
93: ReadObs is a pure read of the supplied snapshot: it emits no Observe effect and
94: makes no oracle truth/currentness assertion. All read nodes cost one.
95: 
96: ReadPre(view,field) accepts metadata view `pre|post` only. `pre` selects F in
97: immutable pre; `post` selects the assembled overridden F. Type is F[field].
98: `post` is permitted anywhere inside an Ensure condition and nowhere else;
99: violation rejects TYPE_POST_SCOPE. `next` rejects TYPE_NEXT_READ. Both views
100: have the same declared field type. A private/unauthenticated observation remains
101: private/unauthenticated; these semantics contain no implicit declassification.
102: 
103: ## ACCESS — projection
104: 
105: ProjectField(record,field) and AccessField(record,field) require
106: record:Record<R> and metadata field in R. Result type is R[field], result value
107: is that exact field, no copy-as-authority or ownership claim. A wrong field is
108: TYPE_NAME; a non-record child is TYPE_MISMATCH.
109: 
110: ProjectIndex(collection,index) and AccessIndex(collection,index) require
111: Collection<T,N> and UInt64. Result type T. After both children evaluate, require
112: index < actual length; otherwise INDEX_RANGE. This is runtime length, not just
113: capacity. The finite index check never performs an unchecked host array read.
114: Empty collections therefore reject every index. Aliases have identical rules.
115: 
116: ## BUILD — finite constructors
117: 
118: ConstructRecord(recordType,fields): metadata recordType R must be declared.
119: First resolve R. Then scan the entire field-name list in lexical order for
120: duplicates, rejecting TYPE_DUPLICATE_FIELD at the first repeated name. Only
121: after that scan succeeds compare the set to R; missing/extra fields reject
122: TYPE_RECORD_FIELDS. Perform both checks before any child typing/evaluation.
123: Each named expression field therefore occurs once and exactly covers R. Evaluate field expressions in lexical order; require each type equal
124: to R[field]. Return Record<R> sorted by name for representation. Extra evaluation
125: order is not introduced by sorting. Aggregate value bounds checked last.
126: 
127: ConstructEnum(enumType,member): both metadata; member must belong to declared E;
128: return Enum<E>(member), otherwise TYPE_ENUM_MEMBER. No runtime payload or effect.
129: 
130: ConstructSome(elementType,value): explicit T metadata; require value:T;
131: return Some<T>(value). ConstructNone(elementType): return None<T>, no child.
132: T must be a finite registered type; never infer None's type from a later use.
133: 
134: ConstructCollection(elementType,capacity,items): metadata T,N (0≤N≤128), ordered
135: expression list length≤N; check shape before children, else TYPE_COLLECTION_BOUND.
136: All children have exactly T. Return Collection<T,N> preserving order. Any result
137: of BUILD exceeding the aggregate value/byte/depth bounds rejects VALUE_BOUND
138: at that constructor, after its children and before returning the value.
139: A well-shaped undeclared nominal type reference rejects TYPE_NAME during
140: schema/static resolution. An unknown wire tag rejects INPUT_SCHEMA. A cyclic
141: type dependency rejects TYPE_SCHEMA_CYCLE during schema validation, including
142: Operation<O> through its operand Record and any Option/Collection wrappers.
143: 
144: ## ARITH — exact overloads and guard order
145: 
146: Arithmetic is strict. Evaluate left then right, then apply the following rules.
147: Unless listed, an operand-type combination rejects TYPE_MISMATCH statically.
148: For scalar integer operations, both operands and result have the identical type
149: T in UInt64, UInt128 or SInt128. Add/Sub/Mul use mathematical +/−/× and reject
150: ARITH_RANGE when the result does not fit T. There is no widening then truncation.
151: 
152: | Operation | Additional admitted overloads | Result and checks after children |
153: | --- | --- | --- |
154: | Add/Sub | Amount<A>, Shares<V,H>, Rate<S>, Quantity<U,S>, with exactly equal indices | Same type; operate on quanta/mantissas and check its underlying UInt128/SInt128 range |
155: | Mul | Quantity<U1,S1>, Quantity<U2,S2> | Quantity<normalize(U1+U2), S1+S2>; result type must satisfy unit/scale bounds; SInt128 mantissa product must fit |
156: | FloorDiv/CeilDiv | Quantity<U1,S1>, Quantity<U2,S2> | Quantity<normalize(U1−U2), S1−S2>; result scale must be 0..18 and units bounded; denominator mantissa positive; floor/ceil of the mantissa quotient, checked SInt128 |
157: 
158: For Quantity Mul/Div, type-level exponent and scale arithmetic uses exact
159: mathematical integers during static checking; no runtime numeric widening is
160: implied. Normalize merges equal symbols and removes zero exponents; reject
161: TYPE_QUANTITY_DOMAIN if the result has >8 symbols, exponent outside −128..127,
162: or scale outside 0..18. These are representation restrictions, not a claim every
163: mathematically meaningful dimensional operation is representable. In particular,
164: no implicit rescale repairs a negative division scale. A later rescale operation
165: requires explicit source requirements and cannot be silently introduced here.
166: 
167: Scalar FloorDiv/CeilDiv require denominator >0, otherwise ARITH_DENOMINATOR
168: before quotient computation. For integer numerator n and positive d, compute
169: unique integers q,r with n=q*d+r and 0≤r<d. FloorDiv returns q; CeilDiv returns
170: q if r=0, otherwise q+1. Check result range, else ARITH_RANGE. This includes
171: negative SInt128 numerators: −5/2 gives floor −3, ceil −2. No floating point.
172: Quantity division uses the same rule on mantissas, retaining the derived scale.
173: 
174: No plain `/`; no Rate*Amount or Amount/Price implicit money conversion;
175: no Price arithmetic, Amount multiplication, Shares across different holders,
176: Width mixing, comparison-driven casts or Debt-as-Amount. Missing target-required
177: overloads stay explicit SP01 decisions; these signatures do not silently remove
178: those financial requirements from RP01. Conversion operations require their
179: separate financial contracts.

181: ## CMP — comparisons and Booleans
182: 
183: Eq requires identical value types T; admits scalar values and finite Record,
184: Enum, Option and Collection recursively, including indexed financial *values*.
185: Operation descriptors may be compared structurally but comparison grants no
186: financial authority. Records compare the same named fields; collection elements
187: compare in order; None equals None of the same T, never Some. Text equality is
188: exact decoded scalar equality. Result Bool. No pointer or host object equality.
189: 
190: Lt/Lte/Gt/Gte accept identical scalar integer types or equal-index Amount,
191: Shares, Rate, Quantity or Price. Compare underlying integer quanta/mantissas.
192: Result Bool. They reject Bool, Text, Record, Enum, Option, Collection and Operation;
193: there is no implicit ordering on identities or records.
194: 
195: Not requires Bool and returns logical negation. And/Or require two Bool children;
196: S0 checks both in lexical order regardless of a runtime selection. After whole
197: input admission, evaluate the left. And returns false immediately on false and
198: otherwise evaluates/returns the right Bool. Or returns true immediately on true
199: and otherwise evaluates/returns the right Bool. A left or selected-right runtime
200: failure propagates with its original provenance and consumed work; an unselected
201: right produces no dynamic failure or work. E specifies the exact contexts,
202: zero-cost contractions and original-occurrence paths. No truthiness, implicit
203: cast or financial composition is encoded by these nodes. Pure short-circuiting
204: does not skip admission or turn a Boolean operand into an effectful statement.
205: 
206: Source inequality has no Core constructor. The source lowering maps `a != b` to
207: Not(Eq(core(a),core(b))): two constructor occurrences, both carrying the span of
208: the source comparison occurrence, with Eq at child path p+[0] of the Not at p.
209: Eq's rule applies unchanged, so the operand types must be identical and the
210: result is Bool. B counts two for the pair and entering them charges two units
211: of work. The `emit X { ... }` field-list form likewise lowers to two nodes, Emit
212: over a ConstructRecord of the operation's operand record; no other source form
213: introduces more than one Core node.

215: ## STMT — staging and descriptors
216: 
217: Require(cond): require Bool. Evaluate cond; false rejects GUARD_FAILED, true
218: returns Unit with unchanged frames. Ensure(cond): same, but legal only in the
219: final suffix and false rejects ENSURES_FAILED. Ensure changes no state.
220: 
221: Let(name,value): name must be fresh in L and not shadow any argument, declaration
222: or reserved pre/post/next name; violation TYPE_DUPLICATE_BINDER. No self reference:
223: check/evaluate value under the old L, then extend L once. Type of binding is
224: value's type. Statement returns Unit. Later locals can read it.
225: 
226: NextWrite(field,value): field declared ordinary; financial fields reject
227: TYPE_FINANCIAL_WRITE. Check field exists and has no previous NextWrite in this
228: action before checking value; duplicate rejects TYPE_DUPLICATE_WRITE. Require
229: value's type equals field type; evaluate value against immutable pre, prior
230: locals/args/obs, never tentative writes. Stage writes[field]=value; return Unit.
231: No state mutation is externally visible. Whole-action static tracking detects
232: all duplicate writes even if an earlier runtime guard would have failed.

235: operand Record<R_O>; require fields:Record<R_O>. Evaluate fields; append

237: else DESCRIPTOR_BOUND. Return Unit. The operation registry supplies an operand
238: schema, not a callable semantic function. No balance, debt, allowance or work
239: transition is inferred from the descriptor. Executable operation interpretation
240: requires a separate reviewed relation and may not treat this return as success
241: of the corresponding financial action.

243: After all statements and Ensure conditions succeed, finalize the ordinary
244: post-state and check aggregate bounds, otherwise VALUE_BOUND. Every non-written
245: field is exactly retained. Stage assembly has no extra expression node charge.
246: A failure's local work count remains diagnostic only, as defined in E.

11: # MPLR-035: Constraint-preserving solver completion
12: 
13: ## Required behavior
14: 
15: When a solver completes, combines or routes a partially specified authenticated intention, the language and acceptance relation shall establish that the completion refines every applicable signed constraint and permitted choice, binds all introduced effects and disclosures, and preserves existing commitments and residual duties. An out-of-scope change requires a separately authorized amendment. No developer or solver membership list may replace proof checking.
16: 
17: Precommit candidates remain distinct from accepted partial stages. Failure to complete a candidate cannot roll back already committed external effects. A solver may choose any solution permitted by the signed policy; optimality and eventual completion require separate claims.
18: 
19: ## Positive and hostile witnesses
20: 
21: Accept an unregistered solver's compatible route exchanging 10 A for at least 20 B at the signed recipient, with a fee no greater than 1 A within the aggregate 11-A debit cap. Accept an alternative route allowed by the same constraints. Preserve explicit obligations when settlement spans stages.
22: 
23: Reject independently mutated extra fee legs, changed recipients or fee denominations, conflicting settlement domains, substituted policy circuits, prohibited disclosure, and silently dropped residual duties, even when transaction balance and cryptographic proofs otherwise pass. Distinguish an incomplete candidate from a committed prefix. Prevent incompatible settle/refund branches consuming the same entitlement.

Define sound completion relative to authenticated policy, evidence and state; prove arbitrary accepted witnesses preserve constraints. Establish non-vacuity with useful allowed completions and bind the relation to actual Midnight ZKIRv3 effects.
