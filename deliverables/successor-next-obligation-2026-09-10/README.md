## Concrete implementation task

**Owner:** MC01, SP01.3, under “Target-driven semantic freeze” and “Complete successor specification and executable K semantics.” [Owning requirements](../../openspec/changes/mc01-bounded-language/specs/mc01-bounded-language/spec.md:25).

**Deliverable:** a complete, reviewable declarative contract for all 40 currently proposed expression constructors, with an executable contract validator and independently derived derivation cases. Start from candidate04's `semantic-contract.md` B.1 and `signing-display-schema.json` `x-exprSignatures`, preserving the archived bytes. The intended product destinations are the sprint-planned `spec/successor/semantic-contract.md` and `spec/successor/static-semantics.md`; exact candidate edits should remain visibly proposed until review. Do not modify the funded-source profile or claim SP02 runtime entry has opened.

Each constructor needs operand/result types and overloads, scoping, finite bounds, evaluation context/order, first rejection and span rule, state/effect frame, work charge and source-requirement link. Resolve, rather than leave implicit, Boolean short-circuit versus strict evaluation, signed division/rounding, `post` access in `Ensure`, observation admission, and access/projection alias behavior. Proposed choices must stay labeled until accepted. The validator must reject missing/duplicate constructor rows and dangling type/operand references; this structural check alone is not semantic validation.

**Independent discriminating case:** a well-typed pre-state field `x=10`, local `y=pre.x+1`, tentative `next.x=y`, then `ensures post.x==11` produces candidate `x=11` while retaining every unrelated field, duty and recovery reserve. Mutate it to `ensures post.x==12`: derive rejection with no candidate state/effects. Separately duplicate the `next.x` write, read `next.x`, put `post` outside the suffix, and add mismatched assets: derive the specified static rejection. Include constructor-wide independent positive/rejection derivations and a two-failure case to discriminate evaluation order. These are proposed contract-validation cases, not claimed executed source/K results.

**Existing prerequisites:** inherited staging requirements; complete provisional AST/grammar; candidate04 constructor names and independent blocker; working funded Transfer/Repay semantics to preserve at the effect boundary. No native verifier or public campaign is needed. **Remaining prerequisite for actual full frontend/K implementation:** accepted full RP01, including the still-open financial-operation equations, full challenge map and signing/contextual corrections. Completing this task removes one whole-layer blocker; it does not satisfy that gate alone.

After that full contract is accepted, implement the corresponding complete typed frontend, then full Core K semantics and the six required metatheorems. Reuse funded repayment and numeric evidence at their exact scope. Keep the user's compiled-language-to-Midnight Preview financial execution, full effects/readback and mandatory-proof milestones intact; this diagnosis changes no SP05–SP12 gate.
