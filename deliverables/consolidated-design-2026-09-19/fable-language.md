## Governing design: one stage relation, one certified core, one native target

**Source facts this design rests on.** The product contract fixes the deliverable as language, semantics, compiler, proof interfaces, verifier integration and tooling, with ZKIRv3 on Midnight as the terminal artifact and Lean excluded as a dependency (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md`, "Product boundary" and "Execution target"). The whole-language review finds that no single source-to-acceptance pipeline exists for the richest source profile, that the Compact mapper is a restricted legacy path, and that several Core representations coexist (`research/moriarty-whole-design-2026-09-19/REVIEW.md`, D1). The ledger builds the contract-call statement itself from address, entry point, gas, effects and transcript operations, does not bind network id, fees, TTL or state root, admits `PartialSuccess` with fees retained, and cannot verify a predecessor contract-call proof inside another call even after recursion ships (`wiki/decisions/pcd-midnight-native-architecture.md`, CLM-0947 to CLM-0949, CLM-0952 to CLM-0953). Reproduced costs put single-level recursive proving at about 35 seconds and 4 GiB at k=18 on one machine, while the loan `initialize` circuit proved at k=14 in under two seconds (CLM-0956 to CLM-0958). DeFiFormal supplies typed cells, net-effect accounting, capability rights that are not owner consent, and a planned but unimplemented claims lifecycle (`research/defiformal-moriarty-scope-2026-09-19/composition-review.md`). Its price orientation is quote-per-base, opposite to Moriarty's (`research/defiformal-moriarty-scope-2026-09-19/libraries-review.md`).

**Recommendation: define Moriarty as a total, first-order, bounded core whose only observable is a typed stage effect frame.** Everything else in the packet is either a refinement of that frame, a way of producing candidates for it, or a way of verifying it. The design has five layers.

1. **Core values.** Exact bounded integers with declared width and scale, tagged by asset identity, so that quantities of different assets or scales do not add. Typed principals, domains, absolute time and relative duration as distinct types (MPLR-010 Simplicity refinement). No rationals reach the circuit. DeFiFormal formulas port only after explicit width, rounding and price-orientation conversion, recorded per formula (`libraries-review.md`, "Exact arithmetic" row and the price-convention paragraph).

2. **Refinements and effects.** Aeon-style refinement types state preconditions and postconditions over Core values. An effect judgment assigns each transition a frame with six separately typed components: per-asset custody deltas, authorized supply deltas, fee legs with recipients, a liability equation (opening plus created or accrued minus explicit discharge equals closing), consumed receipts, and affine authority use. Residual duties are a seventh component that cannot be weakened. This is the distinct-typed-equations resolution recorded in `deliverables/aeon-study-2026-09-19/review/FINAL-CONSENSUS.md` and the requirement text of MPLR-017 (`wiki/research/mplr/MPLR-017.md`).

3. **Linear authority, assets and liabilities.** Receipts are linear and consumed exactly once. Authority is affine: it may go unused, is stage-scoped, and has four separately signed lifetimes: initiate, complete already-authorized work, reconcile evidence, recover or amend (REVIEW.md D6). Liabilities are persistent: no local success result, weakening rule or expiry discharges them (MPLR-004, MPLR-010).

4. **The stage relation.** One versioned relation `Permitted(program, intent, prior, candidate, evidence) ⇒ (frame, successor, duties, outcome)`, where outcome is one of success, guaranteed-only partial, rejected, pending, recovered. Signed intent is an input to the same relation as effects, not a separate check (REVIEW.md D2). Solver-filled holes are typed positions in intent whose fill must refine every signed constraint (MPLR-035). The Midnight phase layout is part of the relation: each candidate declares which frame components land in the guaranteed section and which in the fallible section, and intent states per-phase authority, nonce and fee consumption (`docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md`, phase paragraph; `wiki/research/apss/settlement/explanation.md`).

5. **Certified primitives and intent-to-circuit correspondence.** The core's reference semantics is executable in K, which the completion program already selects as primary executable semantics (`openspec/MORIARTY-COMPLETION-PROGRAM.md`, "Design decisions"). Each primitive carries a certificate binding specification, preconditions, reference semantics, host implementation, ZKIRv3 constraint fragment, version and cost model. Compilation must satisfy two directions: every Core-valid execution has an admitted witness, and every admitted witness corresponds to a Core-valid execution. The second direction is the adversarial-witness obligation that PR #17's `WShape` premises leave open (`wiki/research/apss/certified-basis/explanation.md`).

**Recommendation on history: adopt a hybrid and retarget the recursion objective.** On-ledger contract lineage uses ledger-head induction: each entry point reads and replaces the head it consumes, genesis is constrained, and history compliance follows from ledger acceptance plus `ReadMismatch` linearity (CLM-0946, CLM-0949). Recursive verification is reserved for bounded certificates: off-ledger segments, foreign-chain evidence and private split or join, verified through the ledger-10 `verify_proof` interface once released. Per-transaction recursive verification of predecessor contract-call proofs is not pursued, because it is not a native operation (CLM-0948). This is not a rejection of MC03 and MC06 objectives. It is a change of what statement they prove: the recursive statement must contain legitimate base, compatible predecessor, unique consumption, cumulative bounds and residual duties (MPLR-027; `deliverables/anoma-study-2026-09-19/SYNTHESIS.md`, "Proof aggregation versus historical PCD").

**Open feasibility this design does not settle.** Whether the k=17 or k=18 budget holds a general stage circuit including signature verification is unmeasured (`openspec/MORIARTY-COMPLETION-PROGRAM.md`, "Before MC05 relation freeze"). Whether ledger-10 ships with the accumulator fee and zero-guard hazards fixed is a moving fact (CLM-0953). Whether the DeFiFormal composition operators can be reproduced as native obligations without Lean is a proposed adaptation with no correspondence theorem (`composition-review.md`, "What can be reused"). Whether the private-state completeness domain can be authenticated for solver-visible reservations is open (MPLR-029).

The critical path is A to B to C. D, E and F can start read-only inspection in parallel with C, as the completion program already allows for interface inspection (`MORIARTY-COMPLETION-PROGRAM.md`, "Cross-package empirical decisions"). Native proof feasibility is measured in B before any general proof campaign.

**Alternatives considered.** A backend-first rewrite postpones the intent and authoring decisions that caused the earlier detour, as the completion program already observes. A per-transaction recursive PCD design is blocked by the transcript mismatch fact and the unmeasured cost of nested verification. A managed-router product would be easier to support but does not satisfy the permissionless objective. A wider first slice would delay evidence about circuit fit. I recommend the narrow slice with the full relation, not a narrow relation with a wide slice.

**Proposed EARS requirements.** Each is a candidate for the OpenSpec requirements file, mapped to the MPLR entry that motivates it.

1. When a program is submitted for compilation, the toolchain shall accept or reject it using only the versioned Core typing, bounds and profile rules, without any registry, reviewer or service-membership check. Maps to MPLR-015, MPLR-016.
2. When a candidate is checked against a signed intent, the stage relation shall bind exact asset identity or substitution predicate, recipients, gross debit cap, fee cap, liability cap, net minimum, validity window, replay domain and permitted partial and recovery outcomes as inputs to the same relation that produces the effect frame. Maps to MPLR-018, MPLR-022, MPLR-023.
3. When a stage is accepted, its effect frame shall carry separately typed equations for custody, supply, fees, liabilities, consumed receipts and affine authority, and shall carry residual duties forward unchanged unless explicitly discharged. Maps to MPLR-004, MPLR-017, MPLR-025.
4. When a candidate declares its phase layout, the compiler shall place each frame component in the guaranteed or fallible section as declared, and the intent shall state authority, nonce and fee consumption for each permitted failure outcome. Maps to MPLR-009, MPLR-014.
5. When a certified primitive substitutes for a reference expression, acceptance shall bind its version and certificate and shall reject the substitution if any caller precondition is undischarged. Maps to MPLR-020, MPLR-021.
6. When a ZKIRv3 artifact is produced, the correspondence record shall state both that every Core-valid execution has an admitted witness and that every admitted witness on the pinned surface corresponds to a Core-valid execution, with the adversarial premise discharged or listed as open. Maps to MPLR-014, MPLR-022.
7. When an entry point consumes a contract head, it shall read the head it replaces or read its absence on creation, so that on-ledger history compliance follows from ledger acceptance. Maps to MPLR-011, MPLR-027.
8. When a transition relies on an off-ledger segment, foreign evidence or a private predecessor, it shall verify a bounded certificate whose statement includes legitimate base, compatible predecessor, unique consumption, cumulative bounds and residual duties. Maps to MPLR-027, MPLR-029, MPLR-030.
9. When a solver fills a typed hole or combines two signed intents, acceptance shall establish that the completion refines every applicable signed constraint without widening either solution set, and shall reject out-of-scope changes absent a separately authorized amendment. Maps to MPLR-035, MPLR-031.
10. When an intent's initiation window expires, the relation shall retain unresolved external duties, shall permit only the separately signed reconcile and recovery authorities, and shall not treat absence of evidence as proof of nonexecution. Maps to MPLR-006, MPLR-007, MPLR-010.
11. When a recovery transition is invoked, it shall spend only reserved work and still-controlled custody, shall require its own signed authority, and shall preserve every duty it does not discharge. Maps to MPLR-005, MPLR-009 refinement.
12. When an instance reaches its bounded episode limit, any successor episode shall carry forward replay state, cumulative signed budgets, residual authority and liabilities under an authenticated continuation origin. Maps to MPLR-002, MPLR-021 refinement, MPLR-028.
13. When concurrent tasks commit funds under one delegation, the relation shall reserve before work and account for spent, pending and residual amounts across retries. Maps to MPLR-032, MPLR-034.
14. When a formula is ported from DeFiFormal, the library record shall state the width, rounding, scale and price orientation conversion applied. Maps to MPLR-017 and the libraries review.
15. When a tool reports a simulation or advisory result, it shall label which stages and judgments were covered and shall not present the result as accepted execution or ledger proof. Maps to MPLR-012.

**Recap.** The packet supports one design: a small total core whose only observable is a seven-component typed effect frame, a single stage relation that takes signed intent and evidence as inputs, certified primitives with two-directional correspondence to a pinned ZKIRv3 surface, ledger-head induction for on-ledger history and bounded native certificates for everything else. The main contradiction in the packet, recursive history versus the September 11 decision, resolves by retargeting the recursion objective rather than dropping it. Administrative gates are removed and objective MC predicates are preserved. The roadmap runs semantic freeze, target pin, vertical slice, history, composition, kernel, libraries, with measurable exit evidence at each step and no claimed native proof or ledger acceptance until Phase C records one. Circuit fit for a full stage with signature verification, ledger-10 recursion availability, native reproduction of DeFiFormal composition obligations and private-state completeness remain open feasibility. The next step is to write Phase A as the normative Core v1 specification and the OpenSpec requirements from the fifteen EARS candidates above.
