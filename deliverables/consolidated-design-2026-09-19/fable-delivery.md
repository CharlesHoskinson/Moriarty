## Vision, boundaries and dispositions

**Governing statement.** Moriarty is one thing: a permissionless language whose accepted transitions carry native Midnight proofs that a versioned program, an owner-authenticated intention, an authenticated predecessor, typed evidence and complete effects stand in one bound relation. Everything else is either a library written in that language, a target obligation owed to the pinned ZKIRv3 ledger, or an optional service. This is the position of `/home/charl/Moriarty-aeon-study/docs/MORIARTY-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md` and I adopt it unchanged as the controlling document. Where older P, C, K, MC and SP plans conflict with it, the contract wins and the older objective is remapped, not dropped.

**Source facts I rely on.** The roadmap at `/home/charl/Moriarty-aeon-study/ROADMAP.md` records that source profile 5 prepares multi-action lifecycles locally, that native K results cover 125 expression and 104 lifecycle cases, and that Preview loan and swap runs are scoped effects rather than general source-to-ledger execution. The whole-language review at `/home/charl/research/moriarty-whole-design-2026-09-19/REVIEW.md` reports that the inspected Compact lowerer is a restricted legacy path excluding obligation ledger, authority, proof and settlement acceptance, that the lifecycle engine has a four-operation union, and that state arrays cap at 128 with no rollover action. The PCD decision at `/home/charl/Moriarty-aeon-study/wiki/decisions/pcd-midnight-native-architecture.md` records that no released ledger generation supports application recursion, that a contract-call proof cannot be the inner proof of another call, that ledger acceptance re-runs the read transcript and rejects a mismatch, and that ledger 10 recursion carries unpriced accumulator inputs. The DeFiFormal composition review at `/home/charl/research/defiformal-moriarty-scope-2026-09-19/composition-review.md` records net-effect accounting, capability grants that are not owner consent, and an atomic tracked-net table that is not a claims ledger. The libraries review at `/home/charl/research/defiformal-moriarty-scope-2026-09-19/libraries-review.md` records opposite price orientation between DeFiFormal and Moriarty. Everything labelled recommendation below is mine.

The old identifiers survive as traceability tags on each exit criterion so no objective is lost. Each phase has one exit table, and nothing downstream is admitted until the exit evidence exists.

The P3 obligation reporting and exact advisory checking track runs in parallel from R0 onward, since it needs no target feasibility and the roadmap already orders it before P5.

**Why this order.** Certified primitives precede the slice because the slice must not be built on an uncertified lowerer, which is exactly the D1 gap. Mandatory acceptance precedes composition because MC06 obligations cannot be proved against a relation that is not yet enforced. Libraries precede release because release demands a program outside the fixtures. The alternative of libraries first was rejected because it repeats the earlier detour the completion program warns about. The alternative of recursion first was rejected because no released ledger supports it and the read-transcript route already gives on-ledger linearity.

**The next vertical slice.** I adopt the REVIEW proposal and make it measurable. The program is a two-asset conditional settlement authored through the public language, not a fixture. One owner signs a bounded intention: exact asset A debit cap, fee cap in A, minimum net B credit, exact recipient, accepted domain, evidence policy, expiry and recovery policy. Settlement waits for recipient acceptance plus an authenticated document predicate. Two independent unregistered solvers may each complete an allowed route. One accepted stage leaves a residual duty. A late authenticated outcome races expiry and recovery. Low remaining work and the 128 ID cap are included as cases.

Exit evidence for the slice, all recorded in one result file with source, Core, artifact and policy hashes:

- One versioned program identity is followed unchanged across source, K replay, emitted ZKIRv3 and the Preview verifier. A differing hash at any hop fails the slice.
- Positive witnesses: both solver routes accepted; a partial fill accepted with cumulative bounds preserved; late success reconciled without double delivery.
- Hostile witnesses, each mutating one property while proofs and balance otherwise pass: extra fee leg, redirected B output, changed fee denomination, substituted circuit, conflicting domain, prohibited disclosure, erased residual duty, refund after known delivery, replayed continuation, fee cap counted twice. Each must reject for its specific reason. A wrong-reason rejection counts as failure.
- Phase accounting: the fallible-phase failure case shows retained guaranteed effects and fees exactly as the signed policy states.
- Measured cost: circuit k, proving time, proof size and peak memory per stage on one named machine, alongside the CLM-0956 to CLM-0958 baselines.
- Preview: at most two submissions per case within the existing gross debit ceilings, with independent effect readback. Local Docker precedes every public attempt.
- Reserve and cap cases: repayment after cap with outstanding duty either succeeds through the recovery operation or is recorded as a specified design limit, never silently passed.

None of this exists today. The roadmap explicitly says the end-to-end capability is unfinished, and I do not claim any native proof or financial ledger acceptance beyond the scoped September 17 results.

## Requirements, MPLR mapping, open feasibility and gaps

**Proposed EARS requirements.** These are proposals for OpenSpec review, numbered CONS-01 onward so they do not collide with existing identifiers.

- CONS-01. When a developer submits a program in the supported profile, the toolchain shall compile, prove and produce a deployable ZKIRv3 artifact without consulting any reviewer record, registry or service credential. Maps to MPLR-015, MPLR-016. Phase R0.
- CONS-02. When a program is lowered, the artifact manifest shall bind compiler version, ZKIR major and minor, instruction surface, circuit and key identity, and ledger generation as separate fields. Maps to MPLR-014, MPLR-022. Phase R0.
- CONS-03. When an optimized primitive replaces a reference expression, acceptance shall require a certificate binding its preconditions, reference semantics, constraint fragment, version, adversarial witness soundness and cost, and shall reject substitution on hash recognition alone. Maps to MPLR-020, MPLR-021. Phase R1.
- CONS-04. When a stage is accepted, the effect frame shall satisfy four typed equations: per-asset custody conservation, authorized supply change, fee attribution within the signed cap, and liability opening plus accrual minus discharge equals closing. Maps to MPLR-017, MPLR-025. Phase R2.
- CONS-05. When an intention is signed, the canonical object shall state per-phase authority and nonce consumption, fees retained on fallible failure, and remedies, and the lowerer shall not introduce any failure policy absent from it. Maps to MPLR-009, MPLR-018, MPLR-023. Phase R2.
- CONS-06. When a solver completes or combines a signed intention, acceptance shall establish that the candidate refines every signed constraint, binds all introduced effects and disclosures, and preserves prior commitments, while admitting any alternative route the policy allows. Maps to MPLR-035, MPLR-019, MPLR-031. Phase R2 positive and hostile witnesses, R4 general relation.
- CONS-07. When a transition depends on history, the proof shall bind the head it replaces through the ledger read transcript, or an explicit absence read on creation, and shall not depend on recursive verification of a predecessor contract-call proof. Maps to MPLR-011, MPLR-027, MPLR-029. Phase R3.
- CONS-08. If a bounded certificate from an off-ledger segment or foreign domain is imported, then its statement shall bind origin, predecessor, unique consumption, cumulative budget and remaining duties, and its accumulator cost shall be accounted under the pinned fee model. Maps to MPLR-027, MPLR-030. Phase R3, conditional on ledger 10.
- CONS-09. When ordinary initiation authority expires, completion of already-authorized work, reconciliation of authenticated late evidence, and recovery or amendment shall each require their own signed scope, and no timeout shall be treated as evidence of nonexecution. Maps to MPLR-006, MPLR-008, MPLR-010. Phase R4.
- CONS-10. When an instance reaches a declared state cap, it shall either close through a signed recovery path or continue in an authenticated successor episode that preserves replay protection, cumulative budgets, residual authority and liabilities. Maps to MPLR-021 refinement, MPLR-005. Phase R4.
- CONS-11. When concurrent solver tasks commit funds, reservation shall account spent, pending, sponsored and residual amounts across retries and failover, and shall reject combined commitments exceeding the signed budget. Maps to MPLR-032, MPLR-034. Phase R4.
- CONS-12. When a DeFiFormal formula is ported, the library shall implement it natively with the canonical price orientation, checked width and rounding, and shall record that no DeFiFormal theorem is inherited. Maps to MPLR-020, MPLR-025. Phase R5.
- CONS-13. When a workflow claims atomic settlement, it shall name the domain and exact effects inside the boundary, and any independent-chain leg shall retain pending, settled, refunded and compensated states. Maps to MPLR-024, MPLR-026, MPLR-033. Phase R6.
- CONS-14. When a tool reports on a program, it shall label each finding as formula discharged, correspondence proved, finitely tested, or ledger verified, and shall never present unknown, timeout or unsupported as success. Maps to MPLR-012. Phase R0 onward.

**Composable libraries and breadth.** The recommendation is a closed primitive basis plus open libraries. The basis holds exact bounded integers with declared width, units and rounding, asset identity, shares as a separate type, liabilities as a separate type, authority as affine, evidence as typed with issuer, subject, predicate, freshness and disclosure, and stage and continuation types. Libraries are ordinary programs: ACTUS contract types, lending, constant-product and concentrated liquidity arithmetic, vault conversion, claims lifecycle, netting, escrow and async cross-domain state. The DeFiFormal families table gives the port order: arithmetic first, then vault shares, then claims, then the absent families as fresh designs. Each library carries its own conformance rows and its own declared preconditions. A missing primitive discovered by a library is a language change with sound semantics and a new profile version, never a per-program approval.

**Exact arithmetic and price conventions.** Freeze at R0: representation, width, signed extension, intermediate width, rounding mode per field, comparison policy, calendar and year-fraction classification, and the single price orientation. Rationals in the reference model must not silently become field arithmetic in circuits. A profile change invalidates affected signatures, proofs and certificates for the new version, as the completion program already requires.

**Open feasibility, stated plainly.** These are not settled by any supplied source.

- Native recursion is unreleased. Ledger 10 and pull request 738 are the only route, its fees do not yet charge accumulator work, and a zero guard disables verification. R3's certificate route may end interface-blocked.
- Whether in-circuit signature verification fits any measured k is unmeasured. The completion program calls this a preflight decision, and I keep it there.
- Whether the slice fits within the historical k 17 ceiling is unknown. The R3 native revision recorded row exhaustion at k 17, and the IVC example needed k 18.
- The prover for jet certificates is unchosen. Agda is precedent, not a decision.
- Solver completion soundness for arbitrary accepted witnesses is a research theorem, not a demonstrated result. MPLR-035 says so.
- Confidentiality requires a relational observation theorem in addition to the disclosure policy check. No such theorem is in scope before R4.
- The 277 ACTUS fixtures and 72 DeFi rows have no measured cost estimate under the existing resource ceilings.
- Exactness of price conversion under rounding in both orientations needs proof, not just round-trip tests.

The REVIEW file cites line ranges in those sources and I rely on its description. Claims about K0 to K5 and SP scope above are therefore inferred from the roadmap and review text only. Full source coverage is not claimed.

The next deliverable is the R0 target freeze and the two-asset conditional settlement slice with its hostile mutation matrix. Fourteen EARS requirements map every MPLR entry to a phase.
