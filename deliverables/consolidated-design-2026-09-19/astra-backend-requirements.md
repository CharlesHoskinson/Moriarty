# Requirements for the next native ZKIR and recursion interface

Independent PL semantics supplement, 19 September 2026. **The user's expectation of comprehensive Midnight recursion in approximately six months is a planning assumption, not a verified release date or capability claim.** Plan the complete Moriarty architecture against the required future interface while keeping current support and acceptance evidence explicit. Native Midnight Halo2-derived PLONK/KZG and ZKIRv3 remain the target. No Lean dependency or alternate proof backend is proposed.

The earlier language report recommends separate ledger-induction and recursive-certificate realizations of one history semantics. With the new steering, native general recursive compliance is an explicit target deliverable, not an optional substitute to be abandoned if the immediate ledger interface is absent. Ledger induction remains useful for currentness and unique consumption; it does not close MC03 or MC06.

## Sixteen proposed SHALL requirements

These are proposed acceptance requirements for a joint Midnight/Moriarty interface contract, not claims of existing platform promises. **C** means mandatory correctness or functional capability; **P** means a measurable performance/service requirement. Application-specific duties belong in Moriarty's relation; the backend must expose the cryptographic/constraint facilities needed to enforce them. Implementations may choose different sound constructions.

## Semantic requirements versus implementation choices

Moriarty needs general recursive compliance, not merely aggregation of unrelated valid statements. It needs a legitimate origin, compatible predecessors, preservation of signed policies and complete duties, and durable resource consumption. Those are semantic necessities. The backend need not embed words such as debt, intent or escrow as opcodes: it must faithfully constrain the application relation expressing them.

Fixed versus policy-checked dynamic VK selection, a dedicated join instruction versus repeated bounded verification, sequence IVC versus a bounded DAG wrapper, accumulator representation, commitment data structure and verifier batching are implementation decisions. Any chosen mechanism must satisfy the same statement and soundness contract. No curve cycle, foreign proof scheme or new theorem-prover dependency is mandated.

A final independently verifiable result may include auxiliary accumulator data if the full native verifier checks it and artifact size is honestly reported. “One proof” need not mean a single opaque byte array with no supporting statement. Constant final size, low join overhead and phone-class proving are performance aspirations until quantified. Soundness, complete final verification, bounded supported execution and permissionless relation authoring are mandatory.

## Delivery and preserved acceptance

Use the six-month assumption to coordinate interface specification and implement against versioned native fixtures, while maintaining current and forecast capability columns. Do not wait six months to define statement semantics or adversarial controls, and do not claim a proposed interface is deployed. First freeze ZR01–ZR12's contract and positive/hostile fixtures; then integrate actual native recursion and certify corresponding primitives; then run independent retained verification and exact ledger acceptance.

MC03 retains real native recursive financial steps, not signatures, hashes or ledger induction renamed recursion. MC06 retains private successor handoff, split, two branch histories and join, with complete residual duties and separately controlled principals. MC04/MC05 still establish actual artifact/ledger correspondence and mandatory acceptance. A backend demonstrator that proves generic recursion cannot close the financial claims. A correct local financial relation cannot close native recursion. If the eventual interface does not satisfy a mandatory item, record the precise missing capability and retain its milestone as open; do not silently shrink the product.

Tests above are proposed discriminators, not executed results, and cannot replace the required universal soundness/correspondence arguments. This supplement is suitable as a requirements negotiation draft; native performance feasibility and present deployment support remain unverified.
