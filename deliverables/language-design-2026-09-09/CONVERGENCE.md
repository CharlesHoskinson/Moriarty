## Substantive amendments and adjudication

1. **F02 embedding:** deferred. Future extraction must not execute the enclosing TS module and must reject interpolation independently of TS checking. Specify raw/cooked characters, escaping, committed bytes and source spans. Replacing the tag binding cannot produce a trusted artifact. A runtime tag call is not a safe extraction boundary.
2. **F05 inference:** keep public financial interfaces explicit; the initial pilot varies local inference only, with editor-independent type inspection and equal tools/training. Derived effect summaries remain compatible with declared interface limits.
3. **F06 updates:** immutable `pre`, single-write `next`, no `next` reads, persistent unwritten fields and suffix-only `post`. Named locals express intermediate dependencies. All restrictions require negative cases; parentheses alone are not proven to prevent confident misreading.
4. **F07 effects and authority:** conservative summaries may be inferred and checked against declaration limits. Quantitative signed authority is a separately authenticated input and visible in the proposal. The parent does **not** require an authority variable on every emission, despite Opus retaining that preference. Outcome intent requires no compulsory second exact-plan signature when the chosen route satisfies the original constraints.
5. **F11 duties:** unchanged identified duties carry forward automatically. One funded allocation/domain rule derives changed effects and successor duties; redundant assertions check rather than duplicate author intent. Authorized forgiveness, restructuring or migration needs its own named relation and recorded event. Expiry/cancellation is not a generic debt-deletion operation. Static checks reject decidable structural errors; value-dependent conservation remains preparation/proof work. The parent rejects a universal static-detection promise and any claimed necessity of a general linear calculus.
6. **F12 arithmetic:** rounding direction is instrument-policy-dependent. Reject policy mismatch, overflow and invalid division, not every result favorable to the payer. Policy changes identify affected calculations and duties.
7. **F13/F19 evaluation:** cover early syntax/setup failures as well as financial errors; assess interpretation and safe repair separately. Formative sessions can start now. Confirmatory preregistered comparison waits for a stable source/Core/K/evaluator prototype and calibrated pilot. No study was run here.
8. **F14–F17 identity/evolution:** normalize local binders only where the encoding says so; do not promise all public/nominal renames preserve identity. Compiler changes alter build identity; Core changes depend on actual elaboration. Retain old obligations and consumption history through authorized migration. Database editing is deferred, not permanently forbidden.
9. **F09/F10 work:** consumed accepted work plus all distinct live successors’ remaining allowance cannot exceed the original allowance. Splits partition, joins do not replenish, and recovery reserve is included. Compiler/proof budgets, scheduling liveness and witness availability are separate.
10. **F18 target:** Midnight Preview is fixed. Financial acceptance is unfinished; the settlement destination is not a design option. Preparation, proof checking, submission and observed acceptance remain separate states.

## Withdrawn claims and preserved dissent

Opus withdrew its claim that no supplied source tested financial authoring: U02 Auction/Casino are financial smart-contract authoring tasks. They do not establish Moriarty or TypeScript superiority. Its claim that Elm’s practical advantage is its error culture is retained only as an unsupported design opinion; F13 relies on the scoped diagnostic evidence. Opus also corrected the proposed typechecking-only interpolation guard, the blanket payer-favorable rounding rejection and the treatment of the Midnight target as unsettled.

The final labels preserve three important disagreements. Opus favored permanently excluding a database-centered source of truth on adversarial byte-review grounds; both GPT-6 seats rejected that permanent conclusion as unsupported. Opus converged to defer while retaining its rationale. The resulting requirement keeps ordinary source review initially without declaring database workflows inherently unauditable.

Opus’s revised duty proposal still asked for static rejection on every control path. The two GPT-6 seats and parent instead distinguish structural checks from state-dependent conservation and authorized domain resolution. Generic linearity neither proves quantitative conservation nor correct creditor identity; affinity can permit unwanted discard. The accepted design requirement is checked domain conservation, not a theorem that duty loss is universally unwriteable.

Teachability and identity display remain open. U02’s escape-hatch misuse motivates observing whether developers contort code to satisfy checks, but does not prove the recommended duty discipline is teachable. Full and abbreviated hashes both impose review costs; a readable name plus expandable exact references is a proposal, not a solved usability result. Restructuring with interest, fees and priorities still requires its own domain relations. No vote closes those gaps or the remaining ACTUS/DeFi/private-composition roadmap.
