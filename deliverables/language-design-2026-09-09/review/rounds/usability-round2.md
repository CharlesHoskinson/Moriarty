## Six resolution requirements

1. **Show a complete financial teaching case.** Replace the sole balance-decrement example as the primary copyable lesson with an identified duty, creditor, asset, allocation policy, authority constraints and bounded action; inspect transfer, surviving duty, remaining authority/work and prepared status together. If syntax is still proposed, mark it so and link the fragment to its complete agreement. `next.outstanding = remaining` alone is not an adequate F11 example. Do not invent implemented grammar to fill the gap.

2. **Conserve duties without duplicate author intent.** Agree with semantics: one specified financial operation/allocation determines its effects and successors, with redundant assertions acting as checks. Disagree with Opus's requirement for general linear values and universal static rejection: conservation can require state-dependent preparation/proof checks. A balance type alone is insufficient, but no inspected evidence establishes linear syntax as necessary. Check payment 30/debt 100, unauthorized deletion, authorized forgiveness, fees/accrual and timeout separately; retained history and liability constraints remain mandatory.

3. **Resolve familiarity through the existing update discipline.** Accept semantics' correction to my round-one uncertainty: prohibit `next` reads, rather than leaving Opus's explicit-read alternative open. Use locals for intermediate calculations. Formative tasks should expose confident immediate-mutation assumptions; persistent misreading can motivate a versioned spelling change, without asserting formatting always fixes it.

4. **Separate formative learning from comparative claims.** Paper prototypes can support immediate comprehension sessions. Confirmatory work requires stable executable semantics, comparable tools/training and calibrated tasks. Start with local inference, not inferred public interfaces. Include open-ended partial fills, correct recipients, residual duties and unsafe repair attempts. No human study was performed here; expert agreement establishes no usability advantage.

5. **Correct peer overclaims.** Opus's blanket payer-favorable-rounding rejection is policy-dependent. Its broad “none tested financial authoring” summary must exclude U02: Auction/Casino are financially relevant smart-contract tasks, though not evidence for Moriarty or TypeScript superiority. Settlement destination is fixed, feasibility open. Claims that Elm diagnostics are its actual practical differentiator, that an LSP is cheaper than a builder, or that duty loss becomes universally unwriteable are unsupported here. Retain them only as hypotheses with scope.

6. **Keep scope proportional.** Agree with semantics on derived effect/work bookkeeping. Explicit authority remains visible at its declaration and proposal; outcome-intent authorization must not acquire a compulsory second exact-plan signature. Preserve all ACTUS/DeFi/private-composition roadmap obligations despite deferring general streams, handlers, embedding and editor replacement. Byte-exact review does not by itself justify permanent rejection of database tooling; this is genuine dissent from Opus, not consensus.
