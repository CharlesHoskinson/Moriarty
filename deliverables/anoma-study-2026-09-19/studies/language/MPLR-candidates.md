# Unallocated language research candidates

These are proposals, not requirements implemented in Moriarty and not new MPLR IDs. Reconcile with the existing register before allocation.

## Admission profiles that survive compilation
Behavioral need: code carrying proof of signed formal intention must expose the exact checked language fragment and dependency closure. Evidence: JPL-01–04, including `terminating`, positivity/coverage switches and package-version bypass. Open theory: total core with explicit fuel versus structural recursion plus independently checked ranking certificates; trusted primitive boundary; decidable cost bounds. Negative witness: marked infinite recursion admitted as proved-total. Positive witness: terminating nonstructural algorithm accepted with a checked ranking proof. Do not ban permissionless authors; validate the submitted artifact.

## Closed proof obligations versus proof-shaped output
Behavioral need: never equate generated formal syntax with discharged obligations. Evidence: JPL-08 emits `sorry` and tracks dependency `main`; JPL-09 supplies substantive concrete-IR proof machinery. Open theory: small checker versus proof-producing translation versus per-run validation; dependency/proof-axiom manifests; source/IR/target composition. Negative witness: successful file generation with one admitted lemma. Positive witness: complete pinned certificate proves all transformation steps and links the exact policy and final target artifact. Compilation equivalence alone remains insufficient for policy correctness.

## Resource predicate completeness and linearity location
Behavioral need: each consumed/created resource and residual duty is covered by authenticated policy constraints, uniqueness and conservation. Evidence: JPL-10 Boolean predicates and Proof alias; the helper's all-list test is not a full transaction validity claim. Open theory: linear/effect types versus runtime resource machine plus proof obligations; closed-world effect enumeration; compositional aggregate invariants. Negative witness: vacuous empty predicate list, unmentioned fee debit, or duplicated resource. Positive witness: authenticated complete effect set with invariant-preserving transition. Never add authority units to asset units.

## Backend-relative meaning of proof carriage
Behavioral need: signed intent survives translation into exact pinned Midnight ZKIRv3 public/witness/effect semantics. Evidence: JPL-05–09 distinguish serialized Nockma, generated RISC0 guest projects, execution receipts and Lean IR obligations. Open theory: representation relation, arithmetic bounds, cryptographic primitive assumptions, trace refinement and private relational properties. Negative witness: valid receipt for wrong program/policy or hidden unauthorized effect. Positive witness: independently checked source-policy and target-statement binding. PCD may compose lineage under explicit base and uniqueness assumptions; it does not establish cross-chain finality, atomicity or liveness.

## Conditional settlement as a resource transition family
Refinement to existing conditional-settlement research, not necessarily a new topic. Need pending submitted requests, optional funding, configured combinations of evidence, mutually exclusive settle/refund transitions, partial fills and preserved residual duties. Evidence: legacy resource predicates offer an expression mechanism but no complete lifecycle established (JPL-10). Open theory: staged session/effect types and invariant induction versus one monolithic transition relation. Negative witness: late success followed by timeout refund; positive witness: authenticated late outcome updates an unresolved stage without erasing a known success. External documents and signatures need provenance and policy semantics; proofs cannot authenticate unspecified real-world truth.
