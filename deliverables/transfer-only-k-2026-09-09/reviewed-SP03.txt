## Bounded execution proposal

Campaign identifier: `sp03-bounded-repayment-k-01`. Before execution, the candidate
manifest and resource receipt under `deliverables/bounded-k-2026-09-09/` must bind
the existing command bytes, all K/codec/fixture/toolchain inputs, current reviewers
and initial counters. The proposal alone does not admit execution.

One LLVM O0 compile attempt, at most 180 seconds; at most 16 `krun` invocations,
at most 20 seconds each; 512 seconds cumulative for the entire run. Run only one
heavy command tree, with systemd user-service cgroup limits of 4 GiB memory and
zero swap. The supervisor must terminate the complete process tree on timeout.
Count every actual invocation and retain elapsed time, peak memory and failures.
Stop on the first failed required check or resource limit. No automatic retries,
compiler fallback, installation, network, `kprove`, native proof or public execution
is included. A changed candidate or exhausted allocation needs a reviewed successor.

These limits concern this local experiment. They neither reset old charges nor
establish feasibility of the broader roadmap. Formal claims remain explicitly
unproved; finite agreement is not elaboration, evaluator or ledger correspondence.
