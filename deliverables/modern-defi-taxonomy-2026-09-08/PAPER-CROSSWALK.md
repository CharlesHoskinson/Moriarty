## Selection bias and classification validation

The2023 Gogol selection already supplements TVL with aggregators and algorithmic stablecoins. Its three buckets are therefore not dismissed merely because capital-intensive protocols dominate the sample. The stronger objection is a unit mismatch: a Morpho allocator is a composition that holds lending exposures and issues a token; a payment stream creates conditional payment rights without needing a trading/lending pool; an order book is a matching mechanism regardless of custody. [Detailed cases](VALIDATION.md) test these boundaries with named components.

This benchmark selects structural diversity rather than capital ranking. For a future activity study, report spot/derivative volume for execution, outstanding principal and originations for credit, issued supply/redemption flows for money, managed assets and turnover for management, security capital and service payments for staking, cover exposure/premiums/claims for protection, and primary proceeds for capital formation. Fees, integration breadth and distinctive mechanisms can supplement these measures. No present-day market-share figures were collected here.

Do not add an underlying pool's assets, a vault's pool shares and a collateralized loan against those shares as independent net wealth. Distinguish gross claims, encumbered claims and ultimate underlying assets, retain valuation timestamps, and use a dependency graph to disclose repeated exposure. Recursive collateralization may increase outstanding obligations and leverage without increasing independently owned backing. A token's issued supply is neither its liquid float nor guaranteed redemption capacity.
