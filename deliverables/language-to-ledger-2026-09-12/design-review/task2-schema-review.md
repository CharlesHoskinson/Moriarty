## Supported design predicates and limits

The four closed action kinds and new state/effect versions separate legacy schemas. Exact original transfer amount plus unallocated remainder prevents duplicate or partial origination funding, including Repay-before-Originate and Originate-before-Repay. Reverse-role existing debt is needed to make a same-disbursement partial Repay reach the consumption check. Reusing transfer history across invocations cannot provide a current-step witness. Accrual does not move balances or allowances.

Principal<=initialPrincipal<=incurred<=cap and accrued<=incurred-initialPrincipal are preserved by all three allocation rules, origination and nonnegative accrual; repayment cannot recover cap. They establish necessary internal consistency, not complete historical reachability. The cursor sum and one-to-one origin history are feasible with retained settled obligations and zero-interest period IDs. They do not authenticate per-obligation history or prevent replacement by a different internally consistent projection.

The 0→100→110→80→0 trace, complete effect fields, lender110/borrower0 final balances, payment split20/10 then80/0, preserved unrelated Token rows and kernel remaining249/spent24/reserve16 check out.
