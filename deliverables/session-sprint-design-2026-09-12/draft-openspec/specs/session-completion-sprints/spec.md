## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Identity-preserving sprint scope
The sprint plan SHALL preserve each original task, requirement, scenario, case, classification row, and roadmap obligation by source identity. Historical evidence SHALL remain historical. The working tree MAY contain unrelated preserved changes.

#### Scenario: Retained obligations
- **WHEN** the draft reorganizes the original work
- **THEN** each original identity has an explicit sprint destination or retained wider-program destination

#### Scenario: Unrelated changes
- **WHEN** the checkout contains user/plugin/spec/wiki changes
- **THEN** the plan preserves their preimages and does not require a clean-tree reset

### Requirement: Independent preparation with one implementation writer
The implementation sequence SHALL use LL01–LL09 dependencies from design.md. Independent preparation MAY proceed in parallel. Behavioral edits SHALL have one implementation writer and separate independent review.

#### Scenario: Parallel preparation
- **WHEN** K execution awaits admission
- **THEN** ledger contract and executor fault-matrix preparation may continue without consuming that authority

#### Scenario: Dependent runtime
- **WHEN** LL03 or LL04 is incomplete
- **THEN** LL06 compilation acceptance remains open even if compiler inspection is ready

### Requirement: Retained K author and exact source repair
The K repair SHALL resolve E04-1 through E04-4 together using the retained Grok high author and fresh Astra medium audit. Astra author fallback SHALL require actual Grok credit exhaustion. The repair SHALL preserve the existing preflight-plus-execve and runner boundaries.

#### Scenario: Complete repair
- **WHEN** the exact candidate addresses HOME loading, executable selection, ownership, and stat/lstat findings
- **THEN** independent offline controls and a fresh source audit bind those exact bytes

#### Scenario: Unavailable author
- **WHEN** a provider times out or reaches a planning turn limit
- **THEN** the plan records that failure without claiming exhaustion or substituted author approval

### Requirement: One separately admitted diagnostic
Diagnostic dispatch SHALL require finalized current canonical authority for the sole C7/D1/G60 action. The diagnostic SHALL retain timeout20 including preflight, grace5, full charge60, exact trace106 artifacts, stack8MiB, and original accepted bounds. Source approval SHALL NOT confer dispatch authority.

#### Scenario: Admitted diagnostic
- **WHEN** all source, runner, pin, ownership, candidate, and debit identities are finalized and independently validated
- **THEN** the guarded plugin may execute the sole diagnostic and retain actual process evidence

#### Scenario: Missing admission
- **WHEN** authority is stale, missing, exhausted, or contains placeholders
- **THEN** the diagnostic remains blocked and no compile, retry, refund, or reusable slack is inferred

### Requirement: Complete scoped K acceptance
K acceptance SHALL require actual execution of every identified case and complete K-owned observations under the reviewed lifecycle contract. Backend correction and full-suite execution SHALL have separately reviewed resource authority. Differential agreement SHALL NOT establish a metatheorem or compiler/ledger proof.

#### Scenario: Complete observations
- **WHEN** all identified lifecycle and expression cases execute on the corrected backend
- **THEN** independent comparison checks complete state, errors, work, effects, identities, and ordered history

#### Scenario: Known-bad control
- **WHEN** debt components, allowance spent, work, cursor, effect metadata, or history order is mutated
- **THEN** the comparator rejects the result even when an aggregate total remains equal

#### Scenario: Native execution failure
- **WHEN** K crashes, times out, becomes stuck, or fails codec validation
- **THEN** the result is an execution failure and cannot count as semantic rejection or a passing case

### Requirement: Authenticated generated lifecycle consumer
The actual Source5-to-Compact and SDK path SHALL constrain source/Core/profile/program/head/deployment/network identity, authenticated state, authority, time, complete effects, and fees. The complete frozen ledger contract SHALL be independently reviewed before implementation.

#### Scenario: Complete lifecycle binding
- **WHEN** the consumer prepares originate, accrue, partial repayment, and settlement
- **THEN** the generated contract constrains all state transitions, liabilities, allowances, work, history, native assets, and required consents

#### Scenario: Substitution or forged context
- **WHEN** source, head, state, role, time context, amount, asset, or artifact is substituted
- **THEN** the actual consumer or contract rejects it without relying on a host validity flag

### Requirement: Actual compiler artifact acceptance
Compilation acceptance SHALL require the actual generated lifecycle Compact compiler invocation, retained toolchain and raw output, and exact source-to-artifact binding. Proposed commands and paths SHALL remain distinguished from inspected current interfaces.

#### Scenario: Generated compilation
- **WHEN** LL03 and LL04 pass and current compilation authority exists
- **THEN** the actual compiler produces retained Compact/ZKIR/runtime evidence that the consumer binds to the reviewed source

#### Scenario: Unrelated build artifact
- **WHEN** a mocked compiler, generic package build, or old fixed-loan artifact is presented
- **THEN** it cannot satisfy lifecycle compilation acceptance

### Requirement: Complete inherited executor and lifetime contract
LL05 SHALL retain every normative requirement, classification row, fault disposition, deadline, startup race, and audit requirement in the frozen afk-loan-executor specification. The real runner SHALL consume durable contained invocation-bound results. Process evidence SHALL remain separate from financial acceptance.

#### Scenario: Callable isolated source
- **WHEN** the actual executor, authority validator, runner handoff, static helper, and consuming runner pass all inherited isolated controls
- **THEN** source readiness is recorded with both required audits and live predicates remain pending

#### Scenario: Missing or contradictory evidence
- **WHEN** stdout reports success but main exit, invocation identity, or containment evidence is missing or contradictory
- **THEN** the runner preserves the specified failure or unresolved result and reservation rather than inferring success

#### Scenario: Namespace support unavailable
- **WHEN** the actual helper acceptance test requires unavailable namespace support
- **THEN** the test remains blocked and simulated output cannot replace the missing acceptance

### Requirement: Docker before separately admitted Preview
Docker acceptance SHALL require deploy and initialize followed by all four lifecycle actions, complete canonical native readback, rejected-action invariance, raw main exit, containment, and independent financial audit. Preview SHALL require successful Docker audit plus fresh current accounting, funding, capacity, and bounded guarded admission.

#### Scenario: Complete local acceptance
- **WHEN** the actual compiled consumer completes the admitted Docker lifecycle
- **THEN** each stage and the rejection control has canonical evidence with independently audited complete financial effects

#### Scenario: Source approval without current resources
- **WHEN** source audits pass but current accounting or live capacity is unavailable
- **THEN** Docker or Preview dispatch remains blocked without resetting history or creating new authority

### Requirement: Independent finalized Preview acceptance
Preview acceptance SHALL bind actual transaction bytes and identities to canonical finalized state, complete fee-inclusive native and financial effects, exact source/compiler artifacts, raw exit, and containment. Indexer status alone SHALL NOT establish finality.

#### Scenario: Finalized complete result
- **WHEN** all admitted lifecycle actions finalize with consistent canonical observations
- **THEN** independent audit records complete effects and explicit financial acceptance

#### Scenario: Partial public result
- **WHEN** a transaction is partial, unverified, or missing raw exit or containment evidence
- **THEN** the sprint remains incomplete and neither a new attempt nor a new wallet identity is inferred

### Requirement: Truthful completion and wider gates
Final reconciliation SHALL attach evidence or a precise open blocker to every retained obligation. A blocked sprint SHALL remain incomplete. Branch closure SHALL require all original branch outcomes. Native-goal closure SHALL additionally require its full retained scope. Wider program acceptance SHALL remain separately qualified.

#### Scenario: Branch evidence complete
- **WHEN** all six original branch outcomes have current independent acceptance
- **THEN** LL09 may record branch completion while separately evaluating the actual retained native goal

#### Scenario: Open wider obligation
- **WHEN** local tests or branch results are present but a proof, MC/SP, PCD, ACTUS, DeFi, or private-handoff gate remains unaccepted
- **THEN** the final report preserves that exact open gate and does not promote branch evidence into program acceptance
