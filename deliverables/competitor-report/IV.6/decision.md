# O10 destination-finality integration decision

Defer the two-chain order. The requested O1 fill and owner recovery settle on one Midnight ledger. Neither operation calls another chain. The recorded integration inventory identifies no committed venue, partner route, destination chain or destination verifier for this change. The beta bridge example uses a placeholder foreign test domain. Its escrow, claim and recover actions all return `Unsupported` on expansion and simulation and publish no effects.
