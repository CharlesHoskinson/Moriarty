# MIL/4 DeFi K proposal — family relations

**Status, 2026-09-29:** specified-only authoring artifact. `sol-defi.k` exports `M4D-SYNTAX`, `M4D-DEFI`, and the pure `m4dEval` relation for a master K module. It declares no configuration and makes no claim of parsing, execution, source/Core correspondence, native proof, or Preview settlement. I did not run tests or a K build, as requested. The file uses the `M4D` prefix throughout.

## Boundary and shared admission

`m4dEval` checks a common seal before a family relation. It requires the selected program to equal the family name and rejects empty signed bytes, heads and effect strings. The seal still compares caller-supplied copies of the predecessor, successor and effect digest. Family relations return `m4dProjected(family, postHead, effects, retainedDuty)` or a named rejection. `m4dProjected` is **only local arithmetic over supplied values**. `m4dAdmit` is the product-facing entry and returns `FAMILY_STAGE_ADAPTER_ABSENT` for every first profile, including bridge receipt and refund. Later profiles return `LATER_PROFILE_UNSUPPORTED`. Neither entry writes a ledger cell. This is a projection of MIL/3's six judgments, not their implementation.

The `m4dLocal`, `m4dForeign`, and `m4dNoReceipt` constructors distinguish evidence classes, domains, objects, rounds or epochs, payloads and verifier premises. They are assumed trusted inputs. An actual admission boundary must verify canonical bytes, issuer/key epoch, policy, finality and freshness before constructing them. A raw applicant must never be allowed to assert these constructors merely by writing their names. The head and effect digests likewise require a trusted derivation from registered cells and complete canonical effect lines. This is M4-C1, C4 and C5 work, not established by this file.

Kernel responsibility is to route, reserve, dispatch, observe and reconcile typed receipts while narrowing the one Moriarty-signed authority. The language owns the signed intent, selected program, exact effects, duty and history relation. A venue adapter owns native payload decoding, verifier/finality facts and exact observed financial effects. Kernel `unknown` is durable; a timeout starts reconciliation, never an automatic refund. This follows the [kernel API recommendation](../../wiki-llm/kernel-api-recommendation-2026-09-29.md).

## Exact gaps before this can serve as a reference

1. **Canonical and authority contract:** The K constructors currently accept strings and claimed evidence directly. Freeze `/4` bytes, digest and unknown-tag rejection; verify signatures, grant epochs, replay/tombstones, current authenticated heads, cell footprints and source-set preservation. The seal's `signedBytes` comparison is necessary but plainly insufficient.
2. **Complete effects and state:** The seal compares two effect digest strings. A successor must construct canonical balance, supply, reserve, custody, lock, debt and duty lines from the operation, then derive the digest and post-head. That is required to reject a hidden debit, altered recipient or missing retained duty. `m4dProjected` carries no admission authority; `m4dAdmit` fails closed until that derivation and authentication exist.
3. **Arithmetic widths and units:** K `Int` is unbounded; `m4dMax` guards selected operands and products but does not fully bound every intermediate in the AMM, CDP, option or vault expressions. This file does not choose target limbs or prove total rejection. `Price<Base,Quote,Scale>` orientation, decimal compatibility, exact fee-cap unit, protocol-reserve whole-unit line and fractional residue treatment must be frozen per primitive under [U0 D1–D4](../../docs/decisions/u0-numeric-profile-decision.md). A fractional quotient remainder is metadata, not a `Qty` effect.
4. **Family state registers:** AMM needs reserve-to-custody correspondence, pool authority and complete four-line transfer accounting. Loan needs actual existing obligation, creditor settlement and work/allowance consumption. CDP needs issue grant and quota, lock aggregate, global debt and supply heads. Option needs signed premium/funding, selected fixing policy, claim ID and reclaim transition. Oracle needs verification locus, key epoch and branch-independent policy admission. Governance needs queue/cancel state, an independently checked distinct approval set and explicit protected-duty migration. Bridge needs source/destination message and nullifier bytes, verifier/finality epoch, cumulative delivery, fee beneficiary, receipt count and a terminal nonreceipt premise that excludes later delivery. Vault needs share issue authority, total holder shares, donation/recognition policy and reserve beneficiary.
5. **Failure precedence and history:** The current relation has `SEAL` before family guards and one family code per failed conjunction. It does not yet provide the MIL/4 requested six-way `stage/intent/effect/authority/history/failure` classifier, a complete constructor/cell register, or trace continuity. In particular, changing only predecessor commitment or unpaid duty must select the correct judgment. These are design and implementation tasks before admission.
6. **Evidence and native path:** Foreign receipt and terminal nonreceipt are premises, not implemented verifiers. If no qualified foreign verifier exists, bridge stays specified-only. No profile here has passed source/5 → Core/4 → K differential checks, U1 primitive certification, U2 native proof binding or ledger effect observation. The guarded status remains SP01.6 blocked; this proposal does not consume its active delivery slot.

The first-profile predicates remain **specified-only**. A `m4dProjected` is a prepared arithmetic relation over supplied values; it is neither profile admission nor proof of any venue effect. For every family, the admission boundary is a combined stage with authenticated cells, verified evidence, signed authority, a complete exact effect vector, native proof correspondence and observed ledger settlement. The K file intentionally has no test claims. Positive and hostile rows above are **specified vectors** to be encoded only after the missing cells, evidence adapters and versioned choices are settled. No financial acceptance or investment safety follows from this proposal.
