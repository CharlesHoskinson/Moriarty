# MIL/4 finite family effect schemas

**Status, 2026-09-29:** specified-only K proposal. `sol-family-effects.k` exports `M4E-SYNTAX` and `M4E-EFFECTS`. It has no configuration. I did not run tests or a K build. The module is separate from `sol-defi.k` so a master stage can require both `m4dEval` and `m4eCheck` for the same operation and authenticated pre-state.

## What the module checks

`M4ECell` is a finite register of balance, reserve, debt, lock, supply, aggregate, grant, replay, observation, policy, queue, duty, claim, receipt, nullifier, managed, surplus, custody, share and budget cells. `M4EEffect` is a cell delta, exact tag update, one-shot consumption or retained-duty marker. Every first-profile rule pattern matches its **entire ordered read list, write list and effect list**. An omitted, reordered or extra entry reaches `CELL_OR_EFFECT_SCHEMA`; a later profile reaches `LATER_PROFILE_UNSUPPORTED`. This avoids acceptance through an opaque, caller-supplied effect digest alone.

The module checks **footprint shape and exact proposed deltas only**. It does not authenticate a cell's old value or prove that `m4eAdd` yields a valid post-value. It does not verify an oracle, foreign receipt, signature, grant or policy. Its `m4ePrepared` result carries a short identifying subset of the matched schema for the master stage; it does not itself commit ledger effects. The master stage must derive the canonical complete effect vector and post-head from the accepted packet and compare it with the signed intention and observed settlement. A typed constructor is a proposed representation, not a verifier.

| First profile | Exact effect lines in the proposed first action | Remaining precision gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AMM exact input | Trader A debit, pool A credit, pool B debit, trader B credit, both reserve deltas, gross budget debit, replay consumption and pool-head advance. | This uses rational fee retained in pool custody. A fee sent to a beneficiary adds a new balance line and must get a new schema. |

Originate/accrue are excluded. |
| CDP mint | Collateral owner debit and custody credit, lock increase, line debt and global debt increase, synthetic supply and owner balance increase, grant quota use and replay consumption. | Health, price orientation and decimals, grant ownership, cap and aggregate heads must be checked with authenticated pre-values. Burning/redemption has no rule. |
| Capped option exercise | Reserve custody debit, holder credit, one-shot claim and replay consumption. | **Not yet exact as an entire option lifecycle.** Funding, premium, selected fixing and permitted reclaim need frozen terms; `REM` cannot be trusted as a post-reserve amount without an authenticated pre-reserve. A one-unit underpayment must fail the companion payoff relation. |

## Combined-stage conditions

For an accepted result, the master stage must authenticate every read cell; check that each write was in the derived footprint; check before/after arithmetic and one-shot consumption; compute a canonical ordered effect vector from this exact schema; enforce per-domain asset conservation including supply and custody; retain obligations and replay tombstones; then bind the full vector to the signed bytes, proof public statement and actual ledger effects. No such master combination, native verifier or Preview settlement is established here. In particular, a matching `m4eAdd` line is a proposal, not evidence that the balance existed or was updated. Numeric post-state and evidence admissibility are **open premises for every family** in this module; `sol-defi.k` checks selected first-profile arithmetic, but that separate rule currently trusts evidence constructors and caller-supplied seal digest strings.

`m4eCheck` supports hostile schema discriminators without changing the operation's nominal quantities: remove the creditor credit from a repayment, add a hidden AMM debit, omit CDP debt, omit the governance retained duty, insert a bridge refund line, or supply vault shares without the share-supply line. Each loses the exact constructor pattern and rejects. These are specified cases only; no execution result is claimed.

The unresolved choices above come from the [MIL/4 working design](../mil4-successor-2026-09-29/DESIGN-MIL4-WORKING.md) and the [kernel API recommendation](../../wiki-llm/kernel-api-recommendation-2026-09-29.md). The kernel may observe and reconcile venue receipts, but only the Moriarty stage relation can accept a complete effect vector under the one signed authority. Unknown cross-domain outcome retains its duty and reservation; it never produces an automatic refund.
