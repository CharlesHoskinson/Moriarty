# MIL/4 composed contract relation

**Status:** specified only, 2026-09-29. [sol-contract.k](sol-contract.k) imports the static and transition proposal modules and declares no configuration. `m4cAccept(candidate)` returns either `m4cPrepared(effects,post)` or `m4cRejected(judgment,code)`. No K execution, native proof or ledger result is claimed.

The candidate contains one selected source and program ID, static environment, outcome formula and concrete `M4FFrame`, policy, authenticated predecessor, source event, successor head, proposed complete effects and post state, finite cell snapshots and effect lines, and three explicit premises. The `M4C` layer composes the `M4S` formation checker, `M4F` formula evaluator and `M4T` conditional escrow stage. It does not admit a `M4D` DeFi result by treating its returned label or digest as proof; each DeFi family needs a concrete state/effect adapter and separate qualification before joining this acceptance relation.

The six judgments run in this order:

| Judgment | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| `stage` | Frame heads match the predecessor and successor; the bounded, well formed outcome formula evaluates to `m4fTrue()` on that frame; `m4tPlan` defines the event. False rejects as `FORMULA_FALSE`, and undefined or unsupported evaluation rejects with `FORMULA_UNDEFINED` plus its cause. The frame and ledger predecessor are checked first for diagnostic precedence. |
| `intent` | An externally qualified statement covers the exact source/program, formula, policy, event, cells, effects and post state. A string or caller Boolean cannot create this premise. |
| `effect` | Pre and post snapshots equal the entire closed `M4T/1` cell register; effect lines equal its entire ten-line register; balance deltas conserve; the proposed post state equals the calculated one. |
| `authority` | The separate transition native premise covers profile, episode, intent digest, predecessor, stage, event, effects and post state. |
| `history` | A ledger premise reserves the current head and consumes the event receipt; the local consumed set rules out serial reuse. |
| `failure` | Unknown progress has zero financial effects. Refund closes escrow, enters recovering and retains gross, fees, delivered net, unpaid duty and residual authority. A changed duty is selected before effect equality as `DUTY_DROPPED`. Rejection publishes no successor in this relation. |

`m4cNativeQualified(statement)`, `m4tNativeQualified(statement)` and `m4tLedgerReserved(receipt,head)` are **named external premises**. No rule derives any of them from strings, host flags or a successful family calculation. The contract statement includes the exact `M4FFrame` and evaluation depth; the transition native statement now binds the entire signed policy. The intended native boundary must verify signatures, selected program/source correspondence, **authentication and completeness of every typed frame value at its stated head**, foreign observation provenance, predecessor authentication, complete ledger effects and the proof's public inputs. The K relation checks frame head equality and concrete formula truth; it does not independently authenticate frame values against a ledger or prove that an arbitrary formula cell corresponds to one of the `M4T` state fields. The ledger boundary must atomically compare the current head, consume the receipt and publish the successor. These mechanisms and their byte encodings are unresolved.

The closed registers are complete only for the one-domain, one-asset escrow slice: head, stage, phase, four balances, gross, fees, net, duty, remaining authority, remaining work, consumed IDs and escrow tombstone; plus four balance deltas and six accounting/consumption lines. Returned value is derived as gross minus net, fees and current escrow, so refund and final surplus stay visible without restoring gross headroom. Other policy, reserve, custody, supply, aggregate, obligation or disclosure cells require a larger registered profile. `M4T` uses mathematical integers; native finite widths, numeric correspondence, qualified history and actual Midnight settlement remain open. The one-slot closure reservation does not prove recovery liveness; a recovering duty has no discharge transition in this slice.
