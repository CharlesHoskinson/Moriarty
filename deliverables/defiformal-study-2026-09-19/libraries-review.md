Moriarty implication: use a small coherent language surface with composable source-bound financial libraries. Real reuse means one shared arithmetic/state/authority/invocation contract supports contrasting cases such as liquidity arithmetic and a stateful vault. Protocol-name coverage or wrappers around independent campaigns are weaker evidence.

Moriarty remains native Midnight/ZKIRv3; inspecting this Lean reference introduces no Lean tool dependency. Ported formulas require native implementation and checks; external formal results need explicit source/IR/runtime correspondence. Parent cross-comparison additionally found opposite price conventions: DeFiFormal Price(base,quote) is quote-per-base, while Moriarty Price<A,B,S> is base-per-quote. Normalize orientation explicitly alongside checked widths and rounding rather than copying type spelling.
