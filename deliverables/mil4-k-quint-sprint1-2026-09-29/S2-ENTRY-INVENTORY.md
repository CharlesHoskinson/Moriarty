## Decision gate and implementation packet

W-D1 must select exact scheme, digest and verifier locus plus the signature/proof/effect binding; W-D2 must select canonical tags and `/3`→`/4` policy. M4-C1 must settle Source/Core identity and price orientation; M4-C3/C4/C5 must settle certified arithmetic, footprint derivation and protected signed fields. Current scale caps also differ: the provisional wire codec admits scale 38 while Source/6 S0 admits 18. A rule must either reconcile them or reject the larger wire domain before a cross-layer claim.

After the S0 gate, the first bounded Sprint 2 implementation packet should add a shared Φ₀/selection/filling slice around the already executed S0 transfer. Freeze the independent cases above first. Adapt surge `sol-static.k` and `sol-formula.k` into one MIL/4 K root, add missing nominal sorts or named rejects, and model immutable scope and narrowing in Quint. Historical Core/4 expression constructors and K expression modules are adaptation candidates only after a field-by-field compatibility result; prior SP03 passes do not discharge MIL/4 coverage or proof obligations.
