Write, check and test your first agreement

This tutorial installs the Moriarty beta command-line tool, creates a transfer agreement, checks it, runs it against a local scenario and then makes a test fail on purpose. The interactive page needs JavaScript; the same steps are in packages/moriarty-beta/GETTING-STARTED.md.
