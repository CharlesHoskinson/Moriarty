Marlowe and ACTUS

Registration is Closed

Time & Location
---------------

03. Juni 2020, 16:00 – 17:00 MESZ

Online Event

About the event
---------------

Marlowe is a language for defining financial smart contracts, running on Cardano. Our invited speakers, Simon Thomson (senior research lead at IOHK and emeritus professor at University of Kent) & Dmytro Kondratiuk (Financial Contract Engineer at IOHK), will describe the rationale and high-level design of Marlowe and how ACTUS contracts can be implemented using Marlowe.
