    ACTUS Competition
    Coming Soon

    The ACTUS Financial Research Foundation is organizing a

     

    COMPETITION

    ​

    for building prototype applications that use ACTUS. The challenge for competitors is to provide services or applications utilizing ACTUS to provide increased value for financial institutions and other users with critical reliance on financial contracts. Examples of such services might include programs that increase the ease of incorporation of ACTUS into existing systems as well as applications enabling special types of analysis, such as the field of risk analysis. Both commercial proprietary and open-source applications are welcome.
