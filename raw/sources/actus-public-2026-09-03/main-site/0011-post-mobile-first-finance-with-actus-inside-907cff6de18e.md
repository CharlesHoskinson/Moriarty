Mobile First Finance with ACTUS Inside
======================================

* ![Writer: ACTUS FRF]()

  [ACTUS FRF](https://www.actusfrf.org/profile/actusfrf/profile)
* Jul 13, 2020
* 1 min read

Updated: Sep 29, 2020

**Speaker:**

Shirish Kalangi, Founder of Mobile First Finance, Partner at Ariadne Business Analytics and ACTUS Contributor

**Abstract:**

Mobile First Finance is a rapid app development platform for Fintech.  Using the new platform financial services organisations can launch their mobile apps faster and with less coding involved.

In this webinar Mr. Kalangi narrates the journey with ACTUS, shares user success stories and describes how ACTUS technology is remodelling digital financial services.
