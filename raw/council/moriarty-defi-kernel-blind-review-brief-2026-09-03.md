# Moriarty council round 2: blinded critique

Date: 2026-09-03 UTC

This is a tool-free advisory task. Three independent proposals were normalized
below as A, B, and C. Their order does not identify their authors. Critique the
positions, not the likely provider. Do not browse, edit, or claim verification.
Return no more than 1,200 words.

## Shared evidence

Use the evidence and qualifications in the round-1 brief. The draft XML now
contains 12 workstreams, 25 required experiments, 22 deliverables, 18 numeric
release gates, a Scrapling-only web policy, the wiki/graph loop, a 72-row
manifest, seven family demonstrations, 13 legacy regressions, and a library-only
stop path.

## Position A

- Keep M5 as the sole internal formal profile and generated Compact as the only
  initial backend.
- Treat M2+M3 only as a versioned corpus index. Reduce the economic families to
  four primary mechanisms: exchange, credit, derivative, and consensus claim.
- Demote F5 to an off-chain claim-source facet, F6 to a mandate facet, and
  Prediction to a conditional-token mechanism. Facets are explicit but not all
  imply an external dependency.
- Admit at most authority, bounded mandate, finite strategy-as-mandate, and
  conditional-token split/merge beyond the Marlowe-shaped Core.
- Stop if generated Compact contains an unbounded container or unconstrained
  witness callback.

## Position B

- Retain M2+M3 as a falsifiable human-facing taxonomy and M5 as the
  machine-checkable semantic profile. They never determine Core constructors.
- Keep F1-F6 and Prediction provisionally, but add explicit multi-membership,
  liquidity, insurance, governance, sequencing, oracle, custody, and promotion
  rules.
- Start with seven minimal applications: swap, loan, option, staking claim,
  redeemable external claim, bounded mandate, and binary conditional token.
- Use a small finite Core, typed surface, capability protocols, generated
  Compact, translation validation, client verification, and hard proof/cost
  gates.
- Keep a serious no-language fallback: seven audited Compact templates plus an
  obligation and assumption manifest.

## Position C

- Add F0 conditional transfer for escrow, vesting, streams, and payments. Rename
  Prediction to contingent claims. Keep F4/F6 ordered dual membership and define
  off-chain as state not governed by the target ledger.
- Define Moriarty Core as an explicit delta from Marlowe in the same proof
  environment. Add bounded naturals, reject rather than clip partial payments,
  add one finite atomic Step and typed Attest, and produce a certifying
  elaborator.
- The audit object is a triple: normalized Core, generated Compact, and a
  translation-validation certificate.
- Use seven demos, but use a sealed-bid auction for F1 and compose F6 with F2 and
  contingent claims with F1 to test cross-family behavior.
- Require a full proof run, deterministic artifacts, and a disclosure manifest;
  a negative answer on ledger time can stop the project.

## Draft synthesis choices to critique

1. Keep the seven labels only as a candidate human taxonomy. Require the study
   to test the four-mechanism alternative, F0, Insurance, Payments/Streaming,
   Privacy, and formal-only M5.
2. Make the facet schema mandatory, with `none` or `not-applicable` allowed, so
   no facet silently implies an oracle, custodian, or legal dependency.
3. Use atomic swap, not auction, as the smallest F1 proof demo. Add auction as a
   privacy and sequencing stress test.
4. Specify Core as a semantic delta against Marlowe, but do not preselect a proof
   assistant until contributor and proof-reuse evidence is measured.
5. Treat partial-payment reject-versus-warning, bounded-natural representation,
   atomic Step, Attest, authority, mandate, and split/merge as open semantic
   motions, not accepted syntax.
6. Require 72 compilable bounded instances or honest outside-kernel manifests,
   not 72 full protocol clones.
7. Keep generated Compact as the initial backend and direct ZKIR deferred.
8. Require a client-checked artifact/disclosure manifest, 10,000 differential
   traces per demonstration, full proof generation, two clean builders, zero
   critical/high audit findings, and two written non-toy pilots.
9. Fall back to an audited Compact library if the DSL has no measured assurance
   or review advantage.
